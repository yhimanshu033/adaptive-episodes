In this episode, Quinn meets with Layla to conduct experiments with her blood. He collects blood samples in test tubes and uses his "Inspect" skill to gather information. Quinn discovers that drinking blood from the same person doesn't grant additional stat points, but blood from new individuals might. He also learns that the blood can heal him if he's injured.

Meanwhile, Peter and Vorden discuss Quinn's potential new girlfriend, with Vorden showing a brief moment of changed expression when hearing about it.

As Quinn and Layla finish their meeting in a secluded area of a park, they overhear Rylee, a student known for bullying, threatening another student for credits. Quinn, seeing an opportunity to test his theory about gaining stat points from new blood, puts on a mask he bought earlier, implying he plans to confront Rylee and possibly take his blood.

The episode reveals more about Quinn's developing vampiric abilities and the potential for him to gain strength by consuming blood from different individuals. It also hints at ongoing tensions and secrets among the group of friends, particularly involving Vorden's reactions to Quinn's relationships.
