Quinn, desperate for blood, bites Layla in the library, regaining his health and gaining a strength point. Realizing what he's done, he takes the unconscious Layla to the infirmary. The doctor, Hayley, treats Layla's wound without suspecting Quinn's involvement. Quinn contemplates his new vampiric nature and the need to keep it secret. He considers using Layla as a test subject to understand his blood-drinking abilities better and attempts to draw her blood with a syringe. However, he's interrupted by Peter, who urgently seeks Quinn's help as Vorden is in trouble.
