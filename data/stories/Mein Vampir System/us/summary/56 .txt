After his intense battle with Nate in the VR game, Quinn reflects on the lessons learned. He realizes his blood swipe attacks were ineffective against Nate's metal ability, but his Hammer strike skill proved to be a powerful internal attack. Quinn also recognizes the importance of stamina management, especially when using skills like Flash Step.

Quinn meets with Layla on a Sunday to learn more about managing his Halfling condition. The sun severely affects him, halving his stats and causing discomfort. Layla arrives carrying a large bag, which she claims to be lightening with her ability.

They go to a quiet park where Layla unpacks various items to help Quinn cope with sunlight, including thick black clothing, sunscreen, and an umbrella. She explains that she bought these items from the marketplace, an online store that sells everyday items as well as combat-related gear for military personnel and travelers.

The marketplace uses teleporters for instant shipping, and the military school has a collection point for students. Quinn is concerned about the cost, but Layla assures him that her family provided the credits and she used what the academy gave her.

As they prepare to test the various sun protection methods, Quinn reflects on the importance of getting stronger in a world where peace may be temporary. The episode ends with Layla asking Quinn which item he wants to try first.
