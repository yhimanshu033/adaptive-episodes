Quinn and Vorden explore the academy's library, which features advanced technology like orbs that create isolated spaces for private conversations. Quinn searches for information about abilities similar to his own, particularly those affected by sunlight. Vorden, unaware of Quinn's true ability, laughs off the idea of an ability weakened by sunlight.

Unable to find relevant information in the ability-related books, Quinn ventures into the fiction section and reads a book about vampires. Although most of the vampire traits don't apply to him, he does relate to the weakness to sunlight.

As Quinn closes the book, he unexpectedly receives a system notification, gaining 10 experience points for acquiring "more knowledge about the system." This brings his total experience to 15/100. The sudden appearance of the notification surprises Quinn, suggesting that the vampire book may have some connection to his mysterious ability.
