Quinn discovers that defeating opponents in the VR game "Power Fighter" grants him experience points in his real-life system, albeit at a reduced rate. Excited by this development, he decides to use the game to level up and grow stronger without consuming blood. He quickly defeats several low-level opponents using his Blood Swipe ability, which doesn't drain his health in the game.

After reaching level 4, Quinn increases his Agility stat and learns the Flash Step technique. He notices that the game's white room allows for unlimited practice without fatigue, unlike actual matches where stamina is consumed.

Quinn then challenges a stronger opponent, a caped man with wind-based dagger attacks. During an intense battle, Quinn uses his newly learned Flash Step to surprise his opponent and secure victory. The match is observed by the caped man's senior, who is shocked by the outcome.

Intrigued by Quinn's abilities, the senior challenges him to a fight. Unbeknownst to Quinn, this new opponent is one of the strongest students from another military school, setting the stage for a potentially more difficult battle.
