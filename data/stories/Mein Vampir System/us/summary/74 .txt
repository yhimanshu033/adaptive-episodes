Quinn wakes up on the alien planet, realizing his situation is real. He receives system messages about his hunger and completing a daily quest. Upon opening the container door, he discovers a dangerous Scordana beast in the hangar. Using his inspect skill, Quinn learns it's an intermediate-level beast. He also notices Scordana eggs nearby, realizing the creature won't leave soon.

Faced with two options - waiting or fighting - Quinn decides to attempt sneaking out. As he reaches the hole he originally entered through, a Rattaclaw appears. The Rattaclaw's squealing alerts the Scordana, which attacks. Quinn narrowly avoids the attack using his flash step skill, but the Rattaclaw is killed instantly. Quinn realizes he must now face the Scordana in battle.
