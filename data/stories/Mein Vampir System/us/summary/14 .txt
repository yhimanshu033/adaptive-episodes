Quinn, Peter, and Vorden are escorted by second-year students to meet Mono, a high-level student. Mono warns them about the unwritten rules of the school, stating that high-level students shouldn't associate with low-level ones. He threatens that powerful people within the school might get involved if they continue their friendship.

Quinn attempts to use his Inspect skill on Mono but finds the status screen blurry and unreadable. Frustrated by Mono's attitude, Quinn tries to punch him but misses. Vorden also attempts to grab Mono, but fails.

After Mono leaves, the group discusses the situation. Quinn explains that the school allows this bullying because it motivates weaker students to seek power through the military. Quinn reluctantly suggests that they comply with the unwritten rules and separate from Vorden for their own safety.

Vorden, angry at the situation, storms off. Quinn and Peter feel bad about the outcome but believe it's necessary for now. Meanwhile, Vorden, though understanding Quinn's reasoning, is determined to challenge the system and go after those trying to control his life.
