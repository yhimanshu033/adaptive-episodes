Quinn encounters a beast called a Rattaclaw on the unknown planet. He uses his blood swipe ability to injure it, but the creature adapts and attacks again. Quinn manages to defeat the Rattaclaw after a struggle, gaining experience points and leveling up to Level 5. He also unlocks a new skill called "Blood spray."

Quinn inspects the Rattaclaw's black blood and discovers it's poisonous if consumed. He contemplates why his body only accepts human blood. Before he can search for the beast's core, ten more Rattaclaws appear. Outnumbered, Quinn flees into a nearby building and up a staircase, preparing to use his new Blood spray ability to defend himself from the pursuing creatures.

The experience highlights the dangers of the unknown planet and the potential for Quinn to gain strength and new abilities through combat with alien beasts. It also emphasizes his need to adapt and strategize in this hostile environment.
