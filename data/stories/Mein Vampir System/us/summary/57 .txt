Quinn and Layla meet in a park to test various methods of reducing or eliminating the effects of sunlight on Quinn's half-vampire condition. They try several approaches:

1. A special UV-blocking umbrella, which successfully restores Quinn's stats when he's fully covered.

2. UV-blocking sunscreen, which proves ineffective.

3. Layered black clothing, which only works when Quinn is completely covered in multiple thick layers, rendering him immobile.

They conclude that blocking direct sunlight contact with Quinn's skin is crucial, but the methods tested are impractical for combat situations. Quinn recalls the cape used by Sam in the VR game, which was both flexible and strong enough to block his blood swipe attack. He realizes that a suit made from similar beast-derived material might solve his sunlight problem while being combat-ready.

The episode ends with Quinn excited about the prospect of hunting beasts to obtain this material, looking forward to their upcoming portal excursion.
