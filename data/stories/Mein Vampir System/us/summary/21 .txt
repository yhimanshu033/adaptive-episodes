Quinn and Peter discover that their friend Vorden is in trouble. They rush to the assembly hall, where they find evidence of a violent fight. Quinn uses his "Inspect" ability on blood traces and confirms some belong to Vorden. Unable to get information from other students, they return to their dorm room.

They find Vorden there, badly injured and emotionally distressed. Vorden refuses help and tells them to stay away, calling himself a monster. Quinn, overwhelmed by the smell of Vorden's blood, leaves the room abruptly to control his urges.

Quinn realizes he needs to deal with Layla, who might wake up and remember his attack. Meanwhile, in the doctor's office, Layla regains consciousness and begins to recall the events in the library where Quinn bit her.

The episode highlights Quinn's ongoing struggle with his new vampiric nature, the mysterious situation surrounding Vorden's fight, and the potential consequences of Layla remembering Quinn's attack.
