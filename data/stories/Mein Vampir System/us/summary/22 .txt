In this episode, Layla wakes up in the doctor's office, remembering Quinn biting her. She experiences a tingling sensation and finds the memory oddly addictive. Layla covers for Quinn by telling the doctor it was a beast attack. Meanwhile, Quinn rushes to the doctor's office, worried about Layla's reaction, but finds she has already left.

Layla confronts Quinn, and they go to the library to talk privately. She reveals that she has been observing Quinn for a while and has concluded that he is a vampire. To Quinn's shock, instead of threatening him, Layla begs him to turn her into a vampire as well. She explains that it's her childhood dream to become a vampire, something she thought only happened in books and fairy tales.

Quinn is stunned by Layla's request and realizes that she kept his secret because of her own fascination with vampires. He finds her desire to become a vampire crazy and is left unsure of how to handle this unexpected turn of events.
