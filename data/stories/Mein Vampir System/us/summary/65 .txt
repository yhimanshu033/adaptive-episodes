The episode provides background on the discovery and use of teleportation devices in this world. Initially hoped to enable space colonization, these portals instead led to unknown planets harboring deadly beasts. The discovery of beast crystals and the creation of beast weapons became crucial in the war against the Dalki.

Vorden jumps through a red portal to follow Quinn, experiencing a strange sensation during teleportation. He arrives on a dark planet with crumbled buildings, suggesting a former civilization. Vorden realizes the danger of the situation, as red portals often lead to hazardous planets.

Meanwhile, Quinn also arrives on the same planet, confused by Peter's actions. He finds himself in a similar ruined city environment. Quinn notices something moving quickly around the buildings, using them as cover. As he backs away cautiously, a beast suddenly emerges and charges towards him.

The episode highlights the dangers of unexplored planets, the limited abilities of the characters in this new environment, and sets up a tense situation for both Vorden and Quinn as they face potential threats on this mysterious world.
