In this episode, Quinn faces the aftermath of the surprise attack by Fei and Brandon during combat class. Despite being stabbed, Quinn remains calm and attempts to retaliate, but is stopped by Leo, who punishes Fei and Brandon for their actions. Quinn is sent to the doctor's office but instead goes to the bathroom to heal himself.

As Quinn removes the blades from his stomach, he discovers that his body has used up its blood energy to heal, triggering his hunger earlier than expected. His vampire teeth grow slightly, and he plans to target Fei and Brandon for blood.

Quinn overhears an argument between Fei and Brandon, revealing tension in their relationship. Quinn decides to follow Fei, seeing an opportunity.

Meanwhile, Peter and Vorden return to their dorm room and find Quinn's bloodied clothes. Vorden becomes agitated and worried about Quinn's safety. When Layla arrives looking for Quinn, Vorden aggressively confronts her, demanding to know what happened to Quinn.

The episode ends with tension building around Quinn's whereabouts and the growing concerns of his friends.
