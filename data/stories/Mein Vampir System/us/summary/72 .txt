Quinn explores a storage room filled with ability and skill books. He discovers he can absorb books he can't learn for experience points, with ability books giving more points than skill books. He levels up twice, reaching level 8, and distributes his stat points evenly.

While searching, Quinn finds a level 6 Shadow element ability book that is compatible with his system, unlike any other book he's encountered. He stores this book in his system storage for later examination, wondering about its connection to his vampire abilities.

Quinn reflects on his transformation from human to something else, realizing this might be why he can potentially learn multiple abilities. He also remembers Peter's betrayal and decides not to help him anymore.

Feeling tired, Quinn decides to rest inside the container, which he believes is safe from the beasts outside. The episode ends with Quinn lying down and closing his eyes.
