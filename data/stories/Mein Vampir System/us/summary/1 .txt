The episode introduces Quinn, a bullied high school student on his last day of school. He is harassed by other students and even attacked with a magical ability, showing that in this world, people have special powers. Quinn lives alone in a small apartment provided by the government, as his parents died when he was young.

Quinn possesses a mysterious book left by his parents, which he has been trying unsuccessfully to open for years. The world is described as having faced a war with an alien race called the Dalki, which led to the discovery and development of special abilities among humans.

After another failed attempt to open the book and breaking his glasses, Quinn accidentally drops blood on the book. This causes the book to open and emit a bright light. As Quinn loses consciousness, he hears a partial congratulatory message, suggesting he has been granted some kind of power or ability.

The episode sets up a world where special abilities exist, social inequality is prevalent, and introduces the mystery of Quinn's inherited book and its potential significance to his future.
