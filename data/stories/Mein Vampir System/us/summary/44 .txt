The episode focuses on the chaotic situation in the assembly hall of the military academy. Peter, feeling guilty for his role in Vorden's capture, leaves to make amends. Meanwhile, Erin and Layla face off against two level four second-year students. Erin easily defeats her opponent, showcasing her superior fighting skills and natural talent. Layla struggles against her water-using opponent and is knocked down.

Vorden, still restrained, calls out to Layla for help. Together, they use Layla's telekinesis to free him from his shackles. Once free, Vorden's behavior changes dramatically, suggesting a shift in personality.

Vorden engages the water-using student who had defeated Layla. He displays unexpected agility and clever use of telekinesis, manipulating the black balls to attack the student from behind. Vorden breaks the student's ankles and arms, rendering him unable to use his water abilities effectively.

The episode ends with Vorden declaring, "Finally, I, Raten, have returned," implying that a different personality has taken control of Vorden's body.
