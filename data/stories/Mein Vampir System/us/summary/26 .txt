Quinn, wearing a mask, confronts Rylee who is bullying another student. Quinn tests his new ability, "Blood Swipe," which allows him to attack from a distance. Layla assists by shooting an arrow at one of Rylee's friends. Quinn defeats Rylee and his two companions, collecting blood samples from each of them.

Quinn and Layla retreat to the school roof, where Quinn tests the blood samples. He discovers that different blood types grant him different stat bonuses:

- A type blood gives him strength
- B type blood gives him agility
- AB type blood gives him stamina

Quinn also learns that drinking blood restores his HP, but he only gains stat bonuses the first time he drinks from a specific person. He wonders what effect O type blood might have, as it's the only blood type he hasn't consumed yet.

This episode shows Quinn's moral development as he uses his abilities to stop a bully, but also reveals his growing comfort with using others for his own gain. The blood type system introduces a new layer of complexity to Quinn's abilities and potential future actions.
