In this episode, Quinn deals with the aftermath of his fight with Loop and Fei on the school rooftop. He drinks Loop's blood, discovering it's the rare O type, which fills his blood bank and gives him a free stat point. Quinn decides to add the point to his Agility, bringing it to 14.

Quinn realizes that the bite marks on Fei's neck have disappeared, likely due to his ability. As he contemplates what to do with the bodies, Layla arrives and warns him that guards will be coming soon. They escape by jumping off the roof, with Layla using her ability to slow their descent.

Quinn explains the situation to Layla, who suggests forming their own group for protection. She also mentions that something happened with Vorden and tells Quinn to ask Peter about it.

Meanwhile, in the doctor's office, Dr. Hayley examines Loop and Fei. She notices puncture wounds on Loop's neck, similar to what she had seen on another student before. Concerned, she contacts her father, a General, to investigate a possible stray beast in the school.

The episode ends dramatically with guards bringing in a critically injured student named Brandon Richardson. Despite Dr. Hayley's efforts, Brandon dies, and she records his time of death as 9:30 PM.
