Quinn faces off against a dangerous Scordana, a scorpion-like beast with a human upper body, on the alien planet. He uses his Blood Swipe ability and Flash Step to dodge attacks, but the creature's exoskeleton proves tough to penetrate. Quinn is severely injured by the Scordana's hidden tail, forcing him to use his Blood Bank to heal.

In a desperate move, Quinn uses Flash Step to get behind the beast and delivers a powerful Hammer Strike, killing it. The fight leaves Quinn exhausted and with low health. He receives experience points for killing the Scordana and its eggs, leveling up to 9.

However, Quinn's hopes that leveling up would restore his health are dashed. He realizes that despite game-like elements, his situation is very real. To make matters worse, he receives a message that he can no longer suppress his need for human blood, and his HP will decrease hourly until he consumes some.

The episode highlights Quinn's growing combat skills, the dangers of the alien world, and the increasing urgency of his vampiric nature. It also emphasizes the reality of his situation, despite the game-like system he experiences.
