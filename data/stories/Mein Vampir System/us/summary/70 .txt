Vorden and Ian, a wounded traveler, continue to explore the alien planet in perpetual darkness. Ian explains that the planet's day-night cycle lasts a year, and they are currently in the six-month night period. Ian shows Vorden a high-quality beast crystal he obtained from an advanced beast.

As they explore, they discover a dead Rattaclaw with its head bashed in, still containing its beast crystal. They follow a trail of black blood, hoping to find Vorden's friend Quinn. The trail leads them to a building where they find ten dead Rattaclaws with their beast crystals removed, suggesting a powerful force killed them.

From the roof, they spot an intermediate beast - a six-legged creature with a large mouth and four claws. The beast enters a well-maintained storage building. Ian advises against approaching it due to his injured state and the beast's strength.

Vorden continues to communicate with his alter ego, Raten, who suggests killing Ian for his valuable crystal. Vorden refuses, recognizing the need for Ian's abilities and knowledge to survive on the planet.

The situation raises questions about Quinn's whereabouts and capabilities, as well as the presence of other potential survivors or teams on the planet. Vorden and Ian must navigate the dangerous environment while searching for a way back to Earth and potentially locating Quinn.
