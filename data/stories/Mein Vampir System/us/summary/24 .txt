In this episode, Peter is concerned about Quinn and Vorden's strange behavior. He overhears Vorden talking to someone, but can't see anyone else present. Quinn and Layla are seen leaving the library together, and Quinn promises to meet her later.

Quinn reassures Peter that they'll figure out what's happening with Vorden together. In class, Vorden seems to be back to normal, but Quinn notices he's holding something back when asked about the incident at the assembly hall. Other students whisper and call Vorden names like "monster" and "freak," hinting at a school-wide secret.

The teacher, Dell, introduces combat classes, presenting a list of categories based on students' abilities. Quinn and Vorden's names are not on the list, as their abilities are unique or don't fit into standard categories. Students are allowed to choose their preferred combat class, with the option to switch later. Quinn contemplates which class would be best suited for him.
