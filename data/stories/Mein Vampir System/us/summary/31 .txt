In this episode, Quinn explores his new abilities after leveling up. He gains 5 HP and one stat point, which he adds to agility. He also unlocks a new skill called "Blood Bank," allowing him to store up to 100 liters of blood and use it to heal himself or consume for pleasure. This skill solves his blood storage problem and acts as a second life.

Quinn returns to his dorm to find Peter and Vorden roughed up from their combat classes. They share stories about their experiences, with Vorden impressing others by defeating a level 3 user.

The next day, Quinn receives a message about his growing hunger and the need to consume blood. He uses his Blood Bank skill to satisfy his hunger without needing to drink fresh blood immediately.

In the following week, combat classes continue as students prepare for their first portal outing to hunt beasts. During a sparring session where students can choose their opponents, Layla approaches Quinn. However, their teacher, Leo, intervenes and asks Layla to sit out. Instead, Leo challenges Quinn himself, telling him not to hold back. This unexpected turn of events sets up a potentially revealing confrontation between Quinn and his observant teacher.
