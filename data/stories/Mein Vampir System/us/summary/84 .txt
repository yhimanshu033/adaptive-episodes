In this episode, a meeting is held at the military academy to discuss the disappearance of Quinn and Vorden. General Nathan and Fay inform a select group of teachers that the students have entered a red portal leading to the abandoned planet Pioletic. They plan to send a rescue team to retrieve Vorden's body, prioritizing him due to his family connections, while considering Quinn less important.

Meanwhile, Layla and Erin learn about the students' location through gossip. Layla rushes to her room and uses a secret communication device to contact "Agent 83," revealing her involvement in a covert organization. She inquires about agents on Pioletic to help Quinn, whom she believes will be valuable to their cause. However, she learns that there are no agents currently on the planet.

The episode highlights the different reactions to Quinn and Vorden's disappearance, the academy's attempt to manage the situation, and reveals a hidden aspect of Layla's character and her mysterious affiliation with a secret group.
