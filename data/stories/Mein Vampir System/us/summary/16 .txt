In this episode, Quinn and Peter return to their normal school routine, avoiding other students after recent conflicts. They notice Vorden's absence at dinner but find him in their dorm room later. Vorden apologizes for his earlier behavior and shows signs of having been in a fight.

Quinn, still hungry after dinner, receives credits from Vorden to buy snacks at a nearby convenience store. On his way there, Quinn tests his new physical abilities, discovering improved stamina and night vision.

At the store, Quinn encounters Rylee and his friends. He decides to follow Rylee, buying a black mask with a blood-splatter design to conceal his identity. Unbeknownst to Quinn, Layla observes his suspicious behavior and decides to follow him.

Rylee separates from his friends, heading back to the school through a deserted park. Quinn prepares to confront Rylee, putting on the mask and referring to the situation as "showtime." This sets the stage for a potential confrontation between Quinn and Rylee in the isolated park area.
