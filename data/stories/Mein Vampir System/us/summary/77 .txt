Quinn, in a desperate situation with low health, finds a piece of metal from a destroyed mech and uses it to distract the Rattaclaw guards at the entrance of a dome-like building. He rushes to the entrance and attempts to open the steel doors using an access code he obtains with his inspect skill. However, the Rattalcaws return with reinforcements, forcing Quinn to fight them off using his abilities like Flash Step, Blood Burst, and Blood Swipe. He manages to kill all four Rattalcaws but is left with only 2 HP.

Quinn enters the building, which appears to be a well-maintained training center. He searches frantically for a portal, hoping it will be his way back home. Despite exploring various areas like the main training hall, preparation rooms, offices, and gym, he finds no sign of a portal.

As his health continues to decline, Quinn collapses in the center of the arena, feeling despair and questioning why his parents left him with the mysterious book. His vision blurs, and he can no longer think or move properly. When his HP reaches zero due to hunger, a message appears indicating that a transformation will now begin.
