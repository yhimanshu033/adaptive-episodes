In this episode, Vorden struggles with sleep and his multiple personalities while on the dangerous planet. He and Ian, his injured companion, take turns keeping watch in the library where they've sought refuge. Vorden's alter ego, Raten, suggests killing Ian for his advanced crystal, but Vorden refuses.

Their rest is interrupted when they discover they're surrounded by slug-like creatures with large eyes and tentacles. Ian uses his ability to create a protective ball of metal scraps around them, then repels it outward, killing the creatures. They collect crystals from the dead beasts, with Ian sharing some with Vorden.

As they leave the library to continue searching for a portal home, they spot a large dome-like structure that Ian believes could house a secret base and potentially a portal. The building appears to be a well-built training center that has withstood attacks from the planet's creatures. They decide to head towards this new destination, hoping it might be their way back to Earth.
