Quinn, disguised with a mask, ambushes Rylee in a dark park to complete his quest. The fight begins with Quinn having the upper hand, but he soon discovers Rylee's ability to harden parts of his body. Quinn adapts his strategy, targeting unprotected areas, and eventually knocks Rylee out.

After defeating Rylee, Quinn receives experience points and reaches the maximum amount needed to level up. He eagerly accepts the level-up option, despite a warning about irreversible changes.

As Quinn levels up to level 2, he undergoes an unexpected and painful "evolution process." After five minutes of agony, he is informed that he has successfully evolved into a "Halfling," a term unfamiliar to him.

Quinn's stats increase, and he unlocks a new skill called "Blood swipe," which allows him to fire a red energy line from his hands at the cost of 1 HP per use. However, the most shocking development is that Quinn now has an insatiable hunger for human blood. His HP will decrease by 1 point every hour until he consumes human blood, presenting him with a dire and morally challenging situation.
