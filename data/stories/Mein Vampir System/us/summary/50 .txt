Quinn and Vorden explore the city, discussing Vorden's ability to wield multiple powers. Vorden, belonging to an Original family, can't reveal much about his abilities due to family rules. They visit a game store where Vorden introduces Quinn to a popular VR game called "Power Fighter."

The game allows players to fight in a virtual environment, replicating their real-world physical attributes. While it can't record actual abilities, it has a database of known powers that players can select and practice with. This explains how Vorden can effectively use various abilities despite his power resetting daily.

Vorden demonstrates the game's interface, showing Quinn his user profile and the extensive list of abilities available. He explains that the game is used as a training tool in military schools and can match players based on their power levels.

Quinn realizes this game could be perfect for practicing his fighting skills without revealing his identity. Although the VR pods are expensive to buy, Vorden informs Quinn that the academy has a room full of them for students to use. They decide to head back to the academy to try out the game.
