In this episode, the first-year students are gathered in the assembly hall by second-year students, led by Momo. They reveal a badly beaten Vorden tied to a wooden plank as an example of what happens when rules are broken. Momo introduces beast weapons and explains how to activate them using special metal balls infused with beast crystal power.

The second-years force the first-years to participate in "target practice," using Vorden as the target. When a student questions the activity, they are violently beaten. The first-years are terrified and reluctant to participate, but the second-years threaten them with similar treatment if they refuse.

Momo explains that Vorden has been targeting second-year students, and this is their way of punishing him and warning others. When a female student refuses to throw a ball at Vorden, Momo hits her with one instead.

Quinn and Layla watch from the back, horrified by the situation. Layla suggests Quinn should get Leo, a teacher, to stop this. However, when she turns around, Quinn has disappeared. To her shock, Quinn volunteers to participate, stepping forward with a smile. The episode ends with Layla stunned by Quinn's unexpected action.
