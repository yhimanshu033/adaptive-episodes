In this episode, Peter is waiting in the dorm room when Momo and two second-year students arrive, forcing Peter to go with them for unknown plans. Meanwhile, on the school rooftop, Loop discovers Quinn attacking Fei and engages in a fierce battle with him. Loop uses his bone manipulation ability to create weapons and attack Quinn, who struggles initially but manages to heal himself using his blood bank ability. Quinn eventually overpowers Loop using a combination of his vampire abilities, including blood swipes and rapid healing. After defeating Loop, Quinn prepares to drink his blood, curious about the effects of blood type O-. The fight demonstrates Quinn's growing combat skills and reliance on his vampire abilities, while also revealing the strength of other students' abilities.
