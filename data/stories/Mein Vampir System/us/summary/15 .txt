In this episode, Layla observes Quinn and his friends from afar, confused by Quinn's seemingly weak punch during their encounter with Mono. Quinn and Peter are confronted by three level 2 students who demand credits from them. Quinn receives a new quest to defeat a student named Rylee, with the reward of 50 Exp and additional exp due to the power level difference.

Quinn decides to pay the credits to avoid a fight in his weakened state but plans to identify Rylee once inside the building. However, the bullies remain outside, forcing Quinn to improvise. He shouts Rylee's name and threatens him, causing the red-haired boy to react angrily, revealing his identity.

Quinn and Peter run away to their next class. Quinn checks his Quest menu and is pleased to see that the quest remains active, allowing him to complete it when the opportunity arises. The episode ends with Quinn determined to defeat Rylee to level up.
