The episode continues the story after the fight between first and second-year students at the military academy. A silver-haired woman named Fay Dallum, a sergeant in charge of squad training, enters the scene and quickly stops the fight using her super-speed ability. She orders injured students to the doctor's office and the rest to their dorms.

Quinn and Vorden, both injured, go to the doctor's office. The doctor, Hayley, is surprised by Quinn's quick healing and assumes another student healed him. Quinn lies to cover up his abilities.

Back in their dorms, Quinn, Vorden, and Peter discuss the potential consequences of the fight. Vorden suggests that the academy might look for one person to blame for the incident.

Meanwhile, Momo is being interrogated by Duke King, a general and head of the second-year students. It's revealed that Duke had instructed Momo to instigate the conflict. Duke promises to protect Momo from consequences as long as their "little secret" remains hidden, implying a deeper conspiracy within the academy.
