In this episode, Quinn faces his opponent Brandon in a combat class at the military academy. Despite Brandon's higher power level, Quinn easily defeats him using his enhanced strength and speed, which are a result of his vampire-like abilities. Quinn breaks Brandon's spear and quickly overpowers him, winning the match and leveling up to Level 3.

After the fight, some students accuse Quinn of cheating, suggesting he used abilities which were forbidden in the arena. However, the teacher, Leo, who is revealed to be the famous "Blind Swordsman," defends Quinn. Leo explains that his own ability allows him to see people's auras and detect when abilities are being used, confirming that Quinn did not cheat.

Throughout the episode, Quinn continues to struggle with his desire to consume blood, noting it as a "waste" that he couldn't drink Brandon's blood after the fight. He also unlocks a new skill called "Blood Bank," though he doesn't have time to examine it immediately.

The episode ends with Leo becoming suspicious of Quinn. He notices that Quinn's aura is different from other students, resembling that of beasts or Dalki rather than humans. Leo checks Quinn's file, which shows no ability and a power level of 1, leaving him wondering what Quinn might be hiding.
