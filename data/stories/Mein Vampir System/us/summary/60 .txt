In this episode, Erin, the strongest student in Del's class, approaches Vorden to join his team for the upcoming portal expedition. This surprises everyone, as Erin had previously shown disinterest in teaming up. Vorden initially declines, stating he's already committed to a team with Quinn, Layla, and Peter.

Erin, not recognizing Quinn and the others, questions Vorden's choice to team up with "weak" people. She suggests that joining her would lead to better grades, which are crucial for future opportunities. Vorden, however, prioritizes having fun over grades.

Quinn intervenes, stating that Erin can join only if Peter stays on the team, refusing to abandon his friend for someone stronger. Erin reluctantly agrees but requests a re-testing of the team's abilities.

The group heads to the training center, where other students are also assessing their abilities in preparation for the expedition. Layla demonstrates her telekinetic strength, scoring an 8 on the strength machine. Erin acknowledges Layla's ranged abilities as useful for the team.

Peter, despite being a Level 1 earth user, also scores an 8 on the strength test, surprising Erin. The episode ends with Erin waiting for Quinn to demonstrate his abilities, building tension around his performance and how it might affect the team dynamics.
