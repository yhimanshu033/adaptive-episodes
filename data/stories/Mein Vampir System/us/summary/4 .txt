The episode provides background on the ability system in this world:

- Abilities were introduced to humans during the war with the Dalki.
- There are eight power levels, with subcategories within each level.
- Abilities can be obtained by being an "Original" or learning from ability books.
- Originals are generally stronger and can access higher levels.
- Lower-level ability books (1-5) are sold publicly, while higher levels are kept by the military and corporations.

Quinn, who previously had no ability, now has a mysterious power from an unusual book. He's at the military school for testing, but his new ability is affected by sunlight, halving his stats.

Quinn meets Vorden, a friendly student who offers help. However, when they shake hands, Quinn's system detects and rejects an ability use attempt by Vorden. This makes Quinn suspicious of Vorden's intentions.

The episode ends with Quinn, Vorden, and three others being called for their ability test. Quinn is worried about his performance due to the sunlight weakening his abilities.
