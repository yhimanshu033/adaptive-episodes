In this episode, chaos ensues among the school staff after two students, Quinn Talen and Vorden Blade, accidentally enter a red portal. The incident is particularly concerning because Vorden belongs to an influential family. Nathan, the head of first-years, instructs Sergeant Fay to keep the situation secret, especially from Vorden's family.

The next day, Layla notices Quinn and Vorden's absence in class. Peter, their friend, acts suspiciously when questioned. The school cancels the planned portal outing, citing staff being busy with another matter.

Layla becomes increasingly worried about her missing friends. Erin informs her about rumors of two students entering portals, which Layla connects to Quinn and Vorden's disappearance. Realizing Peter might know something, Layla confronts him. Peter, feeling guilty and scared, runs away. Layla and Erin decide to chase after him, believing he has crucial information about what happened to Quinn and Vorden.

The episode highlights the tension between the school administration and the powerful original families, as well as the growing mystery surrounding the missing students and the red portal incident.
