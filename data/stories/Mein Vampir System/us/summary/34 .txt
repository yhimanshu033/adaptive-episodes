In this episode, tensions escalate as Vorden aggressively confronts Layla about Quinn's whereabouts. Layla defends herself by stabbing Vorden with an arrow, revealing her telekinesis ability. After Layla leaves, Vorden displays erratic behavior, seemingly arguing with multiple personalities.

Meanwhile, Quinn follows Fei to the school rooftop, intending to drink his blood. Quinn ambushes Fei, using his enhanced strength and gauntlets to quickly overpower him. Quinn then drinks Fei's blood, replenishing his health and gaining stat bonuses.

Just as Quinn finishes feeding, Loop arrives on the rooftop and discovers the scene. He sees his friend Fei covered in blood and Quinn, masked, with his teeth in Fei's neck. Loop reacts with shock and anger, calling Quinn a monster.

The situation has become increasingly complex and dangerous, with Quinn's secret at risk of exposure and the potential for serious consequences from his actions.
