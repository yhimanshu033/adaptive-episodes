The episode begins with Quinn being taken to military school, a mandatory two-year program for all individuals due to the ongoing tension between humans and the Dalki. As Quinn steps outside, he experiences a new system notification that halves his stats when exposed to direct sunlight. He also receives a daily quest to avoid sunlight for 8 hours.

Quinn boards a bus full of unfamiliar students, hoping for a fresh start. During the journey, a sleeping gas is released, affecting most students. Quinn shows some resistance but eventually succumbs. The students wake up in an unknown location, which is revealed to be their new home for the next two years.

The military school is located in a hidden, technologically advanced city with futuristic buildings and machinery. The students are given wristwatches that serve as their identification and access devices. These watches will also display their power levels.

Sergeant Griff informs the students that they will immediately begin testing to determine their respective levels. Quinn realizes that his life at military school might not be as different from his previous experiences as he had hoped.
