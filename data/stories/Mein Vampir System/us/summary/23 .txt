In this episode, we learn more about Layla's background and her fascination with vampires. Her father was a writer, and she grew up surrounded by fantasy stories. When abilities became real, her interest in vampires only intensified.

Quinn explains to Layla that he's not a true vampire, but gained his abilities from a mysterious book that disappeared. He reveals that he can't turn her into a vampire because she already has an ability, and a person can only have one ability.

Layla offers to keep Quinn's secret on the condition that she can help him. She suggests that if he needs to feed, he should feed on her to avoid suspicion. Quinn agrees to this arrangement, seeing the advantages of having an assistant who knows about vampires.

Meanwhile, Vorden enters the library and sees Quinn and Layla talking. He becomes jealous and possessive, stating that Quinn is his and he won't let anyone hurt him.

This episode explores the developing relationship between Quinn and Layla, as well as introducing a potential conflict with Vorden's possessive behavior.
