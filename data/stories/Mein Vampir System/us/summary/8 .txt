In this episode, Quinn deduces that Vorden's ability is to copy other people's abilities through touch. Vorden confirms this and it's implied he's an Original - someone from a family with unique, powerful abilities. After the tests, the students are teleported to the academy and given a tour by their teacher, Del. Quinn notices social dynamics forming based on ability levels, with he and Peter being the only level 1s in their class. Vorden, however, befriends Quinn and includes Peter in their group.

The tour includes various facilities, but Quinn is most interested in the library, hoping to find information about his own ability. After the tour, students are assigned dorm rooms. Quinn and Vorden discover they're roommates, with Vorden exclaiming it's fate bringing them together. However, it's revealed that Vorden actually forced another student to swap room numbers with him to be with Quinn. This suggests Vorden has a particular interest in Quinn and is willing to use his high-level abilities to manipulate situations.
