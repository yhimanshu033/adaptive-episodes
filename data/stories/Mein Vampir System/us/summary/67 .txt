Quinn faces off against a group of Rattaclaw creatures on a staircase in a ruined building on an unknown planet. He uses his new "Blood Spray" ability to knock back multiple enemies at once, but it's not powerful enough to defeat them. He then resorts to using "Blood Swipe" repeatedly to kill all the Rattaclaws, depleting his HP in the process.

After recovering, Quinn extracts beast crystals from the defeated Rattaclaws. He discovers he has an inventory system where he can store the crystals. In total, he collects 10 basic tier beast crystals, each worth 10 credits.

Quinn climbs to the roof of the building to survey his surroundings. He notices that the structures resemble human buildings and spots one with the same crest as his school. He identifies it as a military storage facility, similar to the one in his home city. Quinn believes this building might house the portal back home or, at the very least, contain valuable equipment and resources he can use.

The episode ends with Quinn planning to investigate the military storage building, hoping to find a way back or take advantage of the opportunity to acquire useful items for his survival and potential return.
