In this episode, Quinn undergoes a dramatic transformation into a "Crazed Bloodsucker," losing his human consciousness and becoming a feral creature driven by bloodlust. His appearance changes drastically, with his hair falling out, skin tightening, and developing claw-like hands. The transformation doubles his stats but halves his health and renders his blood skills unusable.

The Bloodsucker, formerly Quinn, roams the training center, destroying lights due to its sensitivity to brightness. When Vorden and Ian enter the arena, the creature attacks them, drawn by the scent of Ian's blood. Ian and Vorden discover that the beast is sensitive to light, but this proves insufficient to deter it.

The Bloodsucker engages in a fierce battle with Ian, demonstrating incredible strength and agility. It manages to bite Ian's arm and begins consuming his blood, partially paralyzing him. Vorden, initially hesitant to help, eventually intervenes by using his newly acquired metal manipulation ability to attack the creature with a chair-turned-spear, knocking it away from Ian.

The episode ends with Vorden standing in the arena, surrounded by metal chairs, declaring his intention to find his pet Quinn - unaware that the beast he just attacked is actually his transformed friend.
