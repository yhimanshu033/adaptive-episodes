Quinn volunteers to throw a ball at Vorden, but instead targets one of the second-year students guarding him. This sparks chaos in the hall as Quinn attempts to create havoc and gain experience points by attacking weaker second-year students. His goal is to level up and potentially gain a new ability to help him in the fight.

Momo, the leader of the second-years, orders his classmates to capture Quinn. He also threatens the first-year students, telling them to help catch Quinn or face punishment. This leads to Layla questioning Momo's authority, and when Momo orders an attack on all first-years, Erin steps in to defend her classmates. Layla joins Erin in the fight, distracting the higher-level second-year students.

Quinn continues his plan to target weaker second-years, but Momo intervenes with a beast weapon whip, severely injuring Quinn. Quinn finds himself surrounded by six second-year students, in a dire situation.

Meanwhile, Vorden, still tied up, feels guilty for Quinn's predicament and wants to help. A voice in Vorden's head offers to take over, hinting at his multiple personalities.

The situation has escalated into a full-scale conflict between the first and second-year students, with Quinn at the center of it all.
