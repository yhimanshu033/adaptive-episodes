Quinn and Vorden visit the VR room at the academy to play "Power Fighter," a popular virtual reality game used for combat training. The room is filled with VR capsules, and students must pay credits to use them. Vorden covers the cost for both of them.

Once inside the capsules, Quinn enters the game as a guest and joins Vorden's room. Vorden demonstrates the game's character customization feature, showing how players can change their appearance.

Quinn watches Vorden play a few matches to learn how the game works. The game simulates an arena-style combat environment where players use different abilities to fight each other. Vorden chooses to use Earth abilities, as he's trying to improve his skills with this power.

During the matches, Quinn observes that each player has a health bar, but the exact amount of health is not displayed. The game simulates combat without causing actual physical harm to the players. Hits on vital areas are treated as critical hits, but players can continue fighting until their HP reaches zero.

Although Quinn doesn't get to play himself due to time constraints, he's excited to try the game the next day. The VR system offers a way for Quinn to train and improve his abilities anonymously, without revealing his true identity or special powers.
