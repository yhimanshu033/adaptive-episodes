Vorden and Ian enter a large dome-like building, which appears to be a training center. They find dead Rattaclaws near the entrance, indicating someone else had been there recently. Using their combined metal-controlling abilities, they force open a locked door and narrowly escape a group of pursuing Rattaclaws.

Inside, they discover the building is unusually dark, with all the main lights smashed. They use small light-emitting stones for visibility. Vorden reveals to Ian that he can copy abilities, explaining how he used Ian's metal-controlling power.

As they explore, they reach a reception area and then enter the arena stands. Suddenly, they hear fierce growls and the sound of a creature rushing towards them. Despite Ian's warning to turn off their light stones, it's too late - the creature has spotted them and is charging at full speed, letting out a loud screech.

The episode ends on this cliffhanger, with Vorden and Ian facing an unknown but clearly dangerous creature in the dark arena.
