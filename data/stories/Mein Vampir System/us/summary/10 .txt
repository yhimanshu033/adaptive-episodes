Quinn discovers that reading a fantasy book about vampires unexpectedly gives him experience points. He tests other books but doesn't receive any more experience, leading him to theorize about the system's mechanics. After spending six hours in the library, Quinn completes a daily quest and gains more experience points.

While heading to the battle arena, Quinn encounters a bullying situation involving Peter, his roommate. Despite initially hesitating, Quinn decides to intervene, seeing it as an opportunity to test his strength against a weak opponent. He purposely bumps into the bully, interrupting the attack on Peter.

The bully, a level 1.2 ability user, confronts Quinn. As the situation escalates, Quinn's system detects an enemy ability user and initiates "battle mode." Quinn receives a new quest to defeat his opponent, with a reward of 50 experience points. Peter, unaware of Quinn's abilities and motivations, worries about Quinn's safety and reflects on his own past experiences with bullying.

Quinn, excited about the opportunity to gain experience quickly, prepares to face the bully, much to Peter's confusion and concern.
