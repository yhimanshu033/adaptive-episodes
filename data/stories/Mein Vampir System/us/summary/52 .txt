Quinn and Vorden return to their dorm room, finding Peter already asleep. They discuss keeping an eye on him due to concerns about bullying. Quinn stays up late watching tutorial videos for the Hammer Strike skill.

The next morning, Quinn wakes up early and heads to the VR room to create his character and test his new skill. He rents a capsule for two hours and notices some students bringing their beast weapons, realizing the game can recognize them.

Quinn creates an account with the username "Blood Evolver," reflecting his evolving abilities from consuming blood. He designs his character with red spiky hair and simple villager clothing.

In the game's white room, Quinn discovers that his real-life abilities and system are still functional. He can move freely, access his stats, and even use his blood swipe ability. This revelation surprises Quinn and opens up new possibilities for his training in the virtual world.
