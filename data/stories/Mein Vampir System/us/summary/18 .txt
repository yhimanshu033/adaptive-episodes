Quinn realizes he has become a vampire-like creature after his transformation. He struggles with the moral implications of needing to consume human blood to survive. After defeating Rylee, Quinn is tempted to drink his blood but ultimately can't bring himself to do it. Layla secretly observes this strange behavior.

Quinn returns to school, checking his appearance and stats. His HP has increased by 50%, and he gains one stat point which he uses to increase his Agility. He considers the optional quests to gain more stat points by drinking blood but remains hesitant.

Back in his room, Quinn lies to his roommates about his whereabouts and tries to sleep. However, he's in pain and can't rest easily. The next morning, Quinn discovers he has lost 10 HP over 10 hours due to not consuming blood. His HP is now critically low at 5/15, and the system warns it will continue to decrease hourly. Quinn faces an urgent dilemma of how to sustain himself without compromising his humanity.
