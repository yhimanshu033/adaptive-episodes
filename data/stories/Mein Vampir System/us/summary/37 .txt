The episode continues the story of Quinn, a student with vampiric abilities, at a military academy. After his recent fight with Loop, Quinn returns to his dorm room to find his roommates, Vorden and Peter, absent. When they return, both seem troubled, but Quinn decides not to pry.

The next morning, Quinn reflects on his need to improve his combat skills and level up to access the system's shop function at level 10. He also realizes he needs to be more careful about using his abilities to avoid detection.

While walking to the canteen, Quinn encounters Loop, who surprisingly asks to speak with him privately. Expecting a confrontation, Quinn is shocked when Loop apologizes for the previous day's events and begs for forgiveness. Loop promises to keep Quinn's secret and offers help if needed.

Loop's behavior confuses Quinn, especially when he mentions not wanting "what happened to Brandon" to happen to him. This cryptic statement leaves Quinn puzzled about what might have occurred to Brandon, one of Loop's friends who had previously bullied Quinn.

The episode ends with Quinn questioning Loop about what happened to Brandon, setting up a potential revelation in the next episode.
