In this episode, Quinn faces off against Leo, the blind swordsman, in a sparring match. The other students watch with interest, especially Brandon and his friends who are suspicious of Quinn's abilities. Quinn uses his inspect skill on Leo but gets no information, suggesting Leo's power level is too high. The system offers Quinn an instant level-up reward if he defeats Leo.

The fight begins, and Leo gradually increases his speed and skill, testing Quinn's abilities. Quinn manages to keep up, impressing Leo. However, Leo ultimately wins by using a technique that phases his blade through Quinn's defenses. Leo estimates Quinn's skill level to be around 13 or 14, which is impressive for a first-year student.

Quinn asks Leo to teach him how to fight, but Leo suggests he gain experience through a popular VR fighting game instead. As a reward for the enjoyable match, Leo allows Quinn to keep the gauntlets.

After the match, Brandon devises a plan with his friend Fei. In the next round of sparring, Fei faces Quinn while Brandon faces Layla. During the matches, Brandon uses his wind blast ability to knock Quinn off balance, allowing Fei to strike Quinn with his blades. This attack reduces Quinn's HP to 10/20, leaving him injured and angry at the underhanded tactic.
