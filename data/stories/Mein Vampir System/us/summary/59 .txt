The episode explains the portal system used for interplanetary travel in this world. There are three types of portals: green (explored planets with human shelters), orange (partially explored planets), and red (unexplored planets, potentially dangerous). The portal technology was discovered from a captured Dalki ship and developed by a legendary scientist named Richard Eno.

Del, an instructor, briefs the students about an upcoming portal expedition. He explains that students must form teams of five for the trip, which will take place next week. The destination will remain secret until the day of the expedition to ensure fairness.

Quinn, Layla, Vorden, and Peter quickly form a team of four. As they consider who should be their fifth member, Erin approaches Vorden, expressing interest in joining their team. This development sets the stage for the upcoming portal expedition and potential challenges the team may face together.
