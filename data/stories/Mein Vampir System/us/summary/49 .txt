After the incident with the second-year students, Quinn realizes he needs to become stronger to protect himself and his friends. He considers consuming different types of blood to increase his power but decides against it due to increased security at the academy. Instead, he focuses on improving his fighting skills.

Quinn, Vorden, and Peter go into town for a day out. While there, Peter spots a group of high-level first-year students he knows and excuses himself to meet them. Concerned for Peter's safety, Vorden offers help, but Peter insists he's fine and leaves.

Peter meets with five first-year students led by Earl, who is angry about Peter involving teachers in their previous incident. Earl and his group brutally attack Peter, breaking his fingers repeatedly and healing them with Jerry's ability. They demand Peter's cooperation for another task.

Initially, Peter refuses to betray Quinn and Vorden again, enduring an hour of torture. However, the continuous pain eventually breaks his resolve, and he agrees to their demands. Earl warns Peter to complete the task correctly this time without involving anyone else.

The episode highlights the ongoing tensions and power dynamics within the academy, as well as the pressure and manipulation faced by weaker students like Peter.
