Quinn is questioned about Brandon's death by a general and a woman with truth-detecting abilities. He truthfully denies involvement and is cleared of suspicion. After returning to class, Layla suggests Vorden might be responsible, given his strange behavior. Quinn considers this possibility, remembering Vorden's absence the previous night.

Later, first-year students, including Quinn and Layla, are gathered in the assembly hall by second-year students. Momo, a second-year student, addresses the crowd, warning against challenging the established system. He then reveals Vorden tied up and beaten on stage, shocking Quinn and the other students.
