In this episode, Layla and Erin confront Earl's group about Quinn and Vorden's disappearance. Erin demonstrates her powerful ice abilities, freezing the group and intimidating them for information. They learn that Peter allegedly pushed Vorden into a portal, possibly explaining Quinn's disappearance as well.

As Layla and Erin leave, they encounter Peter. Erin quickly restrains him using her ice powers. Peter, initially defiant, breaks down and admits he didn't want to push Vorden but felt he had no choice due to ongoing bullying and fear of retaliation.

Layla, disgusted by Peter's actions, reminds him that he always has a choice. She reflects on Quinn's efforts to help Peter and is disappointed by his betrayal. The episode highlights the complex dynamics between the characters and the consequences of their choices, while also showcasing Erin's formidable abilities and Layla's compassionate yet firm approach to the situation.
