Quinn, now a halfling with vampire-like traits, is struggling with his new condition. His HP is rapidly decreasing due to not consuming blood, and he's running out of time. At breakfast, he nearly attacks Rylee but is stopped by Dan, another student. Vorden and Peter intervene to prevent a fight.

Throughout the morning classes, Quinn's condition worsens, and he finds it increasingly difficult to control his urges. By lunchtime, his HP has dropped to 1/15, causing him to experience violent visions and lose control of his body.

Desperate for a solution, Quinn searches the library for information on vampires in fiction books but finds nothing useful. As his condition deteriorates, Layla finds him in the library looking pale and unwell. She approaches to check on him, but Quinn, unable to resist his urges any longer, apologizes weakly before biting into her neck.
