In this episode, Layla and Erin search for Peter to find out what happened to Quinn and Vorden. After failing to locate Peter, they encounter Earl and his group of boys who seem to know something about the situation. Layla attempts to get information from them peacefully, but they refuse to cooperate.

Erin, frustrated with the lack of progress, takes matters into her own hands. She demonstrates her powerful ice abilities by creating an ice spear and pinning Earl to a testing drum, which registers her strength at 20. She then freezes the entire group in place using her ice powers.

The episode ends with Erin demanding information from the trapped group, showing her determination to uncover the truth about Quinn and Vorden's disappearance. This scene highlights Erin's formidable abilities and her no-nonsense approach to problem-solving, in contrast to Layla's more diplomatic methods.
