In this episode, Quinn faces a challenging opponent named Nate Snell, a second-year student from another academy known for his strength. Nate, impressed by Quinn's flash step technique, challenges him to a battle in the VR game.

The fight begins with both charging at each other. Quinn uses his blood swipes, but Nate's ability allows him to turn his body into metal, rendering Quinn's attacks ineffective. Nate counterattacks with a metal pole, pushing Quinn back and dealing damage.

Quinn struggles against Nate's metal ability, which can shift to protect different parts of his body. After taking several hits, Quinn decides to use his flash step technique. He manages to get behind Nate and land a powerful blow using the hammer strike, dealing significant damage.

However, the use of flash step twice and the hammer strike exhausts Quinn completely. He collapses on the ground, unable to move, and Nate finishes the match by defeating him.

Despite losing, Quinn's performance impresses Nate, who looks forward to the upcoming Military tournament. The episode highlights Quinn's growing skills and the challenges he faces as he continues to develop his abilities in the VR game.
