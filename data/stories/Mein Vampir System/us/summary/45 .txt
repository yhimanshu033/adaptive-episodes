In this episode, Quinn finds himself surrounded by second-year students and under constant attack. Despite his ability to heal using his blood bank, he's unable to escape the situation. He manages to use one student as a shield but is eventually cornered against a wall.

As Momo prepares to attack Quinn with his powerful whip, Vorden, now under the personality of Raten, intervenes. Raten challenges Momo, demonstrating his telekinetic abilities by manipulating black balls as weapons. However, Momo easily avoids and destroys these attacks.

Quinn attempts to help Raten by attacking Momo from behind, but Momo anticipates his moves before Quinn even acts. This leads Quinn to suspect that Momo possesses either mind-reading or future-seeing abilities, making him a formidable opponent.

Meanwhile, Layla and Erin are engaged in combat with other second-year students, having already taken down some of them. The situation remains tense as the fight between Raten and Momo begins, with Quinn realizing the difficulty of the battle ahead due to Momo's apparent precognitive abilities.
