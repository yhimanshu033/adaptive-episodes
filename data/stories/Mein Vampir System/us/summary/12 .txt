Quinn, feeling a strange surge in his body, decides to go to the bathroom alone instead of the doctor. He discovers his wounds are healing rapidly, and his HP is regenerating in his status screen. This healing process makes him hungry, suggesting his system uses food energy for healing.

Later, Quinn sneaks into the empty training room to test his abilities without others watching. He uses the same equipment from the earlier field test, confirming that his strength level matches his status screen stats. He scores a 10 on the drum test and lasts 20 seconds on the hologram spike machine, double his previous time.

Unknown to Quinn, Layla, another student, is secretly observing him in the training room. She's surprised to see Quinn, whom she remembers as having no ability during the initial test, now demonstrating significant strength. Layla becomes intrigued by Quinn's apparent hiding of his true abilities and decides to keep a close eye on him, thinking he might be useful in some way.
