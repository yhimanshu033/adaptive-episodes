In this episode, the confrontation between Vorden, Ian, and the transformed Quinn (now a Bloodsucker) intensifies. Vorden, having copied Ian's metal manipulation ability, creates metal spikes from chairs to attack the Bloodsucker. Despite being injured, the Bloodsucker demonstrates incredible strength and healing abilities.

The Bloodsucker becomes faster and more agile, avoiding Vorden's attacks. Ian, partially recovered, manages to injure the creature with a metal spike. However, when Vorden realizes the Bloodsucker is actually Quinn, he hesitates to attack further, overcome with guilt for hurting his friend.

Taking advantage of this hesitation, the Bloodsucker charges at Ian. Despite Ian's attempts to defend himself, the creature overpowers him, severing his legs. The episode ends with the Bloodsucker dragging Ian's upper body away from the arena to feed on him, leaving Vorden conflicted and the situation dire.
