Quinn prepares for his day at the Military Academy by packing blood tubes and a mask. He asks Peter to keep an eye on Vorden, who is being targeted by second-year students. Quinn attends the beast weapons class, where he meets Layla and notices the unexpected presence of Erin Heley, an ice ability user.

The class is led by Leo, a bald-headed man with a unique katana. Leo instructs the students to choose their weapons carefully, emphasizing their importance when abilities fail. Quinn struggles to choose a weapon but discovers he can use his "inspect" skill to gather information about each weapon, including its compatibility with his "blood swipe" ability.

Quinn realizes that his inspect skill will be crucial in selecting the right weapon for him, as it provides details on the weapon's stats and compatibility with his abilities. He decides to focus on finding a weapon that complements his need for speed and agility, rather than one that might slow him down.
