In this episode, Quinn watches a tutorial video featuring a blonde man with vampire-like characteristics. The man demonstrates two combat skills: "Flash Step," a defensive move requiring 15 agility points, and "Hammer Strike," an offensive move needing 15 strength points. Quinn realizes these skills could be useful in future fights.

The video ends, and Quinn discovers a new tutorial tab in his system containing detailed explanations of the skills. He wonders about the identity of the man in the video and his connection to the system and Quinn's parents.

As Quinn begins to practice the Hammer Strike, Leo approaches him with two military men. Quinn is taken away for questioning, causing a commotion among the other students. Leo assures them they will return soon.

Quinn and Leo are teleported to a small, windowless room. Inside, they find a hooded female sitting at a table with a general standing behind her. Quinn is instructed to sit and is told not to worry if he has done nothing wrong, creating a tense atmosphere.
