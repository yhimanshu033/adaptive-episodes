Quinn and Vorden find themselves on a dangerous alien planet after being pushed through a red portal. Vorden, who suffers from multiple personalities, fights two Rattaclaws in an abandoned warehouse. He switches between his personalities - Vorden, Raten, and a third unnamed one - to deal with the situation.

After defeating the Rattaclaws and extracting their beast crystals, Vorden encounters Ian, an injured adult traveler. Ian explains that he came through another portal while hunting a beast and sought shelter in the warehouse. He's impressed by Vorden's skill in defeating the Rattaclaws.

The two agree to work together to find a way back to Earth, as they share the same goal. Ian mentions seeing other buildings nearby that they could check for a portal. As they shake hands to seal their agreement, Vorden feels a surge of energy enter his body, and he smiles.

The episode highlights the dangers of the alien world, Vorden's complex personality disorder, and introduces a new character who may play a significant role in their attempt to return home.
