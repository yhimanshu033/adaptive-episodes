Quinn wakes up after passing out and discovers his vision has improved dramatically, no longer needing glasses. He searches for the mysterious book but finds it has vanished. Suddenly, a game-like status screen appears before his eyes, showing his stats, skills (all locked), and quests. Quinn realizes the book may have granted him a unique ability system.

He attempts to use various abilities like water bending and wind control, but nothing works. Frustrated, Quinn decides to complete the daily quest of drinking two liters of water to gain experience points. After doing so, he receives 5 XP.

Just then, Sergeant Griff, a muscular military officer, knocks on Quinn's door. He informs Quinn it's time to leave for military school, reminding him of his scheduled move that Quinn had forgotten about due to the recent strange events.
