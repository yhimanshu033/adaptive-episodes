Quinn has absorbed blood from various sources, significantly increasing his strength, agility, and stamina. He now surpasses regular human capabilities but is still weaker than those with abilities. Quinn realizes he needs more skills to become stronger and compete with higher-level ability users.

Quinn asks Layla to gather information about Vorden, as the second-year students are preventing Quinn and Peter from learning anything. Quinn expresses his desire to get stronger and eventually confront Momo, one of the second-year students who had previously warned them.

The episode introduces the concept of "awakening," which allows ability users to get a power boost and form a "soul weapon," a unique weapon formed within their body. This is different from beast weapons, which can be used by anyone.

Quinn decides to test how long he can go without blood to prepare for potential missions or situations where he might not have access to it. He keeps a test tube of Layla's blood as a precaution.

Meanwhile, Peter successfully manipulates mud using his Earth ability, though he needs to carry mud with him as a level one user. Vorden demonstrates his superior control over the ability, explaining that he copies the power level of abilities he mimics.

Quinn returns to his dorm room and tells his friends that he has decided to join the beast weapons class instead of the elemental class with them. He also clarifies that Layla is just an acquaintance, not his girlfriend as Vorden had suggested.
