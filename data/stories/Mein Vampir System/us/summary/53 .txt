Quinn discovers that his blood swipe ability works in the VR game "Power Fighter" without depleting his HP, unlike in the real world. He tests this by repeatedly using the ability, confirming that it doesn't affect his in-game health. Quinn realizes that while his blood bank ability won't work in the game, he can use his blood swipe freely.

He practices the hammer strike technique he learned from a video, successfully executing it in the game. For his first match, Quinn chooses to fight against a level 1 opponent, despite believing himself to be around level 2.5 in power.

During the match, Quinn allows his opponent to attack him to test how damage works in the game. He confirms that while his in-game HP decreases, it doesn't affect his real body. Quinn then defeats his opponent using a combination of blood swipes and physical attacks.

Unexpectedly, Quinn's personal system rewards him with 25 experience points for defeating the opponent in the game. This discovery excites Quinn, as it means he can potentially level up his abilities by playing the VR game.
