In this episode, Quinn engages in a fight with a bully named Kyle who has a transformation ability that turns his hands into tiger claws. Despite being injured, Quinn manages to overpower Kyle using his enhanced strength. The fight concludes with Quinn defeating Kyle, earning 50 experience points and unlocking a new skill called "Inspect."

The Inspect skill allows Quinn to see information about others, including their name, race, ability, HP, and blood type. Quinn tests this skill on both Peter and Kyle, discovering its usefulness in combat situations.

After the fight, Quinn receives an optional quest to drink Kyle's blood for a stat point. Although disgusted by the idea, Quinn feels a strange attraction to the blood, hinting at a possible vampire-like nature to his abilities.

Peter urges Quinn to leave the scene before they get caught. As they walk away, Peter misinterprets Quinn's backward glances as concern for Kyle, when in reality, Quinn is still considering whether to drink the blood for the stat boost.

This episode further develops Quinn's abilities, introduces a new skill, and hints at a potentially dark aspect of his powers related to blood consumption.
