In this episode, the ability testing continues at the military school. Layla demonstrates her skills with a beast weapon bow and telekinesis, achieving a level 2 rating. Quinn notes that the academy seems to focus solely on ability power rather than overall combat effectiveness.

Erin then showcases her impressive ice abilities, easily completing the tests and achieving a level 5 rating. She displays confidence and skill throughout her performance.

Finally, Vorden takes the test, surprising everyone by perfectly replicating Erin's ice abilities and achieving the same level 5 rating. This reveals his unique copying ability and explains his earlier confidence.

The episode highlights the different types of abilities and their applications, as well as the academy's rating system. It also further develops the characters of Layla, Erin, and Vorden, giving insight into their personalities and abilities.
