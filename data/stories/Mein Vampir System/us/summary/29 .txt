In this episode, Quinn explores the weapons room at the Military Academy, using his inspect skill to find a suitable weapon. He chooses the Black Horned Gauntlets, a basic tier beast weapon that increases his strength and defense, and is compatible with his "Blood Swipe" skill.

The class instructor, Leo, explains the importance of weapon mastery and announces a sparring session without the use of abilities. Erin, known as the Ice Queen, volunteers first and quickly defeats her opponent, showcasing her unexpected skill with a sword.

Several other fights follow, including one where Layla demonstrates her proficiency with a bow. Finally, Quinn is called to the arena to face Brad Richardson. Quinn feels confident for the first time, as the indoor setting allows him to use his full stats, and his opponent can't use his ability.

The episode highlights the importance of weapon skills in addition to supernatural abilities, and sets the stage for Quinn's first combat test with his new gauntlets.
