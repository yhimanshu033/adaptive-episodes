Quinn, a student without an apparent ability, is taken to a military school for testing. When offered a free Earth ability book, he surprisingly refuses it, causing confusion among the instructors. Despite not having an ability, Quinn is still required to take the tests.

The tests involve destroying targets, measuring strength, and testing agility. Quinn struggles with the target test due to his impaired vision in sunlight. For the strength test, he punches a drum-like machine, scoring a 5. In the agility test, he dodges holographic spikes until he's hit, also scoring a 5.

Quinn notices that these scores align with his secret system's attributes, which are halved in sunlight. He realizes he could have performed better at night. Jane, the instructor, informs Quinn that his overall power level is that of a level 1 ability user.

Throughout the process, Quinn's refusal of the ability book and his performance intrigue the instructors. A mysterious man overseeing the process finds Quinn interesting and decides to observe how he'll survive in their world.

Quinn acknowledges that his days ahead as a perceived level 1 user will be challenging, but he hopes this will change over time as he continues to evolve his hidden abilities.
