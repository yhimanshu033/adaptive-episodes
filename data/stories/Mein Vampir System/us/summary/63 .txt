In this episode, Quinn's unusual abilities in the VR game "Power Fighter" gain attention on an online forum. Logan Green, son of the game's creator and a student at the school, investigates the matter but can't find any evidence of hacking.

Meanwhile, Quinn continues his training in the VR room, rapidly gaining experience and improving his skills. He's unaware of the growing online discussion about his mysterious abilities.

In a separate development, General Duke instructs a group of first-year students, led by Earl, to carry out a plan against some unspecified targets.

The next day, Peter sends a message to Quinn and Vorden asking for help. They rush to a training room where portals to other worlds are stored. They find Peter seemingly injured, but it turns out to be a trap. Peter pushes Quinn into a red portal, which is considered the most dangerous type as it leads to unexplored, potentially hazardous planets. Vorden is left shocked by this betrayal as Quinn disappears through the portal.
