In this episode, tensions continue to rise between Quinn, Layla, and Vorden. Vorden apologizes for attacking Layla, claiming he thought she was an intruder, but Layla remains suspicious of his unstable behavior. Quinn is conflicted about Vorden's actions but still doesn't feel threatened by him personally.

Vorden is seen struggling with multiple personalities, having an internal dialogue about their actions and Quinn's involvement. He appears to be trying to suppress certain aspects of himself.

In the beast weapons class, Quinn and Layla notice the absence of Fei, Brandon, and Loop. The teacher, Leo, instructs the students to practice individually and find ways to combine their abilities with their weapons.

Quinn, unsure of how to proceed, wishes for someone to teach him how to use his weapon. This activates a tutorial within his system, showing him a video of a tall, blonde man with red eyes (likely a vampire) teaching hand-to-hand combat. The man in the video addresses the viewer as "one of us," suggesting a connection to Quinn's newfound vampiric nature.

The episode ends with Quinn watching this mysterious tutorial, potentially gaining insight into his abilities and the nature of his transformation.
