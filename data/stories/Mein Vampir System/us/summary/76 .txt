Quinn finds himself in a dire situation after his battle with the Scordana. His health is slowly depleting due to his need for blood, giving him only 13 hours before reaching zero health points. Realizing he can't wait for rescue, Quinn decides to search for the portal back to Earth.

He carefully explores the shelter, using his enhanced vision to avoid encounters with beasts. After two hours of searching, Quinn's health has dropped to 11/50 HP, and he's struggling with hunger and focus.

Quinn discovers a large, dome-shaped building that he believes could house the portal. The training center is well-protected and in good condition compared to other structures. Outside, he notices destroyed mechs, reminding him of the societal changes following the Dalki war.

At the entrance of the training center, Quinn encounters two Rattaclaws guarding the only way in. He waits for them to move, but as his health drops to 10/50 HP, he realizes he needs to come up with a plan to get past the creatures and into the building, where he hopes to find the portal back to Earth.
