Alex proposes to Debbie at West Lake, and she accepts. They share their happiness and discuss getting married soon. When they return to Debbie's home, Alex asks Charles for his blessing, which he gladly gives, believing the Ambrose family can protect Debbie from the Cliftons. 

Charles reveals Debbie's true identity as the granddaughter of Jessop Clifton, explaining the need for protection. Alex then admits he's been disowned by the Ambrose family, shocking everyone and worrying Charles about potential consequences.

Meanwhile, in Baltimore, Jessop Clifton discovers Debbie's existence through a wedding photo. He's furious that Charles Marvel didn't inform him about Debbie's whereabouts as previously instructed. Jessop orders his son Rufus to send people to Washington D.C. to deal with Charles and bring Debbie back.

Rufus informs Jessop about the chaotic wedding and injuries to notable fighters, raising questions about why they're only hearing this information now. The situation sets up potential conflicts between the Clifton family, the Marvels, and Alex as they all have different interests regarding Debbie's future.
