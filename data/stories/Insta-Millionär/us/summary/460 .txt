Alex continues to assert that he is the principal of Oak Ridge High School, despite the disbelief and mockery from other party guests, particularly Tabitha and Sasha. He calls his driver to pick him up, which further fuels the skepticism of the group. Joe offers Alex to stay the night, but Alex declines, determined to prove he's not lying.

The situation becomes increasingly tense as more guests join in to witness the arrival of Alex's supposed "principal's car." Neil, Joe's father, becomes concerned about the treatment Alex is receiving and tries to help by offering him a ride. However, the other guests insist on waiting to see the car.

Tabitha escalates the situation by asking Alex to help get a child into Oak Ridge, to which Alex responds that he doesn't believe in cheating. Neil eventually loses his patience and scolds the group for bullying Alex, whom he believes to be a security guard.

After a long wait of nearly twenty minutes, the guests become impatient and start making jokes about why the car hasn't arrived. Just as Neil suggests Alex should apologize, a car engine is heard, and bright lights flood the villa's entryway. The arrival surprises the mocking guests, but Tabitha claims it must be an expensive car sent by her husband to pick her up.
