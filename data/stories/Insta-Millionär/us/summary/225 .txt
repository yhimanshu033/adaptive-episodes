The episode continues the dramatic scene at the Sultan of Brunei's banquet. After the Sultan announces his intention to award Alex the Brunei National Medal of Honor, several guests, including Darryl and Myriam, attempt to discredit Alex by spreading rumors and false accusations. However, the Sultan dismisses these claims, revealing that he invited Alex because of his connection to the late queen.

The Sultan then surprises everyone by asking Alex to decide where to invest $5 billion, either in New York or Washington D.C. This causes panic among the guests, who fear Alex will choose New York out of spite. After considering the Sultan's investment preferences in real estate, service industry, and tourism, Alex concludes that New York is the better choice.

Before Alex can announce his decision, the guests, realizing the potential consequences of losing the investment, begin apologizing profusely. They single out Darryl, Colin, and Jason as the main instigators and pressure them to apologize to Alex. Despite their reluctance, the three men comply to save face and secure the investment for their city.

Alex accepts their apologies, much to Nelly's disappointment. Finally, Alex turns to the Sultan and recommends investing the money in New York, based on his analysis of the two cities' economic strengths.
