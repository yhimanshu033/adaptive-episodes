In this episode, the aftermath of Henry Moore's death unfolds at the Yellow Garden restaurant. Slayer, who killed Henry, is initially panicked but is comforted by Alex. When Cory's reinforcements arrive, a fight breaks out, with Alex, Slayer, Ben, and Carl defeating Cory's men. They force Cory and his group to kneel and humiliate themselves.

Alex's friends, concerned about the killings, urge him to turn himself in to the police. However, Alex refuses, saying he has someone who can handle the situation. He calls Sam, asking him to deal with the bodies and ensure his friends' safety.

Sam informs Alex that Vivian and her daughter have been captured by the Flag Sect. Alex decides to personally rescue them instead of waiting for Sam's team. He instructs his friends to stay at the restaurant until someone comes to take them to safety.

As Alex leaves, Slayer offers to help, but Alex asks him to stay and protect his friends. Alex rushes out, worried about Vivian and her daughter's safety in the hands of the Flag Sect.
