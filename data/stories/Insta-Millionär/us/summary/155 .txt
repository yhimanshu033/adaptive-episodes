Alex finds himself on a cruise ship with Minnie, who apologizes for hitting him earlier. She convinces him to teach her swimming, then unexpectedly throws him overboard with a life ring attached to a rope. Lee Dempsey, the ship's owner, appears and praises Minnie for her actions. However, Lee then betrays Minnie and throws her overboard as well.

Alex, still in the water, manages to save Minnie despite her earlier betrayal. On deck, some passengers are amused while others are horrified. Lee threatens to cut the rope but is persuaded not to, though he forbids anyone from pulling Alex and Minnie back on board.

A tropical storm approaches, causing concern among the crew and passengers. The captain attempts to steer the ship away from the storm's center. However, the ship suddenly hits a reef, causing panic and chaos on board. The ship's power system malfunctions, and water begins to flood the lower decks.

The captain, overwhelmed by the situation, sends out an S.O.S. to the coast guard. Lee becomes increasingly angry and violent towards the captain as the situation worsens. The episode ends with the ship in serious danger of sinking, leaving Alex and Minnie still in the water and all passengers at risk.
