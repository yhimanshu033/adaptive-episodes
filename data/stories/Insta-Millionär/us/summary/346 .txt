Alex and Jessop Clifton return to the Clifton building to retrieve important documents from Jessop's office. They encounter Chelsea Wood and Captain Evan Bryce at the scene of the previous bombing. Despite concerns for safety, Jessop insists on getting the documents himself due to their sensitive nature.

While Chelsea investigates the bomber's identity, another explosion occurs on the fifteenth floor where Jessop's office is located. Chelsea rushes to the scene, fearing for Alex and Jessop's lives. She finds the floor in ruins and begins frantically searching through the debris.

To her shock and relief, Alex appears unharmed. He explains that he carried Jessop to safety when the bomb went off. Chelsea, overwhelmed with emotion, hugs Alex before composing herself. Alex retrieves a briefcase and leaves via the fire escape, surprising the officers present.

The episode ends with Alex helping Jessop into a car, surrounded by heavy security. They prepare to leave the scene, with Jessop ordering the driver to take them home.
