Alex receives a call from Lindsey asking for help to raise $1.5 billion for the Drake family, who are facing a financial crisis. The Fidelity Group, owned by the Drakes, has suffered coordinated disasters across all its subsidiaries, resulting in massive losses and contract cancellations. Alex suspects the Mitchell family might be behind the attack and decides to fly to D.C. immediately to investigate and help.

At the Fidelity Group headquarters, Reginald Drake holds an emergency meeting with shareholders and executives. During the meeting, Noah Freeman, a senior director secretly working with Colin Patterson, unexpectedly resigns. This triggers a chain reaction, with several other executives also resigning. 

David Drake tries to defend his father and the company, but Noah convinces more executives to leave, claiming the Fidelity Group is doomed. Reginald is devastated by this betrayal, while David angrily confronts the resigning executives. Noah continues to persuade the remaining executives to resign, warning them that staying with the Drake family will only lead to their downfall.

The situation at Fidelity Group headquarters is tense and chaotic, with the Drake family's business empire seemingly on the verge of collapse.
