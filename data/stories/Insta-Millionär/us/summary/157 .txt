Here's a summary of the episode:

The situation on the sinking cruise ship escalates as Lee Dempsey tries to push Minnie overboard again. Alex intervenes and saves Minnie, threatening Lee. When a private yacht arrives, it turns out to be Justin Ambrose's. He rescues Alex, Minnie, and Hannah, leaving the other passengers behind. 

On the yacht, Alex reads a letter from Cathy expressing her lingering feelings and decision to quit school. Alex feels relieved and lets go of the letter.

Back in Miami, Justin and Alex have a serious conversation about Debbie's disappearance. Justin becomes furious at Alex's perceived lack of action and challenges him to search for Debbie himself. This motivates Alex to immediately begin a personal search for Debbie.

Alex returns to New York to prepare for his search mission. He visits the Azalea Guest House to collect Debbie's diary and meet with Sam Woodsworth and Ken Stokes. Sam expresses concern about Alex searching alone, but Alex is determined. Just then, Ken arrives with exciting news, presumably about Debbie.
