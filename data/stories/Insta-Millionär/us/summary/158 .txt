Alex receives news from Ken Stokes that Debbie's phone was found in Washington D.C., giving hope that she might be there. Despite Ken's advice to let professionals handle the search, Alex decides to go to Washington himself. He books a train ticket for the next day at 11 AM.

That evening, Alex receives a call from Sharon Williams, Kelly Phillips' friend, who is concerned about Kelly's withdrawn behavior. Alex agrees to meet Kelly at Ramsey Lake on the university campus at 9 AM the next day.

Later, Rose calls Alex, wanting to meet before he leaves. Alex reluctantly agrees to meet her at 10 AM at Ramsey Lake, after his meeting with Kelly.

The next morning, Rose arrives early at the lake with a plan to stage a fake attack, hoping it will make Alex confess his feelings for her. She brings a man to act as the attacker and they start rehearsing.

Kelly and Sharon arrive at the lake and witness what they think is a real attack. They intervene, causing Rose to explain it was just an act. Kelly and Sharon then wait by the lake for their meeting with Alex.

Rose, realizing her plan might be ruined by Kelly and Sharon's presence, attempts to get them to leave. When they refuse, she begins to explain her scheme to them, setting up a potentially awkward situation as Alex's arrival approaches.
