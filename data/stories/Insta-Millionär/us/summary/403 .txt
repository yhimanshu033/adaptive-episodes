Mr. Cartwright, a powerful politician and Lance's father, rushes to Oak Ridge High School after receiving an urgent call from Alex. Upon arrival, he finds Lance sleeping in class and angrily disciplines him in front of his classmates. This unexpected visit shocks the students, who fear their own parents might be called next.

Lance is confused and scared, denying any wrongdoing to his father. Mr. Cartwright, worried about potential legal issues, demands to see Alex. Meanwhile, Alex is in a meeting with the principal discussing a potential parents' meeting for Class 6, orchestrated by Billy.

When informed of Mr. Cartwright's arrival, Alex makes him wait, which frustrates the politician who is not used to such treatment. Mr. Cartwright reflects on his son's behavior and is prepared to believe Lance has done something wrong. He feels embarrassed waiting outside Alex's office like a student awaiting punishment, highlighting the shift in power dynamics caused by Alex's presence at the school.
