Alex Ambrose, now a teacher at Oak Ridge High School, organizes a parents' meeting for Class 6. Initially, no parents show up, leading to mockery from Billy, the director of education. However, later in the day, numerous luxury cars arrive at the school, bringing some of Baltimore's most influential and wealthy individuals.

The parents, including powerful figures like Ronald Cartwright and Antonio Chapman, attend the meeting because they received personal invitations from Alex, whom they recognize as a famous martial arts champion. Their presence astounds Billy and Leonard, who watch enviously from afar.

During the meeting, Alex surprises everyone by announcing that the purpose is to collect donations from the parents. He criticizes them for their children's disruptive behavior and suggests that donating to the school would fulfill their parental responsibilities. Alex implies that if they don't comply, he will take matters into his own hands, effectively threatening these powerful individuals.

This unexpected turn of events showcases Alex's influence and unconventional approach to addressing the issues in Class 6. The success of the meeting in terms of attendance is seen as a significant achievement for Oak Ridge High School, potentially improving its reputation.
