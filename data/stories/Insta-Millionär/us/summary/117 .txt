Alex's involvement in Rose and Suzan's rescue becomes more apparent as they discover the connection bracelets in the rescue car. Suzan grows suspicious of Alex's true identity, while Rose remains in denial. The young men who rescued them show deference to Alex, further hinting at his hidden status.

Alex meets with Ken Stokes to discuss the rescue and the ongoing search for Debbie. Later, he receives a call from June Douglas, the manager of Angel bar, asking for help. Alex agrees and goes to work at the bar for the night.

After a busy evening, the bar staff, including Alex, clean up and wait for the owner, Vivian Carter, to arrive. Vivian makes an impressive entrance and treats the staff warmly. When Alex accidentally spills a drink while serving, Vivian gracefully handles the situation, impressing Alex with her poise and leadership.

Throughout these events, Alex continues to maintain his facade as an ordinary student and part-time worker, despite his growing influence and connections behind the scenes.
