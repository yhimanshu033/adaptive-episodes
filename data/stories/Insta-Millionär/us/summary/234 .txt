Alex confronts Leona at her wedding to David Drake, hoping to remind her of her past as Debbie. Lindsey tries to protect Alex from Reginald's security team, but Alex pushes her away, angry about her earlier deception. When Reginald orders his team to shoot Alex, Ken Stokes and his supporters arrive just in time to save him.

Nathan Ambrose appears, warning Ken not to reveal Alex's true identity. The confrontation escalates, with Nathan slapping Ken and mocking Alex. Alex makes a final plea to Leona, asking her to remember their past and come with him. However, Leona rejects Alex, stating that she loves David.

Heartbroken, Alex collapses and cries. The Moon society girls comfort him while Ken Stokes sees this as an opportunity for Alex to finally apologize to his family. In the end, Alex congratulates Leona and tells Nathan that he's giving up on Debbie, seemingly ready to move on and reconcile with his family.
