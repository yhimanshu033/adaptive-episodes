Alex's body begins to emit a bright, moon-like light during the Black Orchid cult's ceremony. The light forms into a phoenix, which rescues Debbie from the sacrificial altar. This miraculous event stuns everyone present, including the cult leader Cassius. The phoenix's actions are interpreted as divine intervention, showing that the gods want Debbie to live.

Cassius, realizing his mistake, kneels before Debbie and asks her to become the new leader of the Black Orchid cult. The other cult members support this decision, believing Debbie to be chosen by the gods. Initially hesitant, Debbie accepts the leadership role to save the cult members from exile.

After a month at the Black Orchid temple, Debbie has recovered and assumed her position as leader. She sits on a throne, with Alex beside her, as they follow the cult's customs. Cassius offers Debbie protection for her departure, but she declines. Instead, she instructs the cult to reform their practices, follow the law, and stop killing indiscriminately. She appoints Cassius to oversee these reforms.

Throughout this transition, Cassius shows a softer side, crying with gratitude for Debbie's leadership and the chance for redemption. The episode ends with Debbie and Alex preparing to leave the Black Orchid cult, having set it on a path of reform.
