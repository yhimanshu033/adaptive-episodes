Alex confronts Jonah at the harbor and speaks to Jacob Randolph, the leader of the Ghosts, on the phone. Despite Jacob's threats, Alex kills Jonah. He then returns home, where Debbie brings him food and they share an intimate moment.

The next morning, while using Debbie's phone to order breakfast, Alex discovers a photo in her album. The picture shows Caroline Hall, Joe's mother, embracing an unknown man in the background. This discovery deeply troubles Alex, as Caroline was married to Neil Hall, Joe's father.
