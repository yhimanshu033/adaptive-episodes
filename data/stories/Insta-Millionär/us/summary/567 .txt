Alex, now in charge of the Woodside Clinic, finds himself alone with Sophie after all the staff and patients have left due to their distrust in his abilities. Alex remains calm, believing they'll return within three days. He explains to Sophie that he can financially support the clinic for a year if needed.

Unexpectedly, Kendall Payne, a former assassin, arrives at the clinic looking for a job. Initially, she doesn't recognize Alex and leaves when she does. Alex follows her outside, where Kendall explains that she's no longer in the assassin business, is struggling financially, and is ill. She reminds Alex that she had helped him in the past by arranging Clark Fairfax's assistance at the Hall family home.

Alex, suspicious at first, decides to help Kendall and invites her back into the clinic to examine her illness. Kendall is surprised and touched by Alex's willingness to help her. Alex plans to cure her, though not completely, to prevent her from potentially returning to her assassin ways. Kendall, who has had a difficult past, is grateful for Alex's kindness and considers the possibility of friendship between them.
