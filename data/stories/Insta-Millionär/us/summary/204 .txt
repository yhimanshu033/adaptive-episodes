Alex and his friends attend an Olympic event where a white Ferrari suddenly appears, drawing everyone's attention. The car is revealed to be voice-controlled and belongs to Alex, though it's currently being used by Kelly Phillips, his friend from New York. Kelly and her friend Sharon arrive in the car, causing a stir among the crowd.

Jason tries to arrange a horseback riding date with Lindsey, but she insists on bringing Alex along. This leads to tension between Jason and Alex. The group then joins the crowd admiring the Ferrari, with everyone attempting to start it using voice commands.

When Alex refuses to try starting the car, Jason and his friends mock him. Alex, knowing he can actually start the car but wanting to avoid drawing attention to himself and potentially encountering Kelly, challenges Jason, asking what he would do if Alex successfully starts the Ferrari. Jason and his friends laugh at the idea, considering it impossible.

The episode highlights the growing rivalry between Alex and Jason, Lindsey's preference for Alex's company, and the complex social dynamics surrounding Alex's hidden wealth and identity.
