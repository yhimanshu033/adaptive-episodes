1. Students who donated over $100 are invited on stage to speak, with many praising an anonymous donor who gave $130,000.

2. Darren, who donated $350, criticizes those who gave less while trying to flatter the big donor.

3. The top 30 donors are invited for a group photo, causing some controversy as Darren tries to bring his girlfriend Donna on stage.

4. The identity of the $130,000 donor remains a mystery, causing excitement and speculation among the students.

5. As Alex and Debbie try to leave, a girl named Laura recognizes Alex as the big donor and reveals his identity to everyone.

6. Alex is invited on stage and confirms his identity, bringing Debbie with him.

7. This causes a shift in the dynamics, with Alex and Debbie now at the center of attention.

8. Donna, who was previously looking down on Debbie, refuses to leave the stage and ends up standing on a chair below it, becoming an object of ridicule.
