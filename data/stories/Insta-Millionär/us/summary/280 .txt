Alex unexpectedly wins the fight against Damon Walker, shocking the audience and changing the power dynamics in Baltimore. The Clifton family celebrates their victory, while the Steadmans face humiliation and loss of influence. Jessop Clifton begins the process of taking over the Steadmans' interests, solidifying the Cliftons' position as the dominant force in the city.

Nathan, watching from Chicago, is enraged by Alex's victory and his own significant financial loss. He receives a call from Colin Patterson, who seeks Nathan's help in taking down the Drakes and gaining control of Washington D.C. Nathan agrees to assist, outlining a plan to destabilize the Drakes' company and allow Colin to acquire it cheaply.

The episode highlights the far-reaching consequences of Alex's victory, affecting not only the local power structure in Baltimore but also potentially influencing events in Washington D.C. through Nathan's machinations.
