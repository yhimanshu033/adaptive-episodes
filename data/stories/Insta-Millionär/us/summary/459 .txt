Alex attends a party where he encounters men claiming to be part of the "Martial Arts Association" who brag about his own accomplishments without realizing he's present. Frustrated, Alex inquires about meeting "Master Alex," leading to an invitation to a supposed gathering in Baltimore. Joe, the party host, and others express interest in attending.

The situation becomes complicated when Luke, one of the men, struggles to accommodate everyone's requests to attend. Alex notices Neil's disappointment and offers to help, but Luke eventually claims he can get everyone in.

As the party winds down, Alex mentions he needs to return to Oak Ridge High School. When asked about his role there, he honestly states he's the principal. This revelation is met with disbelief and mockery from Tabitha and Sasha, who accuse him of lying about his position.

Tabitha belittles Alex, emphasizing the prestige of Oak Ridge and the impossibility of someone like him being the principal. Despite the ridicule, Alex maintains his claim. Neil, embarrassed for Alex, offers to arrange transportation, but Alex declines, saying he can have a car pick him up.

The episode ends with Tabitha continuing to mock Alex, convinced he's lying about his position and transportation arrangements. Alex remains calm and steadfast in the face of their disbelief and derision.
