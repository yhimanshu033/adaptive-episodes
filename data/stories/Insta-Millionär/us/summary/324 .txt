The episode describes a tense confrontation at Silver Lake between Lee Harshaw of the Blood Brothers and various martial arts groups. Lee has already killed several prominent leaders, including Brody Kent of the Sunrise sect. The martial arts community is struggling to defeat Lee, who continues to taunt and challenge them.

Alex, Debbie, and Callisto rush to Baltimore after learning of the dire situation. Upon arrival, Alex takes over driving to reach Silver Lake faster.

At the lake, Lee continues to defeat and decapitate his opponents, washing their blood off in the water. He mocks the remaining martial artists for their hesitation to face him. Many are reluctant to challenge Lee due to his demonstrated power.

Finally, Belinda Hawkins of the Iron Fist sect decides to confront Lee, both for the sake of her sect's reputation and to defend the entire martial arts community. She draws her sword and attacks Lee as the episode ends.
