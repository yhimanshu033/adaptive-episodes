Alex Ambrose, now the head of Birchwood Real Estate, welcomes Jessop Clifton, Celia Turner, and Ethan Cox to Birchwood House. The visitors are amazed to find the property in a spring-like state despite it being winter everywhere else. The grounds are lush, with blooming flowers and active wildlife, creating a paradise-like atmosphere.

Alex reveals he's considering selling the smaller villas on the property for half a million dollars each, which initially shocks the visitors due to the high price. However, after Alex shows them a cherry blossom tree in full bloom, their perspective changes dramatically. The impossibility of such a sight in winter convinces them of the property's unique value.

Jessop immediately offers to buy two villas, while Celia expresses interest in purchasing one. They no longer view the price as excessive but rather as reasonable for the extraordinary experience of having spring during winter.

Celia then attempts to recruit Alex as an instructor for the Baltimore Military Academy, referencing his previous help with foreign agents. However, Alex reiterates his lack of interest in the position.

The episode highlights Alex's growing influence and the mysterious nature of his abilities, particularly in creating an otherworldly environment at Birchwood House.
