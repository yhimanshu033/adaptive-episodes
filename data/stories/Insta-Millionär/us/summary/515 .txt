Alex prepares for his upcoming battle with Jacob, the leader of the Ghosts. He sends the Clifton family to recruit help from the Appalachian Mountains and asks Debbie to invite the Moon Maidens for assistance. Alex spends the night before the battle refining his skills and internal focus at the Fairfax compound.

Clark and Sarah visit Alex, expressing concern about the fight. Clark informs Alex that Kenneth Petrie is sending experts to watch the battle and warns that if Alex loses, Kenneth will try to take Debbie away. Other martial arts associations, including representatives Hugo Sherridan and Seth Paxman, will also be observing the match.

Alex remains confident in his abilities and adds Kenneth to his list of opponents to defeat. Clark requests that Alex become Sarah's teacher if he wins the match, believing Alex's skills far surpass his own. Alex reluctantly agrees, though he doesn't feel qualified to be a true teacher.

The battle with Jacob has been moved up to the following afternoon by the American Martial Arts Association. Despite the change, Alex feels prepared and eager to face his opponent.
