Alex finds himself in a dangerous situation as Georgina and Zora's powers flow through him, causing his body to heat up dangerously. Nelly guides Alex on how to manage the power, helping him disperse it safely. Despite pleas from others, Georgina and Zora continue their battle until they both collapse from exhaustion.

Both women die after a final argument about Xavier's love. The Sultan of Brunei mourns Zora, while the Moon Maidens pay respects to Georgina. Commissioner Anthony Billings arrives with police but is asked to leave by the Sultan, who wants to avoid escalating the situation.

Alex, having promised Georgina to become the Lord of her Moon cult, is now faced with the responsibility. The Moon Maidens turn to him for guidance on how to handle Georgina's body. Alex, unsure of any specific ceremonies, suggests contacting the church for a funeral. The Moon Maidens pledge their obedience to Alex as their new Lord.
