Alex takes the stage at the school assembly and calls for Felicity to come up and apologize publicly, as he had promised. The students and teachers are skeptical, believing Felicity would never apologize. To everyone's shock, Felicity actually comes forward and delivers a sincere apology, thanking Alex for helping her see the error of her ways. This unexpected turn of events leaves Billy and the troublemakers in Class 6 stunned.

The teachers, except for Billy and Leonard, praise Alex for his handling of the situation. Alex feels confident about his teaching job. However, the peace is short-lived as Lance, one of the troublemakers, severely beats a substitute English teacher. When confronted by Alex, Lance denies any wrongdoing and asserts that he, not Alex, is really in charge of Class 6. This incident demonstrates that despite Alex's recent success, he still faces significant challenges in controlling the problematic students.
