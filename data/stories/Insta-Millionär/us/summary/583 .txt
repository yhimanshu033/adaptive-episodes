Alex faces a challenge when Michael Murdoch announces a new clinic, Trinity Medical Center, offering $1 treatments. This causes many patients to leave Woodside Clinic. Alex, however, sees this as an opportunity and encourages spreading the news about the $1 treatments widely.

As a result, Trinity Medical Center becomes overwhelmed with patients from all over Baltimore, including those with severe illnesses. The eight doctors there struggle to keep up with the demand, running out of medicine and becoming exhausted.

By afternoon, the situation at Trinity becomes chaotic. Patients are fighting in line, and the doctors are completely worn out. When Michael tries to close the clinic for the day, Alex uses a megaphone to incite the crowd, pointing out that Michael is going back on his promise of $1 treatments for everyone.

The angry patients refuse to leave, accusing the doctors of cheating them. Michael attempts to calm the crowd, explaining that the doctors are exhausted after seeing nearly a thousand patients. However, the situation remains tense, with patients demanding to be seen immediately.

Alex's clever strategy has turned Michael's plan against him, creating a crisis for the new clinic and potentially damaging its reputation.
