Alex encounters a tense situation at Johns Hopkins University when the Moon maidens confront a group of arrogant young men. The Moon maidens defend Alex and physically subdue the men. Alex then drives the Moon maidens away, discussing their new roles at the university and their continued desire to support him.

Later, Alex visits the Clifton family home at Jessop's invitation. Jessop and Rufus intend to ask for Alex's help with a problem, but before they can explain, Chris Steadman arrives unexpectedly with bodyguards.

Alex is asked to hide in the study while Chris meets with Jessop and Rufus. Chris behaves provocatively, sitting uninvited at the dinner table and criticizing the food. The atmosphere is tense, with Jessop initially ignoring Chris and Chris deliberately trying to anger the Cliftons.

Rufus attempts to address the events of the previous night, implying that Chris is there to declare war on the Clifton family. The situation appears to be escalating between the two rival families.
