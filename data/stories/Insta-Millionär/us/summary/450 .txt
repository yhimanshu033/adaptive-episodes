Alex, having lost 5 million dollars at the casino, accuses the establishment of cheating. He challenges the casino to play ten more games, this time with a guest spinning the wheel and using a brand-new roulette wheel. The casino director agrees to Alex's terms, adding that if Alex wins more than three out of ten games, he'll receive an extra 5 million dollars. However, if he loses, his hands will be broken and he'll be thrown into the sea. A portly man suggests that Alex should choose who spins the wheel to ensure fairness. The casino agrees to these conditions, and a new roulette wheel is quickly delivered. The director then asks Alex to choose who will spin the wheel, setting the stage for a high-stakes showdown that will either vindicate Alex's accusations or result in severe consequences for him.
