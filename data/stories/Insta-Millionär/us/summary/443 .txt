In this episode, Alex confronts Dominic Mitchell and his allies at Mitchell's villa. He easily defeats Dominic's bodyguard, demonstrating his superior strength. Theo, an elderly-looking but highly skilled martial arts master who has long protected the Mitchell family, steps up to challenge Alex.

Theo and Alex engage in an intense fight, with Theo realizing that Alex has reached the level of a martial arts master at an unusually young age. Theo reveals his true strength and skill, transforming from his frail appearance into a formidable opponent.

The two engage in a fierce battle, using both hand-to-hand combat and knife-throwing techniques. Their fight causes extensive damage to the room and injures several bystanders. Throughout the confrontation, Alex remains unharmed, leading Theo to question whether Alex has reached an even higher level of mastery than himself.

The episode ends with Theo wondering how it's possible for Alex to be so powerful at such a young age, setting up for a potentially even more intense confrontation in the next episode.
