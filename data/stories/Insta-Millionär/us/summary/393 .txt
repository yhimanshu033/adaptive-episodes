After Alex's impressive display of superhuman abilities in the athletic competition, he begins teaching his class. He leads them through a rigorous workout of squats and jumping jacks, surprising other teachers with his ability to control the normally unruly Class 6. Dylan, previously Alex's challenger, develops respect for Alex and seeks his advice, becoming supportive of him. This change in Dylan confuses his classmates.

Meanwhile, some students, particularly Lance and Eric, plot to undermine Alex. They devise a plan to exploit Alex's apparent low salary by asking him to buy meals for the entire class, hoping to either financially strain him or turn the class against him if he refuses.

As Alex enters the school canteen with his own modest meal, he is approached by Eric and other students from Class 6. They plead with him to buy them meals, claiming they have no money. The episode ends with Eric dramatically crying as part of this scheme to manipulate Alex.
