David Drake and Leona Marvel become engaged after a dramatic incident where David saves Leona's life. David breaks off his engagement with Leona's sister Lindsey, claiming he and Leona are in love. The Marvel family, including Lindsey, accepts this change surprisingly well. David meets with Charles Marvel, Leona's father, to formalize the new engagement, keeping the original wedding date of September 15th.

However, David is secretly upset by Lindsey's apparent relief at their broken engagement. After the dinner, he invites his wealthy friends to celebrate at the Continental Hotel. In private, he thanks Darryl and Colin for helping him win Leona over.

David then approaches his friend Jason Morley, whose company is in debt. He offers to save Jason's business in exchange for a favor: pursuing Lindsey. This reveals that David has ulterior motives and is not letting go of Lindsey despite his engagement to Leona.
