Alex attends a lunch meeting at the Plaza Hotel with Ken Stokes, the head of his family's East Coast Division. Before the meeting, Alex has an awkward encounter in the elevator with a beautiful woman. He accidentally bleeds on her sweater and touches her inappropriately while trying to clean it, leading to her slapping him and calling him a pervert.

At the meeting, Alex discovers that the woman from the elevator is also present. She is shocked to realize that Alex is the heir to the family business she works for. The other attendees are introduced as upper-class individuals from New York and surrounding states who work for the East Coast Division.

The episode highlights the contrast between Alex's casual demeanor and the respect he receives from his employees, as well as the ironic situation with the woman who had just insulted him unknowingly. It also shows Alex's continued struggle to balance his secret wealth with his public persona as a regular student.
