Alex and Nelly are running their food truck at a public event when they are accused of having flies in their pancakes. Felix, the chief of security, tries to impound their truck based on this accusation. The situation escalates when security guards attempt to forcibly remove Alex and Nelly from the truck. A physical altercation ensues, with Alex defending Nelly.

Hunter, who had previously caused problems for Alex and Nelly, arrives and reveals he's behind the setup. The crowd turns against Alex and Nelly, believing the accusations.

Unexpectedly, Lindsey Marvel appears. She recognizes Alex from a previous encounter where he had rescued her. This creates an awkward moment, as Lindsey seems interested in Alex, but he clarifies he's in Washington looking for his girlfriend.

Nelly explains to Lindsey that they're being framed by Hunter and Felix. The security guard presents a live fly as evidence, but Nelly points out that if the fly had been in the pancake while cooking, it wouldn't still be alive.

The episode highlights the complex relationships and tensions between the characters, as well as the challenges Alex faces without access to his family's wealth and influence.
