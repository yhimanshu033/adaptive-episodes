Alex's actions during the bombing incident are questioned by reporters, particularly Lizzy Fairfield. Chelsea Woods, a police officer, defends Alex and dismisses the reporters. Rufus Clifton supports Alex's version of events and invites the reporters to a press conference. The reporters, despite their damaged equipment, follow Rufus for the exclusive story.

Chelsea privately confronts Alex about detonating the bomb, and he admits to doing so to protect his friends and family. Chelsea feels uneasy about Alex's confidence and apparent disregard for the law.

Meanwhile, Art Steadman waits for his son Chris to return home. As time passes without contact, Art becomes increasingly worried. He instructs his PA to call Chris's friends, but no one knows his whereabouts. By early morning, Art is convinced something bad has happened and orders a thorough search of the city for Chris.

The situation in Baltimore remains tense, with various factions vying for power and control. Alex's supernatural abilities continue to develop, and he remains focused on dealing with the leaders of the Blood Brothers gang to ensure the safety of those close to him.
