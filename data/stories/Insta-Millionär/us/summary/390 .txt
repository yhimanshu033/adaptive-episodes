Alex Ambrose, now working as a teacher at Oak Ridge High School, has successfully gained control over the problematic Class 6. He enforces a bet that requires several teachers to run 50 laps around the track, much to the amusement of the students. This event draws a large crowd of students who mock and laugh at the struggling teachers.

Eric, a student leader, publicly exposes Billy's plot to use the students against Alex, embarrassing Billy in front of everyone. The running teachers, including Billy and Leonard, are furious with Alex but are forced to continue due to the bet.

Meanwhile, Alex discusses the situation with the school principal, who has become impressed with Alex's teaching abilities and management of Class 6. The principal apologizes for his initial doubts and expresses hope for the class's future.

Alex sees this event as a way to win over the students of Class 6 and establish better control. He believes that by humiliating the other teachers, he's deterring future attacks against him.

Some students, including a tall, athletic one, express admiration for Alex's character. However, Eric remains suspicious of Alex's motives. The episode ends with one of the exhausted teachers collapsing on the track.
