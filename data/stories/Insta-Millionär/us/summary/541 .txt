Alex and his companions are involved in a dangerous car chase on the freeway, with attackers firing explosive crossbow bolts at them. Alex skillfully evades the attacks and manages to turn the tables, using a stolen crossbow to take out one of the pursuing vehicles. However, they crash while avoiding a broken-down car on the road.

After escaping their overturned vehicle, Alex confronts and defeats several attackers. He discovers an access card for an amusement park called "Hell World" on one of the unconscious men. Alex deduces that this wasn't just about Debbie's kidnapping, but an attempt to lure him to Baltimore to kill both of them.

Alex concludes that Debbie is likely still alive, as the kidnappers need her as leverage against him. He administers a special supplement to the unconscious leader of the attackers, hoping to revive him for questioning. Meanwhile, Alex realizes that the Hell World amusement park might be where Debbie is being held, specifically on the tenth floor of one of its buildings.

Without waiting for his companions, Alex takes one of the attackers' cars and speeds off towards Hell World. Rufus and Will follow in another vehicle, trying to catch up with Alex in case he needs their help.
