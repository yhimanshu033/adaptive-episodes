Alex confronts Dr. Adams at the hospital regarding Sarah's condition. Alex claims he can cure Sarah, while Dr. Adams insists it's impossible. Alex questions Clark about recent visits to places with dark energy, leading to the revelation that Sarah and Clark had walked through a cemetery before she fell ill. Dr. Adams becomes increasingly agitated and tries to have Alex removed from the room.

Alex grows suspicious of Dr. Adams' behavior and directly asks if he's one of the "Ghosts," a secretive organization involved in espionage and assassination. Alex notices the scent of cherry blossoms on Dr. Adams, similar to what he had smelled on Tabitha, a previous attacker. This scent, along with a cherry blossom tattoo, is believed to be a way for Ghosts to recognize each other.

As Dr. Adams becomes more flustered, Alex becomes more certain of his suspicions. The situation grows tense as Alex confronts Dr. Adams about his true identity, while others in the room watch the exchange with growing concern and confusion.
