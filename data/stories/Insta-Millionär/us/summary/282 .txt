The episode begins with the Cliftons taking over all of Steadman's businesses in Baltimore following Alex's victory in the fight. Art Steadman, feeling defeated, receives a call from Tyson of the Blood Brothers gang. Art informs Tyson about Damon's loss and subsequent suicide. Tyson is shocked and promises to help Art regain control of Baltimore's underworld.

Tyson then video calls Lee Harshaw, Damon's mentor, to break the news. Lee is devastated and vows revenge against the security guard who defeated Damon. Tyson advises caution, suggesting they need to be strategic in their approach to avoid being ostracized from the martial arts world.

They agree to first deal with the situation in Baltimore before planning their revenge against Alex. Tyson warns Lee not to underestimate Alex and to prepare carefully for the eventual confrontation. The episode ends with Lee agreeing to wait for Tyson's signal before taking action, but firmly resolved to kill Alex to avenge Damon's death.
