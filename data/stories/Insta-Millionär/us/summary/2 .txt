Alex Ambrose visits the First Republic Bank's VIP room, where he meets bank manager Robert Miller. Initially skeptical, Robert allows Alex to use the fingerprint identification system. After a failed attempt with his thumb, Alex's index finger successfully verifies his identity, revealing his access to a wealthy family account.

Robert discovers that Alex has $86 million in his account, along with numerous valuable assets stored in various bank branches worldwide, including luxury cars, jewelry, artwork, and precious metals. Realizing Alex's importance, Robert issues him a Black Card, the highest-grade card available at the New York branch.

As Alex leaves, Karen, the assistant manager, confronts him, believing he has stolen the card. She attempts to search him and threatens to call the police. The commotion attracts other customers' attention.

Robert, now aware of Alex's immense wealth and potential family connections, emerges from the VIP room. He is furious at Karen's treatment of Alex, recognizing that her actions could jeopardize their careers and the bank's relationship with an extremely valuable client.
