Alex desperately tries to save Debbie's life by transferring his internal power to her, temporarily stabilizing her condition. Exhausted, he collapses and rests while they continue their journey to the Moon Palace. Meanwhile, Celeste receives news that three gangs are attacking the Moon Palace, taking advantage of the old lord's absence.

The group arrives near the Moon Palace to find a tense standoff between the Moon Cult members and thousands of gang members. The gang leaders, including Blaze Quinn of Blood Sacrifice, Falcon Truman of Falling Souls, and the leader of Ice Edge, have united to take over the palace.

Phoebe Ackers, temporarily in charge of the Moon Palace, tries to warn the gang leaders about the new lord's imminent arrival and power. However, the gang leaders dismiss her threats, confident in their superior numbers. They demand surrender and make lewd comments about their intentions towards the women of the Moon Cult.

Alex and his group observe this situation from a distance, preparing to intervene and defend the Moon Palace against the invading gangs.
