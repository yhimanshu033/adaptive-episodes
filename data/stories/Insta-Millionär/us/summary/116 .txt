In this episode, Ken Stokes intervenes to rescue Suzan and Rose from Donald and Luciel Brennan. Ken calls the hotel room and orders Donald to release Suzan, but implies that Rose can be left behind. Donald, terrified of Ken's influence, agrees to let Suzan go.

Rose, emboldened by the situation, attacks Luciel and attempts to assault Donald. However, Donald and Luciel realize that Ken only cared about Suzan's safety and decide to keep Rose. Suzan escapes and meets the men sent by Ken to pick her up. She begs them to save Rose as well.

The men eventually rescue Rose from Donald and Luciel. Both women are then taken to safety in a luxurious Maserati. During the ride, Suzan and Rose question their rescuers about Ken's involvement and their mysterious boss. The men remain tight-lipped about the details.

The episode ends with Suzan discovering the woven bracelets from the night market in the car, connecting their rescue to Alex. This revelation leaves both women puzzled about the identity of their savior and his connection to Ken Stokes.
