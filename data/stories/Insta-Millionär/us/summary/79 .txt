Alex encounters Kelly and her friend Sharon on campus. Kelly, who recognizes Alex as the person who helped her previously, tries to get his attention. Alex, suspicious of her motives due to past experiences, initially avoids her. When Kelly falls off her bike while chasing him, Alex helps her and massages her ankle.

Kelly then proposes a date to Alex, surprising him with her forwardness. Alex, still wary, questions her intentions. Kelly explains their previous encounter and how she sought him out at the university. Alex realizes she's not after his money but is genuinely interested in him.

Sharon, Kelly's friend, arrives and expresses her disapproval of Alex, believing him to be a "loser" unworthy of Kelly. To everyone's surprise, Alex calmly declines Kelly's offer to date. This rejection leaves Kelly on the verge of tears and Sharon stunned, as she can't comprehend why anyone would turn down her attractive friend.

The episode highlights the misunderstandings and complexities arising from Alex's attempts to hide his wealth, as well as the different perceptions people have of him based on his appearance and behavior.
