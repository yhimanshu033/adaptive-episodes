Alex Ambrose, now a teacher at Oak Ridge High School, confronts Lance's father, Mr. Cartwright, a powerful council member. Alex demonstrates his authority by making Cartwright wait and then treating him with apparent indifference. In a private meeting, Alex presents Cartwright with a list of Lance's behavioral issues at school. He lectures Cartwright about neglecting his son's education and emphasizes the importance of young people as the future of Baltimore. Cartwright, initially stunned, comes to agree with Alex's perspective. After the meeting, Cartwright instructs Lance to respect Alex's authority and improve his behavior, recognizing Alex as an important figure. This encounter showcases Alex's growing influence and his ability to manage complex situations involving both students and their powerful parents while maintaining his undercover mission.
