Lisa and her group attempt to dine at an exclusive restaurant, demanding special treatment. Despite initial resistance from the hostess, they are seated in an expensive "Supreme room." Lisa confidently orders lavish meals, assuring her friend Joanne that she'll cover the costs.

Manager Hood recognizes Lisa's group from a previous visit and reluctantly serves them. Lisa tries to flirt with him and promises Joanne she can arrange for the manager to accompany her, but her attempts fail.

After the meal, when asked to pay the bill, Lisa insists they shouldn't have to pay, referencing a previous free meal. Manager Hood refuses, stating there was a specific reason for the previous exemption.

Cornered, Lisa pretends to call someone important, implying it's the person who previously arranged their free meal. She tells the manager to wait for this person's arrival to sort out the situation.

As tension builds, Darren arrives at the restaurant. Lisa greets him with relief, suggesting he's the person she called to resolve the payment issue.
