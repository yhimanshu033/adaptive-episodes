Chelsea Wood decides to arrest Chris Steadman and Julian Duvant at the hospital, despite Captain Finn Murphy's objections. A young officer named Layla Napier joins her. At the hospital, Chelsea confronts Chris and Julian, handcuffing Chris despite his threats and protests from Julian's family. She informs Julian that he will be arrested once his medical treatment is complete.

The next morning, Alex meets Debbie at her family home and walks her to class. He hides the truth about the previous night's events from her. Later, Alex calls Don to check on him and speaks with Don's sister Daisy. Don is recovering from his injuries but expects to lose his job as a security guard due to his extended absence and fears of retaliation from the Steadman family. Alex feels sorry for Don's situation, knowing he can't repay the medical expenses Alex covered for him.
