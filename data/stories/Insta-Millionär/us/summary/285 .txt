David Drake, facing a financial crisis in his family's company, reaches out to his friends for support. He asks them to come to the Fidelity Group headquarters to show solidarity and potentially deter the mysterious attacker targeting his family.

Meanwhile, Colin Patterson, revealed to be working with Nathan Ambrose to bring down the Drake family, gloats over their success. Nathan instructs Colin to buy the Fidelity Group the next day for $35 billion, aiming to complete the takeover before his grandfather, Lincoln Ambrose, can discover their actions.

The following day, reporters swarm the Fidelity Group headquarters. David meets the press, asserting that his family will survive the crisis. His wealthy and influential friends, including Jason Morley, Derek Jackson, Kevin Ashcroft, Ralph Baldwin, and reluctantly, Darryl with Myriam, arrive in luxury cars to show their support. This display of solidarity impresses the journalists, though it's unclear if it will be enough to save the Drake family from their financial troubles.
