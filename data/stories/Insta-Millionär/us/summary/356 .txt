Alex, disguised as a bearded man, enters the Shamrock Hotel and confronts Luther Duncan, a member of the Blood Brothers gang. Alex reveals his identity and challenges the gang, declaring war on them as the lord of the Moon Palace. Despite being surrounded by armed gangsters, Alex remains calm and confident.

Two women, revealed to be hired killers, turn on Luther and hold him at gunpoint. Alex demonstrates his authority by showing Luther the jade seal of the Moon Palace, proving his position as the new lord.

Alex challenges Tyson Slade, the leader of the Blood Brothers, to a battle, stating that if Slade doesn't accept, he's a coward. Luther recognizes the significance of the jade seal and begins to see Alex in a new light, realizing he may indeed be the powerful lord of the Moon Palace.

The situation escalates as Alex demands Luther contact his leader to arrange the challenge. The episode ends with Luther being forced at gunpoint to make the call, setting the stage for a potential confrontation between Alex and the Blood Brothers' leader.
