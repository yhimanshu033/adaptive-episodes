Alex intervenes in a bar fight, injuring Slayer and causing Henry and his group to flee. After the incident, Alex helps Vivian, the bar owner, by driving her home due to her injury. At Vivian's house, Alex is suddenly attacked by Monica, Vivian's adopted daughter with learning disabilities. Vivian explains that Monica has the intellectual age of a 3-4 year old despite being 13.

Alex stays the night at Vivian's house. The next morning, he offers to take Monica to a nearby outdoor gym. Initially hostile, Monica warms up to Alex as he shows her how to use the equipment. She then asks to "ride a big horse," which turns out to be Alex himself. Alex allows Monica to ride on his back, realizing that his efforts have helped overcome her initial hostility towards him.
