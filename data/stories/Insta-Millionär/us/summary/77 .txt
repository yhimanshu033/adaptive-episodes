In this emotional episode, Carla, Debbie's mother, is on her deathbed and reveals a long-held secret to Debbie and Alex. She confesses that she is not Debbie's biological mother, but was her mother's maid. Carla explains how she and Debbie's real mother escaped from a dangerous family situation, resulting in Debbie's mother sacrificing herself to save them. Carla then raised Debbie as her own.

Carla gives Debbie a jade pendant that belonged to her biological mother and asks Alex to promise to protect and care for Debbie. Alex solemnly agrees. Shortly after this confession and making Alex promise, Carla passes away peacefully.

Debbie is devastated by the loss of the only mother she's ever known. She grieves intensely, crying for a long time while holding Carla's body. Alex stands by, offering silent support and vowing to himself that he will always be there for Debbie, ensuring she won't be lonely in the future.
