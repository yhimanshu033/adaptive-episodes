Rufus Clifton visits Paul Novak, a skilled martial artist, at a training camp to recruit him for a mission. While waiting for Paul to finish his meditation, Flynn and Josie Gibson, a couple with a grudge against Paul, arrive seeking revenge for their son's death 12 years ago. They attack Paul, but he easily defeats them, demonstrating his superior skills. After the fight, Rufus and Paul go inside to discuss the task Rufus needs help with, which is likely related to the Clifton family's plans regarding Debbie and Charles. Meanwhile, Alex and Debbie continue preparing for their wedding, unaware of the potential danger approaching.
