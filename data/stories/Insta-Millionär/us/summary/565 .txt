Alex Ambrose, the new boss of Woodside Clinic, demonstrates his extraordinary abilities by accurately preparing multiple prescriptions without using scales or traditional equipment. This leads to a confrontation with Dr. Ann Glover, who accuses him of being careless and dangerous. Alex proves the accuracy of his work using a Powder Flow Tester, shocking everyone present.

Dr. Glover, unaware of Alex's true position, attempts to fire him. Sophie reveals that Alex is actually the new owner of the clinic, causing surprise among the staff. The doctors are introduced to Alex, but some express doubts about his ability to run the clinic due to his apparent youth and lack of medical experience.

An elderly doctor questions Alex's qualifications, and a middle-aged doctor suggests that the staff could easily find jobs at another hospital. The situation becomes tense as the doctors challenge Alex's authority and demand to know about their compensation, setting the stage for Alex to assert his leadership and prove his worth as the new clinic owner.
