Alex finds Debbie recovering well after being treated with the thousand-year-old orchid. A doctor confirms she will make a full recovery. Alex then tours the Moon Palace with Celeste, noticing its blend of traditional and modern elements. He learns Celeste has shared the healing orchid porridge with all the injured people at the palace, which pleases him.

Alex visits the injured maidens Callisto, Selene, and Luna. He insists on feeding them the orchid porridge himself, despite their protests that it's inappropriate for the Lord to serve them. This act of kindness deeply touches the maidens, who swear lifelong loyalty to Alex.

Noticing Celeste's jealousy at being left out, Alex includes her when he announces his plan to give all four maidens massages. The women are shocked and try to refuse, considering it improper for their Lord to serve them in this way. However, Alex insists, framing it as an order they must obey. He begins by arranging for hot water to wash their feet, demonstrating his commitment to caring for his followers personally.
