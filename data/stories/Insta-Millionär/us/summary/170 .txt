Alex, convinced that Leona is actually his ex-girlfriend Debbie, disrupts her date with Darryl Brennan at an upscale restaurant. Initially denied entry, Alex causes a scene, leading to him being tasered and removed by security. Leona, feeling sorry for Alex despite her fear and confusion, asks for him to be released. Darryl, trying to impress Leona, invites Alex to join their dinner.

During the meal, Alex continues to insist that Leona is Debbie, while she maintains that she's never met him before. The situation escalates as Alex and Darryl argue, with other diners mocking Alex for thinking he has a chance with someone from the prestigious Marvel family.

Unexpectedly, when Alex asks Leona out on a date, she agrees, shocking everyone present. Leona explains that she's free to choose her own boyfriend and feels sympathy for Alex. Darryl, trying to save face, challenges Alex to prove he has money by producing $500 on the spot. Alex confidently agrees but then realizes his bank account has been frozen again by his family, leaving him in an embarrassing situation.
