Alex Ambrose arrives at Oak Ridge High School as a new PE teacher and is assigned to take over responsibility for the troublesome Class 6. This decision upsets Billy, the teaching director, who had planned to take over the class himself to build connections with wealthy and influential families. Billy tries to protest Alex's hiring to the principal but is unsuccessful.

During Alex's introduction to the staff, Leonard Winters, a long-serving and respected teacher, questions Alex's qualifications in a condescending manner. Alex responds by challenging Leonard's attitude and teaching philosophy, suggesting that teachers should be role models for behavior as well as academic success. Alex's bold and confrontational response shocks the other teachers and creates tension in the room.

The episode highlights the conflicts and power dynamics within the school, with Alex's arrival disrupting established plans and hierarchies. It also sets up potential ongoing conflicts between Alex and other staff members, particularly Billy and Leonard.
