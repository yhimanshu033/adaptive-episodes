In this episode, Rufus Clifton and his men take control of the situation at the racecourse, confronting the Baltimore Horse Gang. Gregory Price, the gang's leader, is forced to surrender all the gang's assets and is exiled from Baltimore. Micky, another gang member, is dragged away in terror. The crowd cheers as the Clifton family dismantles the gang that had been terrorizing them.

Gregory is made to sign over all the gang's property and assets, then forced to kiss Rufus's boots before being kicked away and ordered to leave Baltimore forever. The local community, including an old man whose son had committed suicide due to the gang's actions, express their gratitude to the Clifton family for ending the gang's reign of terror.

The tourists are relieved to have escaped harm, and Davey praises the Clifton family for punishing the wrongdoers. Ted expresses relief at their timely arrival. While Heidi is respected for her bravery during the ordeal, it's noted that Alex and Debbie have quietly slipped away during the celebrations.
