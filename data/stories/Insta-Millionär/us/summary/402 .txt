Alex confronts Lance about his arrogant behavior and claims of power due to his father's political position. Lance dismisses Alex's authority, believing teachers have no real power compared to students from influential families. Alex calmly suggests he'll speak with Lance's father, which Lance doubts is possible.

Eric expresses concern to Lance about Alex potentially contacting his father, but Lance remains confident it won't happen. Billy, the antagonistic teacher, eavesdrops on their conversation, hoping to see Alex fail.

Billy devises a plan to humiliate Alex by arranging a parents' meeting, believing Alex won't be able to get any parents to attend.

Alex calls Mr. Cartwright's office, leaving a message with consequences if not returned promptly. Initially dismissed, Mr. Cartwright eventually recognizes Alex's name and urgently returns the call, apologizing profusely for the delay.

Alex informs Mr. Cartwright that he's Lance's new head teacher and wants to discuss his behavior. Mr. Cartwright immediately agrees to come to the school, demonstrating the real power and influence Alex holds, contrary to Lance's beliefs.
