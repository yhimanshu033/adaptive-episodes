Alex attends a dinner party where he overhears a conversation between Wesley Boone, CFO of the Ambrose Group, and a member of the Mitchell family. Wesley agrees to betray the Ambrose family by providing confidential information about their business dealings in exchange for $15 million. The Mitchells aim to take down the Ambrose family, seeing them as competition in Baltimore's business world.

Alex realizes this is part of a larger threat against his family, with the Mitchells collaborating with others to bring down the Ambroses. He decides not to immediately reveal Wesley's betrayal, instead focusing on the bigger picture of eliminating the Mitchell family and their company, DominiCorp.

After the dinner, Alex walks home alone, deep in thought about his next moves. Celia and Ethan follow him, trying once again to persuade him to become a training instructor at the military academy. As they walk, Alex notices something unusual about their surroundings and warns Celia and Ethan that there are people waiting for them, implying a potential threat or ambush.
