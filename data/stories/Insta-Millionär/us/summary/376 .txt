Alex confronts Micky and his gang, demanding they release Heidi and leave. Micky responds aggressively, ordering his men to attack Alex. Before the fight begins, Heidi offers to go with Micky alone if he lets everyone else go, sacrificing herself to protect the others. As Micky agrees and the situation seems to be resolving, one of the gang members spots a large group of people approaching in the distance. It's revealed that Debbie had secretly sent a message for help using Alex's phone, and the approaching crowd is the response to that call. The students are amazed by the sight of reinforcements arriving to help them.
