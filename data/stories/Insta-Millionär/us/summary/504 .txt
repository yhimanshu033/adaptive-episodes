Alex demonstrates his superior fighting skills by defeating Drew Walton and severely injuring him without killing him. Marie, one of the Ghosts, delivers a challenge from their master, Jacob, for a fight to the death with Alex at Canfield Arena on the night of the full moon.

The Baltimore Martial Arts Association discusses the challenge. Some members, like Kilian Robinson and Jessop, express concern about Jacob's reputation and power. They recount Jacob's past achievements, including unifying the Californian underworld and creating the Cherry Blossom Group, which later became the Ghosts.

While some members suggest using underhanded tactics to defeat Jacob, Russell Chapman argues for an honorable fight to maintain their reputation in the martial arts world. Edward and Jeffrey advise Alex not to accept the challenge, believing he is outmatched.

Alex, however, sees the duel as an opportunity to prove himself and improve his skills. He prefers to make decisions democratically and allows the association members to discuss the matter openly. Despite the concerns raised, Alex appears interested in accepting the challenge and facing Jacob in combat.
