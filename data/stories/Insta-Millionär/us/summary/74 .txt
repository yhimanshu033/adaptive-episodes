In this episode, Debbie faces severe bullying from her fellow band members, particularly Melissa, Katie, and Olivia. The girls, led by Melissa, establish a hierarchy within the group, placing Debbie at the bottom. They force her to clean the practice room and deliberately make messes for her to clean up.

The situation escalates when Debbie refuses to continue cleaning after repeated provocations. Melissa and the others physically confront her, pushing her against the wall and insulting her family, especially her mother. When Debbie defends her mother's honor, Melissa orders the others to attack her.

Rita, the fourth member of the group, intervenes and stops the assault by threatening to call their manager, Janice. After the other girls leave, Rita helps Debbie up and suggests she find a boyfriend for protection.

Alex arrives just as Rita is leaving. He's shocked to see Debbie's condition and initially suspects Rita of hurting her. Debbie corrects him and embraces Alex, crying. Rita observes their interaction and leaves, thinking Alex doesn't seem capable of protecting Debbie from future bullying.

The episode highlights the intense bullying and power dynamics within the girl group, Debbie's vulnerability, and her reliance on Alex for emotional support.
