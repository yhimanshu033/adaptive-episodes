Alex and Debbie unexpectedly encounter Alex's ex-girlfriend Cathy at a dinner event hosted by Graham Stirling. Cathy, along with her companion Billy, begins to harass Alex and Debbie, accusing them of stealing money from their university and sneaking into the event. The situation escalates when Cathy physically provokes Alex, leading him to slap her.

During the event, Graham announces a prize draw. The grand prize is a diamond necklace worth $200,000. As the numbers are drawn, tension builds, especially between Alex and Cathy. To everyone's surprise, Alex wins the necklace.

When Alex is called to the stage to receive his prize, Graham introduces him as "Adam Childs" to maintain his anonymity. However, Cathy, fueled by jealousy and anger, stands up and exposes Alex's real identity to the crowd, calling him a thief and a loser from Preston University. This revelation threatens to disrupt Alex's attempt to keep a low profile at the event.
