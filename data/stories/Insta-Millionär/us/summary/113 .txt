Alex is asked to accompany Suzan and Rose to a night market for their safety. Rose is reluctant but agrees. At the market, Rose ignores Alex while browsing stalls with Suzan. At a jewelry stall, an old lady ties matching bracelets on Alex and Rose, claiming they create a connection. Rose angrily removes and discards hers, while Alex keeps both, thinking of giving them to Debbie.

Alex receives an urgent call from Russell, the manager of Autumn Beauty Boutique, about his ex-girlfriend Cathy causing a scene. Alex decides to handle the situation, leaving Suzan and Rose at the market. On his way out, he bumps into a man pretending to be blind, which he finds suspicious.

At the boutique, Cathy is demanding money and claiming to be the boss. It's revealed that after being rejected by the Phillips family, Cathy witnessed Alex's display of wealth with the Ferrari. She now believes she can win Alex back and regain access to his wealth by apologizing, assuming he still cares for her despite her past actions.
