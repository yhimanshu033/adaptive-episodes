In this episode, Alex finds himself in a dangerous situation at "The Playful Hare" restaurant. Guided by a mysterious voice, he uses martial arts techniques to defend against Fergus Plummer's gangsters. Despite initial success, Alex is eventually overpowered by Fergus.

As Fergus threatens to kill Alex, Lindsey intervenes to protect him. At this critical moment, a beautiful woman in white appears and confronts Fergus. She challenges him to remove her disguise, but when he attempts to do so, she demonstrates extraordinary abilities.

Using a white silk ribbon, the woman swiftly disarms Fergus and severs two of his fingers. When Fergus retaliates, she uses a spoon to knock a dagger from his hand and then uses the ribbon to cut off his right foot.

Throughout the encounter, Alex realizes that this woman is the source of the mysterious voice that has been guiding him. Her actions save Alex and Lindsey from immediate danger, but leave everyone stunned by her lethal and supernatural abilities.
