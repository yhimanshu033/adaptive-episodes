The episode focuses on the aftermath of the New Year's party performance at Richmond University. The students are impressed by the high-quality stage and equipment, which cost $50,000. Initially, everyone believes Colin's father sponsored the event, leading to praise for Colin. However, it's revealed that Alex Ambrose is the actual sponsor, though he wanted to keep it secret.

As the awards are being presented, Alex tries to leave to avoid being recognized as the sponsor. The audience becomes hostile towards Alex due to his mistake during the performance, chanting for him to leave the stage. Colin takes advantage of this to further belittle Alex.

When it's time to present the first prize, the host announces that the sponsor, Alex Ambrose, will present the award. This causes confusion as everyone assumes it's a different person with the same name. Alex is conflicted about whether to reveal himself.

President Merrit quickly intervenes, bringing the certificates to Alex on stage and congratulating him on both winning first place and being the sponsor. This sets up a potentially awkward and revealing moment for Alex.

In a separate subplot, Lindsey, who was waiting for Alex, is abducted by someone in a gray SUV.
