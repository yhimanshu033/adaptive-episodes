Alex confronts Kenneth, who initially refuses to cooperate. Alex uses acupuncture needles to inflict an embarrassing punishment on Kenneth, causing him intense gastrointestinal discomfort. This forces Kenneth to confess to various corrupt activities within the American Martial Arts Association, including bribery and covering up his son's misconduct.

Kenneth also reveals information about Marvin Smith, the current president of the Rumble Club. Twenty years ago, Marvin was hired to attack the wife of the then-president of the club. He did this to pay off gambling debts and has since risen to his current position, all while keeping this secret.

Alex considers informing Clark about this information but decides to gather more evidence first. He plans to confront Marvin in person before saying anything to Clark.

The episode ends with Alex receiving an unexpected phone call from "Grandma Spice," a blind elderly woman who used to work for Georgina at the Moon Palace. Alex is surprised by her call, as he didn't think she knew how to use a mobile phone. The conversation is interrupted at this point, leaving the purpose of her call unclear.
