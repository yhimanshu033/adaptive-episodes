The episode continues the intense confrontation at the Clifton family banquet. Rufus Clifton is brutally beaten by Zeke, one of the Black Orchid Cult priests. Alex and Jessop return to find the hall in chaos, with many family members injured or unconscious.

Jessop reveals the Phoenix stone, which emits a light that connects with Debbie, confirming her as the "chosen one." This revelation strengthens the priests' resolve to eliminate the Clifton family.

As Zeke attacks Jessop, Alex intervenes, surprising everyone with his strength. A fierce battle ensues between Alex and Zeke, with Alex taunting the priest to provoke him. The fight causes the hall to shake, creating a smokescreen of dust.

When the dust settles, it appears that Alex has been overpowered. However, it's revealed that Alex has actually forced Zeke into a defensive position, standing in a deep pit while Zeke is injured and bleeding. This unexpected turn demonstrates Alex's superior fighting skills and strategy against the seemingly invincible priest.
