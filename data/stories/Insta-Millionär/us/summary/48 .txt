Alex and Debbie are taken to Harmony Island, one of Alex's family-owned islands, for medical treatment after their injuries. Alex recovers first and visits the still-comatose Debbie. When Debbie wakes up, she's confused about her surroundings and tries to leave, not realizing she's in Alex's family hospital. The nurses explain the situation to her, and she learns about Alex's wealth and status.

Meanwhile, Alex explores the island, reminiscing about his childhood visits. He meets George Ambrose, a distant relative who manages part of the family business. George informs Alex that his grandfather is coming to visit, which excites Alex.

As Alex continues his walk, he stumbles upon a beach where about ten beautiful women in bikinis are playing volleyball. He sits down to watch, mesmerized by the scene. This episode highlights the contrast between Alex's modest behavior and his family's wealth, as well as introducing new characters and settings that expand the story's scope.
