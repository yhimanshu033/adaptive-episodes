Alex receives news that his mother Flora's helicopter has crashed shortly after takeoff on Fairview Island. The Ambrose family is shocked and divided over Alex's return. Nathan and Marion oppose his involvement, while others are more welcoming. Lincoln is torn but seems to be warming to the idea.

It's revealed that Mickey Doyle, the head mechanic, was forced to sabotage the helicopter after his daughter was kidnapped. He's devastated by his actions but felt he had no choice.

Alex, who was in a decoy helicopter, is furious upon hearing the news. He vows revenge against the Mitchells, whom he believes are responsible for the crash. His hesitation about returning to his family vanishes, and he's determined to see his mother, regardless of his grandfather's potential punishment.

As Alex arrives on Fairview Island, he's greeted by cheering staff members, but no family is present. Meanwhile, Lincoln sits alone in the villa, having decided to allow Alex's return, unaware that he has already arrived. The situation highlights the growing tension and division within the Ambrose family as they face this new crisis.
