The episode focuses on Melissa's attempts to cover up her lies and Alex's desperate search for Debbie. 

Melissa visits a doctor to fabricate evidence of her supposed night with Alex. She blackmails the doctor into supporting her story of rough sex and potential pregnancy if anyone investigates.

Meanwhile, Alex is distraught over Debbie's disappearance. He orders Ken Stokes to search for her. Lou, the man who picked up Debbie at the airport, is brought in for questioning. Under pressure, Lou lies about taking Debbie to the Pavilion Hotel, following Melissa's instructions.

Alex's men find Debbie's suitcase in Lou's home. Alex discovers Debbie's diary inside and reads entries revealing her deep feelings for him and her insecurities about their relationship. The diary entries provide Alex with new insights into Debbie's perspective on their relationship and past events.

The episode ends with Alex reading the diary, particularly interested in the entries about Debbie's time in Chicago, as he continues to search for her whereabouts.
