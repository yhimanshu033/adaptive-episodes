Alex continues his training with Zora, learning martial arts techniques and a special recitation. They practice in a park, drawing some attention from onlookers. Zora demonstrates her impressive skills at a sandwich shop, catching falling trays and rearranging sandwiches mid-air.

After several days of intense training, Alex masters the basic 36 routines of Zora's special skills. While checking the news, Alex discovers that the Queen of Brunei has gone missing in Washington D.C. He realizes that Zora is actually the missing queen, explaining her secretive behavior.

Meanwhile, David, still believing Alex to be dangerous based on Fergus' lie, arranges for two martial arts experts to confront Alex. He brings Leona along to the Tinsdale Hotel where Alex is staying. David accuses the hotel of harboring prostitutes and demands to see Alex.

When Alex emerges from his room, David coldly suggests to Leona that her friend (Alex) is involved with a prostitute. The situation is tense as Alex greets David and Leona, unaware of David's intentions.
