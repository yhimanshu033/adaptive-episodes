In this episode, the situation escalates after Jacob's death. Kenneth Petrie and Hugo Sherridan confront Alex, demanding an apology for Jacob's death and threatening consequences. Seth taunts Alex, believing him to be weakened and easy to defeat. Alex refuses to back down, prompting Seth to call in 18 skilled warriors.

Unexpectedly, Spencer Holliday, a legendary martial arts master, arrives to support Alex. Seth underestimates Spencer and orders his men to attack. Spencer swiftly defeats all 18 warriors, shocking everyone present. Seth, Kenneth, and Hugo realize they are outmatched and attempt to apologize and leave.

However, Spencer refuses to let them go and begins attacking the Ghosts in the arena. He demonstrates his ruthless efficiency, quickly overwhelming most of the Ghosts. Hugo tries to negotiate, but Spencer strikes him down. The episode ends with the Ghosts torn between fearing Spencer's immediate threat and Alex's greater power, realizing they are in a dire situation with no easy escape.
