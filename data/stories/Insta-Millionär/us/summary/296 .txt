Alex Ambrose, a former store employee who inherited $86 million, is now working as a security guard at Johns Hopkins University. Chris, who suspects Alex's true identity, meets Melissa Kennedy at the airport. Melissa, a former member of the Dream Chasers girl group, is looking for Alex, believing him to be wealthy.

Chris manipulates Melissa by pretending to return a watch and taking her to dinner. He learns that Alex is indeed from the wealthy Ambrose family. Chris then takes Melissa to see Alex at his job as a security guard, shocking her with his apparent fall from wealth.

Disappointed by Alex's current status, Melissa quickly shifts her attention to Chris when she realizes he's from the influential Steadman family. Chris, contemptuous of Melissa's gold-digging nature, decides to use her in his plan against Alex. He pretends to be interested in Melissa, and they agree to start seeing each other. The episode ends with Chris instructing his driver to take them to a hotel instead of home, furthering his manipulation of Melissa.
