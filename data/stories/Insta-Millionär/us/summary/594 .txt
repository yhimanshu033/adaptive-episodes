Alex, while in custody, discusses his situation with Detective Sergeant Timothy. He expresses concern about Riley Naysmith's plan to manipulate witnesses, which could backfire and make Alex look guilty. Alex asks Timothy to ensure the witnesses don't change their testimonies.

Timothy informs Alex that the evidence against him is strong, and it will be difficult to prove his innocence. Alex then suggests a plan involving Michael, the deceased, to resolve the situation.

The conversation is interrupted when Timothy's brother, Brian, a forensic medical examiner, enters with Detective Tamara Knox and other officers. Brian confronts Timothy for being there and accuses Alex of trying to involve Timothy in his problems.

Alex explains that he only asked Timothy to prevent witness tampering, but Brian remains skeptical and hostile. He threatens to use his influence to keep Timothy out of the case, potentially putting more pressure on Alex.

The episode ends with the officers present seeming pleased at Brian's intervention, recognizing his ability to complicate matters for Alex.
