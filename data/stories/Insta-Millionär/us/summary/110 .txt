Alex and Karen unexpectedly encounter Karen's parents, Alison and Albert Young, along with James and Zara at a restaurant. The situation quickly becomes tense as Karen's parents scold her for skipping work and criticize her for being with Alex instead of James, whom they consider a better match due to his wealth and status.

Karen defends her relationship with Alex and rejects James, causing her parents to become angry and confused. James tries to persuade Karen to reconsider, emphasizing his wealth and ability to provide for her. Karen remains firm in her decision and attempts to leave with Alex.

Alison and Albert confront Alex, demanding to know why he thinks he's good enough for their daughter. Zara reveals Alex's reputation at school as a poor student who works menial jobs, shocking Karen's parents even more.

The confrontation escalates when Albert points his finger at Alex and insults him. Alex pushes Albert in response, nearly causing him to fall. Alison attempts to slap Alex, but Karen jumps in front of him to protect him, taking the hit instead.

Throughout the encounter, Alex remains mostly calm but becomes increasingly frustrated with the treatment he receives from Karen's family and James. Karen continues to stand by Alex despite her parents' disapproval and attempts to control her.
