Alex and Debbie arrive at the hospital to help Sarah, Debbie's sick classmate and daughter of Clark Fairfax, a respected member of the martial arts community. Sarah is in critical condition, on the verge of slipping into a coma. Dr. Adams, Sarah's attending physician, is skeptical of Alex's presence and ability to help, especially after learning Alex has no formal medical training or license.

Despite Dr. Adams' objections, Clark Fairfax allows Alex to examine Sarah. However, during a heated exchange between Dr. Adams and Alex, Sarah overhears that she might fall into a coma, causing her to panic and lose consciousness. This triggers an alarm, and nurses rush to stabilize her.

The situation becomes tense as Dr. Adams continues to oppose Alex's involvement, while Clark remains hopeful that Alex can help his daughter. Alex prepares to use his healing abilities, despite the skepticism from Dr. Adams and the seemingly dire situation. The episode ends with uncertainty about whether Alex will be able to help Sarah and how his unconventional methods will be received in this medical setting.
