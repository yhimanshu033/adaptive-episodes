Alex returns to the Moon Palace after receiving Xavier's internal force and leaving Debbie hidden safely. He finds the Moon disciples and the four Moon Maidens fighting against overwhelming odds against the invading gangs. As Callisto is about to be killed by Blaze, Alex intervenes and saves her.

Alex then engages in combat with the gang members, displaying significantly enhanced speed and strength due to Xavier's power. He easily defeats many skilled fighters, shocking everyone present. As the tide begins to turn, Blaze attempts a sneak attack on Alex, which fails.

However, Falcon, another gang leader, uses an explosive device to create a distraction. Alex and Falcon fall into a hole created by the explosion. Falcon attempts to strangle Alex with a wire, putting him in a dangerous situation.

The battle for the Moon Palace continues with Alex now fighting for his life against Falcon while the Moon disciples struggle against the invading forces.
