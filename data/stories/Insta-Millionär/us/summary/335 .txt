The episode focuses on a dramatic confrontation at the Clifton family banquet. Four warrior priests from the Black Orchid cult in Canada have infiltrated the event, claiming that Debbie is the "chosen one" and Cynthia's daughter. They attempt to kidnap her, using a noxious gas to subdue her.

The priests, led by Zeke, demonstrate superior martial arts skills, easily overpowering the young men of the Clifton family who try to defend Debbie. They taunt Jessop Clifton, accusing the family of betraying the cult by hiding Debbie's true identity.

As the situation escalates, even women and children arm themselves with makeshift weapons to fight the intruders. However, the priests prove to be formidable opponents, injuring many family members, including a teenage boy named Sawyer.

The family finds themselves trapped in the banquet hall, unable to escape. Rufus Clifton, realizing the gravity of the situation, searches for an alarm but can't find it. He worries about his father's safety and feels a sense of impending doom as Zeke approaches him.

The episode ends with the Clifton family in a state of panic, overwhelmed by the violent and seemingly unstoppable warrior priests.
