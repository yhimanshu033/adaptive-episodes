Alex wins over $100 million at the Paradise casino, causing tension and suspicion among the casino staff and other guests. The casino director attempts to stall but eventually flees to his office and fires shots into the ceiling, causing panic and chaos. Alex seizes the opportunity to escape, taking cash from the abandoned cashing-out station before leaving on an inflatable dinghy.

Returning to Atlantic City, Alex realizes he has more than enough money to repay his debts and buy luxuries for Debbie. He takes a train back to his apartment near Oak Ridge High School, arriving late in the evening.

Upon entering his apartment, Alex notices how clean and tidy everything is, with his night clothes neatly folded. He's overwhelmed with gratitude for Debbie's care and feels truly at home. In the bedroom, he finds Debbie asleep on the bed, still in her regular clothes. She wakes up briefly, asking if he's eaten, and Alex is filled with love and appreciation for her, marveling at his good fortune to have such a caring partner.
