Alex, Phillipa, and Nelly (disguised as the mascot) successfully translate for the Sultan of Brunei at the Olympic Games opening ceremony. Alex repeats Nelly's whispered translations in English, while Phillipa translates into Spanish. Their performance impresses everyone, including the Sultan and the US President, who shakes Alex's hand.

The volunteers who had earlier tried to kick Alex and his friends out are shocked by this turn of events. When Alex returns to the volunteer rest area to retrieve Phillipa's bag, the volunteers' attitudes completely change. They become apologetic and try to ingratiate themselves with Alex, fearing potential consequences for their earlier behavior.

Nelly takes advantage of the situation, claiming that the Olympic committee director gave Alex the authority to punish the volunteers. She orders them to kneel, and most comply out of fear and regret. Only Myriam and Darryl refuse to kneel, unsure of how to react to the sudden change in dynamics.

The episode highlights the dramatic shift in how Alex and his friends are perceived and treated following their unexpected success at the opening ceremony, demonstrating the fickle nature of social status and respect.
