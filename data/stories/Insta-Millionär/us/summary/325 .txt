Alex arrives at Silver Lake to find Lee Harshaw defeating multiple martial arts leaders. As Lee is about to kill Belinda Hawkins from the Iron Fist Sect, a young woman rushes to save her. Lee easily overpowers the woman and is about to kill her when Alex intervenes, throwing knives to stop Lee's attack. Alex rescues the woman and is shocked to discover she is Cathy, his ex-girlfriend from Preston University.

The martial artists applaud Alex for saving Cathy, and many wonder about his identity and skills. Lee challenges Alex, asking for his name before he kills him. Alex, angered by the deaths of his colleagues, introduces himself as the lord of the Moon Palace and declares his allegiance to the martial arts community. He vows to make Lee pay for the lives he has taken.

The encounter between Alex and Cathy is awkward, as they haven't seen each other since their fight at university. The situation remains tense as Alex prepares to face Lee Harshaw in combat.
