Alex Ambrose, now a wealthy man and Lord of the Moon Cult, is called to help the Drake family in a financial crisis. The Fidelity Group, owned by the Drakes, is facing bankruptcy due to a conspiracy led by Colin Patterson and Noah Freeman. Colin offers to buy the company and all Drake assets for $30 billion, demonstrating his newfound wealth.

At a tense meeting, David Drake confronts Noah for his betrayal. Colin reveals his plan to become the most powerful family in D.C., threatening those who oppose him. Most of David's friends stand by him, but Darryl Brennan switches sides to support Colin, hoping to benefit from the new power structure.

Colin gives Reginald Drake one hour to decide on the offer. As the deadline approaches, Alex arrives in D.C., suspecting who is behind the situation. David initially refuses the offer on behalf of his family, but Reginald, seeing no other option, reluctantly accepts Colin's offer to buy the Fidelity Group.
