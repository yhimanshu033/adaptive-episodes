Alex encounters Sam Woodsworth, the hotel manager, who recognizes him as the owner and treats him with great respect. Despite being given a poor room by his friends, Alex decides to stay there to avoid causing trouble. Sam tries to offer Alex better accommodations and services, which Alex politely declines.

Later, Alex overhears an argument between Rose and Zane. Rose discovers that Zane has been lying about his family helping hers. In her anger, Rose accidentally breaks an expensive statue worth over a million dollars. Sam Woodsworth demands compensation, causing Rose great distress as she fears her father's reaction.

Alex intervenes, coming up with a creative excuse to help Rose. He suggests that having a Russian sculpture in the hotel could be problematic due to current political tensions, and its destruction might actually be beneficial. To everyone's surprise, Sam agrees with Alex's reasoning, seemingly accepting this explanation. This turn of events leaves the onlookers confused, as they don't understand why the manager would accept such an implausible excuse.
