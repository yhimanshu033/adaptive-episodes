Alex Ambrose, recently disowned by his family, launches a surprise attack on Dominic Mitchell's villa. Despite Dominic's confidence in his security measures, including the presence of his powerful housekeeper Theo and the Black Hand organization, Alex easily breaches the defenses.

Alex demonstrates superhuman abilities by effortlessly avoiding gunfire and disarming multiple trained bodyguards. His actions shock and terrify Dominic and his associates, who realize they may have underestimated Alex's capabilities.

As Alex continues to advance, Dominic turns to Theo for help, hoping his trusted housekeeper can handle the situation. The episode ends with tension building as Alex approaches, seemingly unstoppable, while Dominic and his allies struggle to find a way to defend themselves against this unexpected and formidable threat.
