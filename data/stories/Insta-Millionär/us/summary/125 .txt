In this episode, Alex witnesses a shocking scene where an old woman kills Dale Granger and drinks his blood. The woman then reveals superhuman abilities, escaping through a window and floating down nine floors. Alex is left stunned and confused by these events.

When Vivian returns, Alex assures her he will handle the situation, despite her fears of retaliation from the Flag Sect. Alex contacts Sam Woodsworth to dispose of Granger's body and gather intelligence on the Flag Sect's movements.

A few days later, Alex goes to check on Vivian and Monica at their hotel. While getting breakfast at a nearby diner, he is shocked to see a beautiful teenage girl who appears to be the same person as the old woman who killed Granger. She is wearing the same clothes and seems to have transformed from elderly to young.

The girl briefly notices Alex but doesn't engage with him. Alex is left confused and nervous, wondering about her supernatural abilities and whether she might try to silence him as a witness to Granger's murder.
