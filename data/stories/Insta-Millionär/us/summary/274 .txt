Alex Ambrose, a former store employee who inherited $86 million, is now engaged to Debbie Clifton and involved in a conflict between the Clifton and Steadman families. The Steadmans have enlisted the help of Damon Walker, a skilled fighter from the Blood Brothers gang, to face Paul Novak, who is fighting for the Cliftons.

Damon arrives in Baltimore and immediately wants to confront Paul. Art Steadman takes Damon to the No Surrender martial arts camp to find Paul. They locate Paul meditating on a cliff, still recovering from his previous defeat by Alex.

Damon challenges Paul, revealing his connection to the Blood Brothers and their century-old vendetta against East Coast gangs. A fierce fight ensues, with Paul initially confident but quickly realizing Damon's superior skills. Damon demonstrates an ancient Chinese martial art technique called Xinxinbu, which Paul recognizes.

The fight ends with Damon brutally defeating Paul, kicking him towards the edge of the cliff. Paul desperately clings to the edge, but Damon shows no mercy and steps on his hands, causing Paul to fall to his death.

This confrontation marks a significant escalation in the conflict between the Steadman and Clifton families, with the Blood Brothers now directly involved in Baltimore's underworld power struggle.
