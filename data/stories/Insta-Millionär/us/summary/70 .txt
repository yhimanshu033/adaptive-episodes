Alex's girlfriend Debbie is recruited by Janice Campbell to join a secret girl group at Sunshine Music Company. Debbie goes with Janice to sign a contract and demonstrate her singing and dancing skills, which impress Janice. Debbie starts training that afternoon.

Meanwhile, Alex helps the cheerleading squad at the university prepare for a basketball game. He faces mockery from other students about his supposed ownership of an expensive car. During the game, the basketball players, including Harry and Dean, continue to tease Alex.

After the game, Zara's cousin Karen Young arrives at the sports field. She's described as very attractive, catching the attention of many male students. To everyone's surprise, Karen ignores the other boys and approaches Alex directly, asking him to go eat with her. This leaves the other students shocked and jealous, wondering about the connection between Alex and the beautiful Karen.
