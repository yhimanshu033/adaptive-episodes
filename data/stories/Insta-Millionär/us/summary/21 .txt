Alex witnesses a confrontation between Karen, Zara, and a mysterious girl named Debbie at the university. Karen defends Alex against Zara's insults, unaware that Zara is actually interested in dating the wealthy man she believes Alex to be. Meanwhile, Alex observes three girls bullying Debbie at a campus restaurant due to her apparent poverty. Despite the harassment, Debbie orders expensive food and leaves. Intrigued, Alex follows her to Ramsey Lake, where he watches her eat, cry, pray, and then unexpectedly jump into the lake. The episode ends with Alex wondering if Debbie is attempting suicide.
