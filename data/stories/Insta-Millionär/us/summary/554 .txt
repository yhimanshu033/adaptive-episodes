Alex Ambrose, now running the successful Birchwood Real Estate Company, attends a gathering at the Imperial Hotel with business leaders, real estate developers, and media companies. The event is meant to attract investors for advertising expenses. Alex brings celebrity singer Cassidy Pike as his guest to create buzz.

At the party, Alex meets Arnie Hampton, the powerful head of the Baltimore Media Association and Hampton Enterprises, who controls most of the city's media advertising. Arnie had previously demanded over a million dollars for Birchwood's advertising, an exorbitant amount.

When Alex and Edward try to renegotiate the advertising fee, Arnie dismisses them. He then makes a suggestive comment about Cassidy, implying he might lower the price if she accompanies him for the evening. Angered by Arnie's inappropriate behavior and attempt to exploit his power, Alex punches Arnie, knocking him to the floor.

The episode ends with everyone in shock, staring at Arnie on the ground and Alex standing over him, having just demonstrated that he won't be intimidated or allow others to be disrespected, even by powerful figures in the business world.
