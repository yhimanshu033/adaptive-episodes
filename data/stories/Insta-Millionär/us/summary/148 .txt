Alex, now the owner of Lawrence Heights, hires Sue Bradley as the new property manager. Sue shares this news with her niece Rose during a video call. The conversation leads Rose to believe that Alex might be in love with her, based on Sue's interpretation of Alex's description of the girl he likes.

Rose reflects on her past interactions with Alex and begins to see them in a new light, convincing herself that Alex's actions were motivated by his love for her. She decides not to let this opportunity pass her by.

Meanwhile, Alex agrees to let a livestreaming service use his villa for a video shoot. When he arrives at the location, he notices an old man cleaning garbage from the lake. Alex takes over the task, rowing around the lake to collect trash.

As he does this, some young women at the villa mock him, assuming he's a garbage collector with no ambition. They make disparaging comments about his perceived lifestyle and job prospects.

After finishing the cleaning, Alex approaches the film crew to give them the key to his villa. The person in charge is initially skeptical about Alex being the owner due to his appearance. As Alex presents the key, Minnie Jones, a classmate, arrives and dismisses Alex as "a loser from our school," not believing he could be the villa's owner.
