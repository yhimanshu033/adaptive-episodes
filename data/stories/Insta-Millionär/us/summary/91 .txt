In this episode, Justin Ambrose rescues Debbie from the artificial lake after she was attacked by Lee's dog, Duke. As Debbie loses consciousness, she reflects on her memories with Alex and his promises to protect her. Justin calls for medical help, and Debbie is rushed to the hospital.

Justin then confronts Lee and the others responsible for the incident. He slaps Lee and exposes Charlie and Melissa's involvement in poisoning Debbie's water. Justin physically punishes both Charlie and Melissa for their actions.

Finally, Justin turns his attention to Duke, the dog that attacked Debbie. Despite Angelina's protests, Justin violently subdues Duke and begins pulling out the dog's teeth as punishment for biting Debbie. The episode ends with Justin continuing this brutal act against the dog.
