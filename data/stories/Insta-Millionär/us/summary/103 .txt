The episode focuses on a family gathering of the wealthy Phillips family. Kelly arrives with Alex, introducing him as her boyfriend, which causes a stir among the family members. Kelly's cousin Yvonne had previously introduced her boyfriend Brian, who was well-received by the family matriarch, Margaret.

When Kelly introduces Alex, Sharon, Kelly's friend, initially describes him as a poor student Kelly brought to experience luxury. This causes the family to pity Alex and make condescending remarks. However, Kelly then announces that Alex is actually her boyfriend, shocking everyone, especially Margaret.

Margaret strongly disapproves of Alex due to his apparent lower social status and orders Kelly to send him home. Kelly refuses, standing her ground against her grandmother's wishes. This defiance is unprecedented in the family, as Margaret's word is usually final.

The episode ends with Margaret becoming angry at Kelly's stubbornness and telling Alice, Kelly's mother, to deal with her daughter. The situation creates tension within the family, particularly between Kelly and her grandmother, who had previously favored her above other grandchildren.
