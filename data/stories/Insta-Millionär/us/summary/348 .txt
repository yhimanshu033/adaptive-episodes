Chris Steadman visits the Clifton family home, believing Jessop Clifton to be gravely ill. He observes Jessop's apparent weakness and a doctor's ominous comments, leading him to believe the Cliftons' power is waning. Chris secretly communicates this information to his father, Art Steadman, through a hidden device.

As Chris leaves, he is suddenly surrounded by Clifton security forces. Rufus reveals that the entire scene was a setup. He confronts Chris about his past misdeeds and offers him a choice: to suffer or to end his life quickly with a knife from the Black Orchid cult.

Terrified, Chris attempts to bargain by offering information about the Blood Brothers' attack on Jessop and pledging the Steadmans' loyalty to the Cliftons. However, Rufus remains unmoved and coldly insists that Chris make his choice between suffering or a quick death.
