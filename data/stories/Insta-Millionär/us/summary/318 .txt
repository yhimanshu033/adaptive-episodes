Alex Ambrose, now the leader of the Moon Palace and the Appalachian Alliance, remains unaware of the brewing threats against him. Meanwhile, Lee Harshaw of the Blood Brothers arrives in Baltimore, seeking revenge for Damon Walker's death. He visits Damon's grave and plans to attack martial arts organizations on the East Coast, starting with the Black Swallow Martial Arts Academy in Philadelphia.

At the Black Swallow Academy, Master Zachariah Holbrook emerges from eight months of self-imposed isolation. He is surprised to find Cliff Munson, his previously expelled student, has returned. Despite his initial hesitation, Zachariah forgives Cliff and allows him to rejoin the academy.

Zachariah learns about recent events, including Cliff's severe injury by an unknown opponent, the power struggle in Baltimore between the Clifton and Steadman families, and the death of Damon Walker at the hands of a fighter named Alex. He is also informed that the Blood Brothers have sent a warning message to all major gangs on the East Coast, threatening revenge for past wrongs.

The situation is tense as Lee Harshaw prepares to attack the Black Swallow Academy, while its members remain unaware of the imminent danger. This sets the stage for a potential confrontation between the Blood Brothers and the martial arts community on the East Coast.
