In this episode, Alex continues his attempt to heal Sawyer using the Harmony Technique. Initially, Sawyer remains resistant, feeling unworthy of help. Dr. Isaiah whispers to Sawyer that Alex is the lost child he's been grieving over, which causes a dramatic reaction in Sawyer. He suddenly stands up from his wheelchair, shocking everyone present.

Sawyer approaches Alex emotionally, convinced that he's the long-lost child. The medical staff is amazed by Sawyer's apparent recovery and begins running tests. Alex, unaware of Isaiah's lie, is confused by Sawyer's reaction but helps steady him.

Isaiah takes Sawyer back to his room to rest, where Sawyer expresses his eagerness to confirm if Alex is indeed the missing child and to reunite him with his family. However, it's revealed that Isaiah fabricated this story to provoke a healing response in Sawyer. Isaiah tries to calm Sawyer down and discourage him from pursuing the matter immediately, suggesting that most people have forgotten about the missing child after 20 years.

Isaiah advises Sawyer to keep quiet about Alex to avoid drawing attention to him. Sawyer, now recovering and seeing the world differently after his time in a wheelchair, begins to reconsider his approach to the situation.
