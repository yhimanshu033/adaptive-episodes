Alex Ambrose, now the lord of the Moon Palace, has left for two weeks with Callisto to check on things at the palace. Meanwhile, the Clifton family holds a banquet to celebrate Cynthia's birthday. During the celebration, Jessop praises Alex's abilities and friendship, proposing a toast in his absence.

The scene shifts to Canada, where Tyson Slade, leader of the Blood Brothers, completes a training session. He learns of Luther's death at Alex's hands but shows little concern. Tyson initially dismisses Alex as a threat but eventually agrees to fight him after learning of Alex's new status as lord of the Moon Palace. Tyson sees this as an opportunity to assert dominance over the martial arts community.

Back at the Clifton mansion, the family gathering is interrupted by two uninvited guests - an old man and a younger companion. The old man challenges Jessop's claim that Alex is the most powerful martial artist in the world, dismissing Alex's defeat of Lee Harshaw as insignificant. This intrusion causes tension, with Rufus angrily confronting the old man's statements.

The episode highlights the growing reputation of Alex, the shifting dynamics within the criminal underworld, and introduces new potential conflicts and challengers to Alex's perceived dominance in the martial arts world.
