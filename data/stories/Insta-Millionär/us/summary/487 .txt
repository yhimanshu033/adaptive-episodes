Alex returns home after escaping from the police station with Chelsea's help. Chelsea faces pressure from higher-ups to release the Murdochs and is ultimately suspended when she refuses to comply. She hands over the case to Mills, feeling frustrated at being shut out.

Alex meditates and uses his inner force to heal his knife wound overnight. Clark visits Alex in the morning and is amazed to see that Alex's wound has completely healed. They discuss the events of the previous day, with Clark expressing gratitude for Alex saving his daughter's life.

Clark reveals some information about Neil Hall's past. He explains that Neil, formerly known as Nicholas Hammond, was once part of Clark's team but betrayed them. Neil had a severe gambling problem and eventually joined the Chamber of Commerce before being recruited by the Ghosts. Clark advises Alex not to waste time mourning for Neil.

Alex expresses concern for Joe, Neil's son, struggling to reconcile Joe's character with his father's monstrous actions. The episode highlights the complex relationships and hidden pasts of the characters involved in the ongoing conflict.
