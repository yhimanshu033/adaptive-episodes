Alex confronts Cassius, the leader of the Black Orchid Cult, in an attempt to rescue Debbie. They engage in an intense fight, with Cassius demonstrating unusual abilities that make him difficult to harm. Alex struggles to find an effective strategy against Cassius's seemingly boneless body and ability to absorb attacks.

As the fight reaches a stalemate, Cassius orders the start of the sacrificial ceremony. Cult members approach Debbie with torches, intending to burn her alive. Alex, injured from the fight, desperately tries to reach Debbie but is stopped by Cassius.

The ceremony begins, and Debbie is engulfed in flames. Just as all seems lost, a strange light emanates from Alex's body, growing brighter and larger. This unexpected development surprises everyone present, including Cassius, and may be Alex's last hope to save Debbie from the fire.
