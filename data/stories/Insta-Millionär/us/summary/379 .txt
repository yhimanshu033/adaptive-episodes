Alex, now living a quiet life as a security guard with Debbie, is approached by two wealthy individuals, Ethan Cox and Celia, who seek his help. Ethan is dismissive of Alex, viewing him as just a security guard and unworthy of respect. Celia tries to maintain politeness, aware of Alex's potential importance. The meeting takes place in a restaurant where Alex was having lunch with coworkers.

Jessop, Alex's associate, facilitates the meeting but remains calm and unconcerned about Ethan's attitude. Alex maintains an indifferent and slightly arrogant demeanor, which further irritates Ethan. The contrast between Alex's current job as a security guard and his apparent connections to influential people confuses his coworkers.

Ethan, a skilled Thai boxer accustomed to respect, becomes increasingly frustrated with Alex's lack of deference. Celia attempts to mediate the situation, unsure of Alex's true capabilities. The meeting ends inconclusively, with Celia calling her supervisor for clarification while Alex and Jessop walk away towards Johns Hopkins University.

This episode highlights the tension between Alex's ordinary appearance and job, and his hidden power and connections, as well as the misconceptions people have about him based on his current occupation.
