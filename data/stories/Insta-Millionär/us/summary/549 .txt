Alex wakes up in a strange room after being accidentally hit by Maryann's car. Maryann apologizes and cares for him, offering him soup. Alex insists on leaving despite her concerns. On the way back, Maryann receives an urgent call about customers reacting badly to her company's wine for the second time. She asks Alex to help investigate the issue. Though reluctant to delay seeing Debbie in the hospital, Alex agrees to assist for ten minutes. They arrive at the Robinson Winery office building to find angry protesters gathered outside, complaining about adverse reactions to the wine. The situation threatens to shut down Maryann's company if not resolved quickly.
