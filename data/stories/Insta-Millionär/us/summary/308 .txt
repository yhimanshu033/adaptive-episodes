Alex, carrying the poisoned Debbie, flees from the gang members attacking the Moon Palace. He enters a pine forest where the pursuers mysteriously disappear. While trying to stabilize Debbie's condition using his internal power, Alex is confronted by a mysterious older man with white hair. This man, who seems to have created some kind of barrier, attacks Alex with impressive strength. The stranger recognizes Alex's fighting style as similar to Georgina's and demands to know who Alex is and how he learned to fight that way. Alex finds himself in a difficult situation, torn between saving Debbie and his responsibility to the Moon Palace, while facing this new, powerful opponent.
