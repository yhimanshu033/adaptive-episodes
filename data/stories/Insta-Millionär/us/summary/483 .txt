Alex confronts Michael Murdoch at a banquet in the Hill Hotel. Michael underestimates Alex, viewing him as a reckless thug with no real skills. He taunts Alex, believing he has intimidated him into silence. The crowd at the party is impressed by Michael's confidence and begins to doubt Alex's abilities.

Meanwhile, Michael's subordinates secretly enter the hall with concealed weapons, ready to attack Alex on command. Michael continues to threaten Alex, mentioning potential legal consequences and his powerful uncle Steven.

As police sirens are heard outside, Alex remains calm and dismisses Michael's threats, calling him a "wimp" and "full of hot air." Louisa, Michael's sister, grows concerned, sensing there's more to Alex than appears.

Michael, still confident, blows cigar smoke in Alex's face and continues his taunts. Suddenly, Alex springs into action, snatching the cigar from Michael and punching him, using the lit end to scrape across Michael's face. This unexpected move demonstrates that Alex is not intimidated and is capable of swift, decisive action.
