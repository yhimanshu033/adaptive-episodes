Alex prepares to fight Damon Walker in a high-stakes underground boxing match that will determine the fate of the Clifton and Steadman families. Justin and Nelly arrive to support Alex, though they're worried about his safety after learning about Damon's reputation. Chelsea Wood, the police officer, decides to support the Cliftons and bets on Alex.

Damon makes a dramatic entrance by jumping through a window into the ring. The audience is allowed to place bets, with most favoring Damon. Julian Steadman taunts Chelsea for her small bet, prompting Celeste and Selene to bet $3 million on Alex. Justin bets $15 million on his brother.

Jessop Clifton shocks everyone by betting $150 million on Alex, which Art Steadman matches for Damon. Suddenly, an anonymous bet of $300 million appears on the screen, believed to be from Nathan Ambrose, trying to discourage Alex.

The tension builds as the fight is about to begin, with massive amounts of money at stake and the balance of power in the city hanging in the balance. Alex faces immense pressure to win against the seemingly unbeatable Damon Walker.
