Alex finds Debbie injured after being bullied by Maddison Robbins and her friends. He takes her to the hospital and then to an expensive restaurant. At the restaurant, Maddison and her friends confront Debbie again, mocking and physically abusing her. Alex intervenes, but Debbie stops him from retaliating. 

Three men from the Kappa Alpha Society arrive, supporting Maddison and threatening Alex. Debbie explains that the Kappa Alpha Society is a powerful organization they can't afford to upset. She also tells Alex she's moving out of the dorm to escape the bullying.

Alex decides to find a better place for Debbie to live. The next day, he goes to Washington Heights, an upscale residential area near Preston University, intending to buy a house for Debbie. At the sales office, he's ignored by the staff due to his appearance, despite the manager Darla's reputation for going to great lengths to sell properties.
