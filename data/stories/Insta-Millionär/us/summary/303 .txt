Alex discovers that Debbie has been poisoned with a deadly substance called "black poison water," a mixture of various toxins with no known antidote. He takes her to multiple hospitals, finally finding one that can prolong her life but not cure her. Alex informs the Clifton family, and Jessop identifies the poison based on the toxins found in Debbie's blood.

Alex realizes that Melissa is likely responsible for poisoning Debbie, as he recalls her suspicious behavior when offering soup at the sports ground. He feels guilty, believing the poison was meant for him. After leaving the Clifton mansion, Alex tries to call Melissa but she doesn't answer, confirming his suspicions.

Upon returning to the hospital, Alex learns that Debbie has woken up and wants to see him. Her condition is deteriorating, showing symptoms typical of black poison water poisoning. The situation is dire, with Debbie facing a painful death as the toxin gradually invades her body.
