Alex Ambrose visits his friend Don in the hospital, bringing good news that the university has accepted responsibility for Don's injury and will cover his hospital fees. This relieves Don's worry about repaying Alex.

Meanwhile, at the Fidelity Group headquarters, David Drake appeals to his wealthy friends for financial support to save the company from bankruptcy. They need to raise 1.5 billion dollars, but only manage to gather about 40 million from their immediate circle. Darryl Brennan, not as close to David, pretends to offer a million dollars but actually only gives 5,000, using a clever ruse with his girlfriend Myriam.

Realizing the collected amount is insufficient, Lindsey, David's sister, decides to reach out to Alex for help. She calls him, overcoming her emotional hesitation, as Alex is visiting Don in the hospital.

Lindsey explains the Drake family's dire financial situation to Alex, revealing they need to raise 2 billion dollars to avoid bankruptcy. They've only managed to secure 540 million so far, and she asks if Alex can help raise the remaining amount.
