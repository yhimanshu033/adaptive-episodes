Alex leaves his family and the Ambrose estate with the four Moon Maidens. He says emotional goodbyes to his parents, brother Justin, and supporters Ken Stokes and Mark Stephens. During the helicopter ride, Alex breaks down crying, and the Moon Maidens comfort him. They arrive in D.C. after a 7-hour flight, where Alex plans to see Debbie.

Meanwhile, Reginald Drake returns home after dealing with the aftermath of the wedding incident. His son David reveals that Leona has left after discovering her old diary, which David had been using to manipulate her. The diary, given to David by Darryl Brennan, contained information about Leona's life before she lost her memory.

Reginald is furious with David for his foolish actions, realizing that this situation could lead to retaliation from Alex, who had previously warned them to take good care of Leona. The Drake family now faces a potential crisis as they fear Alex's response to Leona's disappearance and the revelation of David's deception.
