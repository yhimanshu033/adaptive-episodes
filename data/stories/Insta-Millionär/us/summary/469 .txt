Alex explains to Clark Fairfax that the Ghosts are a dangerous organization seeking to control powerful individuals and institutions. They are rumored to be developing targeted diseases and infiltrating various sectors, including hospitals. Alex reveals that Sarah, Clark's daughter, is not ill but has lost her soul due to exposure to dark energy in a cemetery. Despite Clark's skepticism, they decide to investigate the cemetery.

The group travels to the cemetery north of Baltimore. Alex senses dark energy and distributes protective charms, explaining that he and Clark are strong enough to withstand it without protection. In the cemetery, Alex identifies a freshly covered grave and instructs Clark's men to dig it up.

Upon excavation, they discover a hexagonal box instead of a coffin. Inside the box are scrolls written in an unfamiliar language and something that disgusts Debbie, causing her to retreat to the car. This unusual find suggests there's more to Sarah's condition and the cemetery than initially thought, potentially linking to the Ghosts' activities or other supernatural elements.
