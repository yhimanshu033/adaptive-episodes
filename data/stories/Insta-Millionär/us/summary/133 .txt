Alex confronts Leo and the Flag Sect at Connie's Restaurant to rescue Vivian and Monica. Despite Leo's taunts and threats, Alex remains defiant. As the situation escalates, with Leo attempting to humiliate Vivian publicly, Alex's security team, Squad Three, arrives just in time. They quickly overpower the Flag Sect members, leaving Leo vulnerable.

Alex confronts Leo, who begs for mercy, but Alex executes him with a gunshot to the head. He then instructs his team to destroy the Flag Sect but allows its members to remain in New York if they stay within the law.

Alex rescues Vivian and Monica, taking them to the Azalea Guest House. Vivian learns about Alex's true power and his role in destroying the Azure Dragon Society, but she doesn't hold it against him.

The next day, Alex visits Sam in the hospital, who is recovering well. He ensures Vivian and Monica are safely accommodated at the guest house. Before returning to school, Alex buys a live chicken for Grandma at his villa.

On his way to campus, Alex receives a call from Suzan, inviting him for drinks at the Angel Bar as a thank you for their recent shopping trip.
