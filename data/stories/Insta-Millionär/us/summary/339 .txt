In this episode, two priests of the Black Orchid cult, Orion and Jax, struggle to transport the kidnapped Debbie through a snowy field to their headquarters in Canada. Upon arrival, they report to their leader Cassius, who is displeased with their failure to retrieve the Phoenix stone. As punishment, Cassius orders them to "repent to the gods," which results in their deaths.

Meanwhile, Alex learns everything about Debbie's past from Jessop Clifton and sets out alone to rescue her in Canada. At the Black Orchid headquarters, a sacrificial ceremony begins with Debbie chained to a stone pillar. As she reflects on her life and professes her love for Alex, he suddenly appears, having used his newfound powers to fly to her rescue.

The episode ends with Alex and Debbie reuniting, while Cassius, enraged by the interruption of the sacred ceremony, charges towards Alex to confront him.
