Alex receives a call from his estranged brother Justin, who informs him that the powerful Ambrose family is being targeted by an unknown threat. Despite being disowned, Alex feels protective of his family and decides to investigate. He attends a celebration dinner hosted by Celia and Ethan, hoping to gather information about the Ambrose family.

At the dinner, Celia again offers Alex a position as an instructor at the Baltimore Military Academy, which he shows little interest in. Alex asks Celia and Ethan about the Ambrose family, but they have limited knowledge. Celia mentions the Ambrose Group, a collection of businesses, but is unsure if it's connected to the family.

A senior executive of the Ambrose Group, Wesley Boone, introduces himself at the dinner. He boasts about the company's global presence and expansion plans but speaks condescendingly to Celia, which irritates her. Alex's attempts to gather information about his family's situation remain largely unsuccessful, as the Ambrose family typically maintains a low profile.
