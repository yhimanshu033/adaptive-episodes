Alex Ambrose, now working as a security guard for Debbie at Johns Hopkins University, finds himself embroiled in a complex and dangerous situation. After a violent confrontation with Julian Duvant and Chris Steadman, Alex's actions lead to the death of Danielle and severe injury of Saul. The situation escalates when Saul is murdered in the hospital by a hired assassin named Morag Sparks.

Chelsea Wood, investigating the case, is frustrated by the disappearance of Saul's body and the lack of evidence. Chris Steadman taunts Chelsea over the phone, revealing that Saul's body has been taken to Shady Pines funeral parlor for immediate cremation, destroying any potential evidence.

Meanwhile, Chris Steadman prepares to confront Jessop Clifton, aiming to take control of the Baltimore underworld. He visits his father, Art Steadman, at a private villa to discuss their plans. Art orders an attack on Dukes Casino, owned by the Clifton family, as a declaration of war.

The situation is tense, with the Steadman family, backed by the Blood Brothers Gang, preparing for a major conflict with the Cliftons. The stakes are high, as the outcome will determine control over Baltimore's criminal underworld. Chris feels nervous but tries to appear confident as they set their plans in motion.
