Alex confronts Julian and Chris about the attack on Don. Julian pulls out a gun, but Alex quickly disarms him. Alex then orders Saul to shoot Julian, resulting in Julian being shot in the leg. Chris manipulates the situation to avoid blame, while Julian pleads for help. Alex leaves, and Saul flees the scene. Chris promises Julian revenge but privately plans to deal with Alex as a threat.

Alex rushes to the hospital to see Don, who hasn't received treatment due to lack of funds. Don's sister Daisy pleads with Alex for financial help. Alex leaves the room, initially disappointing Don and Daisy. However, he quickly returns with a doctor and nurses, having arranged for Don's surgery. This act of generosity surprises and relieves Don and Daisy, showcasing Alex's true character and willingness to help his friend in need.
