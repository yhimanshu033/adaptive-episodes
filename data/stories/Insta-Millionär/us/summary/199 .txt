Alex, Phillipa, and Nelly face hostility from Myriam and Darryl at the Olympic Games opening ceremony. Darryl offers money to anyone who will kick them out, causing a chaotic situation. Sebastian Wade, the director of volunteers, intervenes and mentions an urgent need for a Malay translator for the Sultan of Brunei's speech.

Nelly, who can speak Malay, encourages Alex to volunteer. Despite skepticism from others, Sebastian takes Alex to meet with Olympic officials. Nelly demonstrates her Malay skills but requests to translate from inside a mascot costume, with Alex lip-syncing on stage.

Alex also suggests including Phillipa as a Spanish translator. The director agrees, and both Alex and Phillipa are quickly prepared for their roles on stage. As they take their positions next to the Sultan of Brunei, Alex feels a mix of excitement and disbelief at the unexpected turn of events.
