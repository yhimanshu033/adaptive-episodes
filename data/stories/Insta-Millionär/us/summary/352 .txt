Alex confronts a group of aggressive reporters after saving the Moon girls from a car bomb. He destroys their cameras and equipment to make them leave. One reporter, who Alex identifies as a member of the Blood Brothers gang, reveals he has a bomb in his camera capable of destroying the building. Alex remains calm and challenges the bomber to detonate it, claiming no one will die. The bomber is confused by Alex's confidence and hesitates. As they face off, police sirens are heard approaching, and special forces officers arrive on the scene. The bomber believes this development protects him from Alex's actions. Throughout the confrontation, Alex displays supernatural awareness and confidence in his abilities to protect those around him, while the Moon girls and other reporters watch in shock and fear.
