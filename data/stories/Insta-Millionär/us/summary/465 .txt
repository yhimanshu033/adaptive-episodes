Alex confronts Tabitha, the assassin who tried to kill him. He warns her not to attempt it again and refuses to undo the nerve damage he inflicted. Tabitha, revealing her real name as Kendall Payne, begs Alex to teach her and offers to work for him. Alex leaves without agreeing.

Later that night, Debbie calls Alex from a hospital. Her roommate Sarah has fallen ill, and the doctors are not optimistic. Remembering Alex's healing abilities, Debbie asks him to come help. Alex agrees, touched by Debbie's faith in him.

At the hospital's VIP wing, Alex meets Debbie. They enter Sarah's room, where several people are gathered, including Mr. Clark Fairfax, who appears to have a military background. Sarah, despite her pale appearance, smiles at Debbie. Alex prepares to use his inner power to attempt healing Sarah, despite his lack of formal medical training.
