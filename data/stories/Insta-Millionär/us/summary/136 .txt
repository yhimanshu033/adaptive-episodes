In this episode, Philip Gellor, the apartment manager, attempts to sexually assault Sue Bradley, a tenant who owes rent. He closes the curtains in her apartment, creating a dark and threatening atmosphere. Philip offers to forgive Sue's rent in exchange for sexual favors, revealing his true, predatory nature. He becomes increasingly aggressive, holding Sue against her will and showing her condoms and Viagra.

As the situation escalates, there's an urgent knock on the door. The interruption, which turns out to be Alex, distracts Philip. Sue takes advantage of this moment to knee Philip in the groin, causing him intense pain. When Sue opens the door, Alex enters and inquires about the situation.

Sue, feeling embarrassed and worried about being judged, doesn't fully explain what happened. She orders Philip to leave, but it's implied that he hasn't given up on his intentions. After Philip's departure, Sue invites Alex to stay for a drink. She thanks him for his timely intervention, and Alex asks if Philip touched her, showing concern for her well-being.
