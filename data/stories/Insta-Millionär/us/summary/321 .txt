The episode describes the anticipation and preparations for a crucial meeting in Baltimore between various martial arts organizations and Lee Harshaw, a member of the Blood Brothers who has been on a violent rampage. Representatives from different martial arts groups arrive in the city, each with their own concerns and strategies for dealing with the threat.

Brody Kent of the Sunrise sect expresses doubts about their ability to stop Lee. Grayson Ashbury of the Pouncing Leopard school is nervous about the confrontation. Belinda Hawkins of the Iron Fist sect plans to use the situation to elevate her all-female group's status in the martial arts community.

Meanwhile, unaware of these events, Alex and Debbie celebrate New Year's Eve at the Moon Palace in the Appalachian Mountains. They give a speech to their disciples and share a romantic moment on stage, kissing at the encouragement of Celeste and the crowd. The contrast between the tense atmosphere in Baltimore and the festive mood at the Moon Palace is stark, highlighting the different worlds that Alex and Debbie now inhabit compared to their previous lives.
