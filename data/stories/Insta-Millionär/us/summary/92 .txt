In this episode, Justin continues his violent retaliation against those who harmed Debbie. He pulls out several of Duke's teeth, causing distress to Angelina, Duke's owner. Angelina reveals she's pregnant with Guy Avery's child, not Lee's, shocking everyone. She taunts Justin about his family's status, believing she's protected by her connection to the Avery family.

Justin, unfazed by Angelina's threats, physically assaults her by throwing her to the ground, likely causing serious injuries. He then takes Debbie's friends and bandmates to the hospital to check on her condition.

At the hospital, Debbie's injuries are found to be minor. Justin stays with her overnight. When Debbie wakes up, Justin explains what happened and how he dealt with her bullies. He's called away to Washington for a music contest but is reluctant to leave Debbie. She encourages him to go, assuring him she's fine.

Before leaving, Justin instructs Melissa to take care of Debbie, threatening her if anything happens. The episode ends with Justin departing for Washington, leaving Debbie to recover in the hospital.
