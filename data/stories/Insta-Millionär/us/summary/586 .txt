Alex Ambrose, facing accusations of fraud at the Woodside Clinic, reveals that he has been using higher-quality, more expensive ingredients in medications while keeping prices low for patients. This surprises the investigators and patients, who praise Alex for his generosity. However, FBI Agent Johnson insists on closing the clinic for further investigation, causing tension between the patients and law enforcement.

As the situation escalates, with patients rallying to protect Alex and the clinic, Alex attempts to make a phone call to Dr. Isaiah Allbrook for help. Instead, he accidentally dials his estranged grandfather's number. The call is answered by Archibald Pitt, his grandfather's assistant, who becomes emotional upon hearing from Alex. Archibald senses Alex is in trouble and offers help, despite the risk of punishment for violating family rules. The call ends abruptly when it appears Archibald has been caught speaking to Alex.
