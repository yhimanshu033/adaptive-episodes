Alex rushes to Don's apartment after receiving a threatening call. He arrives to find Don badly beaten by Saul, a dangerous enforcer sent by Julian and Chris. Alex engages in a fierce fight with Saul, showcasing his exceptional martial arts skills. Even when Saul pulls out a gun, Alex manages to deflect the bullets with a blade. 

After defeating Saul, Alex helps Don and his sister Daisy leave for the hospital. He then confronts Saul, demanding to be taken to meet Julian and Chris, who ordered the attack. Saul, terrified of Alex, agrees and calls Chris to locate them.

Meanwhile, Julian and Chris are at Julian's villa, entertaining two women. When Saul calls, Julian answers Chris's phone, expecting good news about the attack on Alex and Don. The situation is tense, with Alex now seeking revenge against those who ordered the assault on his friend.
