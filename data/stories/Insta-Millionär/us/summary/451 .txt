Alex continues his high-stakes gambling at the casino on the cruise ship "Paradise." After losing his initial 5 million dollars, he challenges the casino to a fair game with a new roulette wheel and a guest as the croupier. The casino director agrees, and Alex secures access to a large sum of money from the Moon Palace.

Alex bets 15 million dollars on black for the first round and wins. The atmosphere in the casino is tense, with all guests watching intently. For the second round, Alex doubles his bet to 30 million dollars on red and wins again. The crowd is excited by Alex's winning streak, rooting for him as the underdog against the casino.

The man in white with an eagle tattoo, who seems to be supervising the game, appears increasingly agitated by Alex's success. The casino director maintains a calm facade but is visibly anxious about the outcome. Alex has now won two consecutive games, putting him one win away from his goal of winning three out of ten games and potentially exposing the casino's fraudulent practices.
