The episode continues the story of Alex, Cathy, and Billy. After Billy's arrest, Cathy decides to break up with him. In a heated argument at the university cafeteria, Billy demands Cathy pay him back $350,000, threatening her if she doesn't comply. Cathy, now desperate and afraid, considers reconnecting with Alex, believing he is wealthy and could help her.

Alex meets with his friends but doesn't reveal his true financial status. Later, Cathy calls Alex, asking to meet at The Angel bar. She attempts to manipulate Alex's emotions, implying their past relationship still means something to her.

At the bar, Cathy tells Alex she broke up with Billy upon learning he was the thief. She then tries to win Alex back, claiming she's realized he's the perfect partner for her. She emphasizes that she now values integrity and kindness over wealth, though her true motives seem to be driven by her financial troubles and belief in Alex's wealth.
