Alex confronts Cliff Munson in a fierce battle at the wedding venue. Using psychological tactics and his newfound supernatural powers, Alex manages to defeat Cliff, shocking the onlookers. After the fight, Alex approaches Leona and David, causing David to panic and wet himself in fear.

As Alex nears the couple, he begins to doubt his assumptions about Leona's willingness to marry David. Before he can confront them, police sirens are heard, and armed security personnel enter the scene, aiming their weapons at Alex.

Reginald Drake, David's father, takes control of the situation and orders the security team to deal with Alex, claiming it's for everyone's protection. He suggests they can cover up whatever happens to Alex afterward.

Just as Reginald is about to give the order to shoot, Lindsey rushes in and shields Alex with her body. She pleads with Reginald to spare Alex's life and let him go, promising he won't cause any more trouble. Lindsey's father, Charles Marvel, is horrified but doesn't intervene. Reginald refuses to back down, creating a tense standoff between Lindsey, Alex, and the armed security team.
