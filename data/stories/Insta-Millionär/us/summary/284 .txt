Alex Ambrose, a former store employee who inherited $86 million, is now engaged to Debbie Clifton. After winning a crucial fight against Damon Walker of the Blood Brothers, Alex helped shift power in Baltimore to the Cliftons. The Steadmans lost control of their businesses to the Cliftons, while Nathan Ambrose and Colin Patterson plot against the Drake family and Fidelity Group in Washington D.C.

Meanwhile, Reginald Drake introduces his son David to the family business. Suddenly, several subsidiaries of the Fidelity Group face severe problems. It's revealed that Noah Freeman, a long-time employee, is secretly working with Colin Patterson to overthrow the Drake family.

In a shocking turn of events, the Drake family loses nearly $2 billion in one day due to contract cancellations and refund demands. Reginald Drake is unable to understand or stop the crisis. Colin Patterson, working with a powerful backer (implied to be Nathan Ambrose), celebrates this development as a step towards making the Pattersons the most powerful family in Washington D.C.

David Drake, realizing the severity of the situation, attempts to raise $2.5 billion to save the family business. He approaches a bank manager and his wealthy former classmates for loans, but is largely unsuccessful. The episode ends with David discovering numerous concerned messages on his social media, as news of the Drake family's troubles spreads.
