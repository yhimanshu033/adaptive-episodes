Alex Ambrose is called to the Hall family home in Columbia after receiving a distressing call from Joe Hall. Upon arrival, he finds multiple people poisoned and near death. Using his supernatural healing abilities, Alex attempts to save Neil Hall, but the poison proves resistant to his initial efforts. Realizing the poison attacks life energy rather than cells, Alex uses a special seed given to him by Celia Turner. He crushes the seed, mixes it with water, and administers it to the victims. This method successfully counteracts the poison, saving everyone's lives. The episode ends with Alex exhausted but relieved that all the victims will survive, though they remain unconscious. The identity of the poisoner and their motive remain unknown, but it's clear they possess similar energy-manipulation abilities to Alex.
