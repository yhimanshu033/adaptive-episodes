Alex discovers that Rose and Suzan have been kidnapped by Luciel Brennan, a dangerous figure from Rose's past. He contacts Ken Stokes for help, who attempts to reach Luciel's father, Donald Brennan, but fails. Ken informs Alex that the Brennan family is planning to leave New York and may be seeking revenge on Rose before departing.

Meanwhile, Luciel takes Rose and Suzan to a small hotel on the outskirts of New York, where they meet Donald. The father and son duo reveal their intentions to sexually assault the women. Rose and Suzan are terrified and beg for mercy, but their pleas fall on deaf ears.

As Luciel prepares to attack Rose and Donald approaches Suzan, the hotel room phone rings unexpectedly. Donald answers, and it's Ken Stokes on the line, demanding that they stop whatever they're doing to Suzan immediately. This interruption provides a glimmer of hope for the two captive women in their dire situation.
