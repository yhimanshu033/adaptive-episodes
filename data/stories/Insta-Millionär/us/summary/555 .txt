Alex Ambrose, the new owner of Birchwood Real Estate Company, attends an event at the Imperial Hotel with famous singer Cassidy Pike. He gets into a confrontation with Arnie Hampton, the influential head of the Baltimore Media Association, after Hampton makes inappropriate remarks about Cassidy. Alex physically assaults Hampton twice, shocking the crowd of real estate developers and media executives.

Peter Wilson of Windsong Media and other industry leaders demand Alex apologize, warning him that he'll never succeed in Baltimore if he doesn't. Alex refuses and further escalates the situation by kicking Hampton. Security is called, but the head guard recognizes Alex and doesn't intervene. Alex ends the party and leaves with Cassidy.

On the way home, Edward expresses concern about the enemies Alex has made and suggests involving his father, Oscar Steadman, to smooth things over. Alex remains confident, stating that Birchwood will become the most popular real estate company in Baltimore without help from the established players.

Alex reveals he has acquired new supernatural abilities, including weather control, which he plans to use to his advantage in the real estate market. Despite the challenges ahead, Alex believes he's entering the struggling real estate market at the right time and is prepared to run the business on his own terms.
