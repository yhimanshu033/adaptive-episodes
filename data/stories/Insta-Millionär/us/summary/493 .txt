Alex attends a tea party at Caroline Hall's house, where he confronts her about paying for a broken jade unicorn. Caroline dismisses Alex and his friends, claiming it's in Joe's best interest as he prepares for a football career. Alex, disappointed in Joe's acceptance of this, decides to visit Neil Hall's grave. He finds the grave neglected and leaves flowers, reflecting on Neil's past kindness.

Later, Rufus informs Alex about a scam involving the broken jade unicorn. Rufus brings in a group of people, including Heather Lennox, who are accused of running a scheme where they deliberately break expensive items and demand compensation from unsuspecting victims. Alex examines receipts showing multiple instances of this scam.

When questioned, Heather claims they have no right to detain her. Alex warns her to cooperate, implying potential consequences if she doesn't tell the truth about the scam operation.
