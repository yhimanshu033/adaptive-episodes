Alex discusses the challenges at the Woodside Clinic with Sawyer, mentioning the lack of staff and his uncertainty about giving too much responsibility to Kendall, the only person he's recruited. Sawyer informs Alex that Dr. Isaiah Allbrook has signed him up for the Top Doctor Competition, held every five years at the county level. Initially reluctant, Alex agrees to participate after Sawyer persuades him, suggesting it could help legitimize his medical skills and benefit the clinic.

After visiting the hospital chapel, where Sawyer lights a candle for Alex's luck, they have a private conversation with Isaiah about Alex's participation in the competition. Isaiah is relieved that Alex has agreed to compete, as he's looking for a successor.

As Alex pushes Sawyer's wheelchair through a crowded street outside the hospital, they encounter a family with a crying baby. The young mother struggles to calm the child while facing criticism from the grandparents and her husband. The baby's persistent crying causes embarrassment for the family and annoyance among the crowd, highlighting the challenges of parenting in public spaces.
