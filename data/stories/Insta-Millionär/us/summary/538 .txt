Alex attends a Cassidy Pike concert with his mother Flora at the Grand Theater in Philadelphia. They meet secretly due to family restrictions. The concert hall is packed, and Cassidy's performance is met with great enthusiasm. During the show, Alex examines a gift from Holly Lambert, which turns out to be ownership papers for a valuable piece of real estate in Baltimore, including a villa called Birchwood House. Alex considers selling it and remembers a real estate agent named Cole Havers who might help.

Cassidy's performance is emotional and powerful, bringing tears to many audience members, including Flora. At the end of the concert, Cassidy announces a special activity where audience members write wishes on paper, fold them into airplanes, and throw them towards her. She promises to try to fulfill the wish on any airplane she catches, exciting the crowd. This gesture is Cassidy's way of thanking her fans for their support.
