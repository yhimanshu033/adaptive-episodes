In this episode, David Drake stages a rescue of Leona at a lake, pretending to save her from drowning. This act triggers a vague memory in Leona, making her believe David is connected to her past. They share a meal of paella, which Leona finds unusually delicious. David's actions make a positive impression on Leona, who sees him as kind and heroic.

After parting ways with Leona, David meets with Darryl and Colin, revealing that the entire scenario was staged using information from Debbie's diary. They discuss their progress in winning Leona's affections, with David confident in his approach.

Meanwhile, Alex has been trying to contact Leona without success. He focuses on his studies until Phillipa invites him to accompany her to the Olympic Games opening ceremony. Despite some hesitation, Alex agrees to go with her.

At the Olympic venue, Alex and Phillipa encounter Nelly Lane's food truck. While Alex catches up with Nelly, Phillipa leaves for her volunteer duties. The episode ends with Alex helping Nelly at the food truck, interacting with international customers, including a bearded man speaking an unfamiliar language.
