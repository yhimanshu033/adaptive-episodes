The episode continues the confrontation between the students and the Baltimore Horse Gang led by Micky. After initially celebrating their victory, the students find themselves surrounded by a larger, more skilled group of fighters. Micky demands money and respect from the students, who are now terrified.

Some wealthy students offer to pay Micky, thinking money will solve the problem. However, Micky rejects their offers, stating that respect is more important than money. He becomes violent, striking students with his crutches and demanding they show respect.

Eventually, Micky agrees to accept money but demands an exorbitant sum of one billion dollars from the group. The students are shocked by this amount, realizing it far exceeds what they can collectively afford. They are left in a difficult situation, afraid to anger Micky further by suggesting the amount is too high.

Throughout the confrontation, Heidi observes the situation carefully, trying to find a way out while maintaining her dignity. The episode highlights the contrast between those who quickly resort to begging and offering money, and those like Heidi who remain composed in the face of danger.
