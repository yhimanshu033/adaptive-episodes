Alex returns to face Tyson Slade after successfully healing Debbie. At the hospital, everyone is amazed by Debbie's miraculous recovery and improved appearance. Alex refuses offers to join the medical staff, focusing instead on his upcoming confrontation with Tyson. Despite Debbie's and Jessop's concerns, Alex is determined to make Tyson pay for attacking Debbie.

At the Hudson River, a large crowd of martial artists and spectators from around the world have gathered to witness the fight between Alex and Tyson. As time passes without Alex's arrival, tension builds. Suddenly, Alex makes a dramatic entrance, arriving on a boat propelled by the river's current. His appearance causes excitement among the crowd, with many people taking pictures and admiring his composure. Alex's entrance is seen as both cool and magnificent, creating a stark contrast to Tyson, who has been waiting on the shore. The stage is now set for their confrontation, with Alex demonstrating confidence and style in his approach.
