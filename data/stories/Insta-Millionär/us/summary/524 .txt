In this episode, Spencer Holliday announces significant changes in the martial arts associations. Alex is promoted to an envoy role for the American Martial Arts Association, overseeing the arbitrator's office and supervising branch presidents. He's given a token of power and reports only to Spencer.

Two days after the battle, Spencer and Alex discuss the aftermath. Spencer explains that the Ghosts are covering up Jacob's death to avoid shame, and Hugo is suppressing news of Alex's victory. Spencer mentions "the other Alex," a young, talented fighter who defeated Tyson Slade, unaware that this is actually the same Alex.

Spencer warns Alex about potential threats from the Ghosts and other enemies who may unite against him. He advises Alex to continue training and growing stronger, as his inner power is not yet stable enough. Spencer believes that once Alex's inner power is stable, he could become even stronger than Spencer himself.

Alex expresses his lack of interest in fame and glory, preferring to focus on his medical career and life with his wife. Spencer muses about the potential of Alex and "the other Alex" working together, unaware they are the same person. The episode ends with Alex reflecting on how his appearance may have changed, making him unrecognizable as the fighter who defeated Tyson Slade.
