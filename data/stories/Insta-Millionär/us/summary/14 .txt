Alex visits Rose in the hospital, bringing a box of food intended for his roommates. He finds Rose's room full of visitors and expensive gifts. When Alex presents the food, Rose and her friends react negatively, mocking him for bringing cheap food to a hospital visit. Alex leaves, feeling uncomfortable and embarrassed.

Zane Harrison, who was also injured in the motorcycle accident, arrives and receives a warm welcome from Rose, contrasting sharply with her cold treatment of Alex. This disparity in treatment upsets Joe and Alex's other friends.

Rose's aunt, Sue Bradley, arrives and expresses concern for Rose's injuries. She initially scolds Zane for the accident but is informed by Rose that Zane's father helped resolve their family's recent troubles with the Trade Joe's Group.

Sue is skeptical about Zane's ability to influence such a powerful group. Zane, feeling embarrassed, calls his father to confirm his involvement. However, his father reveals that he was unable to meet with Mr. Chase, the president of the Merchant Union, exposing Zane's lie about his family's influence in resolving Rose's family issues.

This episode highlights the social dynamics and misconceptions surrounding Alex's hidden wealth, as well as the superficial nature of some of Rose's relationships.
