Alex receives an unexpected call from Zara, asking him to meet her at the Coffee Palace near the university. Upon arrival, Zara surprises Alex by asking him to date her cousin, describing him as suitable due to his perceived low status. Zara leaves, and her cousin arrives shortly after.

To Alex's shock, Zara's cousin is Karen, the assistant manager from First Republic Bank who had previously caused him trouble. Karen, unaware of Alex's true wealth, had initially planned to use a "loser" boyfriend to make her boss Robert jealous. However, upon recognizing Alex and remembering his wealth, Karen quickly changes her attitude and becomes polite.

Zara, oblivious to the situation, continues to treat Alex poorly, believing he is being used as part of Karen's plan. Karen tries to signal Zara to be nicer to Alex but is unsuccessful. When Zara sends Alex to find a waiter, Karen attempts to go instead but is stopped by Zara.

While Alex is away, Karen realizes he could be a better prospect than Robert due to his immense wealth. When Alex returns, Karen apologizes for Zara's behavior and attempts to charm him. When Alex questions why she wants to date a "poor loser," Karen lies, claiming she wants to understand ordinary life better and is open to a serious relationship, hoping to win Alex's favor.
