Alex, now working as a security guard at Richmond University, encounters Melissa Kennedy, his ex-girlfriend who previously caused problems for him and Debbie. Melissa has been manipulated by Chris, who is seeking revenge against Alex. Chris has given Melissa money and promised marriage, asking her to get close to Alex as part of his plan.

Melissa arrives at the university pretending to be a new student. When she approaches Alex, he reacts angrily, pushing her away and causing her to fall. A male student witnesses this and confronts Alex about his behavior. Melissa apologizes to Alex for her past actions and claims she wants to make amends, stating that she will be studying at the university.

Alex is conflicted about Melissa's presence and her apparent desire to change, but he remains wary and unforgiving due to her past actions that hurt Debbie. Meanwhile, Alex continues his daily routine as a security guard, using the time to practice meditation and strengthen his mind.
