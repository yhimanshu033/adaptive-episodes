In this episode, Tyson Slade demonstrates his extraordinary power by subduing a polar bear and effortlessly catching fish, impressing Luther Duncan. Luther seeks Tyson's help to deal with the security guard who killed Lee, but Tyson dismisses the idea as beneath him. Luther realizes he must handle the situation himself and plans to send the Blood Brothers' top fighters instead.

Meanwhile, Alex and Debbie return to Baltimore and visit Jessop Clifton, who appears to have aged significantly. The Clifton family's position as overlords remains secure due to Alex's support, despite the Steadman family's desire to challenge them.

Later that night, Art and Chris Steadman engage in a covert operation, retrieving a hidden envelope from a brick wall. Art appears stressed and insists they have no choice but to proceed with their plan, though the details of this plan are not revealed.
