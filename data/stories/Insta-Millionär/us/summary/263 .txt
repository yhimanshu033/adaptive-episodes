Alex is released from police custody after evidence proves his innocence in Julian's shooting. Captain Finn Murphy reluctantly lets him go, pressured by Officer Chelsea Wood and lack of evidence to hold Alex. Finn reports to Chris Steadman, who is angered by Alex's release but accepts it due to Chelsea's influential family background.

Chelsea then demands to arrest Chris and Julian based on Saul's confession about their criminal activities. Finn strongly opposes this, trying to protect Chris and Julian. He attempts to dissuade Chelsea, citing lack of evidence and asserting his authority as captain. However, Chelsea remains determined to make the arrests, stating her commitment to justice and serving the people. She leaves Finn's office, intent on pursuing the arrests despite his objections.

The situation creates tension between Chelsea's dedication to law enforcement and Finn's apparent corruption and loyalty to Chris. The episode ends with Chelsea defying Finn's orders and heading out to arrest Chris and Julian, setting up a potential conflict between law enforcement factions.
