Alex Ambrose, posing as a teacher, confronts Devon Boardman and his men at the Night Banquet club to rescue Eric and three female students. Despite being outnumbered and facing armed opponents, Alex easily overpowers several attackers. When Devon points a gun at Alex, he fires multiple shots, but Alex remains unharmed and continues advancing.

Art Steadman and his nephew Julian arrive with reinforcements. Art quickly distances himself from the situation, claiming it's a misunderstanding and that his family doesn't directly run the club. He treats Alex with great respect, revealing Alex's true identity as the famous martial arts fighter, Master Alex.

Upon realizing who Alex is, Devon's men drop their weapons in fear. Devon surrenders and begs for forgiveness. Alex slaps Julian for his involvement and declares the four students as his own. Art and Julian are relieved that Alex doesn't take more severe action against them.

The students are shocked and overjoyed to learn their teacher's true identity and power. The incident demonstrates Alex's influence and the respect he commands from powerful figures like the Steadmans, potentially shifting the power dynamics in Baltimore.
