Alex encounters a difficult situation at the Autumn Beauty boutique where a customer, who turns out to be his ex-girlfriend Cathy, is causing trouble by demanding a refund for clothes she bought a month ago. Despite the staff's refusal, Cathy becomes increasingly aggressive, even setting fire to some clothes.

When Alex arrives, he recognizes Cathy and, to everyone's surprise, agrees to refund her the full amount of $20,000. However, Cathy, feeling humiliated, throws the money back at Alex and physically assaults him. Alex refuses to press charges and lets her go.

Later, Cathy regrets not taking the money but realizes she couldn't have swallowed her pride in front of Alex. She's shocked to learn that Alex is now wealthy and owns the boutique, lamenting her decision to break up with him.

At the university, Cathy sees a photo exhibition featuring a picture of her and Alex from their past. The photo reminds her of a conversation they had about Alex becoming rich and staying together. Overwhelmed with emotion, Cathy tears down the photo and runs away crying, realizing what she has lost.
