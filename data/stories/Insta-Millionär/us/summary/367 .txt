The episode continues the story of Alex Ambrose as he prepares to face Tyson Slade in a showdown at Hudson River Falls. Various characters, including Alex's supporters and admirers, gather near the falls, but access is restricted by martial arts-clad guards. Jack, a former martial artist, is among those controlling access.

Sharon, Kelly, Rose, and Lindsey are initially denied entry but are eventually allowed in by the old man (Tyson) sitting by the waterfall. The women, along with other spectators, wonder about the logistics and safety of the impending fight.

Meanwhile, at the hospital, Jessop anxiously guards the room where Alex and Debbie have been secluded for three days. When the door finally opens, everyone is amazed to find the room warm and bright, with Debbie fully recovered and even more beautiful than before.

The episode ends with Debbie asking Alex if he's ready for the fight, expressing concern about his exhausted state after healing her. The question remains whether Alex will have enough strength to defeat Tyson in the upcoming battle.
