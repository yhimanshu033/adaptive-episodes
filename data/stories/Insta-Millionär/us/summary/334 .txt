Four priests from the Black Orchid cult infiltrate the Clifton family banquet, seeking Debbie, whom they believe to be the "chosen one." They demonstrate their power by easily defeating a security guard, shocking the family. Rufus tries to handle the situation diplomatically while waiting for his father and Alex to return.

The cult leader, Zeke, explains they're not there to harm Debbie but to bring her to her supposed destiny. They identify Debbie based on her appearance and resemblance to Cynthia Clifton. A family member confirms to Rufus that these men are indeed from the Black Orchid cult.

Rufus, aware of the cult's diminished influence and the family's efforts to distance themselves from it, realizes the importance of keeping Debbie's blood relation to the Cliftons secret. However, it's too late as one of the priests approaches Debbie.

The priest tells Debbie that her mother is waiting for her in Canada, which confuses Debbie as she believes her mother died falling off a cliff. The priests then kneel before Debbie, proclaiming her as the chosen one and worshipping her, leaving the situation tense and uncertain.
