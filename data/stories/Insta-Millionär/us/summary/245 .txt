Rufus Clifton and Paul Novak travel to Washington D.C. to confront Charles Marvel. Paul, a skilled martial artist, is intrigued by the prospect of facing another expert fighter who allegedly defeated Cliff Munson.

Meanwhile, preparations for Alex and Debbie's secret wedding are underway at the Marvel family home. The ceremony is small, with only close family and friends in attendance, including the Moon maidens, Reginald, and David. Alex and Debbie are nervous but excited as they prepare to exchange vows.

As the wedding is about to begin, Rufus and Paul arrive at the Marvel residence with two henchmen. They are confronted by Shania, a guard, who refuses them entry as they don't have an appointment. Rufus, fueled by hatred for Charles Marvel, orders his men to attack. A fight breaks out, and Rufus' men quickly overpower ten of Marvel's guards, leaving only Shania standing. The situation escalates, threatening to disrupt Alex and Debbie's wedding and potentially leading to a violent confrontation between the Clifton and Marvel families.
