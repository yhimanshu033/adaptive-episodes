Alex challenges Jason to start the Ferrari, betting that if Alex can start it, Jason will leave on his hands and knees. Despite everyone's disbelief, Alex successfully starts the car using voice commands. When Jason refuses to honor the bet, Alex demonstrates more of the car's features, including opening doors and tying Jason to the car with ropes. He then drags Jason around the square, humiliating him.

After thoroughly defeating Jason, Alex and Lindsey exit the car. The onlookers, including Jane and her friends, now fear and respect Alex. They beg for forgiveness, which Alex grants at Lindsey's request.

Lindsey questions why Alex pretends to be poor when he's clearly wealthy. Before Alex can fully explain, Kelly arrives. Their interaction is brief but emotional, with Alex assuring Kelly that his current situation will be resolved soon. Kelly hugs Alex before leaving in the Ferrari with Sharon.

The episode ends with Sharon questioning Kelly about her true intentions for coming to D.C., but Kelly expresses contentment with just seeing Alex and wishes him happiness with Debbie.
