Alex Ambrose arrives at the Lambert mansion to help Keira Lambert, who is in a coma after a mysterious gas attack. He's greeted by Jesse Lambert and taken to a room that's been converted into a medical lab. There, a team of top doctors, led by Dr. Sophie Allbrook, are struggling to diagnose and treat Keira's condition. 

Holly Lambert, Keira's mother, enters seeking updates. The doctors inform her that Keira is getting weaker and they're running out of options. When Alex is introduced, Holly expresses relief and faith in his abilities.

However, Dr. Allbrook strongly objects to Alex's involvement. She questions his credentials, experience, and qualifications, pointing out that he seems too young to be more than a medical resident. She and her team are skeptical of Alex's presence and reluctant to let an unknown factor interfere with their work on such a high-profile case.

The situation becomes tense as the established medical team, seeing the opportunity to make their careers by saving a Lambert, challenges Alex's right to be there and his ability to help where they have failed.
