Alex, now secretly wealthy, continues to face misunderstandings and social challenges. At a restaurant, he's mistaken for a beggar by Zara and her friends, including Dean and Harry Hamilton. Rachael O'Connor, who knows Alex from the cheerleading squad, invites him to join their group for dinner, causing tension.

Howard Hamilton, Harry's brother, arrives and shows interest in Rachael. He tries to impress her by mentioning his new E-class Mercedes. The group, except for Rachael, treats Alex poorly, making him sit by the door and mocking his perceived poverty.

When the group goes to see Howard's new car, Alex quietly questions whether the parking attendant should have the keys. This leads to more ridicule from Howard and Harry, who assume Alex is ignorant about expensive parking services.

As Howard attempts to show off his car, he accidentally dents a G-class Mercedes in the parking lot, creating an embarrassing situation that contrasts with his earlier boasting. Throughout these events, Alex remains calm, knowing his true financial status far exceeds that of those mocking him.
