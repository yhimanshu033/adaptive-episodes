Alex reunites with his younger brother Justin on Harmony Island. Justin initially mistakes Alex for a worker but quickly recognizes him. The group of women who had been rude to Alex earlier apologize profusely upon realizing his true identity. Alex assigns them to do chores for the island workers as a form of punishment.

Alex then meets his grandfather, Lincoln Ambrose, the patriarch of the family. He finds Ken Stokes, the East Coast division manager, being punished for Alex's recent injuries. Lincoln decides to fire Ken to maintain order within the family, despite knowing it wasn't entirely Ken's fault.

As Alex arrives, he sees Ken leaving dejectedly. Realizing what happened, Alex immediately kneels before his grandfather and pleads for Ken's punishment to be revoked, insisting it wasn't Ken's fault. This impresses Lincoln, who notes that Alex's first action upon seeing him after seven years is to defend a subordinate.
