Alex and Debbie arrive at Johns Hopkins University, where Debbie has been secretly enrolled to continue her studies. They are instructed to keep Debbie's connection to the Clifton family a secret. Alex applies for a job as a campus security guard to watch over Debbie. The Moon girls plan to also enroll at the university to stay close to Alex.

At the university, Debbie registers at the Student Administration office, where a fabricated background story has been created for her. She is directed to meet her music teacher, Jordan Greenfield. 

Meanwhile, Alex goes to apply for the security guard position. He is greeted enthusiastically by a young man who leads him through the security department. Alex notices that most of the staff are older men who seem disinterested or asleep on the job. He is brought to the Security Chief's office to apply for the position.

The episode highlights the challenges Alex and Debbie face in maintaining their relationship while keeping Debbie's true identity a secret in their new environment at Johns Hopkins University.
