Alex demonstrates his medical skills and ethics at Woodside Clinic after his encounter with the abusive family. Upon returning to the clinic, he finds it filled with patients who heard about his earlier intervention. Alex efficiently diagnoses and treats numerous patients, impressing them with his quick and effective care. His methods range from acupuncture to prescribing medication, and many patients experience immediate relief. Sophie, the other doctor at the clinic, is amazed by Alex's speed and effectiveness in treating patients. The clinic's reputation grows rapidly, with patients promising to spread the word about Alex's exceptional care. This sudden influx of patients transforms the previously struggling clinic into a potentially busy and successful practice. Meanwhile, Sawyer informs Alex that the Murdoch family has reached a compromise, revoking the assassination order and offering compensation. Alex decides to temporarily leave the matter alone, though he still plans to retaliate in the future.
