In this episode, Dr. Vincent Cook strongly supports Alex's medical abilities, criticizing Dr. Sophie Allbrook for her skepticism and removing her from the medical team. Vincent expresses a strong desire to learn from Alex, but Alex politely declines to teach him, citing the difficulty of passing on his skills. Alex instructs Vincent to monitor Keira's recovery closely.

Alex receives a call and prepares to leave for a Cassidy Pike concert at the Grand Theater, which he plans to attend with his mother, Flora. This highlights the strained relationship with his family, who have forbidden contact with him.

Before Alex leaves, Holly Lambert insists on giving him a gift as thanks for saving Keira. Despite Alex's initial reluctance, Holly persists, stating she would be offended if he refused. The gift is implied to be something significant, possibly related to housing in Baltimore.
