Alex, initially reluctant to take on the dangerous mission of protecting a student, changes his mind after seeing a photo of the girl named Heidi. He agrees to work undercover as a physical education teacher at Oak Ridge High School.

Oak Ridge is a prestigious school with excellent facilities and students from wealthy families. However, Class 6, a group of final-year students, is notorious for their disruptive behavior and violence. The class includes both the best and worst students in the school, and their powerful parents prevent any meaningful disciplinary action.

The school has struggled to manage Class 6, with numerous teachers quitting or ending up in the hospital. The principal and staff are eagerly awaiting the class's graduation in three months.

Billy, the teaching director at Oak Ridge, is furious to learn that Alex has been appointed as the head teacher for Class 6. Billy had hoped to take this position himself to network with the wealthy parents of the students. He confronts the principal about the decision, but the principal explains that it wasn't his choice.

Alex's arrival at Oak Ridge as the new head teacher for Class 6 sets the stage for potential conflict with Billy and challenges in managing the troublesome students.
