Alex emerges victorious from the hole, overpowering Falcon in a fierce fight. He then takes on the other gang members, easily defeating them. Alex tracks down Blaze and easily defeats him, allowing the Moon disciples to exact their revenge.

Alex agrees to this plan. As he's about to announce the decision, Celeste stops him and publicly declares that Alex is now the leader of the Moon Palace, having inherited the position from Georgina two months ago.
