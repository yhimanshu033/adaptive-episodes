The episode continues the story at Oak Ridge High School, where Alex Ambrose is working undercover as a PE teacher. After humiliating the other teachers with a running challenge, Alex faces his first PE class with the troublesome Class 6.

Dylan, a popular and athletically gifted student, challenges Alex to a sports contest. Dylan is known as the school's best athlete and has won competitions at the city level. He arrogantly believes he can easily defeat Alex and proposes that if Alex loses, he must leave the school.

Alex calmly accepts the challenge, surprising the students who believe Dylan is unbeatable. Billy and Leonard, Alex's adversaries among the staff, eagerly anticipate Alex's humiliation.

Billy boasts about Dylan's impressive athletic achievements, including near-world-record performances in long jump and 800-meter running, as well as his silver medal from international championships. The stage is set for a showdown between the overconfident student athlete and the undercover teacher with hidden abilities.
