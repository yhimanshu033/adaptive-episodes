After the chaotic events at the Hill Hotel, Chelsea takes control of the situation and orders the arrest of the Murdochs. She arranges for Alex to stay in a room at the police station for his safety. Concerned about potential retaliation from the Murdochs, Chelsea secretly gives Alex a key to escape the station.

Alex uses the key to sneak out of the police station. Outside, he's met by a mysterious chauffeur who claims to have been sent by Chelsea. The driver informs Alex that Chelsea advises him to leave Baltimore immediately, suggesting he go to the Moon Palace or New York to lay low. Chelsea hopes Alex won't forget about her and wishes him a safe trip.

Despite his reluctance to run away, Alex gets into the car, realizing he's about to embark on another adventure. The episode ends with Alex asking the driver where they're headed.
