Alex Ambrose, facing trouble at his medical clinic in Baltimore due to FBI interference, accidentally calls his grandfather Lincoln Ambrose instead of Dr. Isaiah Allbrook. Lincoln coldly reminds Alex that he's been disowned and refuses to help, warning him not to use the Ambrose family name. As the FBI attempts to close the clinic and arrest Alex, Clark Fairfax unexpectedly arrives with support from the Baltimore Martial Arts Association and Rumble Club, forcing the FBI to leave. The clinic's reputation improves after the incident. Later, as Alex leaves with Kendall and Sophie, a truck crashes into the clinic, causing significant damage. Alex manages to push Kendall and Sophie out of harm's way, but the clinic is left in a terrible state with broken furniture and exposed pipes and wires.
