Alex confronts Grayson Turner, who was attempting to sexually assault singer Cassidy Pike in Alex's hotel room. Alex throws Grayson out the window, killing him. Cassidy is shocked but remains quiet at Alex's command. Alex allows her to stay in his room for the night due to the late hour and to avoid potential paparazzi. Despite her initial fear, Cassidy begins to feel drawn to Alex. They end up sharing the bed, though neither sleeps well. In the morning, Cassidy leaves her contact information before departing.

Later, Rufus informs Alex about an investigation into the events at Hell World. The Murdoch family, particularly a woman named Melanie, was involved in the incident. Melanie has since been sent abroad by her family for violating their rules, and her current whereabouts are unknown.
