Alex and Debbie attend a grand banquet hosted by the Clifton family to honor Alex's recent victory and welcome him into their family. Jessop Clifton, the family patriarch, shows great respect and admiration for Alex, even insisting that he sit at the head of the table. The event, called "The Hundred Cattle Banquet," features specially imported meat from Canada.

The Clifton family members, dressed in their finest attire, warmly welcome Alex and Debbie. There's a mix of admiration and fear in their eyes as they greet the couple. Jessop expresses his gratitude to Alex for saving the family and treats Debbie, his granddaughter, with great affection.

Chelsea Wood, also present at the banquet, feels a sense of loneliness as she observes Alex and Debbie's happiness. The celebration is mostly limited to family members, with few outsiders invited.

Throughout the evening, Alex receives numerous handshakes and words of appreciation from the Clifton family members. Jessop sees this as preparation for Alex's future role in leading the family.

The banquet is filled with toasts, laughter, and a relaxed atmosphere. As the event comes to a close, Jessop asks Alex to follow him, saying he has something to show him in private.
