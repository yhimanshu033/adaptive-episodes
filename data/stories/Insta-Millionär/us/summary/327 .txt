After defeating Lee Harshaw, Alex pulls his body from the water, confirming his death. The martial artists gathered at Silver Lake have mixed reactions, with some expressing relief and others mourning their fallen comrades. Many congratulate Alex for saving the martial arts community.

Debbie reunites with Alex, expressing her fear and relief. Cathy, Alex's ex-girlfriend, watches them with envy, which is noticed by her fellow Iron Fist sect members. Melissa, hidden in the crowd, is relieved to learn that Alex and Debbie are alive.

Chris Steadman and Julian Duvant, shocked by the turn of events, sneak away from the scene. Chris informs his father, Art Steadman, about Lee's defeat, causing Art to react with disbelief.

The martial arts community begins to disperse, with various sects inviting Alex to visit them in the future. Alex reveals some information about his background and the Moon Palace but doesn't mention his connection to the Ambrose family.

As the Iron Fist sect prepares to leave, Debbie recognizes Cathy, creating an awkward moment. Cathy denies knowing Alex, and he chooses not to contradict her. Belinda, the leader of Iron Fist, dismisses the matter and introduces her disciples to Alex.
