Alex encounters Vivian Carter, the beautiful owner of The Angel bar, in an embarrassing situation when he accidentally walks in on her while she's undressing. To prevent him from spreading the story, Vivian offers Alex a job as a waiter at the bar, which he accepts as it gives him an opportunity to learn more about the business he's interested in buying.

Later, Alex joins his friends in the dorm, where they are watching Cathy's live broadcast. Cathy is desperately trying to earn money to pay off Billy. Alex's friend Ben makes a dangerous request for Cathy to jump on a table, which results in her falling. Alex is disappointed by his friend's behavior.

The broadcast then introduces a new segment called "The Duel," where two broadcasters compete for a prize of $100,000. Cathy sees this as an opportunity to earn a significant portion of the money she owes. Her opponent is a popular broadcaster named Minnie, who has a much larger audience and is confident about winning the competition.
