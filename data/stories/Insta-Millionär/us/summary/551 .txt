Alex and Maryann arrive at Robinson Winery to find a group of angry protesters demanding answers about allegedly tainted wine that has caused health issues. Alex manages to calm the crowd temporarily with his authoritative presence. Inside the building, Alex and Maryann taste the wine but find nothing unusual. 

The protesters become agitated again, threatening to break windows and demanding immediate compensation. As the situation escalates, Alex protects Maryann from the mob. A winery employee pours cold water on the protesters from a second-story window, shocking them.

Maryann urges Alex to go inside with her, while the winery employees are unsure how to handle the situation. Alex realizes that resolving Maryann's problems will take more time, and he remains anxious to see Debbie.
