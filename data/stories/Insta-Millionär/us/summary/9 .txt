Alex, a secretly wealthy student, is called to the sports field by Zara Fitzgerald, the cheerleading captain, to assist the squad. He buys water for the team and fans Zara, reinforcing his perceived low status. While watching the cheerleaders practice, Alex meets Rachael O'Connor, a beautiful and kind girl who shows interest in talking to him.

Dean Marshall, a basketball player, interrupts their conversation, boasting about his wealth and status while belittling Alex. Rachael politely rebuffs Dean's advances, choosing to continue talking with Alex instead.

Zara returns and expresses surprise at Rachael's interest in Alex, warning him not to pursue Rachael and emphasizing his perceived low social standing. The conversation is interrupted by a phone call from Zara's cousin, who is facing pressure to get married.

Zara discusses her cousin's situation with Rachael, implying that marrying someone of low social status (like Alex) is unacceptable. This conversation further highlights the contrast between Alex's hidden wealth and his public persona, as well as the social dynamics and prejudices within their circle.
