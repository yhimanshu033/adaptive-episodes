Alex Ambrose, now the head of Birchwood Real Estate, continues to demonstrate his supernatural abilities by transforming the Birchwood House grounds into a spring paradise despite it being winter. This impresses visitors, including Celia Turner and Ethan Cox, who express interest in purchasing villas on the property. Alex agrees to reserve villas for them, pricing them at half a million dollars each.

Later, Clark visits with his daughter Sarah, concerned about her health. Alex determines it's just a common cold, but Clark is amazed by the unseasonable environment of Birchwood House.

Sawyer Landon is a core member of the Rumble Club who rescued Maryann from kidnappers when she was a child. Twenty years ago, while protecting the wife of the Rumble Club's master, Sawyer was severely injured by a poisoned arrow, leaving him paralyzed and wheelchair-bound. He returned to Baltimore to recuperate but has since struggled with depression and alcoholism.

Despite no longer being active in the Rumble Club, Sawyer, as one of the four great masters, is still targeted by enemies. There have been five attempts on his life this year alone. Maryann visits him often and is determined to protect him, feeling indebted for his past rescue of her. She is deeply worried about his current condition and hopes Alex can help save him.
