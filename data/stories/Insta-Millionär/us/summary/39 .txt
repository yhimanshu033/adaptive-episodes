Alex finds himself in a difficult situation as he cannot prove his wealth due to his frozen account. Debbie unexpectedly confesses to stealing the money to protect Alex, but he refuses to let her take the blame. Alex then falsely admits to the theft, claiming he used Debbie as part of his plan. This confession hurts Debbie, who slaps Alex and runs away in tears.

The university officials decide not to involve the police to protect the institution's reputation. They order Alex to repay the stolen money within five years. Alex returns to his dorm, exhausted and worried about Debbie.

The next day, Zara calls Alex to remind him about meeting her cousin Karen for lunch. Despite his reluctance, Alex agrees to go. Meanwhile, Zara and Karen discuss Alex, with Zara recounting the incident at Shrimp King where Alex appeared to be asking for money. Karen, who knows about Alex's wealth, pretends to agree with Zara's perception of Alex as a "loser." Zara mentions Alex arriving in a Mercedes-Benz but dismisses it as borrowed, unaware of his true financial status.
