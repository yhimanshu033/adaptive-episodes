Alex Ambrose returns to Oak Ridge High School to find chaos, with students burning furniture and threatening to burn the library. Upon his arrival, the situation immediately calms down. Class 6, the main instigators, quickly obey Alex's orders to extinguish the fire and line up. The other students and teachers are relieved and impressed by Alex's ability to restore order so quickly.

Billy Wilde, who had replaced Alex as the class teacher, is jealous of Alex's influence over the students. He accuses Alex of orchestrating the chaos to make himself look good. The students defend Alex, denying any involvement on his part.

Billy, attempting to assert his authority, reminds Alex that as teaching director, he has control over Alex's employment, performance reviews, and salary. He also implies that Alex must have connections to have gotten the job at the school. Alex responds to Billy's threats by laughing, which further exposes Billy's incompetence and poor character to everyone present.

The episode highlights the stark contrast between Alex's effective leadership and Billy's ineffectual attempts at control, as well as the students' and staff's clear preference for Alex's presence at the school.
