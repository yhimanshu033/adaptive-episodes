In this episode, Brian and Timothy are at the hospital where Brian's daughter Haley is in critical condition after a severe horse-riding accident. They meet with Dr. Susan Ware, the hospital administrator, who informs them that Haley's condition is very serious, with internal injuries, fractures, and a severe concussion. The most pressing concern is a broken rib that has punctured her lung and is dangerously close to her main pulmonary artery.

Brian becomes emotional and angry, demanding that the hospital save his daughter and threatening to pull his funding if anything happens to her. Timothy tries to calm his brother and suggests they wait in the lounge for updates.

While waiting, Timothy proposes that they allow Alex, who is currently in custody, to examine Haley. He mentions Alex's reputation as a skilled medical practitioner and martial arts master. However, Brian adamantly refuses, stating that he doesn't trust Alex and will only rely on the hospital's doctors or other experts if necessary.

The episode highlights the tension between Brian's desperation to save his daughter and his refusal to consider Alex's help, despite Timothy's attempts to persuade him.
