Alex, now the leader of the Moon society, spends the night in a luxurious villa with the four Moon Maidens: Celeste, Selene, Callisto, and Luna. He learns about the society's purpose of helping women in need and its substantial financial resources. The Maidens insist on serving Alex, including offering to massage him, which he reluctantly accepts.

The next day, Alex attends his classes and tries to find Lindsey to explain the incident at the Tinsdale Hotel, unaware of the changes in her wedding plans. He receives a call from Phillipa inviting him to the Olympic Games closing ceremony.

Alex takes the Moon Maidens to the ceremony, where they meet Phillipa. They encounter Myriam, who fearfully avoids Alex due to their previous interaction. After the ceremony, Phillipa stays behind to take pictures with other volunteers.

The episode ends on a tense note as three Spanish-speaking strangers observe Phillipa with malicious intent, planning to target her before leaving America the next day.
