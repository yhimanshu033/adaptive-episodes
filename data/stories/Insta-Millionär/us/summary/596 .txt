The episode begins with Louisa and Riley discussing their plans regarding Alex's case. Riley decides to hold off on involving Brian, Timothy's brother, in their scheme.

The next morning, despite Alex's arrest, Kendall opens the Woodside Clinic as instructed. However, a large group of protesters, organized by Louisa, gather outside the clinic demanding justice for Michael Murdoch and calling for the clinic's closure. The protesters, some wearing bandages and holding banners, accuse Alex of being a murderer.

Kendall initially tries to threaten the protesters with a knife but is stopped by Sophie. The clinic staff forms a human chain to protect the building. As the situation escalates, patients arriving for treatment are blocked by the protesters. Many of these patients, aware of Michael's previous actions against the clinic, decide to defend the establishment.

The confrontation between the protesters and the clinic's supporters intensifies, with both sides pushing and shouting at each other. The event attracts media attention, with reporters and photographers documenting the chaos.

As more people join both sides, the crowd grows to hundreds. Louisa, observing the scene, hopes the escalating situation will force the clinic to close. The episode culminates in violence as protesters begin throwing stones at the clinic, and despite the arrival of police officers, the situation remains out of control.
