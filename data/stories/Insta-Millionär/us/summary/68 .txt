The episode reveals that the Azalea Guest House, where Lisa, her sisters, and Carla are staying, is actually one of the world's top hotels. They discover this after witnessing the arrival of a foreign minister and learning about the high-profile guests the hotel has hosted. The food served at the hotel is even better quality than at the expensive Sunset restaurant they had previously visited.

Lisa and her sisters regret not taking advantage of the hotel's amenities during their stay. They attempt to extend their stay by asking Carla to convince Alex to book them more nights. Carla agrees and calls Alex to arrange it.

The sisters begin to reassess their opinion of Alex, realizing he must be more influential and wealthy than they initially thought to be able to book rooms at such an exclusive hotel. They speculate about his background and status, concluding that he is likely from a powerful family.

Lisa starts to scheme about potentially setting up her daughter Donna with Alex, seeing him as a better prospect than Darren. The sisters agree that Alex's calm and collected demeanor, despite his apparent wealth, indicates that he is likely to be very successful in the future.
