Alex Ambrose, the newly appointed president of the Baltimore Martial Arts Association, accepts a challenge from Jacob, the leader of the Ghosts, to a duel. Despite concerns from his associates about Jacob's experience and reputation, Alex confidently decides to fight. He sees this as an opportunity to defend the honor of his organization and prove his abilities.

Many of the younger martial artists are in awe of Alex's confidence and offer their support, while some older members express caution. Clark and Oscar strongly back Alex's decision, with Clark seeing a younger version of himself in Alex's determination.

The martial arts community is abuzz with excitement and concern about the upcoming battle. Some doubt Alex's abilities, believing he might be exaggerating his skills, while others worry about his youth and overconfidence against the seasoned Jacob.

Jacob's reputation is formidable, having defeated Tyson Slade of the Blood Brothers gang years ago with his exceptional swordsmanship. This history adds to the tension surrounding the challenge.

Alex, however, remains unworried. He has been honing his skills and inner power through moonlight training for months. He believes in his martial arts abilities and is determined to win not just for himself, but for the reputation of the entire Baltimore Martial Arts Association.
