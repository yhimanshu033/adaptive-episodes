Alex investigates the scam involving broken figurines and confronts Heather Lennox, who reveals they work for Desmond Lang. The group targets unsuspecting people, including Debbie, to extort money. Heather explains their methods and mentions Caroline Hall's involvement with Serenity Gifts, though she's unaware of the scam. Alex records Heather's confession and reports the situation to Ronald Cartwright, who informs the police.

Later, Alex attends a reception for the Baltimore Martial Arts Association at the Regal Hotel. Many prominent families are present, including the Halls. Desmond, Caroline, and Joe are surprised and displeased to see Alex there, questioning how he obtained a ticket. Caroline expresses her contempt for Alex, believing he doesn't belong in their social circle.

Joe approaches Alex, but Caroline quickly intervenes, rudely telling Alex to leave and scolding Joe for associating with him. She emphasizes the importance of maintaining their social status and instructs Joe to stay away from Alex to avoid damaging their reputation.
