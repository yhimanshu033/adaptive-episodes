Alex visits a luxury real estate office in Washington Heights to buy a house for Debbie. Initially, he is treated poorly by the staff and other customers due to his modest appearance. When he expresses interest in buying a villa, he is met with skepticism and ridicule.

A wealthy couple is also interested in buying a villa but struggles with the down payment. Alex surprises everyone by easily purchasing a $1.5 million villa using his credit card. This sudden revelation of his wealth shocks the staff and other customers, causing them to completely change their attitude towards him.

Not satisfied with just one villa, Alex decides to purchase the most expensive property in the complex, the "Emerald Heights" villa, priced at $40 million. He makes a phone call to transfer the funds, leaving everyone in disbelief.

The episode highlights the stark contrast between Alex's appearance and his immense wealth, as well as the superficial nature of people's attitudes based on perceived financial status. It also showcases Alex's casual approach to his newfound wealth and his discomfort with the sudden attention from the female staff members, leading him to quickly leave the office.
