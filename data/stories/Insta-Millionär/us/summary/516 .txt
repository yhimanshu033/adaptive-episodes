Alex arrives early at Canfield Arena for his match against Jacob. While waiting in the locker room, Kenneth Petrie and his followers burst in, threatening Alex and bringing a coffin as an intimidation tactic. Hugo Sherridan, the vice president of the American Martial Arts Association, intervenes and demands Alex apologize to Kenneth. Alex refuses, leading to a tense confrontation with Hugo.

As the situation escalates, Jacob and Mr. Paxman arrive for the match. Kenneth, relieved by the interruption, suggests letting Jacob teach Alex a lesson during the fight. He later explains his plan to Hugo, who agrees.

The episode sets the stage for the upcoming match between Alex and Jacob, with tensions high and multiple parties looking to see Alex defeated or taught a lesson. Alex remains defiant despite the pressure from Kenneth, Hugo, and others in positions of authority within the martial arts associations.
