Alex spends a few days training with Zora at the West Lake guesthouse. Suddenly, Georgina appears with her Moon Maidens (Celeste, Selene, Callisto, and Luna). Georgina brings Nelly, a former maid of Zora's, as a hostage. A fierce battle ensues between Zora and Georgina, while Alex fights off the Moon Maidens to rescue Nelly. The Sultan of Brunei arrives with bodyguards, distracting Zora and allowing Georgina to strike her. Alex confronts Georgina about her cruelty, mentioning Xavier as the source of their feud. Georgina angrily denies stealing Xavier from Zora, claiming it was the other way around. The situation remains tense with both Zora and Georgina injured and the true nature of their conflict over Xavier still unclear.
