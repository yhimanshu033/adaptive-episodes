Alex Ambrose is accused of murdering Michael Murdoch by throwing him out of a window at the Nirvana nightclub. Kendall and Sophie defend Alex, believing he wouldn't commit such a public murder. David Drake insists on Alex's guilt, while Lindsey Marvel, an eyewitness, is conflicted.

Riley Naysmith arrives at the police station and offers to help Alex by manipulating witnesses and evidence. He suggests that Michael's death could be ruled an accident, potentially reducing Alex's charges to negligent manslaughter. Riley's unexpected intervention surprises everyone, especially given his previous connection to Michael.

Lindsey is grateful for Riley's offer of assistance, though the group realizes that even a short prison sentence could be fatal for Alex due to the Murdoch family's influence. Kendall, who has recently reconciled with Alex, also pleads for Riley's help. The situation remains tense as they consider their options to protect Alex from the serious accusations against him.
