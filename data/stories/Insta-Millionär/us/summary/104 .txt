The episode focuses on the tense family gathering at Kelly's family home, where Alex has been introduced as Kelly's boyfriend. The situation quickly becomes hostile as Kelly's family, especially her grandmother Margaret, disapproves of Alex due to his apparent poverty and shabby appearance.

Simon, Kelly's cousin, arrives with his girlfriend Cathy, who is Alex's ex. Simon reveals embarrassing details about Alex's poor lifestyle to the family, further damaging Alex's reputation. The family is shocked by Alex's poverty and questions Kelly's judgment.

When Simon introduces Cathy, the family initially approves of her beauty. However, Margaret cleverly points out that if Cathy was with Alex before, she must also be poor. This leads to the family questioning Cathy's motives for dating Simon.

Realizing the implications, Simon quickly abandons Cathy to maintain his family's approval. He cruelly dismisses her, offering her money as compensation before kicking her out.

The focus then returns to Kelly and Alex. Margaret demands that Kelly admit her mistake in bringing Alex, but Kelly refuses to give up on him. This angers Margaret, who threatens to disown Kelly if she leaves with Alex.

Throughout the episode, the stark contrast between the wealthy Phillips family and Alex's perceived poverty creates intense conflict and drama, testing Kelly's loyalty to both her family and Alex.
