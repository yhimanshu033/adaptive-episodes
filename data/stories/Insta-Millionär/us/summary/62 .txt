Alex, Debbie, Donna, and Darren meet Donna's mother Lisa, her two sisters, and Debbie's mother Carla at the airport. Lisa and her sisters are wealthy and look down on Carla and Debbie due to their lower social status. Donna introduces Darren as her boyfriend, impressing Lisa with his apparent wealth. She then introduces Alex as Debbie's boyfriend, describing him as poor like Debbie.

Lisa criticizes Debbie and Carla, advising them to be realistic about their social standing. Alex wants to defend them but Debbie stops him. Lisa and her sisters scold Alex for his perceived disrespect.

Darren suggests going to a restaurant and offers to treat everyone. He tells Alex, Debbie, and Carla to take a taxi, claiming his car is full. Alex realizes the restaurant is the Sunset, where Debbie used to work. Despite feeling uncomfortable with the situation, Alex decides to go along since he has booked accommodations for them at the Azalea Guest House.
