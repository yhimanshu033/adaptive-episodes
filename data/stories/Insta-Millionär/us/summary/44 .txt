In this episode, the situation at the restaurant escalates when Bruno, a local gangster, assaults the manager for trying to protect Debbie. Alex intervenes, confronting Bruno by throwing wine in his face and hitting him with a bottle. This unexpected action shocks Bruno and his men.

Alex reveals his name and claims to be just a student, which initially makes Bruno think he can overpower him. Bruno threatens to call Sam Woodsworth, a powerful local figure, for backup. However, when Bruno makes the call and mentions Alex's name, Sam's reaction reveals that Alex is actually someone important.

Alex takes the phone and suggests that Bruno should follow his own earlier demands: groveling, begging for forgiveness, and cleaning the floor with his tongue. After speaking with Sam, Bruno realizes his grave mistake and desperately apologizes to Alex, begging for mercy.

This turn of events demonstrates Alex's true power and influence, despite his earlier claims of being a simple student. It also shows how quickly the dynamics can shift when Alex's real identity is revealed.
