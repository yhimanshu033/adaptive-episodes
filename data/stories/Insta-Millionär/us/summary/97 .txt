Alex and his group arrive at the Azalea Guest House in New York, a courtyard-style hotel. Lisa, Natalie, and Fiona are unimpressed with the accommodation but stay due to exhaustion. The next day, they refuse the free breakfast offered by the hotel, believing it to be low quality. Over the next week, they spend money on male escorts and expensive meals outside the hotel.

One day, Lisa encounters Joanne Ludlow, the wife of a hospital CEO with whom Lisa's family is trying to negotiate a business deal. Seeing an opportunity, Lisa invites Joanne to lunch at the upscale Sunset restaurant, hoping to impress her. Despite Joanne's hesitation due to the restaurant's expensive reputation, Lisa insists on treating her.

At the restaurant, they find a long wait for tables. Lisa confidently approaches the hostess, believing she can get a table by mentioning Darren's name, as they had done on a previous visit. Joanne is skeptical and embarrassed, but Fiona and Natalie assure her that Lisa can get them a table due to her daughter's connection to Darren, whom they describe as an important and influential person.
