Alex is confronted by Mr. Berkley and Cathy about the stolen money, with Cathy accusing him of theft. They take Alex to the hospital to confront Mr. Morgan. At the hospital, Alex finds Debbie being mistreated by other students. He tries to defend her and himself, but the situation escalates.

Pressured to explain his recent spending, Alex decides to reveal his true wealth. However, when he attempts to prove it using his bank card, the account shows as temporarily unavailable. This leads to more mockery and disbelief from those present.

Alex calls his bank manager and learns that his family has restricted his access to the account due to concerns about his recent spending habits. This leaves Alex unable to prove his claims about his wealth, further complicating the situation and leaving him vulnerable to the accusations against him.
