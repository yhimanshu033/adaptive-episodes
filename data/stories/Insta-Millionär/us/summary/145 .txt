In this episode, Sue confesses to her family that she and Alex are not actually a couple, causing shock and disappointment. Alex cleans up the villa after Sue's family leaves. Grandma returns and gives Alex a chicken sandwich. She then takes interest in a bracelet Alex had bought, revealing it has magical properties. Grandma ties the bracelet to Alex's wrist and a thread to her own finger, explaining that she can now contact Alex but not vice versa. She then begins to teach Alex some defensive moves, starting with throwing blades. Alex struggles at first but eventually manages to hit the center of a chopping board. Throughout the episode, Grandma's supernatural abilities and knowledge are further emphasized, deepening the mystery surrounding her character.
