Alex confronts Ferdinand Thornton at Birchwood House, demonstrating his power and influence. Ferdinand backs down, realizing Alex's strength and connections. The incident spreads quickly through Baltimore, increasing Birchwood House's fame.

Alex then focuses on improving the Woodside Clinic by rebranding it as a wellness suite, bringing in staff from the Moon Maidens, and sourcing high-quality medicines from Oscar Steadman's family. Celeste, one of the Moon Maidens, stays to help for a few days.

The next day, Alex unveils the new Wellness Suite to much applause. Many patients arrive, drawn by stories of Alex's healing abilities and reduced medicine prices.

However, Michael Murdoch arrives with a group of people, challenging Alex's authority in Baltimore. Alex dismisses Michael's threats, leading to a heated exchange. Michael then reveals his plan to undermine Alex by bringing in a team of medical professionals, including Dr. Ann Glover and other doctors who previously left the clinic. Michael presents them as kind-hearted professionals fighting against Alex's management, setting the stage for a conflict over the clinic's future.
