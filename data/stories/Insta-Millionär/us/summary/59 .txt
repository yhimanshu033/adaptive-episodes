In this episode, Cathy unexpectedly wins the live streaming competition against Minnie, thanks to a last-minute donation from Adam Childs (who is actually Alex). Cathy attempts to flirt with Adam via messages, unaware that it's Alex. She lies about her character and past relationship with Alex, causing him to become angry and end the conversation.

The next day, Cathy confronts Alex on campus, gloating about her newfound connection with "Adam Childs" and threatening to have Alex arrested for impersonating Adam at the banquet. She believes she has the upper hand and taunts Alex, unaware that he is actually Adam.

After an uncomfortable class, Alex receives a call from Russell, the manager of Autumn Beauty boutique, about a disruptive customer. Alex agrees to come and handle the situation.

The episode highlights the growing complexity of Alex's dual identity and Cathy's manipulative nature, setting the stage for potential future conflicts.
