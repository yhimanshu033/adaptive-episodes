Alex attends a meeting of former Texans at the Ruby Hotel, where he is mocked and ridiculed for his appearance and job as a food truck worker. Myriam further humiliates him by recounting his encounter with Miss Marvel and Darryl Brennan. When Alex tries to leave, Lester Yates, the organizer, convinces him to stay.

Meanwhile, Alex's mother Flora is in another room with Ken Stokes, discussing Alex's situation. She asks Frank Dreck, the hotel owner, to check on Alex.

Back in the meeting room, Lester continues to humiliate Alex by sharing a story about him asking an old lady for money after helping her. The group becomes even more hostile towards Alex. When Alex tries to leave again, Lester demands he pay $100 for his meal.

Frank Dreck enters the room and observes the situation. Lester explains the circumstances to Dreck, who then leaves to report back to Flora. The episode ends with Alex still trapped in the humiliating situation, unaware that his mother is in the same hotel.
