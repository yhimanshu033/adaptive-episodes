Alex Ambrose attends a celebration at the Barside Club where Maryann Robinson, impressed by his abilities, makes a romantic advance. Alex gently rejects her, maintaining his professional boundaries. Their conversation is interrupted by Maggie, Maryann's COO, who brings news of a crisis at Robinson Winery.

Elaine and Derek, recently fired executives, have taken several team members with them and are spreading rumors about the company's impending shutdown. More critically, Elaine has leaked the formula for Revive, the winery's flagship wellness wine, online. This leak threatens to destroy the company's competitive edge and has already led to order cancellations.

Maryann and Maggie discuss potential solutions, including developing an enhanced version of Revive. However, Alex surprises them by suggesting they abandon Revive altogether. Instead, he proposes creating a wine based on "Idun's Apple," an ancient recipe said to be far superior to any existing wellness wine.

Maggie explains that Idun's Apple is a mythical wine from Norse mythology, rumored to have briefly dominated the market decades ago before disappearing. She expresses skepticism about its existence and the possibility of recreating it. However, Alex confidently claims he knows how to make it and will do so, leaving Maryann and Maggie stunned by his assertion.
