Alex encounters Caroline Hall at a store where she pays for a supposedly broken expensive unicorn figurine. Caroline berates Alex and Debbie for their perceived lack of manners and financial status. She invites them to brunch at Alcott the next day.

Alex attends the brunch alone, finding a cheerful gathering despite it being the day of Neil Hall's funeral. Caroline is in high spirits, sitting next to the man from the photo Alex had seen earlier. Joe is present but appears depressed and withdrawn.

Alex is surprised by the celebratory atmosphere and questions Caroline about the funeral timing. She explains that the funeral was held earlier, with cremation planned for the next day. This contradicts Joe's social media announcement, leaving many unable to pay their respects. Alex questions the rush, sensing something unusual about the situation.

The episode highlights the stark contrast between Caroline's behavior and the expected mourning for her recently deceased husband, as well as the apparent disconnect between Joe and his mother's actions. It also raises questions about Caroline's relationship with the unknown man and her true motives for inviting Alex to the brunch.
