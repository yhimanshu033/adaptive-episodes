Alex decides to donate the money Flora gave him to the university to sponsor the New Year's party and cover his expenses. He goes to the finance department with a garbage bag full of euros. There, he encounters Colin and Ian, who are discussing sponsorship with the finance director, Mr. Parker. Initially mistaken for a poor student seeking financial aid, Alex waits until Colin and Ian leave before revealing his true intentions.

Mr. Parker is skeptical at first but is shocked when he sees the amount of money Alex has brought. Alex explains he wants to sponsor the party and use the remaining funds for other school expenses. He requests that his expenses be covered and that his donation remain anonymous.

Mr. Parker is impressed by Alex's generosity, especially in comparison to Colin's family's hesitation to sponsor the event. He agrees to Alex's terms and warns his staff not to be rude to Alex in the future.

Later, Colin's father calls to offer sponsorship, but Mr. Parker informs him that the party has already been sponsored. Mr. Patterson quickly withdraws his offer. The finance director reflects on the stark contrast between Alex's generosity and the Pattersons' reluctance to donate.
