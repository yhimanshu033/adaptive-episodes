The situation takes an ironic turn when Will, Alex's wealthy friend, arrives in a luxury car, surprising the boss.

Alex then visits the Baltimore Martial Arts Association with Will, where he meets with Edward and Jeffrey. They discuss the ongoing issue with Kenneth, a stubborn member of the arbitrator's office who refuses to cooperate with their reorganization efforts. Kenneth's connections within the office make it difficult to pressure him into compliance.

The group considers various approaches to deal with Kenneth, including having Alex intimidate him. Alex suggests investigating Kenneth's past transgressions to gain leverage and potentially encourage others in the department to follow the rules. He emphasizes the need to eliminate corruption and restructure the department.

Finally, Alex confronts Kenneth in the basement where he's being held. Kenneth, looking pale, tells Alex to skip the pleasantries and get straight to his questions, setting the stage for a tense interrogation.
