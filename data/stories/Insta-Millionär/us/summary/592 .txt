Alex Ambrose is arrested and interrogated for the alleged murder of Michael Murdoch, who fell to his death from a window at Lindsey Marvel's nightclub. Alex maintains his innocence, claiming Michael committed suicide to frame him. He believes the Murdoch family has been plotting against him since he took over the Woodside Clinic.

Lindsey Marvel, torn between loyalty to Alex and confusion over Michael's apparent suicide, agrees to let Riley Naysmith help manipulate witness statements to portray the incident as an accident.

During the interrogation, Detective Tamara Knox, a former classmate of Lindsey's, pressures Alex to confess. She cites multiple witness testimonies claiming Alex pushed Michael out of the window. Alex remains calm and insists on his innocence, frustrating Tamara.

Alex requests to make a phone call, which Tamara reluctantly allows, curious to see who he thinks can help him in this situation. The interrogation reveals the tension between Alex's version of events and the evidence against him, as well as the complex relationships and power dynamics at play in Baltimore's business and law enforcement communities.
