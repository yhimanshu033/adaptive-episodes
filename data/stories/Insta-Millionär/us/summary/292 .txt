Alex Ambrose intervenes in a critical meeting where Colin Patterson, backed by Nathan, is attempting to buy the struggling Fidelity Group from Reginald Drake. Alex threatens to expose Nathan's involvement to the "owner," causing Nathan to call off the deal. Colin reluctantly withdraws his offer, disappointing those who had sided against the Drakes.

Nathan denies responsibility for the Fidelity Group's troubles and refuses to help save the company. Alex questions Nathan's loyalty to the Ambrose family, but Nathan dismisses him as an outsider.

As the situation seems hopeless for the Drakes, Alex confidently claims he can restore the company to normal operations within an hour. Reginald reveals they need about a billion dollars to reverse the crisis, but can only raise half that amount.

In a surprising turn, Alex makes a phone call to someone he addresses as "Mr. Clifton," asking for help with the situation. This leaves everyone in the room stunned, as it appears Alex might have access to the necessary funds to save the Fidelity Group.
