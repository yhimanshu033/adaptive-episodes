Alex Ambrose, believed to be dead, becomes the center of attention for the Steadman family and their allies. Chris Steadman informs his father, Art, about Alex's presumed death in the Appalachian Mountains due to black poison water. Chris also reveals his suspicion that Alex is the disowned son of the wealthy Ambrose family.

Art Steadman, seeing an opportunity to regain his lost power, contacts Tyson Slade, the leader of the Blood Brothers Gang. Art shares the news of Alex's supposed death, explaining that Alex and his female students haven't been seen for weeks after going to the Appalachian Mountains. Tyson is initially skeptical but ultimately relieved, as Alex's presence was a significant obstacle to their plans in Baltimore.

Lee Harshaw, a member of the Blood Brothers Gang seeking revenge for Damon Walker's death, is informed of Alex's presumed demise. While initially frustrated that he couldn't personally avenge Damon, Lee eventually expresses joy at the news.

The Steadmans and the Blood Brothers Gang view Alex's apparent death as an opportunity to regain their influence in Baltimore and potentially challenge the Clifton family's power. They begin to plan their next moves, believing that Alex's absence will make their goals easier to achieve.
