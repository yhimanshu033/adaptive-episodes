The Ambrose family meets on Fairview Island to discuss their crisis. Nelly suggests bringing Alex back to help, but Lincoln is conflicted due to his principles and Alex's past disobedience. Nathan strongly opposes the idea. The meeting ends without a decision.

Meanwhile, Alex, now the principal of Oak Ridge High School, discovers the Ambrose Company's troubles in the news. He realizes the Mitchells are behind it and suspects Wesley Boone's involvement. Alex considers helping his family despite their past treatment of him.

Lincoln calls a second meeting after giving Gideon some instructions. Gideon reports progress in paying off debts, loans, and restoring business relationships. The family's mood improves. Gideon then reveals that Alex is on his way to Fairview Island, adding a new dynamic to the family's situation.
