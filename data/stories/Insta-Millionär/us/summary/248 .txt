Alex and Debbie attend their wedding ceremony, which is interrupted by Rufus Clifton, Debbie's uncle. Rufus tries to convince Debbie to return to the Clifton family, revealing that her grandfather misses her. Debbie, torn between anger over her mother's death and curiosity about her family, agrees to go with Rufus to Baltimore. Alex decides to accompany her, along with the Moon girls.

Rufus objects to Alex and Debbie's marriage, insisting that Clifton family members need family consent to marry. The wedding is postponed, and plans are made to visit Jessop Clifton, Debbie's grandfather, in Baltimore.

After the guests leave, Alex and Debbie share a private moment, exchanging rings and kisses, affirming their commitment to each other despite the lack of an official ceremony. They playfully refer to each other as husband and wife.

The next day, Rufus picks up Alex, Debbie, and the Moon girls. They fly to Baltimore and drive to the Clifton family mansion. The group arrives at Clifton Manor, ready to face whatever challenges await them with Debbie's estranged family.
