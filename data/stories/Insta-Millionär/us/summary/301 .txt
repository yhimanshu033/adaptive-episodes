Alex visits his injured colleague Don at the hospital, where he unexpectedly encounters Melissa. Melissa has been following Alex and manipulating the situation to gain his trust. She pretends to be there out of concern for Don, impressing both Don and Alex with her apparent kindness.

After the hospital visit, Alex and Melissa share a friendly taxi ride back to the university, with Alex warming up to her. Melissa then meets Chris at the Hilton Hotel for a romantic dinner, where she reports her progress in gaining Alex's trust.

Chris reveals the next step of their plan: he gives Melissa a bottle of lethal poison and instructs her to use it on Alex. Melissa is initially shocked and reluctant to commit murder. However, Chris manipulates her emotions, claiming his father won't allow them to marry unless Alex is eliminated.

Pressured by her desire to marry Chris and swayed by his promises of protection from legal consequences, Melissa reluctantly agrees to poison Alex. Chris assures her that the poison is diluted and will take days to kill Alex, making it difficult to trace back to her.

The episode ends with Melissa accepting the task, despite her moral reservations, and seeking reassurance from Chris that she won't suffer any consequences for her actions.
