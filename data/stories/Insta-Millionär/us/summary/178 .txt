Alex's mother Flora visits him in Washington D.C. and arranges for him to transfer to Richmond University. Before leaving, she secretly gives Alex a large sum of euros hidden in garbage bags, circumventing the family's restrictions on helping him. Alex decides to store the money under his bed for now.

Alex prepares to start at Richmond University and informs Nelly that he will be leaving the food truck job in three days. When he tells her, Nelly becomes unexpectedly emotional. She reveals a childhood memory of being mistreated by a boy she liked, which still affects her. Alex tries to comfort her and encourages her to pursue the man she likes, but Nelly insists she's not good enough for him due to their different social statuses.

Alex argues that love shouldn't be limited by wealth or status, using his own pursuit of Miss Marvel (Leona/Debbie) as an example. However, Nelly becomes upset by Alex's encouragement to change her mindset and pushes him out of her room, ending the conversation abruptly.
