Alex encounters Nelly Lane, a young girl working at a food truck in Arlington. Despite initial hostility, they develop a friendly rapport. Alex helps her and learns about her difficult circumstances.

Later, at the Crown Hotel in Washington D.C., Alex meets Myriam Henley, a former high school classmate. Despite their past friendship, Myriam is hostile towards Alex. She resents him for getting into Preston University while she ended up at a less prestigious college. Myriam believes Alex "stole" her opportunity and is bitter about her perceived failure.

Myriam, accompanied by her boyfriend Jake Grant, mocks Alex's appearance and assumes he can't afford to stay at the luxurious hotel. She reveals embarrassing details about Alex's impoverished past to Jake, believing Alex is still struggling financially. However, Alex remains calm, knowing his current wealthy status, which Myriam is unaware of.

The encounter highlights the stark contrast between Alex's past poverty and his current wealth, as well as the misunderstandings and resentment that can arise from people's assumptions about others' circumstances.
