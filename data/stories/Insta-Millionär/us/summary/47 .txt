Alex, severely injured, faces Maddison who is about to shoot him. Suddenly, a bullet knocks the gun from her hand. Six helicopters appear, carrying a large black curtain. They descend, surrounding the area and blocking out the light. A group of highly skilled operatives emerges, quickly subduing the Azure Dragon Society members.

The operatives identify themselves as Alex's family security team. They inform Alex that Debbie is still alive, much to his relief. Alex orders them to take Debbie to a hospital and to strip the Azure Dragon Society members, including Maddison, leaving them naked. He instructs the team to cripple the Azure Dragon Society and prevent them from controlling the underworld.

The security team helps Alex into a helicopter and destroys the luxury cars belonging to Quentin and his men. The naked gang members are left to fend for themselves on the streets of New York, causing a scene and becoming the subject of many photos shared on social media.

Alex faints due to his injuries, and the security team receives orders to take him to Harmony Island. The helicopters fly eastward as the sun sets, carrying Alex to safety and medical attention.
