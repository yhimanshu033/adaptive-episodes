Chris Steadman, confronted by Rufus at the Clifton estate, attempts to threaten and bargain his way out of the situation. Rufus, unimpressed, offers Chris three choices: a quick death, prolonged suffering, or a fight. Chris chooses to fight but ends up accidentally killing himself with his own knife while attempting to attack Rufus.

Jessop Clifton, no longer pretending to be injured, discusses the situation with Rufus and Alex. They decide to move Chris's body to avoid direct implication in his death. A man is sent to stage Chris's death as an accident, driving his car into a reservoir.

Meanwhile, Art Steadman, Chris's father, is unaware of his son's fate. He's confident in their recent victory against the Cliftons and plans to use the Blood Brothers gang to eliminate any remaining opposition, with Alex as his primary target.

The episode highlights the escalating conflict between the Steadman and Clifton families, the unexpected turn of events with Chris's death, and the strategic maneuvering by both sides to gain advantage in their power struggle.
