Alex is unexpectedly invited to lunch by Karen, Zara's attractive cousin, causing confusion and jealousy among other students. Despite Zara's attempts to stop them, Karen forcefully takes Alex away in her car. Zara, to save face, tells everyone it's part of a plan to make James Watson jealous.

James, who has been trying to win Karen's affection, calls Zara and decides to propose to Karen with roses and a diamond ring. Unable to reach Karen or Alex by phone, Zara uses a location service to find them at a Japanese restaurant in Wanda Plaza.

Zara, James, and a group of students head to the restaurant, expecting a confrontation between James and Alex. Zara anticipates that Karen will reject Alex and that James might physically confront him. The group is eager to witness the drama unfold, believing Alex is being used as part of a scheme to make James jealous.
