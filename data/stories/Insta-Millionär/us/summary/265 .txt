Alex visits Don in the hospital and learns that Don fears losing his job as a security guard due to the recent incidents. At the university, Alex overhears other guards discussing the shootings from the previous night, including the attack on Julian Duvant and Chris Steadman. 

Alex speaks with Chief Lewis about Don's situation, revealing the truth about what happened with Julian and Chris. This changes Lewis's mind about firing Don, and Alex convinces him to consider the incident as a work-related injury to cover Don's medical expenses.

Meanwhile, Chris Steadman is being held at the police station. Despite receiving preferential treatment, he remains angry and uncooperative. Chelsea arrives to question him, but before she can, Chris's father, Art Steadman, shows up to take his son home. Chelsea objects, insisting that Chris must stay for questioning due to his suspected involvement in hiring murderers.

The situation remains tense, with Alex navigating the aftermath of the violent events while trying to protect Don's job, and Chelsea attempting to maintain justice despite the Steadman family's influence.
