Alex Ambrose, now involved with the Clifton family, finds himself at the center of a brewing conflict between the Cliftons and the Steadmans, two powerful families in Baltimore's underworld. Chris Steadman visits Jessop Clifton to negotiate terms for a showdown between the families. Three options are presented: an all-out war, a one-on-one fight to the death, or the Cliftons' immediate surrender. Jessop rejects the surrender option, and they agree to a one-on-one fight in three days at the Clifton underground boxing ring.

Jessop asks Alex to represent the Clifton family in the fight. Alex agrees, motivated by his love for Debbie and his sense of belonging to the Clifton family. The Steadmans have the support of the Blood Brothers, a large and powerful gang in Baltimore, which makes the fight more challenging for the Cliftons.

Meanwhile, Art Steadman, Chris's father, is confident in their plan to take over Baltimore's underworld. He has secured the support of Tyson Slade, presumably a leader of the Blood Brothers, who will provide a skilled fighter for the Steadmans. The stakes are high, as the outcome of this fight will determine the control of Baltimore's underground scene.
