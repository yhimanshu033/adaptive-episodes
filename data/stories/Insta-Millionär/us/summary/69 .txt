Alex and Debbie are enjoying a day out together when they notice a woman staring at them. This leads to a playful moment of jealousy from Debbie, which Alex tries to resolve by approaching the woman. However, this results in an embarrassing misunderstanding as the woman, Janice Campbell, was actually interested in Debbie, not Alex.

Janice introduces herself as a professional manager from Sunshine Music Company and offers Debbie an opportunity to join a girl group similar to Rocket Girl. Initially skeptical, Alex and Debbie verify Janice's credentials online. Debbie is hesitant about the offer, feeling unsure of her abilities, but Alex encourages her to try. After some consideration, Debbie agrees to give it a shot, potentially opening up a new chapter in her life as a potential celebrity.

This encounter introduces a possible new direction for Debbie's future, while also showcasing the strength of Alex and Debbie's relationship as they navigate this unexpected opportunity together.
