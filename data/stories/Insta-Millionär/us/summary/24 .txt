Alex's sudden display of wealth at the real estate office causes a stir, with Claire, the lady in shades, pretending to have bought a villa to save face in front of her sister-in-law Sue. Alex then purchases a custom Ferrari at a car dealership, causing excitement among the staff.

Lucy Smith invites Alex for a meeting, picking him up in her Mercedes. During the drive, there's sexual tension between them, with Lucy attempting to seduce Alex, but he politely declines.

They arrive at the Ledi Shopping Mall, where Lucy reveals that Alex is now the owner of her boutique, Autumn Beauty, as part of his integration into the family business. Lucy then leaves for a business trip to the southwest.

Alex drives Lucy's Mercedes around New York, enjoying the experience. He stops at a Shrimp King restaurant to buy lobster for himself and Debbie. As he's entering the restaurant, he picks up some dropped money from the person in front of him. At that moment, he's confronted by Zara Fitzgerald and her group, who mockingly ask if he's begging for money.
