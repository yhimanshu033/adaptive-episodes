Alex and Vivian chase after Monica's kidnappers, only to find them already beaten up. They learn that a masked man had rescued Monica. At a nearby park, they encounter Slayer, the masked man Alex had previously fought at the bar. Initially mistaken for a kidnapper, Slayer is revealed to be Monica's rescuer. 

Monica pleads with Slayer to remove his mask, revealing a disfigured face with white spots and a purple birthmark. Slayer explains his troubled past due to his appearance, leading to isolation and near-suicide until Henry saved him and gave him the mask.

Alex realizes that Slayer had been following him out of admiration, not revenge. He tries to convince Slayer that Henry is exploiting him rather than helping him. Alex points out how Henry mistreats Slayer and suggests that there are people who won't judge him based on his appearance. Slayer struggles to accept this, insisting that Henry is his savior and not treating him like a dog.
