Alex Ambrose, now working undercover as a PE teacher at Oak Ridge High School, prepares to face the notorious Class 6 seniors. The students, led by Eric, a politically connected troublemaker, plan to drive Alex away to win a $15,000 reward offered by Billy, the teaching director who holds a grudge against Alex. The class has a history of tormenting teachers, even resorting to violence in the case of Mr. Goldberg.

As Alex approaches the classroom, the students set up an elaborate prank involving a water bucket, oil, and metal balls to humiliate him. However, Alex senses the trap and kicks the door off its hinges, avoiding the prank entirely. He enters the classroom triumphantly, leaving the students stunned.

Meanwhile, other teachers discuss Alex's chances of surviving Class 6, with Leonard doubting his ability to control the students. Billy, plotting against Alex, believes the students will "eat him alive." The episode also introduces Heidi, a studious girl who stands out from her troublemaking classmates and is the daughter of the kidnapped researcher Alex is meant to protect.
