In this episode, the confrontation between Alex and the Ghosts escalates. After Alex easily defeats Marie Walton with a series of powerful slaps, sending her flying across the room and leaving her badly injured, the focus shifts to her brother Drew. Shocked by Alex's display of strength, Drew questions whether Alex is truly a master of martial arts at such a young age. Alex claims to be even more powerful than a master, which Drew finds hard to believe.

Drew, who considers himself just slightly less powerful than his own master, challenges Alex to a fight to test his abilities. He expresses disbelief that someone as young as Alex could have mastered their inner power and ascended to such a high level of skill. Drew draws his blade, preparing to engage Alex in combat.

The onlookers, including members of various martial arts families, are amazed by Alex's power and begin to believe the rumors about his abilities. The episode highlights the growing respect and fear for Alex among the martial arts community, while also setting the stage for a potentially intense battle between Alex and Drew.
