Alex and Emma decide to go to Maddison Park, an upscale restaurant where Cathy and Billy are already dining. Upon arrival, Alex and Emma encounter Cathy and Billy, leading to a confrontation. Cathy and Billy mock Alex and Emma, accusing them of sneaking in to use the bathroom without being customers. They call for the manager to have Alex and Emma removed.

The manager asks Alex and Emma to leave, but Alex reveals he has a reservation. Cathy and Billy are skeptical, claiming they have reserved table eight. The manager checks both reservations and confirms that Alex indeed has a booking for table eight in the VIP section, while Billy's reservation is for table eight in the general section.

This revelation surprises everyone, especially Cathy and Billy, who had been mocking Alex's apparent inability to afford such a restaurant. The episode ends with the manager apologizing to Alex for the misunderstanding and offering to show him to his VIP table, leaving Cathy and Billy stunned by the turn of events.
