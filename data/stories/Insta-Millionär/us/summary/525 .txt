Alex, now on equal footing with Spencer as Lord of the Moon Palace, accepts the position of envoy. Spencer explains his brutal actions at Canfield Arena as a calculated move to instill fear and prevent future attacks. Spencer leaves for six months to visit an old friend.

Alex returns home and discusses building a house with Debbie. The next day, while walking, Alex receives a call from Will who wants to meet. A salesman named Cole approaches Alex with a flyer for an upscale housing complex. Cole's sales director rudely interrupts, assuming Alex can't afford a house based on his appearance. The director takes back the flyer and dismisses Alex.

Alex enters a café to wait for Will. Cole follows him to apologize for his boss's behavior. When Alex asks why Cole gave him a flyer, Cole explains that a person's clothing doesn't always reflect their true financial situation, demonstrating his non-judgmental attitude.
