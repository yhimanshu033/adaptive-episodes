Alex and Debbie are enjoying their time at the Moon Palace, but Debbie reminds Alex of his family obligations. This causes Alex to worry about his family's precarious situation back home, facing threats from Nathan and the Mitchells. Despite his concerns, Alex and Debbie share a playful moment by the lake.

Meanwhile in Baltimore, Chris Steadman receives a report from his men who followed Alex and Debbie. They inform him that Debbie appeared ill and the couple drove into the Appalachian Mountains, never to return. Chris and Melissa conclude that Alex and Debbie are likely dead.

Melissa, expecting to marry Chris now that Alex is presumed dead, is shocked when Chris cruelly reveals he was only using her. He offers her money and dismisses her. Devastated, Melissa realizes her actions were driven by a foolish dream and leaves, regretting the lives she took for nothing.
