In this episode, Tyson Slade confronts the Clifton family at Cynthia's birthday banquet. He threatens anyone who helps Alex, demonstrating his power by destroying a clock. Debbie, as the leader of the Black Orchid cult and Alex's partner, stands up to Tyson, refusing to betray Alex. Impressed but unmoved by her integrity, Tyson uses his supernatural cold power on Debbie, cursing her to suffer increasingly severe cold attacks every 24 hours until she freezes to death unless Alex returns.

Tyson explains that this is revenge for Alex killing members of his gang. He leaves a message for Alex to meet him at the mouth of the Hudson River. The Clifton family is shocked and terrified by Tyson's power and cruelty. Jessop is devastated, feeling helpless to protect his granddaughter. As Tyson leaves, Jessop collapses and urges Rufus to find Alex and bring him back. Debbie, though in pain and freezing, remains strong and refuses to cry, determined not to let Alex down.
