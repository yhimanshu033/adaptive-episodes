Alex attends a party where he is initially mocked by some guests, including Tabitha and Sasha, for claiming to be the principal of Oak Ridge High School. His driver arrives and confirms his position, embarrassing those who doubted him. Later, Alex receives a call from Jessop about attending a martial arts reception in Baltimore, which he reluctantly agrees to.

That night, Alex has a disturbing dream about his wife Debbie. He wakes up to find himself being drugged and narrowly avoids an assassination attempt. The attacker is revealed to be Tabitha, one of the women who mocked him at the party. Alex manages to shake off the effects of the drug and confronts Tabitha, who is surprised by his resilience and ability to identify her despite her mask.

Alex deduces that Tabitha and Sasha had deliberately provoked him at the party as part of their plan. He confidently tells Tabitha that it's impossible to kill him, demonstrating his supernatural abilities and quick thinking in the face of danger.
