Alex Ambrose, now running the Birchwood Real Estate Company, faces challenges in managing his new business. Edward Steadman, who helps manage the company, informs Alex of issues with the sales department. The sales director demands a 30% salary increase, threatening to leave with his staff if not met.

Alex meets with the sales director, who tries to leverage his experience and connections for a higher salary. Alex, knowing the business is new and tight on funds, offers a small raise. When the sales director insists on his demands, Alex unexpectedly fires him, demonstrating that he won't be manipulated.

To prove his point, Alex calls Cole Havers, an industry expert, who confirms that such a large raise is unreasonable in the current market. The sales director, realizing his plan has backfired, becomes angry but is escorted out by security.

Meanwhile, Jessop Clifton, Debbie's grandfather, calls Alex for a meeting. Jessop is surprised to learn that Alex is now living and working at Birchwood House, running the business instead of working in security as he had assumed. Jessop plans to ask Alex to return as head of the family, unaware of Alex's new position and success in real estate.
