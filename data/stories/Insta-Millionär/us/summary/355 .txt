Alex and Rufus meet with Art Steadman, who reveals that the Blood Brothers gang is occupying the Shamrock Hotel and planning to expand their influence in Baltimore. Art offers to help Alex find those who want to kill him and proposes an alliance against the Blood Brothers. Alex accepts the offer and plans to investigate the Shamrock Hotel that night. Meanwhile, Art Steadman discovers his son Chris's body in a reservoir, believing it to be a cover-up for murder. He vows to avenge Chris's death. The Clifton family prepares for potential conflict, with Jessop taking time off work and spending time with Debbie. Tensions between the Steadman and Clifton families remain high, with both sides cautious of each other's motives.
