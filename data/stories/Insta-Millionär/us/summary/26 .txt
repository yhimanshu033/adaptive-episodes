Alex's true wealth is revealed when Howard Hamilton accidentally dents Alex's Mercedes in a parking lot. Initially, Howard and his friends mock Alex, believing him to be poor. They discuss the potential cost of repairs, estimating around $15,000, which visibly distresses Howard.

Alex suggests leaving the scene, but Zara criticizes this idea. Howard then pretends the cost is insignificant to save face. Rachael, disgusted by the group's behavior, decides to leave with Alex.

When a parking attendant arrives, he recognizes Alex as the car's owner. This revelation shocks the group, especially when Alex casually drives away in the Mercedes. He offers Rachael a ride, leaving the others stunned.

Zara, realizing her mistake, tries to get a ride with Alex but is rejected. The episode ends with Zara chasing after the car, shouting Alex's name, while he drives away with Rachael.

This incident dramatically shifts the power dynamics within the group, exposing their superficial judgments and highlighting the contrast between appearances and reality in Alex's life.
