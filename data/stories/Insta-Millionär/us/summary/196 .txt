The episode focuses on David Drake's pursuit of Leona Marvel. At a gathering of elite young people, David expresses interest in Leona, despite being engaged to her sister Lindsey. Darryl Brennan offers to help David win Leona's affection by using Debbie's diary, suggesting that Leona might be Debbie with amnesia.

The next day, David meets Leona at her university, dressed plainly and riding a bicycle. They tour the campus and go to the school restaurant, where three girls, secretly hired by Colin Patterson, mock and harass David for appearing poor. Leona tries to defend him, but David stops her from revealing his true identity.

After leaving the restaurant, Colin pays the girls for their performance. He explains that the scenario was based on something written in the diary, aiming to recreate experiences from Leona's past as Debbie.

David and Leona then walk by a lake on campus. Following a plan orchestrated with Colin, David leads Leona to a specific spot where she slips and falls into the lake, recreating another event from the diary. The episode ends with Leona calling for help in the water.
