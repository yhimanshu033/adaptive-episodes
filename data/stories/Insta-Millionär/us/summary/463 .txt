In this episode, Tabitha, an assassin hired by the Chamber of Commerce, attempts to kill Alex in his sleep at the school. Despite drugging him, Alex quickly recovers and easily evades her attacks. Alex recognizes that Tabitha is not who she claims to be and confronts her about her true identity. Frustrated by Alex's superior skills and ability to see through her facade, Tabitha becomes more determined to kill him to protect her secret.

As the fight progresses, Alex continues to dodge Tabitha's attacks effortlessly, demonstrating his exceptional martial arts skills. He taunts her, pointing out her miscalculation in underestimating him and criticizing her role as an assassin. Alex easily disarms Tabitha and toys with her, showing he could have killed her if he wanted to.

Tabitha admits to being an assassin sent by the Chamber of Commerce members but refuses to give up. She employs a new, more advanced sword technique, which Alex recognizes. Despite her increased efforts, Alex still manages to defend himself effectively. The episode ends with Tabitha believing she has finally struck Alex, but the outcome remains uncertain.
