Alex, the secret millionaire, finds himself in an awkward situation when his former classmate Minnie, now a popular online streamer, mistakes him for a villa caretaker. Minnie, unaware of Alex's true wealth, treats him condescendingly and invites herself and her friend Hannah to stay at the villa, believing Alex is just house-sitting.

Alex reluctantly agrees to their stay, maintaining his facade as a simple caretaker. Minnie and Hannah make themselves comfortable in the luxurious villa, reveling in the lifestyle of the wealthy. They order Alex to fetch them Chinese takeout, treating him like a servant.

Meanwhile, Minnie is struggling to increase her fan base on her streaming platform, Trist. She's frustrated by her inability to find a new angle to attract more followers. As she vents her frustrations to Hannah, her friend suddenly comes up with an idea that might help Minnie boost her popularity.

The episode highlights the contrast between Alex's true status and how others perceive him, as well as the superficial nature of online fame and the lengths people will go to increase their popularity.
