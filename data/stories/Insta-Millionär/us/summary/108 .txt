Alex Ambrose receives his custom-made Ferrari, worth over two million dollars, delivered by Harold Sharp and the president of Ferrari. The car is revealed to be a highly advanced, unique vehicle with exceptional features, including underwater driving capabilities and top-level safety measures. The Phillips family, who had previously looked down on Alex, are shocked and impressed by this revelation.

Alex allows Kelly to record her voice for the car's voice recognition system, giving her permission to drive it. They take the car for a test drive to Purple Dawn Lake, where Kelly learns that Alex is from the wealthy and influential Ambrose family. Alex warns her about potential objections from his family regarding their relationship.

Upon returning to the villa, Alex deliberately crashes his new Ferrari into Brian's car, destroying it completely. The Ferrari executives assure Brian that he will be compensated for the damage. The Phillips family tries to ingratiate themselves with Alex, but he ignores them.

Alex announces to Kelly that he knows her family doesn't want him there and decides to leave. He bids farewell and drives away in his Ferrari, leaving the Phillips family and Kelly stunned by the turn of events.
