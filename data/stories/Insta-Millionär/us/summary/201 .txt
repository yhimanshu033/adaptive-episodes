In this episode, Alex, Phillipa, and Nelly confront the group of volunteers who had previously mistreated them. Nelly manipulates the situation, causing everyone, including Darryl and Myriam, to kneel before Alex and Phillipa. Alex decides not to further punish the group, except for punching Darryl. They leave the event, with Phillipa expressing gratitude to Alex.

Meanwhile, David Drake is reading Debbie's diary to learn about her relationship with Alex, using this information to manipulate Leona Marvel (who is actually Debbie with amnesia). He plans to propose to her.

The next day, David takes Leona to West Lake. He has orchestrated an elaborate scene mimicking a bullying incident from Debbie's past. Three girls pretend to harass Leona, and David "rescues" her. This is followed by a staged attack by fake gangsters, where David protects Leona and gets "injured." The scene ends with David's men arriving in a helicopter to save them, leaving Leona emotionally affected and concerned for David's well-being.

This episode showcases the complex dynamics between Alex and his peers, as well as David's manipulative tactics to win Leona's affection.
