Alex, Celia, and Ethan are ambushed by members of the Thornton family, led by Conrad Thornton, who seeks revenge for Alex's defeat of Ash Thornton. Despite being outnumbered, Alex confidently faces the attackers. He easily deflects Conrad's throwing knife and defeats Jackson with a single punch. Alex then creates a large hole in the ground by stomping his foot and throws Paulie into it. The remaining attackers are shocked by Alex's display of superhuman strength and abilities. Celia and Ethan attempt to help but are told to stay back by Alex, who insists on handling the situation alone. The Thornton family members begin to realize they have severely underestimated Alex's powers and are now facing a formidable opponent.
