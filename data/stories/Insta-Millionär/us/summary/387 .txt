Alex Ambrose, now undercover as a teacher at Oak Ridge High School, has successfully thwarted the initial pranks planned by Class 6. His actions have caused division among the students, particularly affecting Eric, the class leader. Heidi, recognizing Alex as her previous savior, has warned her classmates against further attempts to sabotage him.

Meanwhile, other teachers, including Leonard and Billy, are skeptical of Alex's abilities and expect him to fail with Class 6. They discuss his apparent quick exit from the classroom, unaware of what truly transpired.

Heidi approaches Alex privately, expressing her surprise at seeing him as a teacher. She attempts to flirt with him, but Alex maintains a professional demeanor. He learns that Heidi is unaware of her father's kidnapping, believing he's just busy with work.

Alex informs Heidi that he must protect her constantly for the next three months, explaining that her father requested this. Heidi is skeptical, stating that her father has never shown much concern for family matters. The situation becomes more complex as Alex tries to balance his protective duties with maintaining appropriate boundaries as a teacher.
