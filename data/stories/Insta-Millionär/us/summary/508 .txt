In this episode, Jacob, the leader of the Ghosts, arrives at the Baltimore Martial Arts Association headquarters. He effortlessly defeats multiple association members, including Jeffrey, Edward, and Will. Alex confronts Jacob but is severely outmatched, unable to land a single hit while being easily overpowered.

Jacob demonstrates his immense skill and power, catching and breaking a spear thrown by Alex and defeating him with a series of rapid strikes. Alex is left injured and struggling to stand, realizing the vast difference in their abilities.

Jacob announces that he won't kill Alex immediately but will face him in a fight in seven days. He threatens to kill innocent people if Alex tries to run away or is late for the fight. After delivering his message, Jacob leaves with the freed Ghosts.

Ava, showing her true colors, kicks the injured Alex and calls him a loser. She suggests stripping Alex of his authority to protect the association members. The episode ends with tension and uncertainty about Alex's ability to face Jacob in the upcoming fight.
