In this episode, Drew Walton confronts Alex with the legendary Bloody Blade, a sacred weapon of the Ghosts. Drew boasts about the blade's power and his past kills, threatening to destroy Alex and the Baltimore Martial Arts Association. However, Alex remains unimpressed, claiming that such blades are common in their training and suggesting that the Ghosts copied their designs.

As Drew attacks Alex with the Bloody Blade, the crowd is paralyzed with fear, believing they are witnessing the weapon's true power. The Ghosts prepare to strike while the martial artists are frozen. Drew taunts Alex and brings the blade close to his forehead, gloating about making history.

Surprisingly, Alex remains calm and dismisses the blade as "broken." He easily pushes it away with his finger, causing a strange force to numb Drew's arm. Alex reiterates that he's familiar with such blades and is not afraid of the Ghosts' tactics. Drew is left shocked and silent, questioning the effectiveness of the Bloody Blade against Alex.
