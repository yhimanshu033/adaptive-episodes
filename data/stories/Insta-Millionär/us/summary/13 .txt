Alex attends a business meeting at the Plaza Hotel, where he meets Lucy Smith, the Director of the East Coast Division's Information Department. After the meal, Alex decides to take some of the delicious food back for his friends. Ken Stokes, eager to please Alex, arranges for the dishes to be prepared again.

Alex accepts Lucy's offer to drive him back. In the car, Lucy apologizes for the elevator incident and expresses gratitude towards Alex. Alex decides to go to the hospital instead of the university to visit his friend Rose.

During the car ride, Alex feels nervous being alone with Lucy. She encourages him to look at her, saying it's a blessing to be seen by him. Alex becomes flustered and embarrassed but eventually looks at her directly, admiring her beauty.

They arrive at the hospital, and Alex, still somewhat dazed by Lucy's presence, thanks her and leaves with the food for his friends. The episode highlights the contrast between Alex's new wealthy status and his continued modest behavior, as well as his growing attraction to Lucy.
