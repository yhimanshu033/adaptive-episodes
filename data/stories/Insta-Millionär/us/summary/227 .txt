In this episode, David Drake continues his search for additional security personnel for his family. During a test of martial arts skills, two fighters named Damian and Rick initially stand out, defeating their opponents easily. However, their arrogance is quickly challenged when a middle-aged man named Cliff Munson steps forward.

Cliff, who had previously defeated Damian and Rick in a brothel incident four years ago, easily overpowers Rick, revealing his superior fighting skills. It's disclosed that Cliff is a highly respected martial artist who recently lost his job due to his secret visits to brothels being exposed.

David, impressed by Cliff's abilities, offers him a position as head of security for the Drake family. Cliff accepts the offer, and David also hires the other martial artists, including Damian and Rick, but at lower salaries.

The episode concludes with David planning to use his new security team to deal with Alex after his upcoming wedding, indicating his intention to eliminate Alex as a threat.
