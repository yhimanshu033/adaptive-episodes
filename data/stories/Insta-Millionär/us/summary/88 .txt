The episode focuses on Debbie's performance with her band "The Dream Chasers" at the talent show "Sky's the Limit" in Chicago. Despite suffering from severe stomach pain due to Melissa's sabotage, Debbie insists on performing. 

Just as the band is about to start, they are interrupted by the arrival of Lee Dempsey, a wealthy investor in the show, and his girlfriend Angelina. Their disruptive behavior and their dog biting the show's director cause a commotion.

Debbie struggles through the performance of "Blue Bird," fighting intense pain that affects her singing. The judges and Lee Dempsey are unimpressed with her poor vocal performance, unaware of her condition.

Meanwhile, Alex is in New York celebrating his friend Joe's engagement, unaware of Debbie's predicament in Chicago. 

As Debbie reaches the final solo part of the song, she is on the verge of collapse but determined to finish. Her performance deteriorates further, leading to negative comments from the judges and disappointment from Lee Dempsey. The episode ends with Debbie struggling to complete the song while in extreme discomfort.
