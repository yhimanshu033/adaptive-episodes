Alex and Debbie leave Canada after spending a month with the Black Orchid cult, with Debbie having become their new leader. Meanwhile, Tyson Slade is in seclusion in a frozen northern landscape, ice fishing on a frozen lake. Luther Duncan arrives by helicopter with urgent news, interrupting Tyson's solitude. Despite Tyson's initial annoyance, Luther informs him that Lee Harshaw has been killed by a security guard named Alex in Baltimore. This news has severely damaged the Blood Brothers' reputation, leading to territorial losses and conflicts. Luther urges Tyson to take action to restore the gang's strength and respect. As they talk, a polar bear approaches. Tyson demonstrates his dominance by fearlessly confronting the bear, causing it to submit to him. This display of power impresses Luther and symbolizes Tyson's ability to assert control in challenging situations.
