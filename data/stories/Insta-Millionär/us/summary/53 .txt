Alex, posing as Adam Childs, wins a diamond necklace at Graham Stirling's dinner party. Cathy, Alex's ex-girlfriend, recognizes him and publicly accuses him of stealing $350,000 from their university. Alex denies the accusations and claims to have legitimate business with Graham worth $350,000, which Graham confirms.

Cathy attempts to prove Alex's true identity but is dismissed by other guests who trust Graham's word. Alex gives the necklace to Debbie, and they share a passionate kiss on stage, embarrassing Debbie.

Unexpectedly, police officers arrive at the party. Cathy assumes they're there to arrest Alex, but instead, they arrest Billy, Cathy's boyfriend, for the theft of $350,000 from Preston University. Cathy is also detained for questioning due to her recent extravagant spending with Billy.

The police reveal that they have evidence of Billy's involvement in the theft and are investigating Cathy's potential role. This turn of events shocks Cathy, who begins to realize that Billy might be the real thief, explaining their recent lavish lifestyle.
