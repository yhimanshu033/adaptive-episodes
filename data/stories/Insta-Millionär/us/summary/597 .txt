The episode begins with a chaotic scene at the Woodside Clinic, where protesters have escalated their actions to violence and arson. The clinic catches fire, causing panic among patients and staff. Louisa and her supporters disappear into the crowd as police arrive to arrest those involved.

Meanwhile, Lindsey Marvel visits the Murdoch mansion to negotiate with Jeremiah Murdoch, Michael's father. The family is in mourning, but there are rumors about Michael's poor health and reputation. Jeremiah is already stressed due to the botched protest at the clinic.

Lindsey offers to clear Alex's name and proposes a deal with Jeremiah. Initially dismissive, Jeremiah's attitude changes when Lindsey presents a stack of incriminating documents and photographs revealing the Murdoch family's dark business dealings. These materials, if made public, could have severe consequences for the family.

Lindsey explains that she wants Jeremiah to stop pressuring the police station, allowing Alex to be found innocent and freed. Jeremiah is both angry and impressed by Lindsey's bold move and the extent of her information. He questions her motives and the source of her information.

Lindsey reveals that she received the documents from an unknown source, suggesting she has powerful allies supporting her efforts to help Alex. The episode ends with Lindsey in a position of leverage over the Murdoch family, potentially changing the course of Alex's case.
