Alex Ambrose, now a powerful figure with supernatural abilities, continues his search for his missing wife Debbie. After surviving an attack at an amusement park, he checks into the luxurious Riverside hotel in Baltimore. Upon returning to his suite, he finds famous singer Cassidy Pike being harassed by Grayson Turner, a well-known director and gangster.

Grayson is attempting to coerce Cassidy into sexual favors in exchange for a role in his TV series. When Alex intervenes, Grayson threatens him, unaware of Alex's true capabilities. Alex easily overpowers Grayson and dangles him out of the hotel window. Despite Grayson's threats and attempts to intimidate Alex, Alex remains calm and unfazed.

To Cassidy's shock, Alex ultimately releases Grayson, letting him fall from the window. This dramatic action demonstrates Alex's willingness to take extreme measures against those who abuse their power, as well as his confidence in facing potential consequences. The episode ends with Cassidy stunned by Alex's actions, revealing a darker side to his character as he continues his mission to find Debbie.
