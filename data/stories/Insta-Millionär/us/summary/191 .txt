The episode focuses on the aftermath of the university's New Year's party and the kidnapping of Lindsey Marvel. Key events include:

1. Alex declines invitations to continue celebrating, maintaining his facade of being poor again after donating to the school.

2. Leona discovers Lindsey has been kidnapped by Hunter, a former employee seeking revenge, demanding a $10 million ransom.

3. Leona blames Alex for not picking up Lindsey as requested, unaware he hadn't seen her message.

4. Alex searches for Lindsey throughout the night but fails to find her.

5. Lindsey is rescued by her family and the police. She's taken to the hospital, where Alex visits her.

6. Leona remains cold towards Alex, insisting he stop confusing her with Debbie.

7. Lindsey doesn't blame Alex for the kidnapping and seems to have developed feelings for him, referring to him as her "benefactor."

8. The episode ends with a tender moment between Alex and Lindsey, with Lindsey expressing appreciation for Alex's concern during her ordeal.
