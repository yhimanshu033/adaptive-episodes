Alex, now back at his job as a security officer at Johns Hopkins University, is enjoying a day with Debbie when he receives an urgent call from Ethan. Ethan informs Alex that Heidi has been kidnapped by the gangsters, just as Alex had warned might happen. Despite Ethan's plea for help and the agency's regret for their previous treatment of Alex, Alex coldly refuses to get involved, stating it's no longer his problem.

Meanwhile, the situation at Oak Ridge High School has escalated dramatically. A group of mercenaries, led by Bill, easily overpowered and killed Agent Jude Hutton before successfully kidnapping Heidi. The kidnappers have taken refuge in an abandoned factory in Baltimore, which they've fortified with explosives and booby traps.

The news of the kidnapping has spread fear throughout Baltimore, especially among wealthy families. Gavin, now under immense pressure from his superiors and the public, is struggling to manage the crisis. The authorities have set a ten-hour deadline to resolve the hostage situation before they're forced to storm the building.

As Alex dismisses Ethan's call, he notices Ethan, Celia, and Gavin approaching him and Debbie on the university campus, likely to persuade him in person to help with the rescue mission.
