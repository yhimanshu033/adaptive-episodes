In this episode, tensions escalate at the Barside Club when Derek Lloyd, a manager from Robinson Winery, makes unwanted advances towards Cassidy Pike. Alex intervenes, slapping Derek twice to defend Cassidy. Derek, humiliated and angry, threatens Alex with his connections and influence in Baltimore. He also pressures Cassidy to meet him at a hotel room, implying it's the only way she'll get his help. Alex warns Derek not to threaten Cassidy again, leading to a standoff. Derek calls for backup, suggesting he's hiring someone to deal with Alex. The situation becomes increasingly tense, with Derek's companions warning Alex about Derek's influence and potential consequences for both him and Cassidy. The episode ends with the conflict unresolved and the threat of further escalation looming.
