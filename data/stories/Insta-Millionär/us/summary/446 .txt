In this episode, the aftermath of Alex Ambrose's conflict with the Mitchell family unfolds. Connor Mitchell, celebrating what he believes to be a victory over the Ambrose family, suddenly dies under mysterious circumstances. This event is followed by a series of unexplained deaths among Mitchell family members across the country, with no apparent cause or evidence of foul play.

Alex meets with the leaders of the Black Hand, a feared organization, who surprisingly defer to him as their leader. He instructs them to protect the Ambrose family. Oscar Steadman witnesses this encounter and realizes the extent of Alex's power and influence.

The Mitchell family is decimated within three days, with almost every member dying suddenly and inexplicably. This is widely believed to be Alex's retaliation for the attack on his mother, Flora Ambrose, though there is no concrete evidence.

Oscar reflects on his own insignificance compared to Alex's power and is relieved that his family remained neutral in the conflict. The remaining Mitchell family members abroad regret their attack on Flora Ambrose.

The episode ends with Alex visiting the ruins of the Mitchell house. Oscar approaches him respectfully, kneeling before him, but Alex leaves without acknowledging him, emphasizing his enigmatic and powerful status.
