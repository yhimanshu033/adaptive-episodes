Alex attends a party at his friend Joe's new mansion in Baltimore, where he encounters hostility from some wealthy guests, particularly Tabitha Hadley and Sasha Hayes. They demand an apology from Alex for his perceived disrespect. Suzan's mother, Suranne, intervenes and also insists that Alex apologize.

Alex proposes to revisit the issue at the end of the party, suggesting he'll apologize if necessary but implying that the women's behavior might prove him right. This creates tension, but Joe's father steps in to calm the situation.

Rose, a beautiful and influential young woman, arrives and defends Alex, challenging the notion that older people automatically deserve respect. She knows more about Alex's true identity than others but keeps this information to herself.

The confrontation ends with Tabitha and Sasha backing down, partly due to Rose's intervention and the growing support for Alex. They decide to wait until the end of the evening for their apology, but remain hostile towards Alex. The episode highlights the social dynamics and power struggles within this wealthy circle, with Alex maintaining his composure throughout the conflict.
