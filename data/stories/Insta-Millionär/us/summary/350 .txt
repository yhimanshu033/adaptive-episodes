Alex and Rufus rush to a Clifton family office location for a press conference. As they arrive, Alex senses danger from a passing blue car and chases after it. The car contains four women: Callisto, Luna, Celeste, and Selene, who had just bought it. Alex catches up to the car and forcibly removes the women, urging them to run towards the Clifton building.

Moments after everyone is clear of the vehicle, it explodes. Alex had sensed the danger and protected the women, particularly Celeste, whom he carried to safety. The explosion shocks everyone present, but no one is seriously injured.

The Moon maidens are deeply shaken by their near-death experience and grateful for Alex's intervention. They question how he knew about the danger, and Alex reveals that he heard the bomb ticking, surprising the women who hadn't heard anything. This incident demonstrates Alex's heightened senses and protective instincts, as well as the ongoing threats faced by those associated with the Cliftons.
