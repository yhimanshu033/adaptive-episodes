Alex Ambrose faces a crisis at the Woodside Clinic when a group of people accuse the staff of causing an old man's death. Alex quickly assesses the situation and realizes the old man is not actually dead. Using his acupuncture skills, he revives the man and exposes a plot to discredit the clinic.

It's revealed that the old man was terminally ill and had been convinced to bite a poisoned tooth to fake his death at the clinic. Alex confronts the conspirators, including the old man's son, about their scheme. When pressed for information about who sent them, the group suddenly bites down on their own poisoned teeth, causing them to collapse.

Alex, caught off guard by this turn of events, quickly works to slow the spread of the poison in the conspirators' bodies until an ambulance arrives. He's concerned about the potential impact on the clinic's reputation if these individuals die on the premises.

Throughout the incident, Alex demonstrates his exceptional medical skills, quick thinking, and ability to handle crisis situations. The episode highlights the challenges Alex faces in managing the clinic and protecting it from external threats.
