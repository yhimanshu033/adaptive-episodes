The episode focuses on two main storylines:

1. The Black Orchid cult in Canada, led by Cassius Sumter, is searching for a "chosen one" believed to be Cynthia's daughter. They suspect the Clifton family may have hidden her and consider this a betrayal. Cassius orders his priests to find the chosen one and threatens to destroy the Clifton family if they have indeed betrayed the cult.

2. Alex's victory at Silver Lake has made him famous in the martial arts world, though most people don't know his true identity. Jessop Clifton decides to host a celebration dinner for Alex, partly to see his granddaughter Debbie without arousing suspicion. Rufus is concerned about the attention this might bring, but Jessop insists.

Alex and Debbie agree to attend the dinner. As they approach the Clifton family home, they overhear people discussing the events at Silver Lake, highlighting how widespread the news of Alex's victory has become.
