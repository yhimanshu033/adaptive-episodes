Alex's successful treatment of Keira Lambert using a mysterious pill from the Moon Palace causes a stir among the medical staff. Dr. Sophie Allbrook, initially skeptical and hostile towards Alex, insists on running tests to confirm Keira's recovery. The tests reveal that Keira's vitals are not only normal but better than expected, resembling those of a top athlete.

The situation escalates when Dr. Vincent Cook, a renowned physician, arrives. Initially dismissive of Alex's treatment, Dr. Cook's attitude changes dramatically upon seeing the test results. He becomes excited about the unprecedented nature of Keira's recovery and its potential to revolutionize medical science.

Dr. Cook, to everyone's surprise, begins to plead with Alex to teach him everything about the treatment. This causes shock among the medical staff, particularly Dr. Allbrook, who idolizes Dr. Cook. She tries to intervene, warning Dr. Cook about risking his legacy, but he dismisses her concerns.

The episode ends with Dr. Allbrook questioning her initial judgment of Alex. She wonders if her skepticism and hostility were misplaced, given Dr. Cook's enthusiastic response to Alex's seemingly impossible achievement. She finds herself conflicted and unsure how to react to this turn of events.
