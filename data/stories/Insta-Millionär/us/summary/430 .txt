In this episode, Billy Wilde's position at the school becomes untenable after the revelation that his uncle, Bernard Wilde, is under investigation for serious misconduct. Without his uncle's protection, Billy loses all his power and influence. The teachers and students turn against him, demanding that he leave the school. Billy is forced to flee, but he's not ready to give up. He makes a mysterious phone call, summoning someone from the Mitchell family to help him get revenge on Alex.

Meanwhile, Alex's position at the school is further solidified. The director of the district education bureau and Mr. Cartwright leave after asking some questions about the school. Alex has gained celebrity status among the students, but he's not particularly interested in their adulation.

Unexpectedly, Alex receives a phone call from his brother Justin. This is unusual because Alex's family had disowned him and cut off all contact. Justin reveals that the Ambrose family is in trouble, facing a plot against them. He asks for Alex's help, stating that he trusts Alex more than anyone else in the family.
