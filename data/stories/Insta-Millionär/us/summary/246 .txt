Alex and Debbie's secret wedding at the Marvel mansion is interrupted by Rufus Clifton and his men, including the skilled fighter Paul Novak. Rufus, seeking revenge against Charles Marvel and wanting to reclaim Debbie, breaks in with his team. The Moon maidens successfully subdue two of Rufus's men using their silk scarves with hidden wires. However, Paul Novak proves to be an exceptionally powerful opponent, easily freeing his companions and demonstrating his strength by resisting the cutting wires. The peaceful wedding turns into a tense confrontation, with Charles trying to protect Debbie and Lindsey, while Alex faces off against Paul. The situation escalates as Rufus threatens violence, and the various parties present must navigate this dangerous and unexpected turn of events.
