After Jacob's death, the Ghosts mourn their leader while tensions escalate. Marie, overcome with grief, attempts to attack Alex but is easily disarmed. Seth intervenes to protect Marie, leading to a confrontation with Alex. During their exchange, Marie is accidentally killed by Seth's sword, further intensifying the situation.

Hugo Sherridan and Kenneth Petrie try to assert their authority and incite action against Alex, but they are clearly afraid of his power. Alex remains calm and confident, easily defending himself against attacks and demonstrating his superior skills.

The Ghosts are reluctant to engage Alex directly, having witnessed his strength. They maintain a defensive stance, unwilling to provoke him further. Kenneth, frustrated by their inaction, urges them to attack Alex while he's supposedly weakened.

Throughout the confrontation, Alex remains in control, dismissing threats and challenges from Hugo and others. The situation remains tense, with the potential for further violence as the various factions grapple with the new power dynamics following Jacob's death.
