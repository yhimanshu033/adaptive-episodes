Alex begins his new job as a security guard at Johns Hopkins University, where Debbie is now studying. He works alongside Don, another guard, and they patrol the campus and guard the residential building. Debbie is excited about her studies but working hard to keep up with the demanding coursework.

One day, two wealthy young men in a BMW approach the residential building. The driver, Julian Duvant, arrogantly demands entry. When Alex and Don explain that only residents are allowed in, Julian becomes aggressive. He slaps Don and tries to punch Alex, but Alex quickly knocks Julian down, causing him to lose two teeth.

Don tries to apologize and diffuse the situation, worried about the consequences of upsetting someone from a powerful family. However, Alex stands firm, insisting that Julian should apologize and leave. Julian, humiliated and angry, threatens revenge before departing with his cousin Chris.

The episode highlights the contrast between Alex's principled stance and Don's more cautious approach, as well as the tension between the privileged elite and working-class security guards. It also shows Alex's continued efforts to protect Debbie while maintaining their secret relationship.
