Alex confronts a sniper on a rooftop who attempted to assassinate Jessop Clifton. The sniper reveals that there are more attackers and threatens Alex's relatives. While interrogating the sniper, a massive explosion occurs at the Clifton building. Alex rushes back to find Jessop, Rufus, and Debbie safe but shaken. It's discovered that a bomber disguised as a police officer had infiltrated the building, causing the explosion and killing two bodyguards. Alex believes the attacks are targeting his relatives to get to him, suspecting either the Steadman family or the Blood Brothers gang as the culprits. The situation remains tense as they wait to see what will happen next.
