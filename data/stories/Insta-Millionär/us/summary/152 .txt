Alex is visited by Minnie and Hannah, who apologize for their earlier behavior and ask him to attend a Trist party in Miami. Minnie reveals that Cathy, Alex's ex-girlfriend, dropped out of school and left a letter for him. She uses this information to blackmail Alex into attending the party, promising to give him the letter afterward.

The next day, they fly to Miami for the party on a luxury cruise ship. While waiting, Alex walks along the harbor, calling out to Debbie. At the party, Minnie and Hannah overhear conversations about Lee Dempsey, the wealthy owner of the cruise ship. Minnie briefly considers trying to attract Lee's attention but is discouraged by Hannah.

The party begins, with many internet celebrities in attendance. Alex remains distant, lost in thought about Debbie and Cathy, while Minnie focuses on her livestreaming career and potential opportunities at the event.
