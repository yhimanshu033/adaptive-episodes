In this episode, the situation at the Angel Bar escalates dramatically. Henry and his friends are harassing Vivian and other female staff members when some young men in the bar intervene. A fight breaks out, but Henry's masked bodyguard, Slayer, quickly subdues the attackers with his martial arts skills.

Henry, emboldened by Slayer's protection, becomes more aggressive and attempts to sexually assault Vivian. Alex, who has been observing the situation, suddenly intervenes by kicking Henry and rescuing Vivian.

Enraged, Henry orders Slayer to attack Alex with a dagger. As Slayer approaches, Alex reveals that he has also armed himself with a knife. In a swift move, Alex manages to wound Slayer's shoulder, causing him to drop his weapon.

The episode highlights Alex's bravery and quick thinking in a dangerous situation, as well as the complex dynamics between Henry, Slayer, and the other characters in the bar. It also sets the stage for potential further conflict between Alex and Henry's group.
