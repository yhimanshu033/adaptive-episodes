Alex receives a call from his long-lost cousin Nathan, who claims to have news from their grandfather. Alex meets Nathan at the Continental Hotel, leaving the Moon Maidens behind. At the hotel, Alex encounters Nathan, who is accompanied by a family bodyguard. Their reunion is tense due to childhood rivalries and bad memories.

Ken Stokes and Sam Woodsworth arrive, and Nathan treats them disrespectfully, slapping them for being late. This angers Alex, who attacks Nathan in defense of Ken and Sam, whom he considers family. Nathan then taunts Alex about abandoning his girlfriend, revealing his intention to manipulate Alex into pursuing Leona again, which would keep him rejected by the family.

Ken realizes Nathan's plan and hopes Alex won't fall for it. The episode highlights the complex family dynamics, past rivalries, and potential manipulation within the Ambrose family, setting the stage for further conflict and decisions for Alex.
