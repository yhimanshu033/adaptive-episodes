Alex accompanies Debbie to Carla's funeral, and Debbie begins to recover from her grief. She decides to return to band practice with the Dream Chasers. At the music company, Melissa and other band members mock Debbie and Alex for arriving by taxi, highlighting the class differences within the group. Rita, another band member, shows concern for Debbie but looks down on Alex.

Feeling frustrated, Alex plans to show off his wealth to protect Debbie from further ridicule. He decides to order a Ferrari to impress Debbie's bandmates.

Meanwhile, Kelly Phillips, a girl Alex inadvertently helped during a previous incident, is searching for him on the university campus with her friend Sharon. They overhear Alex on the phone discussing the Ferrari purchase and the transfer of $100,000.

Sharon, skeptical of Alex's apparent wealth, decides to mock him by pretending to order an expensive Rolls-Royce. She blocks Alex's path and continues to tease him, causing tension. Kelly, embarrassed by her friend's behavior, tries to intervene, recognizing Alex as the person who had helped her before.
