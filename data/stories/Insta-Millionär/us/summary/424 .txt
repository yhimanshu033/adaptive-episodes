Alex confronts Ash Thornton, a respected martial arts master, who attempts to attack him. Alex effortlessly defeats Ash, breaking his arm and kicking him to the ground. Ash threatens Alex with his family's power and connections, but Alex remains unfazed.

Alex then turns his attention to Gavin, the agency director who had previously disrespected him. He begins to attack both Ash and Gavin, demonstrating his superior strength and expressing his anger at their earlier treatment of him.

Celia intervenes, trying to calm Alex down and reminding him of Heidi's presence. This appeal to Alex's concern for his students' well-being succeeds in diffusing some of his anger.

However, before walking away, Alex breaks Ash's thigh bone as a final act of retribution. He then approaches Gavin, stating that he must take responsibility for his disrespectful actions. The episode ends with Gavin screaming in pain as Alex attacks him.

Throughout the confrontation, Alex makes it clear that he is not afraid of the agency, the Thornton family, or any potential consequences, asserting his dominance and power over those who had previously underestimated him.
