In this episode, the situation at Joe Hall's house is resolved as Clark Fairfax and his armed group arrive to rescue Alex. Jonah Walton and Michael Murdoch are forced to surrender and are taken away. Clark reveals himself to Alex and checks on his condition. Alex dismisses his injuries as "just a flesh wound" and focuses on helping Rose, who has been poisoned. He administers the remaining tonic to her and himself.

Rose regains consciousness and expresses mixed emotions towards Alex, alternating between anger, affection, and gratitude. She hugs him tightly, revealing her romantic feelings for him. However, Alex firmly rejects her advances and criticizes her past behavior, stating that he only saved her out of duty, not affection. He tells her that all he feels for her is pity.

Hurt by Alex's cold response, Rose composes herself and bids him farewell, promising that he won't see her again. The episode highlights the complex relationships between characters and Alex's detached attitude towards those he doesn't consider close to him.
