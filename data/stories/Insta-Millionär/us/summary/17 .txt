Alex and Emma arrive at Maddison Park restaurant and are shown to their VIP table, which is currently occupied by Cathy and Billy due to a seating mistake. The manager asks Cathy and Billy to move to a regular table, but Cathy refuses. When Billy realizes the high cost of the VIP section, he leaves in anger, forcing Cathy to follow.

Alex and Emma enjoy their meal in the VIP section while Cathy and Billy eat in the regular area. Cathy is jealous and confused about how Alex can afford such luxury. Emma also questions Alex about his sudden wealth, and he lies, saying he won $150,000 in the lottery.

After dinner, Billy drops Cathy off at her dorm without spending more time together. Alex reflects on the evening, feeling satisfied that Cathy made a fool of herself. Meanwhile, Cathy, still curious about Alex's newfound wealth, approaches Emma to inquire about it. Emma reveals Alex's lottery win story to Cathy, who then schemes to get the money from Alex herself.
