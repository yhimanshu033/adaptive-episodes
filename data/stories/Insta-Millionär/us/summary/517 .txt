Alex faces off against Jacob, a powerful martial arts master, in the Canfield Arena. Jacob demonstrates his incredible abilities by creating clouds that can be both hard and soft, using them to attack Alex. Alex manages to dodge the initial attacks, surprising everyone with his agility.

The crowd, including Hugo Sherridan and Kenneth Petrie, believes Alex is outmatched and doomed to fail. Seth Paxman, representing Jacob, discusses with Hugo about stripping Alex of his assets and removing him from the martial arts association after his expected defeat.

Despite the overwhelming odds, Alex remains defiant. He challenges Jacob's status as a "real master," shocking the audience with his audacity. This insult enrages Kenneth, who believes Alex has sealed his fate by disrespecting Jacob.

The confrontation showcases the immense power gap between a regular fighter and a true martial arts master, with Jacob's cloud manipulation ability leaving the spectators in awe. The stage is set for a dramatic showdown between Alex and Jacob, with the former's future in the martial arts world hanging in the balance.
