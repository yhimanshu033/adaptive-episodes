Lindsey Marvel visits Jeremiah Murdoch, presenting him with damaging information about his family's dark dealings. She reveals knowledge of past crimes, including a suspicious car accident and Jeremiah's affair with an employee. Lindsey then presents evidence suggesting that Michael Murdoch, who was terminally ill, committed suicide to frame Alex for murder. She theorizes that Steven Murdoch may have persuaded Michael to do this.

Jeremiah initially dismisses Lindsey's claims as speculation but becomes visibly shaken. He admits to being willing to risk everything to take Alex down. Lindsey mentions the recent demonstration and fire at the wellness center, implying Murdoch family involvement.

Lindsey threatens to expose old murder accusations against Jeremiah's father and reveals she has evidence of Michael's disease and plans to frame Alex. She states her intention to go to the police with this information, which could exonerate Alex.

Two days after the meeting, Lindsey begins reporting the information piece by piece to a mysterious superior, suggesting she may be working for someone else in this complex situation.
