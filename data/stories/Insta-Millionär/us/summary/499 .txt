The episode continues the dramatic events at the Baltimore Martial Arts Association event. After Desmond Lang's arrest, Joe Hall receives a call from the Raptors' head coach, terminating his contract. This revelation shocks Joe and Caroline, who had believed Desmond was responsible for securing the contract.

Desmond, in a desperate attempt to save face, calls his associate Helen Stanley for help. However, when Alex speaks to Helen, she immediately recognizes him and disassociates herself from Desmond, further demonstrating Alex's growing influence and power.

As Desmond is taken away by the police, Alex mingles with the guests, revealing that he had been offered and accepted the role of president of the Martial Arts Association, despite initial reluctance.

Caroline and Joe are left isolated and shocked by the turn of events. Caroline regrets her decision to distance Joe from Alex, realizing it may have led to their current predicament.

The episode concludes with a new threat as five individuals forcefully enter the event. Two of them, a woman in black and a man in blue, demonstrate their fighting prowess by easily overcoming security guards. They approach Alex, with the woman ordering his seizure, setting up a potential confrontation.
