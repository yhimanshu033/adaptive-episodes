In this episode, the situation at Alex's villa takes a dramatic turn when Rose discovers Alex's bracelet and realizes he is the true owner of the house. She confronts Alex about his past acts of kindness, including saving her from Luciel and helping her at the Berkeley hotel. Overwhelmed with guilt for her previous behavior, Rose breaks down in tears and apologizes profusely to Alex, who forgives her.

The revelation shocks Sara, Rose's grandmother, who had been hostile towards Alex. Upon learning the truth about Alex's wealth and ownership of the villa, Sara kneels before Alex to beg for forgiveness. The family's attitude towards Alex changes completely, with Sara now encouraging her daughter Sue to pursue a relationship with him.

Sue, who had previously asked Alex to pretend to be her boyfriend, becomes uncomfortable with the situation. The family continues to praise Alex's wealth and push for a relationship between him and Sue. Rose appears hurt by the idea of Alex with her aunt.

As the misunderstanding grows, with the family believing Sue and Alex are a couple, Sue finally speaks up to clarify that she and Alex are not actually in a relationship, setting the stage for further complications.
