Alex confronts Luther Duncan and the Blood Brothers at the Shamrock Hotel. He challenges their leader, Tyson Slade, to a duel. Luther reluctantly calls Ryan Maxwell, a mediator, to arrange the challenge. Ryan informs Luther that Tyson is expected to return from seclusion soon.

Alex demands that Luther apologize on his knees and promise never to return to Baltimore. Initially, Luther resists but eventually kneels and begs for forgiveness. However, this is a ruse. Luther suddenly attacks, kicking one woman and shooting another. He orders his men to open fire on Alex.

When the gunfire stops, they discover Alex has vanished without a trace. The gang members are shocked, believing Alex's disappearance to be supernatural. Luther, humiliated and enraged, orders his men to find Alex, determined to make him suffer to maintain his respect within the gang.
