The episode continues the story after the Moon Maidens defeated the three Spanish boxers. The crowd praises their skills, and the defeated men are forced to apologize to Alex. To further teach them a lesson, the crowd makes them learn and sing an Ariana Grande song.

Another fight breaks out nearby involving five French basketball players harassing American female athletes. Two middle-aged men, later revealed to be martial arts experts Damian North and Rick Veasy, intervene. Damian easily defeats the basketball players, showcasing impressive skills.

Damian and Rick discuss attending Leona Marvel's wedding, hoping to secure work with the wealthy Marvel family. They mention Eagle Security's involvement in the wedding and decide to be careful not to offend the Marvels.

The Moon Maidens admire Damian and Rick's skills but believe they could defeat them easily. They also express their belief that Alex is even more powerful, though Alex denies this.

Later, Alex receives a call from Nelly, who informs him about the cremation of the Queen of Brunei. The Sultan wants to meet Alex before leaving and has invited him to a banquet for important D.C. families. Nelly is emotional about her friend's death, and Alex comforts her, reminding her that the Sultan promised to protect her.
