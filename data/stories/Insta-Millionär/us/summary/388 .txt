Meanwhile, outside Class 6, Billy and other teachers, including Lynne Bowen, gather to witness expected student unrest against Alex. Lynne, who has a past with Billy, ignores their warnings and enters the classroom. Billy expresses his disdain for Alex, questioning his qualifications and how he obtained the teaching position.
