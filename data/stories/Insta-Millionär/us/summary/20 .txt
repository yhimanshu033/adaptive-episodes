Alex meets with Karen and her cousin Zara at a coffee shop. Karen, now aware of Alex's wealth, tries to pursue a relationship with him, claiming she needs his help to avoid an arranged marriage. Alex agrees but insists on keeping his wealth a secret from Zara.

Zara, unaware of Alex's true financial status, continues to treat him condescendingly, believing him to be inferior to Karen. She sets strict rules for their supposed relationship, stating that Alex shouldn't touch Karen but that Karen is free to date and be intimate with other men.

Karen becomes increasingly frustrated with Zara's behavior, as it threatens her plans to get close to Alex. She tries to defend Alex subtly, but Zara misinterprets this as part of their original plan to trick him.

The situation escalates as Zara continues to belittle Alex and dictate the terms of his relationship with Karen. Finally, unable to contain her anger any longer, Karen slaps Zara in front of everyone in the coffee shop, causing a scene and leaving her embarrassed.

This episode highlights the growing tension between Karen's newfound interest in Alex due to his wealth and Zara's continued mistreatment of him, unaware of his true status. It also shows Alex navigating this complex situation while trying to maintain his secret.
