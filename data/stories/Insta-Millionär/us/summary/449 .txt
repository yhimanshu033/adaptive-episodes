Alex continues his high-stakes gambling at the roulette table on the cruise ship "Paradise". He repeatedly places large bets of $300,000, consistently losing each time. His behavior attracts a crowd of onlookers, some finding him courageous while others think he's being reckless. A concerned portly man tries to advise Alex to stop, but Alex remains calm and confident.

As Alex's losses mount, reaching nearly $5 million in less than an hour, the casino staff begin to take notice. They subtly manipulate the environment to keep Alex playing, moving other gamblers away. The croupier is observed touching something under the roulette wheel, suggesting possible foul play.

From a balcony, the casino boss watches Alex, considering him a foolish but ideal customer. The staff discuss offering Alex a loan when he runs out of money, seeing an opportunity to potentially take everything his family owns.

After losing all $5 million, Alex places his final bet. As he loses again, a beautiful woman approaches, asking if he needs more chips, setting the stage for the casino's plan to lend him money and potentially exploit him further.
