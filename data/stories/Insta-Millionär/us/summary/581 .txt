Alex Ambrose, now the owner of Birchwood Real Estate Company, encounters Ferdinand Thornton at the sales office. Ferdinand, unaware of Alex's position, mocks him for supposedly not being able to afford a property at Birchwood. The situation takes a turn when Cole Havers, an employee, arrives and addresses Alex as the boss, revealing his true status as the owner of the entire estate.

Ferdinand is shocked and disbelieving, even after being shown the property contract with Alex's name. Feeling humiliated, Ferdinand becomes angry. Alex, tired of Ferdinand's attitude, sarcastically tells him he can buy any property for $500 million.

The confrontation escalates when Ferdinand attempts to attack Alex. However, Alex is quicker and slaps Ferdinand across the face. Ferdinand retaliates, and a physical altercation begins between the two men.
