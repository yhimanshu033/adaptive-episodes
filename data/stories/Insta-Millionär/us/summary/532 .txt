Alex arrives in Philadelphia and is met with hostility from Alyssa and her friend Kurt. Kurt arranges for Alex to be stranded at the station and sends some men to intimidate him. However, before any confrontation occurs, Jesse Lambert arrives in a limousine to pick up Alex. The Lambert family is revealed to be very powerful in Philadelphia.

Jesse explains that Alex has been brought to examine Keira Lambert, who is in a coma after being exposed to an unknown gas during a construction site tour. The Lambert family has set up a private hospital at their estate to care for her and maintain security.

As they drive through the city, Jesse points out various landmarks and discusses the Lambert family's long history in Philadelphia. They pass an empty lot, which Jesse explains belongs to Keira. It was given to her as a child because she loved the wildflowers there. The lot is now extremely valuable, with offers up to a billion dollars from the Murdoch Group.

Alex becomes interested upon hearing about the Murdoch Group, revealing that he has met Steven Murdoch from Baltimore. This connection suggests potential intrigue or conflict involving the Murdochs and the valuable property owned by Keira Lambert.
