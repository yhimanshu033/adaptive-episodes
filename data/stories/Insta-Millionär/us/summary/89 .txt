During the "Dream Chasers" performance at "Sky's the Limit," Debbie struggles due to severe stomach pain. Melissa takes over, impressing the audience and judges. After the performance, Debbie collapses, and the group faces elimination. However, Lee Dempsey, a sponsor, allows them to pass to the next round because of Melissa's singing.

Debbie's condition worsens, and an ambulance is called. Some group members criticize her, while others defend her. Meanwhile, Lee's girlfriend Angelina asks Charlie to give her dog Duke some water. Melissa provides Charlie with Debbie's water bottle, which he uses to give Duke a drink.

Later, Duke becomes ill, and Lee confronts Charlie about what he gave the dog. Charlie explains he used water from a bottle belonging to one of the singers backstage. Lee demands to see the bottle, leading to a confrontation backstage.

Melissa quickly deflects blame onto Debbie, claiming Debbie gave her the water knowing it was for Duke. This creates a tense situation where Debbie, already ill, is now accused of potentially harming Lee's dog.
