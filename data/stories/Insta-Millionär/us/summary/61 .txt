Alex meets Debbie at the university cafeteria. Debbie is emotional, missing her mother who lives alone in a small town. Alex comforts her and promises to bring her mother to New York. Donna and Darren join them, curious about Alex's wealth. Alex lies about winning and spending a lottery jackpot to avoid suspicion.

Donna boasts about her mother visiting and offers to bring Debbie's mother along, using this as an opportunity to belittle Debbie. Debbie, desperate to see her mother, agrees to Donna's conditions. Alex offers to pay for the ticket, but Donna refuses, wanting to maintain her perceived superiority.

Darren and Donna challenge Alex to arrange accommodation for their mothers' visit, expecting him to fail. After they leave, Alex calls Sam Woodsworth to book rooms at the Berkeley Hotel. Sam suggests the more exclusive Azalea Guest House instead, indicating it's a high-end, difficult-to-book establishment.
