Alex arrives at the Flag Sect meeting with Sam Woodsworth to rescue Vivian and Monica. The situation quickly escalates as Leo, the sect leader, challenges Sam's authority. When Sam presents Dale Granger's head as a threat, Leo orders his members to attack. 

Despite Sam's efforts to protect Alex, they are overwhelmed by the large number of Flag Sect members. Sam is injured while shielding Alex from a thrown chair. Leo, intrigued by Sam's protective behavior towards Alex, stops the attack and questions Alex's identity.

Alex remains defiant, demanding Vivian and Monica's release and warning Leo of consequences. However, Leo, emboldened by his apparent victory over Sam, mocks Alex and orders him to act like a dog for amusement. The Flag Sect members join in ridiculing Alex and Sam, creating a tense and hostile atmosphere.
