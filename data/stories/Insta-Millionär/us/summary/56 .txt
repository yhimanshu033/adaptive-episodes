Alex meets with his ex-girlfriend Cathy at a bar. Cathy tries to win Alex back, claiming she never stopped loving him and never actually slept with Billy. She attempts to seduce Alex, even suggesting they go to the bathroom together. However, Alex sees through her lies and rejects her advances, stating he's with Debbie now.

Desperate, Cathy asks Alex to lend her $500,000, claiming Billy is threatening her. Alex realizes she's inflating the amount and refuses, saying he doesn't have the money. Cathy becomes angry and throws wine in Alex's face before storming out.

After Cathy leaves, Alex learns from a waiter that the bar, The Angel, is for sale due to losing protection from the now-defunct Azure Dragon Society. Alex considers buying it.

As Alex is about to re-enter the bar, he sees a beautiful woman arrive in a red Porsche. He's stunned by her beauty and elegance, describing her as the most beautiful woman he's ever seen. The woman, addressed as Lady Carter, enters the bar.
