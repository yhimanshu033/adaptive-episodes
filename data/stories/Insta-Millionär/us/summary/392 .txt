Alex, the new PE teacher, is challenged to a sports competition by Dylan, the school's best athlete. The first event is an 800-meter race. Dylan starts running immediately, while Alex waits at the starting line. When Alex finally begins, he quickly overtakes Dylan and finishes the race in an incredible 59 seconds, setting a new world record and shocking everyone present.

Next, they move on to the long jump. Dylan goes first and jumps 8.3 meters, breaking the world record. Confident in his victory, Dylan taunts Alex. However, Alex surprises everyone by claiming he can beat Dylan's jump using only one leg, with his hands behind his back, and without a running start.

To everyone's astonishment, Alex performs an incredible jump that takes him far beyond the long jump pit and lands him on the other side of the field. The students and staff are left in disbelief, with some wondering if Alex is even human. Dylan is completely stunned by Alex's superhuman abilities.

Throughout the competition, Alex maintains a calm and lighthearted demeanor, while the students and other observers, including Billy (the antagonistic teacher), are left in awe of his seemingly impossible feats.
