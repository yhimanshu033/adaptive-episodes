Alex visits the Robinson Winery to address a crisis involving leaked wine formulas. He proposes creating an ancient wellness wine called "Idun's Apple," which he claims is superior to their previous product. Alex spends the night in the lab creating a sample, which Maryann tries and finds amazing.

The next morning, Alex is called to the Woodside Clinic due to a patient's death. He arrives to find a chaotic scene with an angry mob accusing Sophie, a staff member, of murdering an elderly patient. Kendall is protecting Sophie with a knife.

Alex intervenes, using his martial arts skills to subdue several attackers and acupuncture needles to paralyze the main aggressor. He then addresses the crowd, taking responsibility for any potential wrongdoing at the clinic.

Sophie explains that the elderly patient was brought in after a fall, but his condition worsened rapidly. She had recommended going to the emergency room, but the family insisted on treatment at the clinic. The patient stopped breathing during examination, leading to the family's outrage.

The situation remains tense, with the deceased patient's son accusing Sophie of murder and cursing her. Alex must now handle this crisis at the clinic while also managing the new developments at the winery.
