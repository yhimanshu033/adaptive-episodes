In this episode, Rose plans to stage a fake robbery to make Alex confess his love for her. She explains her plan to Kelly and Sharon by the lake, unaware that Kelly is also interested in Alex. When Alex arrives to meet Kelly, Rose overhears their conversation and becomes confused about Alex's feelings.

Kelly, realizing her relationship with Alex is hopeless, asks him hypothetical questions about their relationship. Alex's responses make Kelly understand that she might have had a chance if circumstances were different. She accepts that she's second in Alex's heart after Debbie.

Rose, shocked by this revelation, tries to confront Alex. However, her staged robbery begins at that moment, with a tall man grabbing her. Alex, having been inadvertently warned about the deception by Kelly, sees through the act and leaves in disgust, refusing to play the hero in Rose's scheme.

The episode highlights the complex relationships and misunderstandings between the characters, particularly focusing on the unrequited feelings Kelly and Rose have for Alex, and Alex's unwavering devotion to Debbie.
