In this episode, Cathy and Minnie engage in a live-streaming duel for a $100,000 prize. Cathy, desperate for money, performs increasingly humiliating acts for viewer donations, including pouring water over herself and drinking a shaken bottle of cola. Initially, Cathy takes the lead, but Minnie turns the tables by singing a song that captivates her audience.

Minnie's fan club rallies behind her, sending numerous high-value gifts that quickly surpass Cathy's earnings. As the countdown nears its end, Minnie seems assured of victory. Cathy, looking defeated and on the verge of tears, appears to have lost.

However, in a dramatic twist during the final seconds, two viewers make substantial donations to Cathy's stream. One gifter, Adam Childs, sends gifts equivalent to $2,500, while another anonymous admirer matches this amount. These last-minute contributions push Cathy's progress bar past Minnie's just as the timer reaches zero, potentially securing an unexpected victory for Cathy in the duel.
