Alex, Kayla, and Alyssa are on a train to Philadelphia. Alex has warned Alyssa that she has a blood disease, which she and Kayla don't believe. This leads to tension and arguments between them. Upon arriving in Philadelphia, Alyssa demands an apology from Alex, which he refuses to give, insisting that Alyssa needs medical attention. Kayla is caught between her old friend Alyssa and her new acquaintance Alex, feeling confused about the situation. Alyssa, angered by Alex's refusal to apologize, plans to use her connections to teach him a lesson. Kayla warns Alex about Alyssa's influential background but he remains unconcerned. Alex receives a call from Jesse Lambert, who is sent to pick him up, demonstrating the reach of the Lambert family. As they wait for their respective rides, Kayla hopes Alex will stop his perceived game, while Alex silently hopes Alyssa will seek medical attention despite their disagreement.
