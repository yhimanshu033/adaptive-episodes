Alex, Debbie, and Carla arrive at the Sunset restaurant to join Darren, Donna, Lisa, Fiona, and Natalie. The restaurant is busy, and Lisa fails to secure a table by offering money. Darren then approaches the manager, Mr. Hood, who initially seems unimpressed until he notices Alex. Recognizing Alex as someone even Sam Woodsworth fears, Mr. Hood quickly arranges a luxurious private room for the group.

The room costs $8,000, but Mr. Hood offers it for free, along with complimentary meals. This impresses Lisa and the others, who attribute the special treatment to Darren's family influence. Darren, unaware of the real reason, believes his father's construction company is more prestigious than he thought.

The group discusses their backgrounds, with Darren mentioning his family's "small" $10 million annual income, while Alex downplays his own situation. Lisa and her daughters continue to look down on Alex, Debbie, and Carla.

During the meal, Debbie reveals she used to work at the restaurant, which Lisa mocks. Mr. Hood visits the room to check on them, focusing his attention on Alex but unable to speak directly to him due to Darren's interruptions. The manager offers expensive wine as a welcome gift, further impressing Darren and the others, who remain oblivious to Alex's true importance.
