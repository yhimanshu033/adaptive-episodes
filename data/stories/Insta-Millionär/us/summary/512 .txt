Alex Ambrose, having gained new powers, returns to confront his enemies. He learns that Todd Petrie has taken control of the Baltimore Martial Arts Association headquarters and is holding Debbie, Jessop, and Oscar captive. Alex warns Todd over the phone not to harm his friends, but Todd remains cocky and dismissive.

Alex and his team arrive at the headquarters, where they face multiple groups of guards and skilled fighters. Alex demonstrates his enhanced abilities by swiftly defeating numerous opponents, including masters, without slowing down. His friends, including Rufus, Will, Jeffrey, Dennis, and Edward, assist in the fight.

As they approach the banquet hall where Todd is waiting, Alex and his team continue to overcome waves of enemies. Alex's incredible speed and power are on full display as he effortlessly takes down anyone who stands in his way. The situation builds tension as Alex prepares to confront Todd and rescue his captive friends, showcasing his newfound strength and determination to reclaim his position and protect those he cares about.
