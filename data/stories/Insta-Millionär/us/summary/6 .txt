In this episode, a heated exchange occurs during Edith's livestream. Ghost Rider, a wealthy and boastful user, antagonizes Ben and others by sending multiple $50 gifts and mocking their financial status. He bans Ben and Carl from the chat, causing frustration.

Unexpectedly, a new user named "Esteemed Admirer" enters the stream and sends several $100 gifts, totaling $700. This surprises everyone, including Ghost Rider. Edith, overwhelmed by the generosity, makes Esteemed Admirer the sole manager of her stream at their request.

Esteemed Admirer then unbans Ben and bans Ghost Rider, reversing the situation. Ben and Carl celebrate this turn of events. Edith invites Esteemed Admirer to visit her at Preston University, but they decline.

The episode concludes with Esteemed Admirer sharing a message about respecting others regardless of wealth and not looking down on those less fortunate. This event highlights the complex dynamics of online interactions and the impact of perceived wealth and status in social media environments.
