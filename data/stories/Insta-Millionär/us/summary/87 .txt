Alex plans to surprise Debbie in Chicago but changes his mind when Joe invites him to celebrate his engagement to Suzan. Meanwhile, in Chicago, Melissa plots against Debbie at the "Sky's the Limit" talent show. She gives Debbie a water bottle laced with a strong drug that causes stomach pain. As the Dream Chasers prepare to perform, Debbie experiences severe stomach pain but insists on going on stage to avoid letting the team down. Janice, their manager, is concerned but can't make changes due to strict rules. The group is introduced, and they head to the stage with Debbie struggling to stand up straight due to the pain. Rita supports Debbie as they walk towards the stage, worried about her condition but unable to do anything to help.
