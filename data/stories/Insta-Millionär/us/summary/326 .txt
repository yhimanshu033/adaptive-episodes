Alex Ambrose arrives at Silver Lake and confronts Lee Harshaw, the leader of the Blood Brothers. The other martial artists are surprised to see Alex alive and recognize him as the one who defeated Damon Walker in Baltimore. Chris and Melissa, who thought Alex was dead, are shocked by his appearance.

Lee challenges Alex to a fight, seeking revenge for his apprentice Damon's death. The battle begins with Lee attacking Alex, but Alex demonstrates exceptional skill and power, easily evading Lee's strikes and landing powerful blows of his own. The other martial artists cheer for Alex as he gains the upper hand.

The fight moves into the lake, where Lee, known as the "sea shark" for his underwater skills, attempts to ambush Alex. However, Alex manages to counter Lee's attacks and continues to pummel him. Alex declares that Lee will never harm the martial arts community again and delivers a series of devastating blows, breaking Lee's ribs and jaw.

The fight concludes with Alex kicking Lee hard, sending him flying back into the water. Lee sinks beneath the surface and does not resurface, implying his defeat and possible death. Alex emerges victorious, having avenged the martial artists killed by Lee and protecting the community from further attacks by the Blood Brothers.
