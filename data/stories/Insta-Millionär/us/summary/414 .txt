Alex successfully rescues Heidi from the fire and helps extinguish it. He is praised by students and staff for his bravery, while Billy is criticized for his cowardice. Alex takes Heidi to his apartment for safety.

Meanwhile, Celia and Ethan are informed by an agency representative that they will be taking over Heidi's protection, dismissing Alex as an amateur despite his extraordinary skills. Celia and Ethan protest but are overruled.

The news of Alex's departure spreads through the school. Class 6 remains well-behaved, having gained respect for Alex after hearing about his heroic actions at the nightclub. A substitute teacher arrives to take over the class.

Alex leaves the school without saying goodbye, concerned about Heidi's safety but unable to do anything. Billy and Leonard see him off, with Billy gloating about becoming the new head teacher for Class 6.

Billy confidently enters Class 6 to announce his new position, expecting the same respect they gave Alex. However, the students are surprised and skeptical, with Heidi standing up to challenge Billy's claim that Alex was fired for violating school rules.
