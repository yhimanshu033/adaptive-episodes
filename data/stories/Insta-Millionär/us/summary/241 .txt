Alex and Debbie reunite at Arlington Heights, sharing an emotional embrace and kiss. Their reunion is interrupted by the arrival of Reginald and David Drake, who come to apologize for their deception involving Debbie's diary. Debbie, having regained her memories, thanks David for inadvertently helping her remember her past. She breaks off her engagement with David, choosing to be with Alex instead.

Alex reveals to the Drakes that he is no longer part of the Ambrose family. He and Debbie then leave together for West Lake, where they take a romantic walk. During their conversation, Alex expresses his guilt and sadness about the night Debbie was kidnapped by Lou Rork. Debbie reassures Alex that nothing inappropriate happened between her and Lou, much to Alex's relief and joy. The episode ends with Alex expressing his love for Debbie and the couple embracing.
