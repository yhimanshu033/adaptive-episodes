In this episode, tensions rise as Alex, Debbie, Cathy, and Billy encounter each other at a high-end boutique. Cathy, Alex's ex-girlfriend, tries to cause trouble by implying Alex is cheating on her with Debbie. However, it's revealed that Cathy is actually with Billy now.

Cathy attempts to humiliate Alex and Debbie by flaunting her new relationship and criticizing Debbie's appearance. Alex helps Debbie choose a beautiful dress, which transforms her appearance and makes Cathy jealous.

As Alex and Debbie prepare to leave, Cathy challenges Alex to a shopping competition to see who can buy more expensive clothes. Despite Debbie's concerns about the cost, Alex agrees to the challenge.

Unbeknownst to Cathy and Billy, Alex actually owns the boutique. He finds their challenge amusing, as they will essentially be competing to spend more money in his own store. Meanwhile, Cathy boasts about her boyfriend's wealth, unaware that Alex is far wealthier than she realizes.

The episode ends with Billy reluctantly agreeing to buy an expensive skirt for Cathy, setting the stage for the shopping competition to begin.
