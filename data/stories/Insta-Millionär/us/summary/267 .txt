In this episode, an elderly woman named Morag Sparks, working for Chris Steadman, enters Saul's hospital room disguised as a cleaner. She injects Saul with a rare, undetectable snake venom, killing him. The police guarding Saul are unaware of the assassination.

Later, a nurse discovers Saul's body, and Officer Chelsea Wood is informed. She suspects Chris Steadman's involvement and orders an investigation. However, Chelsea is sent to another precinct for an unrelated case, which delays her return to the hospital.

When Chelsea finally arrives at the hospital, she finds Saul's room empty. Upon checking the morgue, she learns that Saul's body has been claimed and removed, despite being key evidence in a homicide case. The morgue attendant claims the proper paperwork was provided.

Chelsea confronts the officers who were supposed to guard Saul's body. They explain that they oversaw the body's transfer to the morgue and then left to check security tapes, believing the body would be safe there.

The episode ends with Chelsea frustrated by the loss of crucial evidence against Chris Steadman and the apparent incompetence of her fellow officers.
