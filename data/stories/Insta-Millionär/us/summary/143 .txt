Alex experiences conflict and tension at the villa he rented to Sheldon and Jade. Rose, Sue's niece, mocks Alex in front of everyone, causing him to feel hurt and angry. The situation escalates when Sara, Sue's mother, continues to berate Alex.

Unexpectedly, Georgina (referred to as "grandma") emerges from her room, alarming Alex. She displays supernatural abilities, causing a vase to fly and throwing blades at Garry, leaving him with tattered clothes and minor cuts. Georgina slaps Sara twice before leaving the villa, warning Alex that she'll return in two hours and won't care about consequences if the group is still there.

Sara blames Alex for bringing Georgina and demands that he leave. Despite Sheldon and Jade's attempts to calm the situation, Sara remains hostile. Alex decides to leave to avoid further conflict, promising to return in two hours. As he's about to exit, a voice from the second floor (presumably Rose) apologetically states that this is Alex's home and questions why he's leaving.

The episode highlights the growing tensions between Alex and Sue's family, particularly Sara and Rose, while also showcasing Georgina's mysterious and dangerous nature.
