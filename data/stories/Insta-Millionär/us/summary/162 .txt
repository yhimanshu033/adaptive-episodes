Alex encounters Myriam and her boyfriend Jake at a luxury hotel in Washington D.C. Myriam, a former classmate, treats Alex with contempt, assuming he's still poor. Alex attempts to book an expensive room to prove them wrong, but his Metro Sky bank card is declined due to his family freezing his assets. This is because they disapprove of Alex using family resources to search for Debbie.

Ken Stokes calls Alex to explain the situation, offering limited resources if Alex returns to New York. However, Alex refuses, determined to continue searching for Debbie on his own. Unable to pay for the room, Alex is forced to leave the hotel, enduring mockery from Myriam, Jake, and the hotel staff.

Despite this setback, Alex remains optimistic about finding Debbie. As he leaves the hotel, a woman approaches him offering alternative accommodation.
