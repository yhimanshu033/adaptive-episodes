In this episode, Melissa hatches a plan to separate Alex and Debbie and win Alex for herself. She contacts Lou Rork, a known womanizer, and arranges for him to pick up Debbie at the airport under the guise of being Alex's friend. Melissa manipulates Debbie's phone, replacing Alex's number with her own, and sends fake messages to Debbie about Lou picking her up.

Debbie, unaware of the deception, writes in her diary about recent events and her feelings for Alex. At the airport, Melissa ensures that Debbie leaves with Lou, who plans to drug and assault her. After Debbie departs, Melissa stays behind to execute the next part of her plan.

Melissa then calls Alex, pretending to be stranded at the airport with heavy luggage. She lies about Debbie still being in Chicago and asks Alex for help. Despite finding the request strange, Alex agrees to come to the airport to assist Melissa.

The episode highlights the complex web of deception Melissa has woven, putting Debbie in danger and manipulating Alex, all in an attempt to get closer to him and potentially become pregnant with his child.
