Alex, now the new Lord of the Moon cult, arranges Georgina's funeral and finds himself responsible for four young Moon Maidens. The girls insist on staying with him and reveal they have access to millions of dollars. Alex decides to help them buy a villa near Richmond University.

At the "Emerald Hills" luxury gated community, Alex and the girls face discrimination from the sales staff who judge them based on Alex's poor appearance. The manager and saleswomen mock Alex and accuse him of trying to trick the girls.

Alex calls the general manager, Mr. Hutchings, to complain about the unprofessional treatment. When Alex offers to buy a $5 million villa with cash, the staff remains skeptical until they verify the funds on his card.

Shocked by their mistake, the sales team frantically apologizes to Alex. Mr. Hutchings, furious about the situation, decides to fire the entire sales staff. Alex completes the purchase and arranges to move in immediately.

The episode ends with the disgraced sales team blaming each other for their poor judgment and loss of jobs, highlighting the consequences of their discriminatory behavior.
