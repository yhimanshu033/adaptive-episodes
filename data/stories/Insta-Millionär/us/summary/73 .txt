The episode focuses on Debbie's introduction to her new girl group, the Dream Chasers, at Sunshine Music. She meets the other four members: Melissa Kennedy, Katie Jackson, Olivia Taylor, and Rita Lopez. While Rita is friendly, the other three girls are dismissive of Debbie due to her background and appearance.

During a singing practice, Debbie impresses everyone with her natural talent and ability to emotionally connect with the song, despite her lack of formal training. This performance catches the attention of Janice, the manager, and the music teacher, who both praise Debbie's potential.

Melissa, who had previously considered herself the best singer and potential lead of the group, becomes jealous and threatened by Debbie's talent. She sees Debbie as competition for the spotlight and leadership role in the group.

After Janice and the music teacher leave, Melissa, Katie, and Olivia gather to express their dissatisfaction with Debbie. They plan to confront her to "show her who's in charge." Rita warns them against acting rashly, but Melissa dismisses her concern.

The episode ends with Melissa and her allies approaching Debbie, setting up a potential confrontation within the group.
