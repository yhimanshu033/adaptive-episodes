The episode continues the tense confrontation at the Tinsdale Hotel. Commissioner Anthony Billings recognizes Zora as the missing Queen of Brunei, complicating the situation. David Drake, realizing his mistake, attempts to apologize, but Zora demands punishment. She orders Alex to whip David and his men, which he does, causing humiliation but no serious harm.

Leona shows concern for David, confusing Alex. Zora agrees to return with the police, taking Alex with her. They go to the West Lake guesthouse where the Sultan of Brunei greets Zora enthusiastically, though she seems indifferent.

In private, Zora reveals her past to Alex. She explains that her heart was broken over 20 years ago by a man named Xavier Myers. She tried to make him jealous by being with other men, including the Prince of Brunei, whom she eventually married. Zora connects her desire to kill Georgina with her past heartbreak.

The episode ends with Zora informing Alex that she has left clues for Georgina to find them, and urges him to practice the skills she's taught him in preparation for the impending confrontation.
