Alex follows Felicity home and discovers she lives in a poor neighborhood, caring for her blind and sick mother. He realizes Felicity's mother is suffering from chronic poisoning rather than a disease. Using his supernatural powers, Alex demonstrates his abilities to Felicity and convinces her to let him help. He heals Felicity's mother, causing her to expel a strange blue liquid that resembles slow-acting snake venom. Alex learns that Felicity's parents are divorced, and her father provides insufficient money for her mother's medical treatment, which led Felicity to steal from other students. Alex advises Felicity to stop stealing and do things the right way. He finds the situation suspicious but cannot do anything more at the moment.
