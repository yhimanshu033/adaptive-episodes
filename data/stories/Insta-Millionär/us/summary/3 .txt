After class, Billy, a wealthy student, enters and publicly displays affection with Cathy, Alex's ex-girlfriend. Cathy returns the phone Alex had bought her, flaunting her new, more expensive iPhone from Billy. Billy threatens Alex to stay away from Cathy.

Emma, another student, confronts Cathy about her treatment of Alex, reminding her of his past kindness and sacrifices. Cathy dismisses these memories, stating that she values money over anything else. She leaves with Billy, expressing disgust towards Alex and Emma, whom she considers "poor people."

This episode highlights the stark contrast between Alex's new financial status and his current social standing at the university, setting the stage for potential future conflicts and revelations.
