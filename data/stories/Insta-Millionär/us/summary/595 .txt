In this episode, Alex remains in custody for Michael Murdoch's alleged murder. Detective Timothy is forced to step away from the case due to his brother Brian's intervention. Alex decides to stay in prison to uncover his enemies' plots.

Meanwhile, Riley and Louisa discuss the situation while horseback riding. They realize that Brian's involvement in the case might hinder their plans against Alex. Riley reveals he wants to ruin Timothy as well and explains how Brian's influence could work against them.

Louisa expresses her desire to see Alex dealt with permanently. Riley then discusses his plan involving Lindsey Marvel, who has agreed to commit perjury to help Alex. Riley has set a trap for Lindsey, taking advantage of her feelings for Alex and her willingness to break the law to save him.

The conversation reveals that Riley has genuine feelings for Lindsey, comparing her to an elusive rabbit he's trying to catch. Louisa realizes Riley's interest in Lindsey goes beyond using her as a pawn in their schemes against Alex.
