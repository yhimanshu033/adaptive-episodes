The episode continues the dramatic events at the Baltimore Martial Arts Association gathering. Alex's unexpected appointment as president shocks his opponents, including Caroline Hall, Desmond Lang, and the Thornton brothers. Caroline tries to console her son Joe, suggesting that Alex's position is dangerous and that Joe's football career will surpass Alex's success. Desmond Lang supports this view, promising to use his wealth and connections to make Joe a star.

However, the situation takes another unexpected turn when police officers arrive to arrest Desmond Lang on suspicion of fraud and money laundering. Alex reveals evidence of Lang's criminal activities, including a confession from Heather Lennox, a professional scammer working for Lang. The evidence includes fake invoices, illegally sourced jade, and records of money laundering.

This development further cements Alex's power and influence while exposing the corrupt practices of his opponents. The episode ends with Alex confronting Lang about the evidence, leaving Lang and his supporters in a state of shock and disarray.
