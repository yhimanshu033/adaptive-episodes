Alex confronts Michael Murdoch at a nightclub owned by Lindsey Marvel, an old acquaintance from Washington D.C. Alex threatens to drop Michael from a window, causing panic among the guests. Lindsey tries to intervene and calm the situation, pleading with Alex not to do anything rash.

As Alex is momentarily distracted by Lindsey, Michael takes the opportunity to push himself out of the window, falling to his death. However, due to Alex's position, the witnesses believe Alex threw Michael out.

The police arrive and arrest Alex. The next morning, Sophie and Kendall rush to the police station to help Alex. They encounter Lindsey and David Drake, who are discussing the incident. David believes Alex is guilty, while Kendall defends Alex, insisting he wouldn't murder someone in front of witnesses and suggesting that Michael was responsible for hurting people.

The situation has become more complex with Lindsey's unexpected appearance and Michael's apparent suicide, leaving Alex in a difficult legal position.
