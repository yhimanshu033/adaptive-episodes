Alex receives a call from Grandma Spice, a blind elderly woman from the Moon Palace who had mentored him. She asks Alex to visit Philadelphia to check on her ill relative, Keira Lambert. Alex agrees to go the next day.

After the call, Alex stops at a diner for dinner. As he's leaving, he hears a woman's cry for help from an alley. He investigates and finds five men attempting to abduct a young woman into a van. Despite their threats, Alex intervenes.

Using his martial arts skills, Alex quickly overpowers four of the attackers. The fifth man, still holding the woman, tries to use her as a shield. Alex easily defeats him as well, rescuing the woman from her would-be abductors.
