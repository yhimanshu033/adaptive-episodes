Alex Ambrose, after winning a fight against the Blood Brothers, returns to university with Debbie and the Moon girls. Meanwhile, in Washington D.C., Reginald Drake introduces his son David to the family business, the Fidelity Group. During a meeting with department heads, Reginald explains the company's vast operations and impressive financial performance.

However, the meeting is suddenly disrupted by a series of catastrophic reports from various subsidiaries. The Fidelity Group faces multiple crises simultaneously: canceled contracts, government investigations, failed health inspections, drug allegations, and contract terminations. These issues threaten to cause significant financial damage and reputational harm to the company.

Reginald is bewildered by this sudden turn of events and suspects a deliberate attack on his family's interests. He instructs his managers to address the problems and keep him informed. As the executives leave, one of them, Noah Freeman, is revealed to be working against the Drakes. Noah, feeling resentful of Reginald's success, has allied himself with the Patterson family.

The episode ends with Noah visiting the Pattersons' villa, where he encounters an unexpected and seemingly compromising situation involving Colin Patterson.
