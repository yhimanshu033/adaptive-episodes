Alex and Lindsey are confronted by a mysterious woman in white who displays extraordinary martial arts skills. After saving them from Fergus and his men at the restaurant, she insists that Alex accompany her. Despite Lindsey's protests, Alex agrees to go with the woman to avoid further conflict.

The woman reveals she's in the U.S. to kill someone and needs Alex's assistance as she's unfamiliar with the area. They go to the Tinsdale Hotel, where the woman is staying. Hotel staff are curious about her, as she keeps her identity hidden.

Alex is given a new room key for the woman. When he enters her room to deliver it, he finds her naked and practicing some kind of skill. Surprised, he uses a technique taught by Grandma Georgina to close the curtains around her bed.

The woman, recognizing this technique, attacks Alex and demands to know his connection to Grandma Georgina. She demonstrates supernatural abilities, similar to those Alex has seen before. The episode ends with her interrogating Alex about who taught him the blade technique, revealing a potential connection between the mysterious woman and Alex's past experiences.
