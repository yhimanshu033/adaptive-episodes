Alex returns to Clark's compound with newfound strength and power after his intense training. He finds his friends held hostage by Ava and her men from the Schiedsrichterbüro. Ava threatens to kill Dennis and the others if Alex doesn't comply with her demands. However, Alex demonstrates his enhanced abilities by swiftly killing one of Ava's men and then using his powers to shatter the floor, sending deadly shards that kill most of her team. He then incapacitates Ava by throwing a piece of tile at her forehead. In the ensuing chaos, Alex's friends fight back and defeat the remaining enemies. The confrontation ends with only Ava and two of her men left alive, while Alex stands victorious, ready to face whatever challenges come next.
