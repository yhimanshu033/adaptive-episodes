Alex meets Suzan and Rose at the Angel Bar, unaware that they suspect him of being their mysterious rescuer. The girls attempt to uncover Alex's true identity by asking him questions about wealth, business, and his background. Alex, sensing their intentions, maintains his cover story of being from a humble farming family.

The conversation turns to the woven bracelets from the night market incident. Suzan and Rose believe these bracelets could confirm Alex's identity as their savior. However, Alex realizes their plan and cleverly avoids revealing any connection to the Maserati where the bracelets were found after their rescue.

Throughout the encounter, Rose struggles with her growing attraction to Alex, while still uncertain about his true identity. Alex remains cautious and continues to conceal his wealth and power from the girls, leaving them confused and frustrated in their attempts to uncover his secret.

The episode highlights the complex dynamics between Alex, Suzan, and Rose, as well as Alex's ability to maintain his cover despite increasingly probing questions.
