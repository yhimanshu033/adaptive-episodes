Alex Ambrose, despite being severely injured by Harvey Kingston, manages to survive and heal himself using a mysterious elixir. Kingston, shocked by Alex's recovery, is then stabbed with his own knife by Alex, who leaves him to die slowly from his own poison.

Alex receives a phone call from an unknown man who reveals that Rose Scott is being held at Belt's Wharf. However, before Alex can act on this information, he is unexpectedly stabbed in the back by Neil Hall, who reveals himself to be a member of the Ghosts.

Neil explains that Alex shouldn't have come to Baltimore or crossed the Ghosts. He admits that while his son Joe isn't yet a Ghost, he plans to bring him into the organization when the time is right. Alex, severely wounded and losing blood, realizes he has become careless and expresses disbelief at the situation.

The episode ends with Alex critically injured, betrayed by someone he thought was an ally, and facing an increasingly complex and dangerous situation involving the Ghosts.
