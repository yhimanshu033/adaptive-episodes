Alex and Debbie are enjoying a romantic New Year's Eve celebration at the Moon Palace when they are interrupted by Rocky Dalton, a former rival gang leader. Rocky informs them that the Blood Brothers, led by Lee Harshaw, have declared war on the martial arts community in Baltimore. Lee has already killed several prominent martial arts leaders and has called for a meeting at Silver Lake.

Realizing the gravity of the situation and feeling responsible for the conflict due to his previous defeat of Damon, Alex decides to return to Baltimore immediately. He announces his decision to the Moon cult disciples, who initially resist but eventually support him. The four Moon Maidens all want to accompany Alex for his protection, but he insists that only one can come while the others must stay to manage the Moon Palace.

After a quick game of rock-paper-scissors, Callisto is chosen to accompany Alex and Debbie on their journey back to Baltimore to confront the Blood Brothers and attempt to stop the escalating violence in the martial arts community.
