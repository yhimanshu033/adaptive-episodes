Alex encounters David Drake, the head of the most influential family in Washington D.C., who intimidates Derek Jackson and his men. David's presence reveals his powerful status and his connection to Lindsey as her fiancé. Alex and Lindsey share a tender moment in the taxi, with Lindsey expressing her desire to be like Debbie.

The next day, Alex plans to show Debbie's diary to Leona to resolve their misunderstanding. He receives an unexpected call from Myriam, his former high school monitor, who apologizes for her past behavior and invites him to lunch.

At the restaurant, Myriam acts kindly towards Alex, reminiscent of their high school days. She reminds him of how he used to help her with her muscular atrophy by rubbing her hands. Myriam asks Alex to rub her hand again, claiming she feels a twitch. As Alex complies, feeling somewhat uncomfortable due to their current relationship as adults, they are interrupted by a man's angry voice questioning Alex's actions.
