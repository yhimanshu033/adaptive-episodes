The episode describes a violent confrontation between Lee Harshaw of the Blood Brothers and the disciples of the Black Swallow Martial Arts Academy. Lee easily defeats multiple opponents, showcasing his superior defensive skills. He then faces off against Master Zachariah Holbrook in an intense duel. Despite Zachariah's advanced techniques, Lee ultimately prevails, killing Zachariah and beheading him.

Lee then embarks on a revenge mission across Pennsylvania, defeating other martial arts masters and collecting tokens of his victories. He sends out a message calling for a meeting of all martial arts groups at Silver Lake in Baltimore, challenging anyone seeking revenge to confront him there.

The news of Lee's actions spreads rapidly throughout the East Coast, causing uproar and debate within the martial arts community. Some want to seek revenge, while others are curious about Lee's message.

Meanwhile, Jessop receives news of these events but chooses not to inform Alex and Debbie, who are still in the Appalachians for Debbie's recovery. Jessop fears that the information might cause Debbie to worry and return to the city prematurely, potentially endangering her health.
