Alex successfully secures a job as a security guard at Johns Hopkins University after Rufus Clifton intervenes and pressures Tony Lewis, the head of campus security, to hire him. Tony, fearing the Clifton family's influence, desperately tries to convince Alex to take the job, even offering a higher salary. Alex accepts the position but insists on standard pay and duties. He also manages to get Don, who was previously fired, rehired as a security guard.

As Alex and Don tour the campus, Don becomes curious about Alex's sudden influence over Tony. Alex deflects suspicion by claiming Tony feared a potential lawsuit for unfair rejection. They reach the residential building where Debbie is staying, and Alex briefly interacts with her while maintaining their cover. 

Alex expresses interest in working near the residential area to keep an eye on Debbie. Throughout the episode, Alex navigates his new role while balancing his secret relationship with Debbie and his mission to protect her at the university.
