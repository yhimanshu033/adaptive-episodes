Alex, Kelly, and Sharon arrive at a luxurious party in the Green Island Garden residential area. The girls are impressed by the beautiful surroundings, especially a $40 million villa on an artificial island. At the party, Alex's casual attire draws criticism and mockery from other guests. Sharon adds to the ridicule by sarcastically claiming Alex owns the expensive villa and a custom Ferrari.

Kelly defends Alex, declaring him as her boyfriend to the shock of everyone present, including Sharon. The other guests refuse to believe it, suggesting various reasons why Kelly might pretend to date someone like Alex. To prove her point, Kelly unexpectedly kisses Alex on the lips. The kiss catches Alex off guard, and he's conflicted about the situation, feeling guilty about Debbie. The episode ends with Alex pushing Kelly away, confused and remorseful about what just happened.
