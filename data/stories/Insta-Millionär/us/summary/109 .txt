Alex returns home after leaving the Phillips family gathering. The next day, Karen Young unexpectedly shows up at his villa. She apologizes for drugging him in the past, explaining that she did it to discourage James from pursuing her. Alex forgives her, understanding her intentions were not malicious.

Karen asks Alex to continue pretending to be her boyfriend to deter James. Alex reluctantly agrees. Impressed by Alex's Ferrari, Karen asks to sit in it. Alex then drives her to Wanda Plaza for shopping, buying her several outfits.

After shopping, they go to a restaurant for lunch. Upon entering, they encounter Karen's parents, Zara, and James, who are surprised to see Karen out when she had called in sick to work. The situation becomes tense as Karen's deception is exposed.
