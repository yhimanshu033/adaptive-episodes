During a reception for Alex Ambrose's new role as president of the Baltimore Martial Arts Association, five intruders burst in, led by Marie Walton and her brother Drew. They identify themselves as disciples of Jacob Randolph, leader of the Ghosts, seeking revenge for their brother Jonah's death. The Ghosts easily overpower the security and martial artists present, demonstrating their superior fighting skills.

Marie and Drew demand that Alex come with them to California to face their master's judgment, threatening to kill everyone present if he refuses. Alex remains calm and defiant, refusing to go with them. The situation escalates when Marie attempts to attack Alex with a dagger, but he swiftly counters, striking her in the face and disarming her.

The episode highlights the tension between the Ghosts and the Baltimore Martial Arts Association, as well as Alex's confidence and ability to handle the threat. Some guests, including Ferdinand and Caroline, watch eagerly, hoping to see Alex fail. However, Alex's quick action against Marie demonstrates his strength and resolve, surprising both his allies and enemies.
