Alex finally decides to intervene in the tense situation at the riding stable. After the students fail to raise the billion-dollar ransom demanded by Micky and the Baltimore Horse Gang, Alex steps forward to negotiate. His calm demeanor surprises and confuses both the students and the gang members.

When Davey attempts to suggest an installment plan, Micky slaps him and rejects the idea. This interrupts Alex's attempt to help, forcing him to wait for another opportunity.

Micky then changes tactics, threatening to harm the students physically if they can't pay. He grabs Heidi's arm and makes lewd comments about her and Debbie, suggesting he might prefer this form of "payment" instead.

This action finally prompts Alex to get involved. Heidi, despite her fighting skills, is flustered by Micky's crude behavior and struggles to defend herself. The situation becomes increasingly tense as Micky continues to taunt and threaten the students, particularly the girls.
