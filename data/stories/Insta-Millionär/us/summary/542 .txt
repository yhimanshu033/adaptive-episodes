Alex arrives at Hell World, an amusement park, searching for his missing wife Debbie. He enters a building representing Dante's nine circles of Hell, methodically checking each floor. On the eighth level, he notices the ninth floor is closed to tourists.

Suddenly, a commotion breaks out as a young woman experiences medical distress. Paramedics arrive with Dr. Troy Wilkinson, a renowned physician. The woman is identified as Maryann Robinson, from a prominent family.

Dr. Wilkinson diagnoses acute myocardial ischemia and prepares to administer aspirin. Alex, recognizing signs of asthma, intervenes to warn against using aspirin, which could trigger a severe asthma attack. 

Dr. Wilkinson dismisses Alex's concerns, assuming he's an impostor trying to impress Maryann. He threatens to have Alex arrested if he doesn't step aside. The situation becomes tense as Alex tries to prevent a potentially fatal medical error.
