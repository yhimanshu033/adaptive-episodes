In this episode, the fight between Alex and Tabitha reaches its climax. Tabitha's sword breaks against Alex's blade, and she attempts to escape. Alex throws a knife, injuring her shoulder. Tabitha flees but encounters two students outside. She takes one hostage, threatening to kill him if Alex doesn't let her go.

Alex remains calm and approaches, warning Tabitha that he will end her career if she doesn't release the student. Tabitha doesn't believe him and maintains her threat. Alex suddenly rushes forward, striking both her arms and causing nerve damage. This renders Tabitha unable to control her arms properly, effectively ending her career as an assassin.

The students escape unharmed. Tabitha, realizing the severity of her injury and the implications for her future, breaks down in tears. She questions why Alex didn't just kill her, as her career was everything to her. Alex waits silently, having warned her of the consequences of her actions.
