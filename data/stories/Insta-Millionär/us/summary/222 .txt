Alex arrives at the Continental hotel for a banquet hosted by the Sultan of Brunei, but is initially denied entry due to not having an invitation letter. After a confrontation with a waiter and another guest, Alex calls Nelly, who arranges for his entry. The hotel manager apologizes and almost fires the waiter, but Alex intervenes.

Inside the banquet hall, Alex observes Colin Patterson, Jason Morley, and other wealthy guests discussing the Sultan's potential $5 billion investment in D.C. Gordon Parker recognizes Alex, drawing attention to his presence. Colin and Jason question how Alex got invited and reveal his background as a pancake seller to the other guests, portraying him as an opportunist trying to get money from rich people.

Jason also brings up the incident with the Ferrari at the Olympic Games, accusing Alex of being a fraudster. The wealthy guests mock Alex and treat him with contempt, unaware of his true financial status and connection to the Sultan.
