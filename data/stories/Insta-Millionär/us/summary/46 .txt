Alex confronts Maddison for killing Debbie, dragging her into the lake and repeatedly dunking her head underwater. As he's about to kill her, several luxury cars arrive carrying members of the Azure Dragon Society, a powerful criminal organization. Their leader, Quentin Robbins, is revealed to be Maddison's uncle.

Quentin's men attack Alex, forcing him to release Maddison. Despite being outnumbered, Alex fights back effectively. Still determined to kill Maddison, he approaches her and Quentin. Quentin orders his men to shoot Alex, wounding him in the arm and leg. Despite his injuries, Alex continues to crawl towards Maddison, driven by his desire for revenge.

The situation escalates as Maddison takes a gun and aims it at Alex's head, threatening to kill him. The episode ends with Maddison about to pull the trigger, leaving Alex's fate uncertain.
