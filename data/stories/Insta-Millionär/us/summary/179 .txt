Alex prepares to leave Nelly's place and start at Richmond University. Nelly apologizes for her previous behavior and brings Alex pancakes as a farewell gesture. They discuss Nelly's past love interest, with Alex encouraging her to pursue him. The next day, Nelly gives Alex his wages, which he reluctantly accepts.

At the university, Alex meets Phillipa, a former classmate who is also studying there. They encounter Colin Patterson, a wealthy and popular student surrounded by admiring girls. Alex and Phillipa realize Colin's car and driver were waiting for him, demonstrating his privileged status.

As they walk to the dormitory, Phillipa asks Alex about his connection to Ken Stokes and the woman who helped him at the Ruby Hotel. Alex fabricates a story about their help being a result of them enjoying his pancakes, maintaining his cover as a poor student. Phillipa seems disappointed that Alex doesn't have ongoing contact with them, as she had hoped to use the connection for her own benefit.
