Alex continues his pursuit of Leona, whom he believes to be Debbie. At a restaurant, Leona thanks Alex for the corn but firmly rejects his belief that she is Debbie. She also ends her date with Darryl Brennan, leaving both men disappointed. Alex faces ridicule from former classmates Myriam and Jake about his apparent poverty and failed attempt to woo Leona.

For the next twenty days, Alex waits outside Arlington Heights to see Leona. Lindsey, Leona's sister, advises Alex to stop waiting as Leona is not Debbie and will be starting school at Richmond University soon.

Alex continues working at Nelly's food truck. At a stadium, he encounters former classmates Phillipa and Myriam. They mock him for his job selling pancakes. Nelly defends Alex by deliberately burning their order and refusing to make new pancakes. The encounter ends with Myriam and Phillipa leaving without food, while Alex remains determined in his belief about Leona's true identity.
