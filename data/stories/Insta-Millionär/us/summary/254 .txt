Alex, working as a security guard at Johns Hopkins University, gets into a confrontation with Julian Duvant, a wealthy student, and knocks him down. Julian, humiliated, seeks revenge with his cousin Chris Steadman, who comes from a powerful family. Chris investigates Alex and Don's backgrounds but doesn't connect Alex to the wealthy Ambrose family.

Chris hires Saul, a notorious underground boxer and enforcer, to "teach a lesson" to Alex and Don. Meanwhile, Alex continues his duties at the university, keeping the incident from Debbie. Don takes leave out of fear of retaliation.

Julian and Chris plan their evening, anticipating news of Saul's success in beating up Alex and Don. Saul, considering the job easy, heads to the university but learns that Don has already left for home. He decides to deal with Don first at his residence on Fenshaw Road, planning to handle Alex later at the university.

The situation is escalating, with Alex unaware of the danger coming his way, while Saul, a formidable fighter, prepares to carry out his violent mission against the two security guards.
