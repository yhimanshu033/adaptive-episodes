Alex receives a distress text from Eric about an emergency at the Night Banquet club. He rushes there, informing Rufus Clifton about the situation. Rufus contacts Art Steadman, who decides to go to the club personally to handle the matter and potentially gain Alex's favor.

At the club, Eric and three girls from his class, including Stacy, are in danger. Devon Boardman, the club manager working for the Steadman family, has violent intentions towards them. Eric is beaten and his phone is destroyed when he tries to call for help.

Art Steadman, still in his pajamas and robe, hurries to the club with his nephew Julian, sensing the urgency of the situation. He orders Julian to surround the club with men.

Alex arrives at the club first, fights off two security guards, and breaks into the private room where Eric and the girls are being held. He finds Eric badly beaten and the girls in distress, with their clothes torn. Alex's timely arrival prevents further harm to the students.

The situation is tense, with Alex confronting Devon and his men to rescue the students, while Art Steadman is on his way to intervene in what he perceives as a potentially significant incident for his family's future.
