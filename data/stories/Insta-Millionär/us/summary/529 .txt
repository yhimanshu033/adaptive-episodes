Alex saves a young woman named Kayla from attackers in an alley. After ensuring her safety, they exchange numbers. Kayla's friend Alyssa arrives to pick her up and is initially suspicious of Alex, but both women are surprised when they see him drive away in an expensive sports car.

The next day, Alex is on a train to Philadelphia when he overhears Kayla having an angry phone conversation. She notices Alex and sits next to him. Suddenly, Kayla kisses Alex and takes a photo of them together, catching him by surprise. Alex protests, confused by her actions.
