The situation at Oak Ridge High School has escalated dramatically. Students from Class 6, led by Eric and Lance, have started a fire in the schoolyard, burning books and exam papers. They've encouraged other classes to join their rebellion, causing chaos throughout the school.

Billy Wilde, who had taken over as head teacher of Class 6 after Alex's departure, is completely ineffective in controlling the situation. He's too afraid to confront the students, who openly mock and threaten him. The principal and other teachers are furious with Billy's incompetence and inability to handle the class.

The students are now planning to burn the school library, and no one seems able to stop them. The principal realizes he might lose his job over this incident. The police are unlikely to respond due to past experiences with the school.

When the principal orders Billy to take action, he finally attempts to intervene, but Lance threatens him, causing Billy to back down immediately. This further cements Billy's reputation as weak and incompetent, with students openly comparing him unfavorably to Alex Ambrose.

The episode ends with Lance leading Class 6 students towards the library, intent on burning its contents, while Billy faces intense humiliation and scorn from both students and staff.
