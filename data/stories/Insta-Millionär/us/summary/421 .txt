Alex Ambrose remains calm at the command post while gunfire erupts inside the factory where Heidi is being held hostage. Ash Thornton, a renowned martial artist, had entered the building alone but emerges severely wounded and defeated. This shocking turn of events leaves Director Gavin Abbott stunned and worried about his career.

Ash reports that the mercenaries are heavily fortified and well-prepared, making the rescue mission seem impossible. With only ten minutes left until the deadline, the situation becomes desperate. Celia receives a call indicating that Heidi's father might make a deal with the mercenaries, which their superiors cannot allow.

The pressure mounts on Alex to intervene, as he's now seen as the last hope. Gavin, panicking, suggests blowing up the building, which Celia strongly opposes. The team is left scrambling for a new plan to save the hostages before time runs out.
