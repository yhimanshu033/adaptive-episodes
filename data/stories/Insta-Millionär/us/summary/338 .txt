In this episode, Alex faces off against four powerful priests from the Black Orchid Cult who have attacked the Clifton family during a banquet. Despite their superior fighting skills and magical weapons, Alex demonstrates extraordinary abilities, defeating Zeke and breaking his supposedly unbreakable Crescent sword. Alex then engages in intense combat with the other three priests, managing to injure or incapacitate them.

During the chaos of the fight, two of the priests escape with Debbie, who is revealed to be the "Chosen One." Alex searches for her but is unable to find any trace. Returning to the banquet hall, he finds the remaining two priests, Zeke and Neo, severely injured. Before Alex can interrogate them, they commit suicide by taking cyanide pills.

The Clifton family is left traumatized but grateful to Alex for saving them. However, Alex is distraught over Debbie's kidnapping. The police arrive to investigate the scene, and Jessop Clifton promises to share all the information he has about the situation with Alex, assuring him that Debbie is not in immediate danger.
