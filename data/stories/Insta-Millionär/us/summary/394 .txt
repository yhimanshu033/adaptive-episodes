Alex agrees to pay for dinner for his entire class, Class 6, after they claim to be out of money. Billy, Alex's old rival, intervenes and mocks Alex's past financial struggles, offering to pay for all students on the first floor. Billy challenges Alex to a contest of who can afford more meals for the students.

Alex accepts the challenge and surprises everyone by offering to pay for all meals for Class 6 on the second floor for three months. The total comes to $106,000, which Alex pays with a credit card, shocking Billy who didn't believe Alex could afford it.

Billy, still in disbelief, is then faced with his own challenge of paying for meals for 800 students on the first floor. The manager calculates this cost to be $3 million, putting Billy in a difficult position. The episode ends with everyone waiting to see if Billy can follow through on his boast and pay the large sum.
