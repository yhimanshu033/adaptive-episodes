Meanwhile, Ava, representing the arbitrator's team, arrives at Clark's home with armed men, demanding to see Alex. Alex's dramatic entrance into Clark's home, declaring that everyone is going to die, sets the stage for a confrontation with Ava and her team. The episode ends with tension high, as Alex appears ready to face his opponents with newfound strength and power.
