Alex, Debbie, and Callisto leave the Moon Palace, saying goodbye to the disciples and other members. They travel down the mountain by donkey, then by car, and finally fly to Baltimore. Meanwhile, at Silver Lake in Baltimore, the police have closed off the area for a meeting between Lee Harshaw and the martial arts community. Various martial artists arrive, including Brody Kent from the Sunrise sect. Chris Steadman and Julian Duvant also arrive, with Melissa Kennedy sneaking in despite not being on the list. Lee Harshaw is preparing in a small building by the lake, surrounded by blood-stained robes of the leaders he killed. Chris and Julian briefly meet with Lee, expressing their support for his revenge against the martial arts community. As they leave, the martial artists outside begin to taunt and challenge the Blood Brothers to come out and face them. The scene is set for a confrontation between Lee Harshaw and the martial arts community, with Alex and his group on their way to intervene.
