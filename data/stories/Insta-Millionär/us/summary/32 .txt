In this episode, Alex and Cathy engage in a shopping competition at the boutique, with Cathy trying to outspend Alex to prove her superiority. Alex, secretly the owner of the store, plays along until he pretends to run out of money. Cathy and her boyfriend Billy gloat over their perceived victory, unaware of Alex's true wealth.

After Cathy and Billy leave, Debbie returns most of the clothes Alex bought for her, keeping only one outfit. Alex instructs Russell, the store manager, to give Cathy and Billy a discount on future purchases, feeling guilty about the situation.

Alex and Debbie leave the store together, with Alex admiring how beautiful Debbie looks in her new dress. They take a bus back to school, enjoying each other's company.

Later, Alex receives a call from Mark Stephens about attending an event at a restaurant near the school, which is part of another family test. Alex reluctantly agrees to go and takes Debbie with him.

At the restaurant, they encounter Donna Walters, an acquaintance from Debbie's hometown. Donna notices Debbie's new clothes and assumes she's trying to seduce a rich man. When Debbie introduces Alex as the one who bought her clothes, Donna is skeptical due to Alex's modest appearance, highlighting the ongoing misunderstandings about Alex's true financial status.
