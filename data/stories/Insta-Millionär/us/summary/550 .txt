In this episode, Alex finds himself at the Robinson Winery office building, facing a group of about 50 angry customers protesting against the wine. Maryann Robinson has brought him there to help with this urgent situation, diverting him from his original intention of visiting Debbie in the hospital. The protesters are upset about the quality of the wine, claiming it has made them sick. Alex, still recovering from his recent ordeal, is thrust into this new problem involving the influential Robinson family's business. This unexpected turn of events further complicates Alex's life, pulling him deeper into the affairs of the Robinsons while keeping him away from his wife Debbie, who is still in the hospital. The situation at the winery suggests potential issues with product quality or sabotage, adding another layer of mystery to Alex's already complex life.
