A mysterious woman in a blue dress, later revealed to be Chelsea, a high-ranking police officer and Alex's friend, enters the scene at the Hill Hotel. She takes control of the situation, confronting Steven and the Murdochs about shooting Alex. Chelsea orders Randy to arrest everyone involved, including Steven, which causes tension and resistance from the Murdoch family. Melanie refuses to surrender her weapon, leading Chelsea to shoot and disarm her. Throughout the confrontation, it becomes apparent that Chelsea has feelings for Alex, which Louisa notices. The situation remains tense as Chelsea asserts her authority over both the police officers and the Murdoch family.
