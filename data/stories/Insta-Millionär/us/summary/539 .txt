Alex and his mother Flora attend a Cassidy Pike concert in Philadelphia. During the show, Cassidy asks the audience to write wishes on paper airplanes and throw them towards the stage. Flora's plane lands at Cassidy's feet, and her wish for Alex to receive a hug from Cassidy is read aloud. Alex and Flora are invited on stage, where Flora gives Cassidy Alex's contact information despite his reluctance. Cassidy hugs both Flora and Alex, fulfilling Flora's wish. After the concert, Flora gives Alex Cassidy's contact information. As they're leaving, Alex receives an urgent call from Rufus informing him that Debbie has gone missing and is believed to have been abducted.
