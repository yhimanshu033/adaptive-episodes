Alex Ambrose, after rescuing Heidi from kidnappers, faces threats from the powerful Thornton family in Baltimore. Ash Thornton's brother, Ferdinand, calls to warn Alex of severe consequences for hurting Ash. Alex remains unfazed and even challenges them to come after him.

Celia, a colonel at the Baltimore Military Academy, is worried about Alex's safety. She explains that the Thornton family is extremely influential and dangerous, with Ferdinand being even more powerful than Ash. Celia fears for Alex's well-being, believing he can't defeat the entire family despite his impressive skills.

To protect Alex and resolve the situation, Celia offers him a position as chief training instructor at her academy. She believes this role would provide Alex with protection and benefit the academy. However, Alex politely declines the offer.

The episode ends with Celia determined to continue persuading Alex to accept the position, seeing it as the best solution to the dangerous situation he now faces with the Thornton family.
