Alex receives a call from Robert Miller, the manager of Metro Sky Bank, about potential apartment buildings for investment in New York City. This surprises Karen's family, who had been berating Alex for being poor. When Robert arrives at the restaurant, Karen fears being caught in her lie about being sick. Alex smoothly explains to Robert that he had asked Karen to accompany him, resolving the situation. Robert then discusses the apartment buildings with Alex, mentioning an estimated cost of $125 million. This revelation shocks Karen's family, who begin to realize that Alex might actually be wealthy. Throughout the encounter, Alex remains calm and composed, not revealing his true financial status but allowing the situation to unfold naturally.
