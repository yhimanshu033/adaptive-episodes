Alex confronts his ex-girlfriend Cathy at the Autumn Beauty Boutique, where she's causing trouble. Cathy tries to reconcile with Alex, having realized his wealth and worth, but Alex rejects her advances. He explains that their relationship was not true love and that he has moved on. Cathy leaves heartbroken.

Alex returns to the night market to find Rose and Suzan. Meanwhile, Rose continues to express her dislike for Alex to Suzan. The girls accidentally bump into a seemingly blind man and decide to help him out of the crowded market. They accompany him to his car, a Mercedes CLK limousine.

The girls enter the car to rest, and Suzan receives a call from Alex. Rose takes the phone and rudely dismisses Alex's concerns for their safety. While Rose is on the phone, the "blind man" enters the driver's seat and reveals himself to be Luciel Brennan, a person who had previously harmed Rose's family. The girls realize they're trapped in the locked car with Luciel, who snatches the phone and ends the call with Alex, leaving them in a dangerous situation.
