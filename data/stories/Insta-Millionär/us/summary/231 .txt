Alex races to David's house to stop Leona's wedding. He causes a stir among the crowd and police but manages to get past them with help from Nelly, who reminds the police commissioner of Alex's Brunei medal. Inside, Alex faces resistance from David's hired security team. First, he encounters Ryder Dixon, who is quickly defeated by the Moon Maidens. Then, a larger group led by Damien North and Rick Veasy confronts Alex. A chaotic fight breaks out in the front yard, with Alex, the Moon Maidens, and surprisingly Nelly holding their own against the security team. Alex manages to break Damien's wrist and evade Rick's attacks. As time runs out, Alex tries to rush into the house but is briefly held back by Damien and struck by Rick. However, the blow seems ineffective, and Rick attempts to flee. Alex, torn between pursuing his goal and ensuring the safety of the girls, grabs Rick before he can escape.
