Alex and Nelly are running a food truck at Todd Mountain scenic area during a folk festival. Their business is doing well until Hunter, who had a previous encounter with Alex, arrives with Lindsey Marvel and her sister. Hunter recognizes Alex and Nelly at the food truck and devises a plan to cause trouble for them.

Hunter enlists the help of his old acquaintance Felix, who is now the chief of security for the area. Felix, hoping to gain employment with the Marvel family, agrees to teach Alex and Nelly a "lesson."

Felix approaches the food truck posing as a customer and then falsely accuses Nelly of serving a pancake with flies in it. He creates a scene, drawing attention from other tourists and security guards. The situation escalates as Felix, now revealing his position as chief of security, declares that the food truck must be impounded for inspection due to alleged health violations.

This incident threatens Alex and Nelly's business and puts them in a difficult situation, as they face accusations of unsanitary practices and potential confiscation of their food truck.
