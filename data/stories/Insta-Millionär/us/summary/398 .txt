Alex confronts Billy and Leonard at the school, leading to the arrival of police officer Adrian Wolfren, who knows Alex from a previous incident. Adrian takes Billy and Leonard away, giving Alex only a verbal warning. Alex then tries to resolve the situation with Felicity and the girls she stole from, offering to repay them and insisting that Felicity will apologize publicly at a school assembly. 

Intrigued by Felicity's behavior, Alex follows her after school to a run-down part of town. He watches as Felicity transforms her appearance from a rebellious teenager to an ordinary schoolgirl. She enters a dilapidated house where Alex discovers her ill mother lying in bed. Felicity's demeanor changes completely as she cares for her mother, revealing a hidden side to her character and suggesting that her rebellious behavior at school may be a facade hiding her difficult home life.
