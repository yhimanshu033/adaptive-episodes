Alex and Nelly find themselves in a confrontation with a couple, Hunter and his female companion, after Nelly put shoe polish in their pancakes. The situation escalates when Hunter, claiming to be from the Marvel family and a skilled fighter, threatens to beat up Nelly. Alex tries to defuse the situation by offering compensation, but Hunter refuses.

As Hunter attempts to attack, Nelly pushes Alex out of the way and kicks Hunter. They try to escape, but the woman catches them. Hunter then attempts to assault Nelly, but Alex intervenes, surprising everyone with his fighting skills. He manages to subdue Hunter using martial arts and even intimidates him with a blade, causing Hunter and the woman to flee.

Nelly is impressed by Alex's unexpected fighting abilities and demands to know why he didn't reveal this earlier. Alex explains that she never asked, leading to a playful argument where Nelly threatens to deduct from his salary if he doesn't share more about himself. The episode ends with them returning to work at the food truck, with Nelly maintaining her bossy attitude towards Alex.
