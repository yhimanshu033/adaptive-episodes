Alex Ambrose, a former store employee who inherited $86 million, is now involved in complex relationships and supernatural events. He's engaged to Debbie, daughter of Cynthia Clifton, and has become a key player in Baltimore's power dynamics.

The episode focuses on the crisis facing the Drake family and their company, the Fidelity Group. Noah Freeman, working with Colin Patterson, has orchestrated a plan to destroy the Drake family's business. During a crisis meeting, Noah encourages executives to resign, leading to a mass exodus of senior staff.

Colin Patterson and his father Joseph arrive at the meeting with reporters. Colin reveals his intention to buy all the Drake family's assets for $30 billion. The Drake family is skeptical of Colin's ability to afford such a purchase, but Colin proves he has access to over $35 billion by calling Metro Sky Bank.

The Drake family, particularly David, refuses to sell to Colin, accusing him of being responsible for their current crisis. Noah warns that this day will be remembered as the end of the Drake family. The episode ends with tension high and the future of the Fidelity Group hanging in the balance.
