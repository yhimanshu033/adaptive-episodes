In this episode, David Drake asks Jason to seduce Lindsey as revenge for her seeming unbothered by their broken engagement. David offers Jason a Ferrari to help with the seduction.

The next day, Lindsey invites Alex to watch an Olympic tennis match. They meet Lindsey's friend Jane and her group at the stadium. Jane and her friends, aware of Jason's plan, try to belittle Alex due to his apparent lack of wealth.

Jason arrives in a flashy Ferrari, attempting to impress Lindsey. However, Lindsey shows no interest in Jason and remains close to Alex throughout the event. This frustrates Jason and Jane, who try to separate Alex and Lindsey without success.

As the group watches the match from a luxury box, Jane and her friends continue to praise Jason and attempt to push Lindsey towards him. Lindsey rebuffs their efforts and stays focused on Alex and the game.

The episode ends with the arrival of another sports car, which Alex recognizes as his own Ferrari, adding an element of surprise and potential complication to the situation.
