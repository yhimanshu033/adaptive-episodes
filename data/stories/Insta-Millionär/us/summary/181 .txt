The episode describes a confrontation between Alex and Colin at a university auditorium during a rehearsal. Alex's laughter at Colin's hiccup leads to a heated exchange, with Colin attempting to have Alex removed. Alex reveals he's on the performance list, surprising everyone. 

The situation escalates when Colin challenges Alex to a physical fight, offering to let him participate if he can knock Colin down. Despite warnings about Colin's Taekwondo skills, Alex accepts the challenge. The fight begins with Colin having the upper hand, hitting Alex several times.

Leona, who Alex believes is Debbie, tries to stop the fight but is pulled away. As Alex appears to be losing, he suddenly decides to fight back. Colin, not wanting to appear too aggressive in front of Leona, holds back. However, when Alex falls and Colin attempts a finishing move, Leona's cry stops him.

The confrontation seems to end with Colin kicking Alex while he's down and insulting him. However, Alex, feeling humiliated, gets up and punches Colin from behind, hitting his ear and causing it to bleed. The episode ends with Colin, now enraged, threatening Alex more seriously.
