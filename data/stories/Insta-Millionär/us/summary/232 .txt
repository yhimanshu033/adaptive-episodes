Alex confronts David and Leona at their wedding ceremony, interrupting just as they are about to kiss. The guests react with surprise and disdain. David orders his security, led by Cliff Munson, to remove Alex. Cliff, despite his unassuming appearance, demonstrates incredible strength by cracking the floor tiles. A fight ensues between Alex and Cliff, with Alex initially deflecting Cliff's powerful attacks. However, Cliff changes his fighting style, overwhelming Alex with rapid strikes. As the battle continues, Alex begins to feel his inner powers growing stronger, remembering the abilities he inherited from Zora and Georgina. He believes that if he can channel their strength, he will be able to defeat Cliff, despite the man's impressive fighting skills.
