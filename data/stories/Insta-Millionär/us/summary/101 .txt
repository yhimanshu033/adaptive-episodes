Alex confronts Melissa about her involvement in Debbie's disappearance. He kills Lou and his wife but spares Melissa when she claims she might be pregnant. Alex then waits in the rain for news about Debbie, who was seen falling from a hotel and being taken away by an unidentified couple. Despite extensive searches, Ken Stokes and his team are unable to find any trace of Debbie or her abductors.

Alex falls ill from waiting in the rain and spends five days recovering. During this time, he reads Debbie's diary and realizes he needs to stay strong to find her. The search expands to cover the entire East Coast.

After recovering, Alex returns to Preston University and goes to Ramsey Lake, where he has memories with Debbie. He mistakes Kelly for Debbie and rushes to stop her from jumping into the lake. Realizing his mistake, Alex tells Kelly that Debbie has been kidnapped. Kelly tries to help the still-ill Alex, but he refuses her assistance, preferring to sit by the lake alone.
