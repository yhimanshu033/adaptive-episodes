The episode reveals that Alex had anonymously donated over 1.5 million dollars to Richmond University. President Merrit, unaware of Alex's request for anonymity, decides to have Alex present the awards at the New Year's party. This revelation causes shock and initial disbelief among the students.

When Alex is called to present the awards, some students, particularly Colin's supporters, react negatively, accusing Alex of sabotaging Colin and Leona's performance. The situation escalates until President Merrit intervenes, revealing the full extent of Alex's donation and its impact on various university programs.

Upon learning the truth, the students' attitudes change dramatically, with many now praising Alex. Alex reluctantly addresses the crowd, downplaying his donation. He then proceeds to hand out certificates to his team members, including Doug and Fiona, who apologize for their past behavior. Colin reluctantly accepts his certificate, while Leona's interaction with Alex is more cordial.

During the group photo, Ian, the director who had previously been rude to Alex, attempts to apologize. Alex, as a form of revenge, instructs Ian to appear on stage in only his underwear. This leads to a comical situation where Ian, a large man, clings to Colin during the group photo, much to Colin's dismay and everyone else's amusement.

The episode highlights the sudden shift in how Alex is perceived once his wealth is revealed, and it showcases the complex dynamics between the characters, particularly Alex, Colin, and Leona.
