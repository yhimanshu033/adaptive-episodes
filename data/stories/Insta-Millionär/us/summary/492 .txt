Alex attends a brunch at the Hall family home following Neil Hall's sudden death and cremation. Caroline Hall, Joe's mother, displays an indifferent attitude towards her husband's passing and focuses on Joe's future. She introduces Desmond Lang, a family friend who she claims arranged a lucrative football contract for Joe with the Raptors team.

Alex realizes that the contract Caroline presents is actually the one he had arranged for Joe. Desmond takes credit for negotiating it, which Alex finds shameless. When Alex hints that he might have arranged the contract, Caroline dismisses the idea, believing Alex lacks such influence.

Caroline then informs Alex and Joe's other friends that they can no longer associate with Joe due to his upcoming career as a famous athlete. She insists Joe needs to maintain the right image and spend time with the "right sort of people." Joe reluctantly agrees with his mother's decision, citing that she's his only guide for his future now that his father is gone.

Alex attempts to remind Joe of their close friendship and the incident when Neil asked Joe to call him, but Joe claims he can't remember much about it. Desmond interrupts, emphasizing the need to focus on Joe's new career and avoid being seen with his old friends.

The episode ends with Desmond announcing their departure for a martial arts reception at the Regal Hotel, effectively cutting short any further discussion or reminiscing.
