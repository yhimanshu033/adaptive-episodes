In this episode, Art Steadman receives instructions from Luther of the Blood Brothers to leave information about Alex in a hidden compartment. Meanwhile, Alex and Debbie visit Jessop Clifton at his office. During their visit, a bomb threat is called in, demanding $15 million from Alex. The building is evacuated, but no bomb is found.

As Jessop, Rufus, and Alex discuss the situation outside, Alex senses danger and realizes it's a sniper attack aimed at Jessop. He pushes Jessop to safety and decides to pursue the sniper. Alex, now enhanced with skills from the Black Orchid cult, quickly climbs the building across the street where he believes the sniper is located.

On the roof, Alex encounters the sniper attempting to escape via rope. He grabs the rope and begins pulling it up, trapping the sniper halfway down the building. The episode ends with the sniper realizing he's being pulled back up by Alex.
