Alex, convinced that Leona Marvel is his girlfriend Debbie, confronts her at a public event. Despite his pleas and attempts to remind her of their shared memories, Leona insists she doesn't know him. Alex's hopes are further dashed when he notices Leona wearing a different necklace than the one he gave Debbie.

Lindsey Marvel, Leona's sister, intervenes and takes Leona away, leaving Alex heartbroken and confused. Nelly, Alex's coworker, accuses him of trying to trick his way into the wealthy Marvel family. Alex denies this, insisting he comes from a wealthy family himself.

Later, Lindsey and Leona discuss the incident. Leona wonders if she knew Alex before losing her memory, but Lindsey assures her this isn't possible. They also discuss Lindsey's arranged marriage to Mr. Drake, which Lindsey is reluctant about. Leona shows interest in a jade pendant she owns but isn't allowed to wear.

Alex, still convinced Leona is Debbie, researches the Marvel family and discovers they live in Arlington Heights. He begins spending time each day waiting outside their gated community, hoping to see Debbie again.

Weeks pass, with Alex working on the food truck and waiting at Arlington Heights daily. One afternoon, he leaves work early to continue his vigil, despite Nelly's objections and threat to dock his pay. Nelly feels lonely as Alex leaves, dismissing his efforts as a foolish daydream.
