Alex, now working as a security guard at Johns Hopkins University, observes a group of wealthy, fashionable young men on campus. The men initially mock Alex but then focus on pursuing four beautiful women they had seen earlier. These women turn out to be the Moon Maidens (Celeste, Selene, Callisto, and Luna), who have just enrolled as students at the university.

The young men attempt to flirt with the Moon Maidens, but their advances are rejected. As the women walk away, they notice Alex in his patrol car. To the surprise and confusion of the young men, the Moon Maidens enthusiastically greet Alex and begin showering him with attention and offers of massages.

The wealthy students are bewildered by this turn of events and try to intervene, insisting that they are more attractive and suitable companions than a mere security guard. Alex playfully asks the Moon Maidens if women truly prefer men who focus on appearance and fancy clothes, as the young men claim.

The Moon Maidens become increasingly annoyed with the wealthy students' arrogance and insults towards Alex. The episode ends with the women confronting the young men, having lost their patience with their behavior.
