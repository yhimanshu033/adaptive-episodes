Alex Ambrose, a former store employee who inherited $86 million, is now the leader of the Moon Palace and has formed the Appalachian Alliance. He and his fiancée Debbie have survived a poisoning attempt and are running the Moon Palace together.

Meanwhile, their enemies are planning their next move. The Steadmans have allied with the Blood Brothers, led by Tyson Slade, who has declared war on the East Coast martial arts community. Lee Harshaw, seeking revenge for Damon's death, has traveled to Baltimore and Philadelphia with Chris Steadman.

At the Black Swallow Martial Arts Academy, Master Zachariah Holbrook returns from isolation with new knowledge and techniques. He begins training his disciples, including Cliff Munson, who has been forgiven and readmitted to the academy.

As they discuss the history of Hu Juli and the Blood Brothers, Lee Harshaw and his men suddenly break into the academy. Cliff attempts to fight Lee but is quickly overpowered. The other disciples join Cliff in a coordinated defensive technique against Lee, who mocks their tactics. The situation is tense as the two sides prepare for a potentially violent confrontation.
