Alex Ambrose, a security guard with supernatural powers, is approached by Ethan, Celia, and Gavin (the director of a government agency) for help in rescuing Heidi, who has been kidnapped. Alex initially refuses, annoyed by Gavin's disrespectful attitude. Meanwhile, the principal of Oak Ridge High School calls Alex, begging him to return and help with the unruly Class 6, but Alex declines.

Gavin's attempts to persuade Alex backfire, resulting in Alex slapping him. Ethan and Celia criticize Gavin for his poor handling of the situation and leave. The Oak Ridge principal arrives in person to plead with Alex to return to teaching, but Alex firmly refuses.

Just as Alex is about to look for his girlfriend Debbie, he receives a text message from Heidi's phone asking for help. Despite his earlier refusals and frustration with the authorities, Alex decides to take action to rescue Heidi, feeling responsible for her as his former student.
