Alex wakes up in bed with Melissa, realizing they had sex while he was drugged. Feeling guilty about betraying Debbie, he coldly tells Melissa to get an abortion if she's pregnant and offers her $5 million in compensation. Melissa refuses, wanting a relationship with Alex instead.

Alex tries calling Debbie but can't reach her. He discovers she never returned from Chicago as Melissa had claimed. Alex confronts Melissa, who denies involvement but admits lying earlier. Alex suspects Melissa orchestrated Debbie's disappearance.

It's revealed that Melissa worked with a man named Lou to abduct Debbie. However, Lou lost track of Debbie after she fell from a hotel window. Melissa and Lou discuss covering up their involvement, with Melissa threatening Lou to take full responsibility. She promises him $2 million and claims she'll soon marry Alex, whose family she says is extremely powerful. Melissa is confident her plan to win Alex will succeed, despite the complications with Debbie's unknown fate.
