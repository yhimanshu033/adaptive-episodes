At a banquet in Baltimore, Alex confronts the wealthy elites, causing chaos. He punches Edwin, who tries to retaliate but is stopped by Louisa Murdoch. Alex then stabs a young man who threatened him with a knife. The guests are shocked by Alex's violence and strength.

Alex taunts Michael Murdoch, who is initially absent. Edwin challenges Alex again and mysteriously gets his teeth broken without seeing Alex move. Louisa tries to reason with Alex, but he ignores her.

Finally, Michael appears on the balcony and comes down to confront Alex. They exchange insults, with Alex criticizing Michael for not taking responsibility and abandoning his guests. Michael, concerned about his reputation, decides to face Alex directly.

Michael tries to belittle Alex, calling him a "spineless thug" and comparing him to his own subordinates. The confrontation between Alex and Michael intensifies, setting the stage for a potential showdown.
