Alex and Emma have a conversation after Cathy's departure, with Emma offering support and suggesting they go out to eat. Alex declines going to De Luca's to avoid Cathy but jokingly suggests Madison Park, a very expensive restaurant. Emma is surprised by this boast but goes along with it.

Alex's roommates Ben and Carl invite him to the cafeteria, but they end up returning to their dorm after seeing a message from their other roommate, Joe. Joe introduces his new girlfriend, Suzan, and invites everyone to lunch at La Belle Vie. Suzan privately expresses disappointment in Joe's roommates' appearances and financial status.

At La Belle Vie, Suzan's roommates Rose, Stacy, and Betty are waiting. Rose recounts an incident at First Republic Bank where someone accidentally hit her with a door, not realizing it was Alex. The girls discuss the bank's wealthy clientele and speculate about the incident.

As they wait for Suzan and the guys to arrive, the girls express excitement about meeting Joe and his roommates, hoping they'll be attractive like Joe. The episode ends as Suzan, Alex, and the others finally arrive at the restaurant.
