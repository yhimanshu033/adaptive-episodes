After Alex's sudden departure from Oak Ridge High School, chaos ensues. The students of Class 6 rebel against Billy, the new head teacher, refusing to accept him as a replacement for Mr. Ambrose. They throw objects at Billy and chase him out of the classroom. The students then decide to cause trouble in hopes of forcing the school to bring Mr. Ambrose back.

Meanwhile, Alex returns to his job as a security guard at Johns Hopkins University, resuming his normal life. He meets with his wife Debbie for a romantic walk on campus.

Back at Oak Ridge High School, the situation quickly spirals out of control. Class 6 students injure other students, chase teacher Mr. Winters down the stairs (breaking his leg), and set fire to the canteen. The school administration is overwhelmed by the sudden outbreak of violence and destruction, with Billy Wilde struggling to regain control of the situation.
