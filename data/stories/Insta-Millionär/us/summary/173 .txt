Alex receives a call from his former classmate Phillipa, inviting him to a meeting for people from Texas now living in Washington D.C. Despite his financial constraints, Alex agrees to attend when assured he won't have to pay. It's revealed that Myriam, another former classmate, orchestrated this invitation out of spite towards Alex.

The next day, while working at Nelly's food truck, Alex negotiates leaving early to attend the meeting. On his way there, he receives an unexpected call from his mother, Flora, whom he hasn't seen in seven years. Emotional and tearful, Alex agrees to meet her at the Orange Hotel after the meeting.

As Alex arrives at the Ruby Hotel for the meeting, he encounters an elderly woman struggling with her bicycle loaded with recyclables. He helps her, and touched by his kindness, she asks him to call her "mom." The woman insists on giving Alex some money, which he reluctantly accepts to avoid hurting her feelings.

Unknown to Alex, someone observing this interaction from a nearby car misinterprets the scene, believing Alex has shamelessly asked the old woman for money after helping her.
