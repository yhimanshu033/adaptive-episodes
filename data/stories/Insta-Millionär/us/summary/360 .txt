An old man and his younger companion unexpectedly arrive at the Clifton family's private birthday banquet for the late Cynthia. The old man insults the Cliftons, claiming they are insignificant compared to him. He demonstrates his incredible strength by effortlessly lifting a massive clock from the garden and hanging it on a tree branch, which breaks under the weight.

The old man is revealed to be Tyson Slade, the leader of the Blood Brothers gang. This revelation causes panic among the Cliftons, who recognize him as a powerful enemy. Tyson dismisses the idea of seeking revenge on the Cliftons, comparing it to "squashing bugs."

Instead, Tyson asks for Alex's whereabouts. Jessop informs him that Alex left two weeks ago. Tyson, seemingly unconvinced, surveys the crowd and then throws the huge clock at a wall, destroying it and sending bricks flying. This display of power further intimidates the Clifton family and demonstrates the serious threat Tyson poses to them.
