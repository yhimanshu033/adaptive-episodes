Alex, now fighting for the Cliftons, faces mockery and doubt from the Steadmans and others at the underground boxing club. The atmosphere is tense, with most expecting an easy victory for the Steadmans.

Unexpectedly, Justin and Nelly arrive at the venue. Nelly, now part of the Ambrose family but unhappy and withdrawn, suddenly runs to embrace Alex, crying. Justin is surprised but pleased to see his brother.

Nathan contacts Justin via a cellphone, expressing disdain for Alex's situation and warning Justin not to help him. Justin defends Alex, stating he's better than Nathan and will win without help.

Nathan reveals that Alex's opponent is Damon Walker, an undefeated member of the Blood Brothers who recently killed Paul Novak. This information raises concerns about Alex's chances in the fight. The situation becomes more complex as family loyalties and personal relationships intertwine with the high-stakes confrontation between the Cliftons and Steadmans.
