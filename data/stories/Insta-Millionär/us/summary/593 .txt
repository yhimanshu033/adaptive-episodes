Alex Ambrose, arrested for the suspected murder of Michael Murdoch, is being interrogated by Detective Tamara Knox. When given a chance to make a phone call, Alex finds the phone's battery dead. Tamara leaves Alex alone in the interrogation room, believing he will eventually confess.

Meanwhile, Detective Sergeant Timothy arrives at the police station and immediately asks to see Alex. Upon entering the interrogation room, Timothy shows a friendly demeanor towards Alex, offering him coffee and expressing understanding of his situation.

Alex explains to Timothy that he didn't kill Michael, but that Michael pushed himself out of the window to frame Alex. Timothy believes Alex's account and reveals that Riley Naysmith, a well-known figure in Baltimore, is trying to exonerate Alex by persuading witnesses to change their testimonies. Lindsey Marvel is also involved in this effort to help Alex.

Alex, who has never met Riley, suspects that Riley is using this situation to gain favor with Lindsey and keep her indebted to him. The episode ends with Alex realizing the complex motivations behind the attempts to help him.
