In this episode, the Clifton family discovers Debbie's existence and her connection to Charles Marvel. Jessop Clifton, angry at being kept in the dark, orders his son Rufus to send Paul Novak, a powerful martial arts expert, to Washington D.C. to kill Charles and retrieve Debbie.

Meanwhile, Reginald Drake receives a message from the Ambrose family confirming that Alex has been disowned. Reginald sees this as an opportunity to help Alex, believing he may return to the Ambrose family in the future and be in a position to repay their kindness.

Alex and Debbie plan a small, private wedding at the Marvel villa. They visit the Drake house to persuade David and Reginald to keep quiet about the situation with Leona, suggesting everyone should believe David and Leona are still married. Alex also informs them of his upcoming wedding to Debbie.

Reginald asks to attend the wedding with David, which Alex reluctantly agrees to. David is upset about attending but Reginald insists it's necessary to gain Alex's favor. The episode ends with David doubting that Alex will ever return to the Ambrose family.
