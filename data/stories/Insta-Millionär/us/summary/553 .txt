Alex visits Maryann Robinson at her winery, where she explains her difficult situation and asks for his help to save the business. She needs to increase the winery's value from 5 million to 100 million dollars in just three months to avoid an arranged marriage. Maryann also reveals the rivalry with Blue Bottle Winery, owned by the Murdoch family, which has been sabotaging her business.

As Alex considers helping Maryann, they are interrupted by news that Michael has been hit by an arrow. Alex decides to leave, avoiding further involvement, and goes to visit Debbie in the hospital.

At the hospital, Alex finds Debbie recovering well. They spend time together, and Alex realizes Debbie needs something to do. He proposes involving her in his new business venture, which includes managing the recently acquired Birchwood House property.

The next day, Debbie is discharged from the hospital. Her health has significantly improved due to Alex's intervention, making her stronger than the average person.
