Alex, severely injured, confronts Neil Hall, who has revealed himself as a Ghost and attacked Alex. Despite their past friendship, Neil is determined to kill Alex due to pressure from the Ghosts. Alex tries to appeal to Neil's conscience, reminding him of their shared history and Neil's son Joe.

The situation escalates when Michael Murdoch and Jonah arrive with a group of armed individuals. Jonah offers to clear Neil's debts and cancel a contract on his life if he kills Alex. Rose attempts to intervene but is knocked unconscious by Jonah.

Alex continues to try reasoning with Neil, offering to help him and urging him not to do something he'll regret. However, Neil feels he has no choice and proceeds to attack Alex. In a final confrontation, Alex moves at the last second, causing Neil to lose balance. Neil's knife accidentally scratches his unconscious son Joe's cheek as he tries to avoid a fatal blow.

The episode highlights the moral conflict within Neil, the ruthlessness of the Ghosts, and Alex's attempts to appeal to Neil's better nature despite the dire circumstances.
