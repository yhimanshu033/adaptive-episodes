Alex attends a gathering for Texans in Washington D.C. at the Ruby Hotel, organized by Myriam Henley. He arrives underdressed and unwashed, causing other attendees to comment on his appearance and smell. Myriam tells a story about seeing Alex at the Crown Hotel, attempting to book an expensive room but failing due to lack of funds, embarrassing him in front of everyone.

The event's organizer, Lester Yates, is late, so Myriam takes charge. She suggests everyone introduce themselves and their professions. As attendees share their impressive jobs and connections, Alex feels out of place.

Meanwhile, Alex's mother is also at the Ruby Hotel, having reserved a room to meet him. She calls Alex, expressing her eagerness to see him.

The episode highlights the contrast between Alex's current situation and his past wealth, as well as the social dynamics and judgments present in this gathering of successful Texans.
