The episode continues the dramatic events at the talent show in Chicago. Lee Dempsey accuses Debbie of poisoning his dog Duke after the dog drinks from her water bottle, which is found to contain poison. Melissa frames Debbie for the incident, and Lee, believing Debbie is guilty, allows his dog Duke to attack her in revenge.

Debbie, still suffering from stomach pain, tries to escape Duke by running into an artificial lake. The dog pursues her, causing injuries as she struggles to defend herself. Meanwhile, Alex is in New York, unaware of Debbie's predicament, celebrating his friend Joe's engagement.

The situation takes a turn when Justin Ambrose, Alex's half-brother, arrives at the event with four attractive women. Justin, despite his casual appearance, is revealed to be a wealthy and influential figure whom even Lee Dempsey respects. Initially, Justin approves of Lee's actions, not recognizing Debbie.

However, when Justin finally sees Debbie's face, he realizes she is his half-brother's girlfriend. He immediately rushes into the lake to stop the attack, shouting for Lee to call off his dog. The episode ends with Justin attempting to rescue Debbie from the violent situation.
