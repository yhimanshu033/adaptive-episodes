Alex returns to the hospital to help Don get surgery, generously covering the costs. While there, he is suddenly arrested by Officer Chelsea Wood on suspicion of shooting Julian Duvant. Alex protests his innocence but is taken to the police station for interrogation. 

At the station, Chelsea dismisses her biased colleague and questions Alex alone. Alex explains the full story, but Chelsea is skeptical of his claims about subduing Saul and dodging gunshots. To test Alex's abilities, Chelsea suddenly attacks him in the interrogation room. Alex manages to dodge and counter her moves while handcuffed, impressing Chelsea but not fully convincing her. 

The confrontation ends with them accidentally falling onto the table and kissing as Alex pins Chelsea down. This unexpected turn of events leaves the situation unresolved, with Alex's true capabilities and innocence still in question.
