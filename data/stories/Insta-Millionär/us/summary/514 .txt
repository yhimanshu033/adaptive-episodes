In this episode, Alex confronts Todd Petrie in the banquet hall of the Baltimore Martial Arts Association headquarters. After a brief fight, Alex kills Todd with his own sword. The room erupts into chaos, with some guests fleeing and others attacking Alex, but he easily defeats them.

Rufus and other powerful families arrive, signaling Alex's regained control over Baltimore's martial arts community. A wounded attacker warns Alex about Kenneth Petrie's impending retaliation, but Alex remains confident in his allies' support.

Kenneth appears on video screens, demanding to speak with Todd and threatening Alex. Alex counters by invoking Article Seventy-two, which deals with the illegal detention of chapter members. Jessop Clifton, who was previously detained, confirms the severe punishments for such violations.

Alex formally accuses Todd of violating Article Seventy-two and calls for a vote on his punishment. The chapter members unanimously vote for death. Alex then reveals Todd's dead body to Kenneth, shocking and enraging him.

After dealing with a final attacker, Alex ends the call with Kenneth, stating that he still has to face Jacob but will deal with Kenneth later. The episode concludes with the Baltimore chapter cleaning up the aftermath and Debbie being taken to rest. Alex reflects on the irreparable damage to their relationship with the Arbitrator's office and prepares for his upcoming challenges.
