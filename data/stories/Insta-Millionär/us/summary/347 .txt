Alex remembers leaving the sniper tied up on a rooftop, but when someone is sent to retrieve him, they find only a pool of blood, indicating the sniper was rescued by accomplices. This confirms they are still facing an ongoing threat.

The Clifton family discusses how to respond to the attacks. Rufus advocates for aggressive retaliation against the Steadmans, but Jessop disagrees, believing they lack solid evidence and that such action could provoke the powerful Blood Brothers gang. This leads to tension between Jessop and Rufus.

Alex suggests a more cautious approach, recommending that Jessop and Debbie stay inside for safety while spreading word that Jessop was injured in the attack. This would allow Alex to focus on pursuing the attackers while gauging reactions to the news.

Chris Steadman calls, and following Jessop's instructions, Rufus pretends that Jessop is seriously injured. Chris expresses sympathy and offers to visit. After the call, Art Steadman, suspicious of the situation, sends Chris to investigate in person.

The episode ends with Chris arriving at the Clifton estate to assess the situation firsthand.
