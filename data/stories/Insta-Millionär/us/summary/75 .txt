Alex comforts Debbie after she is beaten by her bandmates. Debbie is upset about the insults to her mother but doesn't want Alex to retaliate. They plan to visit Debbie's mother, but receive a call that Carla has been hospitalized. Panicked, they rush to the hospital. 

Unable to get a taxi, Alex stops a car and pays the driver to take them. On the way, they encounter a group of young people blocking the road. Alex confronts them and quickly defeats one who tries to attack him. When more approach, Alex takes over driving the car.

Determined to reach the hospital, Alex speeds towards the crowd, forcing them to scatter. He hits a few people, including Henry, who was confessing his love to a girl. The girl is left stunned by Alex's handsome appearance as they drive away.

Throughout the incident, Debbie remains worried about her mother's condition, while Alex focuses on getting her to the hospital as quickly as possible.
