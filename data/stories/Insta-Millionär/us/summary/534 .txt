Alex Ambrose, a martial artist with supernatural abilities, is brought in to treat Keira Lambert, who is in a coma following a mysterious gas attack. At the Lambert family home, Alex faces skepticism and hostility from the medical team, particularly Dr. Sophie Allbrook, who questions his qualifications and expertise.

Alex surprises everyone by accurately describing Keira's condition and the current treatment plan without looking at the medical records. He points out the flaws in their approach, explaining that their methods might prolong Keira's life but won't wake her up.

To demonstrate his abilities, Alex diagnoses several doctors in the room with various health issues they were unaware of, shocking the medical team. This display of knowledge and insight silences their objections.

Alex is then allowed to see Keira. Upon examining her, he determines that the current treatment won't save her life and that she is dying. However, when Holly Lambert asks if he can wake Keira up, Alex confidently responds that he can.

This episode highlights the conflict between traditional medicine and Alex's unconventional abilities, setting the stage for his attempt to heal Keira using his unique skills.
