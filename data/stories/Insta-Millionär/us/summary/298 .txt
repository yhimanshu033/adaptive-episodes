Alex, disguised as a security guard at Richmond University, encounters his ex-girlfriend Melissa, who has returned to his life as part of Chris's revenge plan. Melissa, manipulated by Chris, attempts to get close to Alex but harbors resentment towards him.

At the university canteen, Melissa causes a scene by cutting in line and refusing to move, disrupting lunch service for other students. Callisto and Luna, two of Alex's associates known as Moon Maidens, confront Melissa about her behavior.

After obtaining food through her disruptive actions, Melissa tries to offer it to Alex as a peace offering. However, Callisto and Luna intervene, revealing Melissa's earlier behavior to Alex. They bring Alex his own lunch and attempt to lead him away from Melissa.

Melissa persists in trying to get Alex's attention, but Callisto knocks the food out of her hand. Despite the Moon Maidens' hostility towards Melissa, Alex feels a twinge of sympathy seeing the food on the ground. Ultimately, Callisto and Luna escort Alex back to the dormitory, leaving Melissa behind.

The episode highlights the growing tension between Melissa and Alex's current associates, as well as Alex's conflicted feelings about the situation.
