In this episode, Michael Murdoch is desperate to save his dying sister Louisa. Harvey Kingston, a member of the Ghosts, offers to help but warns that saving Louisa could put his own life in danger. Michael agrees to do anything in return.

Harvey injects Louisa with a mysterious blue liquid from a black metal syringe. Within minutes, Louisa's condition improves dramatically, and she appears to be out of danger. The doctors are amazed by her sudden recovery.

Grateful, Michael offers to do anything for Harvey. Surprisingly, Harvey only asks for help in opening a clinic in Baltimore, requiring assistance with licenses and paperwork. Michael is puzzled by this seemingly modest request.

Harvey then reveals that the substance he used, called "The Grail" by the Ghosts, is extremely rare and valuable. It can neutralize any toxin and repair cellular damage. The Grail is made from rare herbs and a large amount of human plasma, making its production dangerous and costly.

Harvey explains that he used one of his only two doses to save Louisa without authorization from the Ghosts. He implies that getting more would be difficult and potentially dangerous for him. This revelation makes Michael realize the gravity of the situation and the debt he now owes to Harvey and the Ghosts.
