Alex Ambrose, the new leader of the Moon Palace, addresses the gathered disciples and gang members. He reveals Georgina's death and his new role, showing the master's ring as proof. The disciples and gang members accept Alex as their new leader.

Alex proposes forming the Appalachian Alliance, uniting the Moon Palace with other gangs in the region. The alliance is met with enthusiasm and agreement from all parties.

Alex then remembers Debbie, who is still critically ill. He brings her into the palace, where Celeste recognizes the need for a special thousand-year-old orchid to save her. Disciples and gang members are sent to search for the orchid in the Appalachian Mountains.

After hours of searching, the leader of the Grizzlies returns with a suitable orchid. Celeste prepares a porridge with the orchid, explaining that only a small amount should be used due to its potency. Alex feeds the porridge to Debbie, and her condition begins to improve. She regains color in her face and slowly opens her eyes, signaling her recovery.
