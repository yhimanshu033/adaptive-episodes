Alex meets Kelly and Sharon on campus, where Kelly asks him out. Alex declines, stating he already has a girlfriend (Debbie) whom he loves. Kelly, undeterred, convinces Alex to pretend to be her boyfriend at a party. 

The next day, Kelly picks up Alex for the party at Green Island Garden, an exclusive residential area. To their surprise, Alex uses a key card to enter the complex. When questioned, Alex admits he lives there, but Sharon doesn't believe him, citing his apparent poverty based on information they gathered about him at school.

Sharon accuses Alex of lying and suggests he's trying to take advantage of Kelly financially. Kelly, though disappointed, still wants to believe in Alex. Despite the awkward situation, they proceed to the party, with Alex maintaining his silence about his true financial status.

Throughout the encounter, Alex struggles to keep his wealth a secret while navigating the complex social dynamics with Kelly, who is interested in him, and Sharon, who is suspicious and protective of her friend.
