Alex confronts Michael Murdoch at his birthday celebration in the Nirvana nightclub. He easily overcomes multiple guards and security measures to reach Michael. In a tense confrontation, Alex physically threatens Michael, holding him out of a broken window. Michael warns that his father will seek revenge if he's killed. As the situation escalates, Lindsey Marvel, who owns the club, arrives and recognizes Alex. She's surprised to see him, having not met since he helped the Drake family. Lindsey had moved to Baltimore to start her own business, inspired by Alex. The episode ends with this unexpected reunion, leaving the confrontation between Alex and Michael unresolved.
