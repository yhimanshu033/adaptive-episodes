Alex faces unexpected consequences after his joke backfires. Maryann takes his offer seriously and kisses him, causing Alex to feel guilty and reject her advances. Meanwhile, Sophie informs Alex that her father, Dr. Isaiah, wants to give him the Woodside Clinic instead of having Sophie work as his servant for three years, as per their previous bet. Alex initially refuses the offer, feeling it's too valuable and that the bet was just a joke. However, Sophie insists that he must accept it to preserve her father's reputation and avoid her becoming his servant. She presents Alex with an ultimatum: either accept the clinic or have her as a servant for three years. Alex is left conflicted, regretting the bet and unsure how to proceed with this difficult choice.
