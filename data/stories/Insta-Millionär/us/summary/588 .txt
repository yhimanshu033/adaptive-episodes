Alex discovers the devastation caused by a truck crashing into the Woodside Clinic. He finds Celeste and another Moon Maiden injured. Enraged, he confronts the driver, who initially claims it was an accident but later reveals it was a deliberate attack, warning Alex of more to come. Alex and Sophie work to save Celeste's life using his healing powers and medical skills.

The police arrive and initially treat it as a drunk driving incident. However, the driver secretly admits to Alex that it was intentional, threatening more attacks. Later, the driver is mysteriously killed in a hit-and-run outside the hospital.

Alex, determined to confront the person behind the attacks, heads to a high-end club called Nirvana. He believes Michael Murdoch, who is celebrating his birthday there, is responsible. Alex learns that Michael's family, particularly his father Jeremiah, controls the pharmaceutical industry and has been sabotaging other healthcare corporations in Baltimore.

As Alex arrives at the VIP section of the club, he is stopped by security guards, setting the stage for a confrontation with Michael Murdoch.
