Alex encounters the mysterious Grandma Georgina Fox again, who displays supernatural abilities by killing a chicken with a blade and drinking its blood. Disturbed, Alex leaves without mentioning the upcoming visitors to the villa.

The next day, Alex meets Sue at the university to pretend to be her boyfriend to impress her mother. Sue provides Alex with rented expensive clothes and a fake watch to appear wealthy. They go to Sue's mother's house, where they meet Garry, another potential suitor for Sue.

At the house, Sue introduces Alex as her boyfriend to her mother, Sara. Sara is surprised but impressed by Alex's appearance. Garry, who was initially there to meet Sue, becomes competitive when he sees Alex.

Sara questions Alex about his background, and he follows Sue's instructions, claiming his family owns an internet company earning over a million dollars a year. Sara is delighted with this information, while Garry becomes skeptical and points out the expensive nature of Alex's outfit, unaware that the clothes are rented and the watch is fake.

The situation becomes tense as Garry tries to discredit Alex, setting up a potential conflict between the two men as they compete for Sue's attention and Sara's approval.
