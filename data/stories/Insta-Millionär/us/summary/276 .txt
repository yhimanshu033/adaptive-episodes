Alex Ambrose prepares to fight for the Clifton family against Damon Walker of the Steadman family in a decisive underground boxing match that will determine control of Baltimore's criminal underworld. Jessop Clifton and his family arrive at the venue, with Alex joining them later. Debbie remains unaware of the fight, planning a lakeside outing with Alex.

Chelsea Wood, a police officer, follows Alex suspiciously but is invited to witness the fight after learning about its tacit approval from city leaders. At the venue, the Cliftons face cold treatment from former allies who now support the Steadmans, believing their victory is assured.

When Alex is revealed as the Cliftons' fighter, many are shocked and dismayed, thinking him no match for Damon. The Steadman family, including Art, Chris, and the wheelchair-bound Julian, arrive confidently. Art Steadman mocks the Cliftons for choosing such a young, seemingly inexperienced fighter, believing victory is already theirs. The stage is set for a high-stakes confrontation that will reshape Baltimore's criminal landscape.
