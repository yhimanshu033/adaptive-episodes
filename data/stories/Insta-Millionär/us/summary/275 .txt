Alex Ambrose, now working as a security guard at Johns Hopkins University while his fiancée Debbie studies there, learns that the fighter chosen by the Steadman family, Damon from the Blood Brothers gang, has easily killed Paul Novak, the Clifton family's strongest warrior. This news shocks the Baltimore underworld and martial arts communities, shifting support towards the Steadmans.

Jessop Clifton and his family are deeply concerned about their chances in the upcoming fight. Rufus informs Alex about Damon's identity and Paul's death, expressing worry about Alex's chances in the fight.

Alex tries to hide his concern from Callisto and Luna, two girls who have been helping him at the university. He asks them to take care of Debbie on the day of the fight and not to tell her about it.

As the day of the fight approaches, most people in Baltimore's business and underworld circles believe the Steadmans have already won due to Damon's victory over Paul. The atmosphere at the underground boxing club, where the fight is to take place, lacks excitement as most attendees are there to show loyalty to the presumed victors, the Steadman family.
