Alex confronts a bomber at a press conference and manages to disarm him by tricking him into detonating his own bomb. The explosion causes damage but no casualties among the reporters and bystanders. 

After the incident, a female reporter named Lizzy Fairfield accuses Alex of murder, claiming he deliberately detonated the bomb to kill the bomber. Police officer Chelsea Woods, who seems protective of Alex, questions Lizzy's accusations and points out she has no evidence.

Lizzy then accuses Alex of destroying the reporters' equipment, but Alex explains he did so for safety reasons, as the bomb was disguised as a camera. When Lizzy suggests Alex might be involved with the bomber, Rufus Clifton intervenes, confirming Alex's innocence and explaining they were working with the police to investigate a series of bombings connected to the Clifton group.

Throughout the confrontation, Alex remains calm and confident, while the Moon maidens observe the situation with amusement. Chelsea appears to understand what really happened but doesn't pursue the matter further.
