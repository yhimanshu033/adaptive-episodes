Alex visits Lindsey in the hospital and they discuss her sister Leona's identity. Lindsey maintains that Leona is not Debbie, despite Alex's suspicions. After Lindsey is discharged, they spend the day together at an amusement park and museum.

Meanwhile, David Drake, from a powerful Washington family, returns from Europe. He's surprised when Leona, introducing herself as Lindsey's sister, meets him at the airport instead of Lindsey. David is immediately attracted to Leona and invites her to dinner.

Later, Alex and Lindsey are having a barbecue by the river when they're confronted by four thugs. The thugs demand money, mentioning an upcoming marriage between Lindsey and David Drake. Alex is surprised by this news. When the thugs escalate their demands, Alex tries to leave with Lindsey, but the situation turns threatening. The thugs surround them, with violent and sinister intentions towards both Alex and Lindsey.
