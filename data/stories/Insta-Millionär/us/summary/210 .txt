Alex encounters a mysterious woman in white named Zora Cook, who is revealed to be an enemy of Grandma Georgina. Zora demands to know Georgina's whereabouts, threatening Alex with her supernatural abilities and a robe woven with steel wires. Despite the threats, Alex refuses to disclose Georgina's location.

Zora discovers a bracelet in Alex's possession that connects him to Georgina. She takes it, hoping to lure Georgina to Washington D.C. Zora then asks Alex to help her kill Georgina, explaining that she is weaker in martial arts and fears for her life. Alex reluctantly agrees to help after Zora threatens to make things difficult for him.

Zora begins teaching Alex martial arts techniques, intending for him to assist her in the fight against Georgina. They practice for over an hour, with Zora guiding Alex through the movements. She insists that Alex sleep in her room for protection from Georgina.

Meanwhile, Fergus, who was injured in a previous confrontation, is in the hospital. When questioned by David about his failure, Fergus lies and blames Alex for his injuries, not mentioning the woman in white. David becomes angry, viewing Fergus as a liability to his plans.
