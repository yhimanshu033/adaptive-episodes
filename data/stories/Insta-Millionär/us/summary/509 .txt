Alex Ambrose, severely injured after his confrontation with Jacob, is believed to be defeated and in hiding. Kenneth takes control of the Baltimore Martial Arts Association, stripping Alex of his presidency and installing his son Todd as acting president. Jacob, having demonstrated his superior skills, gives Alex a week to prepare for a final battle, intending to use this as a message to all martial arts clubs across the country.

The news of Alex's defeat spreads quickly, with many celebrating his downfall, including the Thornton family. However, some groups, like the Appalachian Alliance and the Baltimore Martial Arts Association members, are deeply affected by the news.

Despite the rumors and celebrations of his enemies, Alex spends the week in intense, painful training and healing at a secluded spot on Clark's property. He experiences a transformative process, feeling himself grow stronger and his inner force evolving.

As the day of the battle approaches, Alex's allies gather at the Fairfax compound, uncertain of his condition and expecting the worst. Just as they consider searching for him, Ava arrives with armed men from the arbitrator's office, creating a tense situation.
