In this episode, the fight between Alex and Jacob reaches its climax. Alex demonstrates extraordinary powers, forming energy balls and using inner power that surpasses normal master levels. Jacob, despite his cloud skills and experience, finds himself overwhelmed and unable to effectively counter Alex's attacks.

As the fight progresses, Alex shows a mix of powerful energy-based attacks and skilled hand-to-hand combat. He appears to be enjoying the battle, even holding back at times to prolong it. Jacob, on the other hand, struggles to defend himself and begins to feel exhausted and outmatched.

The spectators are amazed by the display of martial arts and supernatural abilities, recognizing Alex's clear superiority. Hugo Sherridan, growing impatient and angry, demands that Alex stop "playing around" and finish the fight. He threatens to declare Alex the loser if he doesn't comply.

Finally, Alex decides to end the battle. He unleashes a powerful strike that sends Jacob flying into a wall, cracking his ribs. As Jacob struggles to get up, Alex approaches to deliver a final blow, kicking him hard. The fight concludes with Alex's decisive victory, shocking the onlookers and leaving Jacob severely injured.
