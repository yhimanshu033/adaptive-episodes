In this episode, Alex finds himself in a tense situation at the Ruby Hotel in Washington D.C. during a gathering of former Texans. The event, organized by Lester Yates, takes an unexpected turn when Alex's mother, Flora, arrives with Ken Stokes and hotel owner Frank Dreck.

Flora intervenes when Lester and others are mocking Alex for his apparent poverty. She confronts Myriam, slapping her for her earlier comments about Alex. Flora then challenges Lester, who had been boasting about his connections to Ken Stokes.

The situation escalates when Lester, unaware of Ken's true identity, continues to insult Alex and Flora. He even calls for others to help him fight them. However, Ken and Dreck step in to protect Alex and Flora.

Lester, still not recognizing Ken, insists on getting revenge for his humiliation. The episode ends with Dreck revealing Ken's true identity to a shocked Lester, who realizes he has made a grave mistake in his boasting and treatment of Alex and Flora.

Throughout the confrontation, Alex struggles to maintain the secrecy of his relationship with Flora, given the Ambrose family's reclusive nature, while also dealing with the emotional impact of seeing his mother after seven years.
