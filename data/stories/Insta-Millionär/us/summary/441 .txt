Alex arrives at Fairview Island and is cautiously received by his family, with Lincoln allowing him to visit his injured mother Flora. Alex uses his healing abilities to dramatically improve Flora's condition, surprising the doctors. After ensuring his mother's recovery, Alex leaves the island, determined to seek revenge against those who attacked his family.

Meanwhile, at Dominic Mitchell's luxurious villa near Baltimore, a meeting of DominiCorp executives and allies is taking place. They discuss their ongoing assault on the Ambrose family, but receive news that the Ambroses are recovering faster than expected. Dominic remains confident, revealing that he has identified a weakness in the Ambrose family structure - their lack of official involvement in their own company.

As Dominic prepares to elaborate on his plan, he notices his son Connor is absent from the meeting, causing him frustration.
