Alex confronts Billy, asserting his newfound confidence and power. He mocks Billy's attempts to bully him and emphasizes how insignificant Billy truly is. The principal interrupts, informing Billy and Leonard that they have been fired by order of the education bureau. Billy, shocked and angry, threatens to use his uncle's influence to fight back.

Unexpectedly, Ronald Cartwright and the director of the education bureau arrive at the school. They confirm Billy's termination and express their disgust with his behavior. The director lists Billy's numerous offenses, including his inaction during the fire, scheming against colleagues, bullying others using his uncle's influence, and inappropriate behavior towards female teachers and students.

Faced with public humiliation and the loss of his job, Billy breaks down and screams, threatening to tell his uncle and seek revenge. However, it's clear that his threats are empty and that he has lost all credibility and power at the school.
