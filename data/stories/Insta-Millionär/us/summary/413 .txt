Alex rushes to save Heidi, who is trapped on a balcony during a fire at the school. He confronts two skilled attackers, Amy and James, who are trying to kidnap Heidi. Despite their advanced training and weapons, Alex manages to defeat them in a intense fight. James is accidentally shot, and Amy commits suicide with a cyanide capsule to avoid capture. Alex successfully rescues Heidi using a curtain to pull her to safety. The students watching from below cheer for Alex's heroic actions, while the teaching director Billy is criticized for his cowardice. Heidi, overwhelmed with relief, hugs Alex before realizing she's only in her pajamas and pulls away embarrassed.
