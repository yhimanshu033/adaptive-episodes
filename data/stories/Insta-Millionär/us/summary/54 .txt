The episode continues the story of Alex and Debbie after the dramatic events at Graham Stirling's dinner. Cathy and Billy are arrested for the theft of $350,000 from the university, clearing Alex and Debbie's names. Alex thanks Ken Stokes for his help in resolving the situation and ensuring Debbie's reinstatement at the university.

Alex and Debbie return to Preston University and visit a food market. They encounter Maddison Robbins, who is now working as a hot dog vendor after losing her family's support. Maddison apologizes to Debbie, and Debbie decides to forgive her, showing compassion despite their past conflicts.

Meanwhile, Billy is bailed out by his father, who angrily tells him to find a way to repay the stolen money. Cathy and Billy's relationship becomes strained, with Billy treating Cathy poorly due to their financial situation.

The next day, Alex learns about Billy's theft being publicized on the school's website. He observes Billy confronting Cathy in the cafeteria, angrily accusing her of abandoning him the previous night. To everyone's surprise, Cathy calmly tells Billy that she wants to break up, ending their relationship.
