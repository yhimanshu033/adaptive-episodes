In this episode, new developments unfold in the ongoing conflict between various martial arts factions. A spy hired by Chris Steadman reveals crucial information about Debbie's connection to the Clifton family. Video evidence shows Debbie referring to Jessop Clifton as "grandpa," suggesting she is his granddaughter. This revelation puzzles Chris, as Debbie was believed to come from a poor background. He considers using this information to his advantage against the Cliftons.

Meanwhile, in Canada, the Black Orchid cult performs a disturbing ritual involving young women locked in a cabin. The cult leader, Cassius Sumter, expresses concern that their rituals are ineffective without the "chosen one" and the Phoenix jade pendant. He fears the gods' anger may lead to global destruction. After praying for guidance, Cassius interprets a shooting star as a sign that the chosen one is in the eastern United States. He orders his priests to find and bring this person to him, believing it crucial for appeasing their gods and preventing worldwide catastrophe.
