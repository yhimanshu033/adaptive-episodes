Alex intervenes when Dr. Troy Wilkinson attempts to give aspirin to Maryann Robinson, who is experiencing a medical emergency. Alex warns of potential complications due to suspected asthma, but is dismissed as he's not a doctor. Dr. Wilkinson administers the aspirin, and Maryann initially seems to improve. However, she soon suffers a severe asthma attack, confirming Alex's suspicions.

As Maryann's condition worsens and the medical professionals are unable to help, Melvin Strang desperately seeks out Alex for assistance. Alex returns and uses his special abilities to heal Maryann, placing his hands on her chest and gradually restoring her breathing to normal.

The crowd applauds Alex's quick thinking and effective treatment, while the paramedics feel ashamed for doubting him earlier. Dr. Wilkinson is left angry and embarrassed, realizing he nearly caused a tragedy and potentially angered the influential Robinson family. Alex advises that Maryann should avoid returning to the park due to the risk of another attack.
