Alex returns to the villa with Chinese food for Minnie and Hannah. They ask him to help with a video, which he agrees to. Minnie has Alex put on a life jacket and rubs dirt on his face, promising him $200 for his help.

Minnie starts a live broadcast, showcasing the villa as belonging to her rich relatives. She then introduces a storyline about a homeless man living nearby. She pretends to search for this man and eventually "finds" Alex sitting on the steps.

Minnie presents Alex to her viewers as a poor, homeless man. She feeds him the egg fried rice he bought earlier, forcing it into his mouth when he resists. She gives him money and offers to let him bathe in the villa, all while portraying herself as extremely kind and generous to her audience.

After ending the broadcast, Minnie becomes angry with Alex for not cooperating fully with her act. She complains to Hannah that Alex almost ruined her broadcast by resisting her attempts to feed him and give him money, making her look bad in front of her viewers when she was trying to help him (in the fictional scenario she created).
