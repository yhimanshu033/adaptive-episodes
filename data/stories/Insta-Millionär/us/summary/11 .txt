In this episode, Rose Scott receives news that her family's problem has been resolved. She initially believes one of her friends at the dinner table might have helped, but after questioning them, she realizes it was likely Zane, another friend she had contacted earlier. Rose calls Zane, who confirms that he asked his father to intervene through his connection with William Chase, a powerful businessman.

Grateful for Zane's help, Rose invites him to join the group at Rookie's Lodge. When Zane arrives, he is greeted with admiration by the others due to his apparent connections. Rose is particularly appreciative and affectionate towards him.

As the group rearranges to make room for Zane, Rose rudely asks Alex to leave, stating that she didn't invite him in the first place and that he should return to his dorm. This action further emphasizes the contrast between Alex's perceived low status and the respect given to those with apparent wealth and connections.

The episode highlights the social dynamics within the group, the importance of connections in their world, and the continued mistreatment of Alex despite his hidden wealth and influence.
