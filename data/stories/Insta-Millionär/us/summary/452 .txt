Alex continues his high-stakes game at the casino on the cruise ship. In the final round, he bets 60 million dollars on black. The man in white, visibly angry, allows a randomly selected guest to spin the wheel. Initially, the ball appears to land on red, causing the casino director to celebrate prematurely. However, in a shocking turn of events, the ball mysteriously moves to black without anyone touching it.

Alex wins the game, having won three rounds in a row as agreed. He mockingly tells the casino staff that they shouldn't count their chickens before they hatch. The man in white and the director are left stunned and unable to explain what happened. Alex revels in their confusion, knowing they can't comprehend how he achieved this feat.

Despite suspicions of cheating, the casino cannot refuse to pay out as Alex followed all the rules and had no opportunity to manipulate the wheel directly. Alex claims his winnings of 120 million dollars and suggests settling the payment downstairs. The credibility of the casino is now at stake, leaving the management with no choice but to honor the results.
