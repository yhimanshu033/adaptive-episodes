Alex, still convinced that Leona Marvel is actually Debbie, waits outside Arlington Heights to see her. He overhears security guards discussing the Marvel family's efforts to find Leona a husband. A wealthy young man named Darryl Brennan arrives to take Leona on a date. When Leona appears, Alex tries to approach her, insisting she's Debbie, but Darryl intervenes and takes Leona away for dinner.

Frustrated and determined, Alex follows them on a rented bicycle to Eden, one of the best restaurants in Washington D.C. His appearance at the upscale establishment, soaking wet and with a rental bike, draws scorn and ridicule from other patrons. As Alex tries to enter the dining area, he's stopped by the maître d', who questions his presence due to his disheveled appearance.

Throughout the episode, Alex faces constant mockery and disbelief from others due to his apparent low social status, but remains focused on his goal of reconnecting with who he believes is Debbie.
