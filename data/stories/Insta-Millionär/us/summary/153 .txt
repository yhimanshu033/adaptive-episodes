Alex and Minnie attend a party on a luxury cruise ship with other internet celebrities. The event is hosted by Lee Dempsey, a wealthy young man who recently broke up with his girlfriend Angelina Sanders after a public scandal.

During the party's livestream, Minnie and Alex are called on stage to discuss their viral video from the previous night, where Alex pretended to be a homeless man. After their appearance, Lee becomes interested in them and asks to speak with them privately.

When they meet, Lee questions their relationship and the authenticity of their story. Minnie, pressured by the situation, lies and claims Alex is her boyfriend. Lee then interrogates Alex, learning his name and that he's a student at Preston University.

Lee, recognizing the Ambrose surname and harboring negative feelings towards it, criticizes Alex for pretending to be a beggar to help his "girlfriend" become an internet star. He suggests that Minnie might abandon Alex when she becomes successful.

The encounter ends with Lee dismissing them, telling them not to cross paths with him again. Minnie and Alex return to their seats, both confused and shaken by the interaction.
