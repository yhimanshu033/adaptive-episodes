In this episode, Michael Murdoch is desperately trying to save his sister Louisa, who has been poisoned and is close to death in Baltimore Central Hospital. Louisa, who is the chairperson of Greenson Biotech and financially supports Michael, is crucial to the Murdoch family. As she drifts in and out of consciousness, she repeatedly calls for someone named "Alex," which confuses and angers Michael, who doesn't know who Alex is.

The Baltimore Chamber of Commerce sends Harvey Kingston, a Ghost with expertise in toxins and medicine, to help Louisa. Harvey examines Louisa and confirms that she has been poisoned with a rare and complex toxin that has spread throughout her body, bringing her close to death. Michael promises Harvey anything he wants in exchange for saving his sister, but Harvey claims he's not interested in money.

The episode highlights the tension between the various parties involved, including the hospital staff, the Chamber of Commerce representatives, and the Murdoch family. It also introduces the possibility of a connection between Louisa and the mysterious Alex, adding another layer to the ongoing storyline involving the Ghosts and supernatural elements.
