Alex enters the classroom, surprising the students by avoiding their trap. The students, led by Eric, attempt a second prank by offering Alex a cake, cigars, and beer, all of which are tampered with. Alex outsmarts them by forcing Eric onto the glue-covered chair and disposing of the tainted gifts. He then asserts his authority over the class and leaves abruptly.

Heidi, sitting at the back, recognizes Alex as the man who recently saved her. She's conflicted about whether to reveal this information.

The students are initially shocked by Alex's behavior, but Eric encourages them to plan future pranks. However, Heidi suddenly stands up and demands that no one try anything against Mr. Ambrose again, threatening consequences if they do. She then rushes out to find Alex.

Eric, realizing the shift in dynamics due to Heidi's intervention, tells the class to abandon any plans against the new teacher. The students are confused by this sudden change, but Eric explains it's because of Heidi's warning, implying her influence over the class.
