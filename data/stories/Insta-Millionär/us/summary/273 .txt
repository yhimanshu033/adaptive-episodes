Alex Ambrose, a former store employee turned millionaire, is now involved in a complex conflict between two powerful families in Baltimore - the Cliftons and the Steadmans. The Steadmans have challenged the Cliftons to a fight, with the winner gaining control of the city's underworld.

Art, representing the Steadman family, contacts Tyson, the leader of the Blood Brothers gang, to request their best fighter for the upcoming battle. Tyson agrees to send Damon Walker, a highly skilled fighter trained by Lee Harshaw in California.

Damon, originally from Baltimore but raised in California by Lee, is chosen for this mission. He has mixed feelings about returning to his birthplace but is determined to complete the task and promote the Blood Brothers' reputation.

The Blood Brothers gang has a long history, founded by Hu Juli, a Chinese immigrant who sought revenge after his family was massacred in New York. The gang has always aimed to settle old scores on the East Coast.

Damon arrives in Baltimore and meets with Art and Chris Steadman. Despite their attempts to brief him and allow him to rest, Damon insists on confronting his opponent, Paul Novak, immediately. He is extremely confident in his abilities and declares that the only outcome of his mission will be the death of his opponent.

This development adds a new layer of tension to the already volatile situation between the Cliftons and Steadmans, with Alex potentially caught in the middle of this dangerous conflict.
