Alex Ambrose, a former security guard turned supernatural protector, is abruptly removed from his mission to protect Heidi at Oak Ridge High School. The school descends into chaos after his departure, with students rebelling and causing destruction. Billy, the new class teacher, is overwhelmed and facing potential job loss.

Meanwhile, a professional agent named Jude Hutton takes over Heidi's protection. Jude is confident in his abilities and dismissive of Alex's methods, considering him an amateur. He believes the school is the safest place for Heidi and decides to use her as bait to draw out the enemies.

However, Jude's plan backfires when intruders successfully infiltrate the school and manage to capture Heidi. As Jude rushes to intervene, he finds seven people dragging Heidi out of the building. One of the kidnappers comments on how easy the abduction was compared to the previous failed attempt, which had resulted in a fire and deaths.

The situation highlights the contrast between Alex's hands-on approach and Jude's overconfidence, suggesting that the agency's decision to replace Alex may have been a mistake.
