In this episode, the situation at the Ruby Hotel takes a dramatic turn when Ken Stokes' true identity is revealed. Lester Yates, realizing his mistake, kneels before Ken and begs for forgiveness. Flora, Alex's mother, takes charge of the situation and punishes the group that had been mocking Alex. She forces them to eat their meals off the floor without utensils as retribution for their behavior.

Alex, showing compassion, allows Phillipa to leave. He then joins his mother and Ken in a private room where he has an emotional reunion with Flora after seven years apart. During their conversation, Flora expresses her desire for Alex to give up his search for Debbie and accept his position within the Ambrose family. However, Alex reveals that he has found Debbie, though she doesn't recognize him, and insists on pursuing her despite the potential consequences.

Flora attempts to persuade Ken to help Alex secretly, but Ken is hesitant due to strict orders from the family. Alex reassures his mother that he will resolve the situation and bring Debbie into the family. The episode highlights the complex dynamics between Alex, his mother, and the expectations of the Ambrose family, as well as Alex's determination to be with Debbie despite the obstacles.
