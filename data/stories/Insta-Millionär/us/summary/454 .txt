Alex returns home to find Debbie has created a warm, welcoming environment. They share a tender moment and enjoy a home-cooked meal together. Debbie shows concern for their health by replacing strong alcohol with light beer. Alex is deeply moved by Debbie's consideration and her vision of their future together.

They discuss going to bed, leading to a brief misunderstanding about intimacy. Alex realizes Debbie meant only sleeping, which makes him love her even more. He sleeps on the floor out of respect for their commitment to wait until marriage.

The next morning, Alex visits Oak Ridge High School, where he's now respected as the principal. He feels uneasy about the students' admiration, believing they should think for themselves.

Unexpectedly, Alex receives a call from his old schoolmate Joe Hall, who invites him to see his new house in Baltimore. This call brings Alex back to his past, contrasting with his current extraordinary life.
