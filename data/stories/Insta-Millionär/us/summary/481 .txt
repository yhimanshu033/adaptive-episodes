In this episode, tensions escalate at a high-society banquet at the Hill Hotel. Michael Murdoch discusses plans to eliminate Alex with other guests, dismissing offers of help and insisting on handling the situation himself. His sister Louisa expresses concern and tries to dissuade Michael from confronting Alex, hinting at a possible romantic interest in Alex.

Suddenly, Alex dramatically enters the banquet room, having overpowered security guards outside. He demands to see Michael, shocking the guests with his audacity. Louisa is secretly excited to see Alex in person, finding him even more attractive than in videos.

Alex taunts Michael, calling for him to greet him. The other guests are incredulous at Alex's behavior, considering it foolish and arrogant. Edwin, one of the guests, confronts Alex and demands he apologize to Michael. In response, Alex throws a glass of wine in Edwin's face and tells him to get out of the way, further escalating the tense situation.
