Alex and Theo continue their intense fight, with Theo demonstrating exceptional skills but ultimately being outmatched by Alex. Despite Theo's efforts, including using grenades and knives, Alex proves to be the superior fighter. The battle ends with Alex fatally stabbing Theo through the heart.

Oscar Steadman arrives at the Mitchell house in time to witness the end of the fight. Alex then returns to the villa to confront Dominic Mitchell, who is trapped under rubble. Dominic pleads for his life, offering loyalty and his family's resources. Alex dismisses these offers and kills Dominic by crushing him with a large piece of rubble, vowing to eliminate the entire Mitchell family for attempting to kill his mother.

Meanwhile, Connor Mitchell and his friends celebrate their perceived victory over the Ambrose family at a hotel. They discuss how the Ambrose family will likely be absorbed by other powerful families. However, one cautious friend warns Connor not to underestimate the Ambroses, mentioning rumors about Alex being Lincoln Ambrose's grandson. Connor dismisses these concerns, confidently stating that anyone who opposes his family will end up dead.
