Alex transfers to Richmond University, posing as a poor student to continue his search for Debbie. He meets his new roommates and attends classes, maintaining his cover story. Meanwhile, Leona Marvel, from a wealthy family, also transfers to the university's music department, attracting much attention.

Alex tries to approach Leona but is rebuffed. He observes her for several days, hoping to build a relationship. However, he soon sees her getting close to Colin, a popular and wealthy student. This causes Alex distress, as he still believes Leona might be Debbie.

Alex decides to participate in the university's New Year's party after learning Leona will perform there. At a rehearsal for the party, Colin and Leona are given preferential treatment due to their status. They perform a duet, with Colin impressing everyone with his singing until he hiccups during his performance. While others try to suppress their laughter, Alex openly laughs at Colin's embarrassment, unable to contain his amusement.
