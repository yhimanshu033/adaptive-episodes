Alex Ambrose returns to the scene of the hostage situation with only five minutes left until the deadline. Despite skepticism from Director Abbott and Ash Thornton, Alex decides to attempt a rescue. As time runs out, the authorities prepare to shell the building as a last resort. Just as they launch the shell, Alex is seen flying out of the factory roof, carrying a heavy burden. In an incredible display of skill and power, Alex manages to intercept the shell mid-air, redirecting it to land safely on the roof without detonating. This astonishing feat leaves everyone at the scene in disbelief, unable to comprehend what they've just witnessed.
