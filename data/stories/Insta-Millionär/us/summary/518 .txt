In this episode, Alex and Jacob engage in an intense battle at the Canfield Arena. Alex surprises everyone by demonstrating his mastery of the Moon Palace's powers, countering Jacob's attacks and even knocking him down. As the fight progresses, Jacob becomes increasingly uncertain of his ability to win, realizing that Alex's power and skill far exceed his expectations. The onlookers are amazed by Alex's abilities, and the atmosphere in the arena is tense with anticipation.

The episode ends with Alex challenging Jacob to continue the fight, while Jacob reassesses his strategy, now aware that he is facing a formidable opponent who might be capable of destroying him.
