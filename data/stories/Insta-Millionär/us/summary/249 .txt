Alex and Debbie arrive at the Clifton family mansion in Baltimore to meet Debbie's grandfather, Jessop Clifton. They are led through the house by Rufus, who introduces them as interns to avoid revealing Debbie's true identity. Upon meeting Jessop, Debbie confronts him about her mother's death. Jessop shows Debbie photos of her mother Cynthia growing up, demonstrating their close relationship. This softens Debbie's stance, and she agrees to call him "grandfather."

Jessop expresses joy at finally meeting Debbie and serves her mother's favorite foods. When Alex introduces himself, Jessop reveals he knows about Alex's family and recent disownment. Alex and Debbie request Jessop's blessing for their marriage, but he asks for a three-year waiting period to assess Alex's character. They reluctantly agree.

Debbie presses Jessop for answers about her mother's death, but he refuses to explain, citing potential danger to the family. Rufus supports this, warning Debbie that asking such questions could be dangerous. The meeting ends with Jessop instructing Debbie to never reveal her true identity, even to other family members, hinting at deeper secrets within the Clifton family.
