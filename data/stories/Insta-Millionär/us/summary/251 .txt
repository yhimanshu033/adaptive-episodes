Alex applies for a job as a security guard at Johns Hopkins University. The head of campus security, Tony Lewis, initially dismisses Alex due to his youth and a desire to hire cheaper, less qualified workers. Tony attempts to have his current staff discourage Alex, but one guard named Don supports Alex's application. In retaliation, Tony fires Don.

Alex and Don leave the building, and Don explains his anemia and difficulty finding suitable work. Alex, determined to get the job, makes a phone call to Rufus Clifton.

Meanwhile, Tony is pleased with himself for getting rid of both Alex and Don. He indulges in fantasizing about college girls when he receives a call from Rufus Clifton. Tony is immediately frightened, knowing Rufus's reputation and influence in Baltimore and Maryland. The episode ends with Tony wondering why such a powerful figure would be contacting him.
