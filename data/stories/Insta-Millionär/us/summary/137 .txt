Alex reluctantly agrees to pretend to be Sue's boyfriend to help her avoid an arranged date set up by her mother. The next day, Sue will pick him up from Preston University.

At his villa, Alex encounters Sheldon and Jade Brown, a couple who had previously mocked him. They apologize and ask to rent his villa for a day to impress Jade's mother. Alex agrees without charging them, as it coincides with his plans to help Sue.

Inside the villa, Alex checks on the mysterious elderly woman who has been staying with him. He finds her meditating and in distress, spitting blood. She mentions her deteriorating powers and expresses fear of someone named Zora Cook finding her before she recovers.

When Alex tries to inquire about Zora, the woman becomes suspicious and hostile. She reveals past betrayal by a man and her distrust of men in general. As Alex attempts to ask more questions, the woman, named Georgina Fox, uses her powers to throw him against the wall, warning him not to pry into her affairs. Alex is shocked by her strength, even in her weakened state.
