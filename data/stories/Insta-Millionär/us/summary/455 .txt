Alex attends a housewarming party at his old college roommate Joe's new villa in Baltimore. The house is worth $12 million, and Joe has invited many guests. Alex reunites with his former roommates Ben and Max, as well as Joe's fiancée Suzan. However, the atmosphere turns hostile when the other guests notice Alex and his friends' casual attire, which stands out among the well-dressed socialites.

Suzan gives Alex and his friends a tour of the house, revealing that they had to take out a large mortgage and receive help from their parents to afford it. As they finish the tour, a group of wealthy women confront Suzan, criticizing her for inviting people like Alex and his friends. They suggest that associating with such people could lower the value of her house and damage her reputation.

Ben becomes angry at the women's comments, but Max restrains him to avoid causing a scene. Alex remains calm, observing the women's snobbish behavior and reflecting on how their luxurious appearance contrasts with their poor attitudes. He decides to try to prove them wrong if given the opportunity.
