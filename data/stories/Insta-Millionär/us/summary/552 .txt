Alex and Maryann encounter a group of people at the Robinson Winery who appear to be infected with a virus similar to rabies. Alex quickly deduces that these individuals were likely hired by competitors to sabotage the winery. He helps manage the situation, calling for medical assistance and police.

The infected people are diagnosed with a form of rhabdovirus, requiring lifelong treatment. Some of them beg Alex for help, revealing that they were hired by the Blue Bottle Winery, owned by Louisa Murdoch.

Maryann thanks Alex for his help and offers him a job, which he politely declines. She then explains her difficult situation with her family. Two years ago, she refused an arranged marriage, and her family challenged her to make the winery successful within two years or be forced to marry an oil baron. They withdrew their support and forbade her from using the family name on the products.

Maryann reveals that she has only three months left to increase the winery's net worth from about $5 million to $100 million, or she'll lose her freedom. She pleads with Alex for help, explaining that she can't turn to her family for assistance as they want her to fail.
