Alex returns to the Moon Palace, unaware of Debbie's critical condition. At the hospital, Dr. Howard informs Jessop that Debbie's mysterious illness is worsening, and he fears she won't survive if it continues. Jessop vows to fight the Blood Brothers with everything he has.

Meanwhile, at the Moon Palace, Alex is visited by an old blind woman who served the previous master, Georgina. After sharing memories of Georgina, the woman leads Alex to a secret cave in the mountains. The cave contains ancient martial arts diagrams on its walls, and the woman explains that all masters of the Moon Palace must train there twice a year to grow their power. Alex realizes this is an exclusive area unknown to other disciples.

The episode highlights the contrast between the celebration at the Moon Palace and the dire situation with Debbie, emphasizing Alex's absence during this critical time. It also introduces a new element of the Moon Palace's secrets and potential for Alex to develop his powers further.
