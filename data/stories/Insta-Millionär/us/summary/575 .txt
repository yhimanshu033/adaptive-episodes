Alex Ambrose, unexpectedly revealed as the chairman of Robinson Winery, attends a gathering where he confronts Derek Lloyd, a powerful manager who was harassing Cassidy Pike. After a heated exchange, Alex calls to have Derek fired. Initially, Derek and others mock Alex's attempt, believing he lacks such authority. However, Maryann Robinson arrives and confirms Alex's position, firing Derek on the spot. She introduces Alex as the chairman who rarely interferes with company affairs but whose decisions must be implemented when he does. Elaine Wallace, Derek's partner, challenges the decision and threatens to quit, citing the potential loss to the company. Maryann responds by firing her as well. The episode ends with both Derek and Elaine dismissed from Robinson Winery, shocking everyone present and solidifying Alex's unexpected authority within the company.
