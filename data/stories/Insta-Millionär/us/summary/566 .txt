In this episode, tensions escalate at the Woodside Clinic as the doctors, led by Dr. Ann Glover, confront Alex with demands for higher salaries, equity shares, and control over the clinic's management. They threaten to leave and open a competing medical center if their demands are not met.

Alex responds by challenging their self-importance, stating that the clinic's reputation made them successful, not the other way around. He criticizes their focus on money over patient care and demonstrates his medical knowledge by correctly diagnosing patients in the waiting room, exposing misdiagnoses made by the doctors.

Alex also reveals that many of the clinic's medicines are low-quality and near expiration, crushing pills to prove his point. This causes unease among the patients and staff.

The doctors, feeling insulted, decide to resign immediately. Dr. Glover warns patients that they will be blacklisted if they continue to seek treatment at Woodside Clinic. All the doctors, along with their assistants, pharmacists, and other staff members, remove their white coats and walk out of the clinic, leaving Alex to face the consequences of this mass exodus.
