In this episode, Alex demonstrates his superior strength and skill against Drew Walton, one of the "Ghosts" who challenged him. Despite Drew wielding the supposedly powerful "Bloody Blade," Alex easily deflects his attacks and humiliates him in front of everyone. Alex slaps Drew multiple times, even using Drew's own hand to hit himself, and effortlessly dodges his sword attacks.

Drew is left physically and emotionally defeated, realizing that his reputation in the martial arts community is ruined. He attempts one last attack with the Bloody Blade, but Alex catches it between his palms and delivers a powerful kick that cracks the floor tiles.

Frustrated and ashamed, Drew asks Alex to kill him, hoping to become a martyr for the Ghosts' cause against the Baltimore Martial Arts Association. However, Alex refuses to grant this wish. Instead, he uses the Bloody Blade to inflict non-fatal wounds on Drew's arms and legs, leaving him alive but injured.

Throughout the confrontation, Alex's immense power and control are evident, shocking the onlookers and the other Ghosts present. The episode ends with Drew defeated and bleeding, while Alex maintains his dominance over the situation.
