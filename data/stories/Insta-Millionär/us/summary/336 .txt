The episode describes a chaotic and violent scene at the Clifton family banquet. Four warrior priests from the Black Orchid Cult have invaded and are attacking the family members. Despite the Cliftons' numerical advantage, they are no match for the priests' superior fighting skills.

Rufus Clifton attempts to organize a defense, positioning younger family members at the front and older ones at the back. He tries to boost morale by claiming Alex will return soon. However, the priests easily defeat the family's fighters, leaving over 100 people unconscious.

As the situation worsens, Rufus desperately searches for an alarm to call for help. Some family members make valiant efforts to fight back, with one managing to wound Zeke, one of the priests. However, Zeke brutally retaliates, breaking the man's arm and leg.

Eventually, Jessop's alarm goes off, giving the family a glimmer of hope. However, Zeke quickly destroys the device. The priests demand to know Jessop's whereabouts, but Rufus remains silent. With no other options left, Rufus decides to fight the priests himself, knowing they need Alex's help to have any chance of survival.

Throughout the episode, the family's desperation grows as they face the seemingly unstoppable force of the four warrior priests, with many family members injured or unconscious.
