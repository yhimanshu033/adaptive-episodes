Alex encounters Xavier Myers, a mysterious man from Georgina and Zora's past. Xavier recognizes Alex's fighting style as Georgina's and learns of her and Zora's deaths. Alex explains his quest for the healing orchid and the siege on the Moon Palace. Xavier agrees to help, feeling indebted to Georgina's memory.

Upon seeing Debbie, Xavier becomes excited, mistaking her for Cynthia Clifton, a woman he once cared for and fell in love with. He uses his internal power to temporarily improve Debbie's condition. Debbie, however, doesn't remember Xavier, and it's revealed that Cynthia was actually her mother who died 20 years ago.

Devastated by this news, Xavier realizes he has wasted years waiting for a woman who had long since passed away, while also pushing away Georgina and Zora who truly cared for him. Feeling cursed and damned, Xavier decides to transfer all of his internal power to Alex.

Xavier believes that with his power added to the combined strength of Georgina and Zora already within Alex, it will be enough to defeat the attackers at the Moon Palace. This act serves as Xavier's attempt to reconcile with Georgina and Zora posthumously, uniting their powers within Alex.
