Alex visits Ben's dormitory to support him as his sister Tanya is coming to visit. Ben has bought expensive clothes on credit to impress his sister, as he's been lying about his financial situation. Alex and his friends go with Ben to pick up Tanya and her friend Britney at the airport.

At the airport, two men harass Tanya and Britney, but Ben, Alex, and Carl arrive in time to rescue them. The group then goes sightseeing in New York, visiting Times Square and the Empire State Building. Ben spends most of his money on snacks and souvenirs.

Britney and Tanya expect Ben to take them to an expensive restaurant for dinner, putting him in a difficult position as he can't afford it. Carl suggests a cheaper option, The Seafood Bar, but Britney dismisses it as too low-class. As the situation becomes tense, Alex intervenes and suggests they go to Yellow Garden, an expensive restaurant, much to Ben's shock and dismay as he cannot afford it.
