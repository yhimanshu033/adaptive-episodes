Alex accepts the offer to take over the Woodside Clinic. Sophie gives him a tour, explaining the clinic's layout, patient volume, and profitability. She mentions that her father, Dr. Isaiah, often donates his time to help poorer families.

Upon entering the clinic, Alex notices the staff chatting idly instead of attending to patients. Dr. Ann Glover, mistaking Alex for Sophie's assistant, dismisses Sophie rudely and orders her to work in the pharmacy. The other doctors and staff show disrespect towards Sophie, ignoring her attempts to introduce Alex.

Dr. Glover threatens to resign if Sophie tries to assert authority and instructs the staff to make Sophie dispense medicine alone as punishment. Alex offers to help Sophie in the pharmacy.

Alex quickly memorizes the locations and details of nearly a thousand medicines. When prescriptions arrive, he efficiently prepares seven at once, impressing Sophie with his speed and knowledge. However, Dr. Glover interrupts, demanding to know who authorized Alex to handle the medications.
