Alex confronts Luther Duncan and the Blood Brothers gang at the Shamrock Hotel. After a shootout, Alex demonstrates his supernatural abilities by avoiding bullets and disarming the gang members. Luther is shot by his own man, Harley Gomez, who sees him as weak for begging for his life earlier.

Alex gives the gang members a chance to shoot him, but they fail. He then offers to spare their lives, but when threatened by Harley, he changes his mind. Alex allows one man to escape as a messenger to Tyson Slade, while he deals with the rest of the gang members in the soundproofed room.

After Alex leaves, Art Steadman sends someone to clean up the scene. It's revealed that Art had sent the two women who were killed earlier to capture Luther for interrogation about his son's death. Art now suspects that Alex and the Clifton family might be responsible for Chris's death, rather than the Blood Brothers.
