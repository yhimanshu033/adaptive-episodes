Alex Ambrose, now the leader of the Moon Palace, remains unaware of the brewing conflict in the eastern United States. Meanwhile, the Blood Brothers, led by Tyson Slade, are preparing for a major assault on the east coast martial arts community. 

Lee Harshaw, eager for revenge after Alex's presumed death, is granted permission to go to Baltimore alone and begin targeting key figures. Tyson sends a threatening message to martial arts organizations in the east, declaring their intent to seek vengeance for a century-old betrayal.

Art Steadman is contacted by Tyson and informed of Lee's imminent arrival in Baltimore. Tyson then focuses on his advanced training facility, which combines cutting-edge technology with traditional martial arts elements.

Lee intensifies his training, vowing to avenge Damon's death and make their enemies pay. The martial arts community in the northeast is thrown into turmoil, with various groups preparing for the impending conflict.

The stage is set for a major confrontation between the Blood Brothers and the eastern martial arts organizations, with Baltimore as the initial battleground. Alex and the Moon Palace, being in the Appalachian region, remain oblivious to these developments for now.
