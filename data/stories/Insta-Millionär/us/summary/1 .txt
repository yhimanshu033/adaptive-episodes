In this episode, Alex Ambrose, a delivery worker at Trader's Joe, receives a call to deliver condoms and tissues to a hotel room. Upon arrival, he discovers his girlfriend Cathy with another man named Billy. Cathy breaks up with Alex, citing his lack of money as the reason. Humiliated and dejected, Alex leaves the hotel.

Shortly after, Alex receives a text message informing him that he has met the conditions to receive his family inheritance. It's revealed that Alex is actually from a wealthy family, but had been living without access to his fortune for the past seven years.

The next day, Alex visits First Republic Bank, an exclusive bank for wealthy clients. He accidentally bumps into a young woman while entering. The bank's assistant manager, Karen Matthews, is skeptical of Alex's presence due to his appearance. When Alex states he's there to withdraw money without a bank card, he's met with derision.

As Alex attempts to enter the VIP lounge, which is reserved for high-value clients, Karen tries to stop him. However, Alex manages to enter the room, leaving Karen flustered and concerned about potential consequences.
