In this episode, Billy and Leonard plan to set up Alex by catching Felicity Wright in the act of bullying other students and blaming Alex for not intervening. Meanwhile, Eric and Lance arrange for a group of thugs to confront Alex.

Eric deliberately damages Billy's car to lure Alex outside. When Alex arrives, he surprisingly doesn't scold Eric harshly, which confuses the student. Before their conversation can continue, Alex is called away to deal with another incident in the girls' dormitory.

Eric then meets with the boss of the hired thugs, who demands $50,000 for their services. When Eric can't pay the full amount, the boss suggests an alternative: Eric should bring some girls from his class to work at a club. The boss claims the girls will only need to stand and look pretty for customers, assuring Eric that nothing else will happen to them. Eric seems hesitant but considers the offer.

The episode highlights the escalating plots against Alex and the dangerous situation developing for the students at Oak Ridge High School.
