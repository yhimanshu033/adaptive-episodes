Alex and his allies storm the banquet hall where Todd Petrie is holding Debbie and other hostages. Alex confronts Todd, who initially mocks him and orders his men to attack. Alex easily defeats Ollie, a large boxer, by breaking his wrist. He then disarms two knife-wielding attackers effortlessly. As Alex approaches, Todd draws his sword and attacks, but Alex skillfully deflects the strike and turns Todd's own sword against him, stabbing him in the chest. The crowd is shocked by Alex's display of superior fighting skills and seemingly superhuman abilities. Throughout the confrontation, Alex remains calm and confident, demonstrating his newly acquired powers and combat prowess.
