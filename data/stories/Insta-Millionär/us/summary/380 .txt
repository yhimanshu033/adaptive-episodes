Alex Ambrose, now a security guard at a university, is approached by Ethan Cox and Celia with a job offer. Ethan initially acts arrogantly towards Alex, unaware of his true identity and powers. Alex demonstrates his authority, causing Ethan to apologize and show respect.

Celia attempts to explain their request: they want Alex to protect a student whose father, a top biological researcher, has been abducted. Eleven previous bodyguards have died trying to protect this student. The kidnappers aim to use the daughter as leverage to force the researcher to reveal his work.

Alex initially dismisses their request, but Celia persists, emphasizing the importance of the mission and the researcher's contributions to the country. As a gesture of goodwill, she offers Alex a special seed - a hybrid of two rare plants with restorative properties - developed by the kidnapped researcher.

Throughout the encounter, Alex maintains a calm and dismissive attitude, while Jessop, his associate, observes the situation. The episode highlights the contrast between Alex's unassuming appearance as a security guard and his true power and status.
