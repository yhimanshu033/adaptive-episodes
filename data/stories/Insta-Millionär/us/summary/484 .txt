Alex confronts Michael Murdoch at the banquet, stabbing his hands to the table and holding a knife to his throat. Louisa attempts to shoot Alex but fails. Police arrive, led by Steven (Louisa's uncle), Melanie (Michael's sister), and Deputy Director Randy Mills. They surround Alex and demand he release Michael. Alex remains defiant, threatening Michael to reveal Jonah's location. Under pressure, Michael discloses that Jonah is in Atlantic City, planning to leave the country. As Alex begins to text this information, Melanie shoots at him, but Alex uses Michael as a human shield, resulting in Michael being shot in the shoulder. The confrontation is interrupted by a voice from the hall entrance commanding everyone to stop.
