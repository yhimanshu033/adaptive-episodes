The episode focuses on the aftermath of the failed attempt to incite a strike in Class 6 against Alex Ambrose. Billy, Leonard, and other teachers wait outside the classroom, expecting chaos, but nothing happens. Lynne Bowen enters the class and conducts her lesson normally, much to Billy's surprise.

Alex confronts Billy and Leonard in the hallway, where Leonard mysteriously gets hit on the head. Billy and Leonard continue to threaten Alex, but he remains unfazed. As the day progresses, the anticipated strike never materializes, leaving Billy and Leonard frustrated and confused.

Later, Billy confronts Eric about the lack of action, but Eric rebuffs him rudely in front of other students. This embarrasses Billy and further undermines his authority.

The episode concludes with Alex announcing that Class 6 should gather at the running track. It's revealed that due to losing a bet about disruptions in Class 6, Billy and eleven other teachers now have to run fifty laps (20 kilometers) as punishment. Billy pleads with Alex to forget the bet, but Alex insists on following through, finding his new life as a high school teacher increasingly interesting.
