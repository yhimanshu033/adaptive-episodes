Alex attends a party at Joe's new villa in Baltimore, where he encounters various guests, including Rose, who shows interest in him. Alex tries to politely distance himself from her advances. He meets a group of men claiming to be from the Martial Arts Association, who boast about their leader's accomplishments. Unbeknownst to them, they are actually talking about Alex himself, sharing exaggerated stories of his feats. Alex listens with amusement and skepticism as they describe battles and connections he supposedly has, realizing these men likely have no real connection to him or the martial arts world they claim to be part of. When pressed for details, the men admit they've never actually met their leader, exposing their fabrications. Throughout the evening, Alex maintains his composure, observing the social dynamics and misconceptions surrounding his reputation.
