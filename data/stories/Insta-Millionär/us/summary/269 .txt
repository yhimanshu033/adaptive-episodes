The Steadman family launches a surprise attack on Dukes Casino, owned by the Clifton family, causing significant damage and injuries. This attack is seen as a declaration of war between the two families. Jessop Clifton, the head of the Clifton family, discusses the situation with his sons and associates. They suspect the Steadmans have allied with the Blood Brothers gang, potentially giving them an advantage.

Chris Steadman arranges to meet with Jessop the following evening. Jessop and his son Rufus discuss their options, including the possibility of asking Alex Ambrose for help. Jessop is hesitant due to concerns about Debbie's safety but ultimately agrees to invite Alex for dinner.

Rufus calls Alex to invite him to dinner the next evening. Alex is worried about the purpose of the meeting, especially since it concerns Debbie. The situation is tense, with both families preparing for a potential all-out war for control of Baltimore's underworld.
