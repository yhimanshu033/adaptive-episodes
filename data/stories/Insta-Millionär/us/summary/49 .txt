In this episode, Alex encounters a group of beautiful women on Harmony Island who are all girlfriends of his younger brother, Justin Ambrose. The women discuss their relationships with Justin, revealing that he spends only a few days a year with each of them. They express curiosity about Justin's older brother, not realizing that Alex is present.

When Alex accidentally makes his presence known, the women mistake him for a worker and verbally abuse him. A particularly beautiful woman named Freya Morrison arrives and trips over Alex's crutch, causing them both to fall in a compromising position. Freya, furious, threatens to call security and have Alex punished.

Just as the situation escalates, Justin arrives on the scene. The women immediately flock to him, showering him with affection. Freya complains to Justin about Alex's alleged misconduct, prompting Justin to confront Alex angrily. However, Justin suddenly recognizes Alex as his older brother, leading to a moment of shock and realization.

This episode highlights the complex dynamics between Alex, Justin, and Justin's girlfriends, setting the stage for potential conflicts and revelations about the Ambrose family's relationships and status.
