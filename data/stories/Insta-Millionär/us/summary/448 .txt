Alex and Debbie plan to board the Paradise cruise ship in Atlantic City for a relaxing vacation. While discussing expenses, Alex overhears a conversation about the ship's casino, where a man lost millions. Intrigued, Alex borrows $5 million from Rufus to investigate.

Upon boarding the ship, Alex uses his powers to bypass security. He observes the luxurious and decadent atmosphere inside. A portly man informs Alex about the casino's high stakes and its connection to the Chamber of Commerce, revealing it as a money laundering operation.

Alex enters the casino and exchanges his $5 million for chips. He approaches a roulette table and places a large bet of $300,000 on black, surprising other gamblers with his casual approach despite his modest attire. He loses the first bet but immediately places another $300,000 on red, drawing attention from spectators and nearby gamblers.

The episode ends with the croupier spinning the wheel for Alex's second bet, leaving the outcome uncertain.
