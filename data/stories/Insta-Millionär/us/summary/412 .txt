Alex confronts the wealthy parents of Class 6 students, demanding $5,000 each for their children's meals. The parents, though surprised, comply to avoid embarrassment. Meanwhile, two professional assassins, James Arnold and Amy Rodgers, plan to abduct Heidi from the school.

That evening, the assassins break into the girls' dormitory, causing panic. During the chaos, a fire accidentally starts, triggering the fire alarm. Students evacuate, but Heidi ends up dangling from a balcony while trying to escape.

Outside, students urge Mr. Wilde (Billy) to rescue Heidi, but he refuses, claiming it's too dangerous and that the fire brigade will handle it. The situation remains tense as Heidi's life hangs in the balance.
