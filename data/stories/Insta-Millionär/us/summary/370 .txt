Alex, Debbie, and Heidi encounter the Baltimore Horse Gang at a riding stable. The gang, led by Micky, demands $2000 from Heidi for riding their horses. When she refuses, Micky suggests she and Debbie spend the night with him and his friends instead. A group of students warns them about the gang's violent reputation.

Heidi, frustrated with Alex's inaction and the students' fear, confronts the gang. She challenges their authority and refuses to pay. When Micky tries to touch her, Heidi unexpectedly fights back, punching him in the face and kicking him in the chest. Micky is thrown out of the building and seriously injured.

This display of skill surprises everyone, including Alex and Debbie, who wonder about Heidi's true identity and abilities. The other gang members back off, now fearful of Heidi. The students are impressed by her beauty and fighting prowess, seeing her as an ideal girlfriend.

Throughout the confrontation, Heidi shows frustration at being mistaken for a young student and insists she's a grown woman. Alex and Debbie remain confused by Heidi's enigmatic nature and unexpected martial arts skills.
