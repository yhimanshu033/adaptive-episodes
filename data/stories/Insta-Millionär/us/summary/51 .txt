Alex meets with his grandfather Lincoln Ambrose on Harmony Island. Alex impresses Lincoln by showing compassion and asking for leniency for Ken Stokes. Lincoln offers to send Alex to a prestigious university, but Alex prefers to stay at Preston. After recovering, Alex and Debbie leave the island and return to New York. 

Alex is invited to a dinner by Graham Stirling, a manager in the family business. At the hotel, Alex realizes Graham has invited many guests, contrary to his expectations of a small dinner. To maintain a low profile, Alex introduces himself as "Adam Childs" to the other guests. 

During the banquet, Alex and Debbie sit at a table far from the stage. They are joined by Cathy Fairweather and her companion Billy Wilde. Cathy recognizes Alex and Debbie, referring to Debbie as a "peasant," and is surprised to see them at such an event. The episode ends with Cathy wondering how Alex and Debbie managed to get invited to this high-profile gathering.
