Alex meets Myriam at a restaurant, but it turns out to be a trap. Myriam's boyfriend Jake attacks Alex, but Alex easily defends himself. Myriam then reveals she was using Jake and breaks up with him. Darryl Brennan appears and kicks Jake, revealing he and Myriam were working together to set up Alex. Darryl and Myriam leave together, taking Alex's backpack containing Debbie's diary.

At a hotel, Darryl and Myriam see messages from David Drake, the wealthiest young man in Washington, who has returned and is asking about Leona Marvel. Darryl becomes interested in Debbie's diary after seeing its contents.

Later, at a gathering of wealthy young people at the Continental Hotel, David Drake asks about Leona Marvel. Jason Morley explains that Leona recently gained attention due to her beauty, and the Marvel family is now looking for a husband for her. The wealthy young people are intrigued by Leona's sudden prominence in Washington society.
