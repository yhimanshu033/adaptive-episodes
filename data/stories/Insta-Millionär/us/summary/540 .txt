Alex receives news of Debbie's disappearance and rushes to Baltimore. He meets with Rufus at the mall where Debbie vanished, learning she was likely abducted while using the bathroom. As they leave the mall, they realize they're being followed. A high-speed car chase ensues on the freeway, involving multiple vehicles.

Alex skillfully maneuvers his car, causing one pursuing vehicle to crash. He then confronts another car, breaking its window and retrieving a bag containing a walkie-talkie and crossbow with special red bolts. These bolts are revealed to be highly explosive when fired at road signs and trees.

The situation escalates as more cars join the pursuit, with attackers firing explosive bolts at Alex's car. Alex, prioritizing the safety of Rufus and Will, decides to focus on escaping rather than confronting the pursuers directly. The chase intensifies, with multiple cars now firing explosive bolts at Alex.

Alex realizes the situation in Baltimore is more dangerous than anticipated, finding himself under attack shortly after arriving to search for Debbie. The episode ends with Alex trying to outmaneuver the pursuers while grappling with the severity of the situation.
