Alex and his group, including Rose, Zane, Suzan, and Joe, go to an amusement park for a day out. During their outing, they encounter Luciel Brennan, who had previously caused trouble for Rose's family. Rose, emboldened by her family's newfound connection to William Chase, humiliates Luciel by making him kneel and having Zane slap him.

After enjoying various rides at the amusement park, the group goes to see a movie. Rose receives a call from her father about visiting Zane's family, but she lies about being in a relationship with Zane to avoid going home.

After the movie, Zane suggests staying at a hotel instead of returning to the university. Despite Suzan's reservations, Rose agrees to stay with Zane. At the luxury hotel, Zane books rooms for everyone, deliberately giving Alex the cheapest single room.

As the others go to their rooms, Alex remains in the lobby, contemplating whether he should tell Rose the truth about Zane's deception. A well-dressed man enters the lobby and recognizes Alex, addressing him with unexpected respect and surprise, hinting at Alex's hidden status.
