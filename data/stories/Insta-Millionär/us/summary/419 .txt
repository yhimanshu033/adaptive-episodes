Alex is approached by his students from Class 6, who have come to plead for his help in rescuing Heidi. Moved by their trust and concern, Alex agrees to assist. Celia calls Alex, who informs her he's changed his mind and will help. A helicopter picks up Alex and brings him to the command center near the factory where Heidi is being held hostage.

At the command center, Alex meets Ash Thornton, a renowned martial artist from a prominent Baltimore family. Ash, who had been unavailable during the Blood Brothers crisis due to training, is now assisting with the hostage situation. There's immediate tension between Alex and Ash, with Ash dismissing Alex's potential contribution.

Director Gavin, still antagonistic towards Alex, tries to discourage his involvement by praising Ash's abilities. Celia attempts to introduce Alex as an equally skilled martial artist, but Ash responds with cold pride and skepticism about Alex's capabilities. The situation becomes tense as Ash makes disparaging remarks about needing to "scrape the bottom of the barrel for heroes." Celia confronts Ash about his attitude, setting the stage for potential conflict between the two martial artists as they prepare to rescue Heidi.
