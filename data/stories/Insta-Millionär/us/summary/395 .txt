In this episode, the situation at Oak Ridge High School escalates after Alex's surprising payment for Class 6's meals. Billy Wilde, the school director, finds himself in a difficult position after challenging Alex to pay for meals for all students on the ground floor, which would cost him $3 million. Unable to follow through, Billy attempts to back out, causing outrage among the students.

The students, particularly those in Class 6, begin to mock and criticize Billy for his dishonesty. The situation quickly deteriorates as students throw food at Billy and Leonard Winters as they try to escape the cafeteria. Alex remains calm throughout the incident, maintaining his offer to pay for Class 6's meals.

Lance and Eric, students who had been plotting against Alex, are now unsure of how to proceed given Alex's growing popularity. Eric suggests calling someone to beat up Alex, but Lance is hesitant.

Meanwhile, Billy and Leonard, now humiliated, discuss how to undermine Alex's position at the school. They decide to target Alex through Class 6, planning to catch a student named Felicity Wright collecting "protection fees" from other students in the girls' dormitory. Their goal is to make Alex look negligent in his duties as head teacher, hoping this will lead to his removal from the position.
