Alex discovers that the conflict between Georgina and Zora stems from a misunderstanding about Xavier's affections. Both women believed Xavier loved the other, leading to years of hatred and rivalry. As they argue, the situation escalates into a physical fight.

Alex tries to mediate, refusing to help either woman harm the other. Both Georgina and Zora attempt to persuade Alex to join their side, offering various rewards, but he remains neutral, wanting both to survive.

The fight intensifies, with Alex intervening to prevent serious injury. The battle moves into a lake, where Georgina attempts to use her internal power against Zora. Alex blocks the attack, but inadvertently becomes a conduit for both women's powers.

As their energy flows through Alex, his body temperature rises dangerously, and he begins to lose consciousness. Despite the perilous situation, both Georgina and Zora remain focused on defeating each other, even as all three sink deeper into the lake.
