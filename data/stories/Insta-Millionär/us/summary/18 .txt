Alex encounters Cathy at the university library, where she attempts to manipulate him by wearing a dress he bought her and acting sweet. However, Alex remains indifferent to her advances. Frustrated, Cathy creates a scene, falsely claiming that Alex abandoned her after she sacrificed everything for him. This draws attention from other students who criticize Alex.

Cathy then reveals her true motive: she's interested in Alex's supposed lottery winnings of $150,000. Alex sees through her scheme and rebuffs her attempts to reconcile. Desperate, Cathy fabricates a story about breaking up with him for his own good, claiming she couldn't bear to see him work so hard for her.

Alex pretends to believe her and offers to transfer money to her. Cathy, thinking she's succeeded, declines the immediate offer, hoping for more opportunities in the future. As they walk around campus, they encounter a charity fundraiser for poor children. Alex donates $130,000, shocking everyone present.

Cathy, realizing Alex has given away most of his supposed lottery winnings, becomes furious. When Alex suggests they can still afford to eat at the cafeteria with his remaining money, Cathy insults him and storms off, revealing her true materialistic nature. Alex, having anticipated this reaction, reflects that Cathy has no idea about his actual wealth, which far exceeds the amount he donated.
