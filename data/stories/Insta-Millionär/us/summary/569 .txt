Alex takes Sawyer to the hospital chapel in his wheelchair. During their conversation, Sawyer asks personal questions about Alex's family and childhood. Alex reveals he has no childhood memories and feels hurt by his parents' lack of involvement. Sawyer then inquires about Alex's feelings towards adoptive versus biological parents.

Suddenly, they sense danger in the courtyard. Sawyer unexpectedly demonstrates extraordinary martial arts skills, effortlessly defeating multiple attackers with daggers. He warns the assailants not to harm Alex, shocking both Alex and the attackers with his prowess.

The defeated attackers retreat, and one of them reports their failure to Mr. Murdoch (Steven). Steven is surprised to learn that Sawyer, not Alex, was responsible for thwarting the attack.

Back at the hospital, Sawyer asks Alex about taking over the Woodside Clinic. Alex admits the clinic is struggling, with no patients and staff having left on his first day. This conversation reveals Alex's unexpected journey into running a medical facility.
