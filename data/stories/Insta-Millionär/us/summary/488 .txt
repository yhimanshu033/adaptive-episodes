Alex meets with Clark to discuss the situation with Michael Murdoch. Clark assures Alex he will handle Michael. Alex receives a text from Jessop informing him that Rufus has located Jonah.

Alex goes to the waterfront where Rufus and the Cliftons have intercepted Jonah's yacht. Jonah and his remaining men are captured. Alex confronts Jonah, who tries to bargain for his life by offering money and assets. Alex refuses, stating he wants to see Jonah dead.

Jonah reveals he is the adopted son of Jacob Randolph, the leader of the Ghosts, and warns Alex of severe consequences if he kills him. Alex remains unimpressed and physically assaults Jonah. As Alex prepares to kill Jonah, Jonah frantically pulls out his phone and connects Alex to Jacob Randolph. Jacob's voice comes through the speaker, saying they should talk.
