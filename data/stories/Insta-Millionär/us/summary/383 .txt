Alex Ambrose, a new teacher at Oak Ridge High School, attends a staff meeting where he immediately clashes with other teachers, particularly Billy and Leonard. Alex's confident and confrontational attitude surprises and offends many of his colleagues. He is assigned to teach Class 6, a notoriously difficult group of senior students.

Alex makes a bold bet with the other teachers: if anything goes wrong on his first day, he'll resign, but if he succeeds, the other teachers must run 50 laps around the school track. The teachers, skeptical of Alex's abilities, agree to the bet.

After the meeting, a math teacher named Lynne Bowen approaches Alex privately. She warns him about the challenges of teaching Class 6, explaining that the students are children of rich and powerful people who believe they can do whatever they want. Lynne reveals that the previous teacher was hospitalized due to an incident with these students.

Despite Lynne's concerns, Alex remains confident in his ability to handle the class. Lynne also thanks Alex for standing up to Billy and Leonard, who have been making life difficult for the other teachers. She explains that many of her colleagues are grateful but afraid to express it openly.
