Alex Ambrose comes to the rescue of the Fidelity Group, which was on the brink of bankruptcy. He makes two crucial phone calls to secure the necessary funds:

1. He contacts Jessop Clifton, who agrees to lend him $250 million immediately.

2. Alex then calls the Sultan of Brunei, who provides the remaining $250 million.

With $500 million secured, Reginald Drake, chairman of the Fidelity Group, is able to renegotiate contracts with their business partners. He offers increased rates to maintain partnerships, which proves successful. The company manages to regain 80% of its business contracts, with the remaining 20% lost due to government intervention.

The crisis is averted, and the Fidelity Group is saved from bankruptcy. This turn of events thwarts the plans of Colin Patterson and Noah Freeman, who had hoped to take over the Drake fortune.

The episode ends with the room full of relieved and jubilant executives. Lindsey draws attention to Alex's crucial role in saving the company, and he is hailed as a hero by everyone present.
