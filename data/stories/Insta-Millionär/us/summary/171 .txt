Alex, unable to pay for an expensive meal at the restaurant Eden due to his frozen accounts, leaves to find a solution. He attempts to contact Ken Stokes and others for help but fails. Outside, he encounters a poor woman selling corn and is reminded of Debbie's mother, Carla. He buys corn with his last $10, recalling a story about Debbie's childhood and her mother's struggles.

Returning to the restaurant, Alex prepares the corn while Darryl cooks shrimp. When presented with both dishes, Leona (whom Alex believes to be Debbie) tries Darryl's shrimp first and compliments it. However, upon tasting Alex's corn, she becomes emotional and starts crying, unable to explain why.

When asked which dish she prefers, Leona surprisingly chooses the corn. This shocks everyone, especially Darryl. Alex, more convinced than ever that Leona is actually Debbie, urges her to remember her past, explaining that corn was her favorite food due to a childhood memory involving her mother. The episode ends with Alex pleading with Leona to admit she is Debbie, while the other diners are left confused by the unexpected turn of events.
