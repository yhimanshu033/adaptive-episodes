In this episode, Michael Murdoch, grateful for Harvey Kingston's help in saving his sister Louisa, offers to support Harvey's clinic. As Louisa recovers, she calls out for "Alex" in her sleep, which angers Michael. Jonah reveals that this might be Alex Ambrose, a martial arts expert who caused trouble for a casino he's involved with.

Michael, protective of his sister and suspicious of Alex's involvement, asks Jonah to find someone to deal with Alex quietly. Jonah explains that Alex isn't easy to eliminate, as a previous assassination attempt failed.

Michael then proposes a deal to Harvey: if he kills Alex Ambrose, Michael will not only fund Harvey's clinic but also provide a lab for producing "The Grail" without involving the Ghosts. He suggests using missing people from Baltimore as a source for the plasma needed.

Initially, Harvey declines, stating he's a doctor, not a killer. However, Michael persists, offering all his family's power and influence in exchange for eliminating Alex Ambrose. The episode ends with Michael making this tempting but dangerous proposition to Harvey.
