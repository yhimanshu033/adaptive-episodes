Alex receives confirmation that the issue with Rose's family has been resolved quickly through his family's intervention. Rose, unaware of Alex's involvement, invites him to dinner with friends to celebrate. Despite her continued hostility towards Alex, he attends the dinner.

At the restaurant, Rose publicly embarrasses Alex by recounting his incident at the bank. She then boasts about how her family managed to get Donald Brennan, the chairman of Trader Joe's, to personally apologize to her father. Rose's friends are impressed by her family's apparent influence.

Curious about who helped her family, Rose calls her father on speakerphone to ask which powerful connection he used. However, she suddenly remembers that earlier her father had said he couldn't do anything to make Donald give in, making her wonder if someone else might have intervened.

Throughout the dinner, Alex remains quiet and interacts minimally with the group, sticking to conversations with his close friends Joe and Ben. The contrast between Alex's hidden power and his public persona continues to be a central theme.
