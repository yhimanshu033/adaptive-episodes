Alex Ambrose, a former store employee, inherits $86 million and disguises himself as a poor pancake seller at Richmond University. He becomes involved in complex relationships and is appointed Lord of the Moon Cult. Alex gets engaged to Debbie, who is revealed to be Cynthia Clifton's daughter. Their wedding is interrupted, and they travel to Clifton Manor.

The conflict between the Steadmans and Cliftons escalates, with Alex defeating Damon Walker of the Blood Brothers in a fight, shifting power in Baltimore to the Cliftons. The Blood Brothers plan revenge, while Art Steadman seeks their help to reclaim Baltimore.

Meanwhile, Noah Freeman secretly works with Colin Patterson to bring down the Drake family. Their plan succeeds, causing the Fidelity Group to face massive losses and potential bankruptcy. David Drake struggles to manage the financial crisis, rallying support from wealthy friends.

Nathan Ambrose instructs Colin Patterson to buy the Fidelity Group for $35 billion, potentially shifting power dynamics in Washington D.C.

In the latest episode, David's friends show up at the Fidelity Group headquarters to publicly support the Drake family. Charles Marvel, posing as David's father-in-law, also expresses support. Colin Patterson and his father Joseph observe from afar, with Colin preparing to take control of the Drake family's assets. Noah Freeman informs Colin that he has convinced eleven executives to resign, further damaging the Fidelity Group.
