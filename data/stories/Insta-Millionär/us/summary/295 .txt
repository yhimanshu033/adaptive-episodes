In this episode, Reginald Drake reveals to Noah Freeman the true reason why he could never be in charge of the Fidelity Group. It stems from an incident 30 years ago when Noah was jailed for an encounter with the daughter of the mayor of Washington D.C. The mayor had been watching Noah ever since and threatened to ruin their business unless Reginald kept Noah out of leadership positions.

Noah, realizing his mistakes, resigns from the company. Colin Patterson attempts to leave but vows to return and rule the city someday. Darryl and Myriam follow Colin, with Myriam showing a change in her attitude towards Alex.

Alex discusses the company's future with Reginald, who plans to rebuild and strengthen the Fidelity Group. Alex then flies back to Baltimore, encountering a beautiful blonde woman on the plane.

Upon arriving in Baltimore, Alex takes a taxi back to the university. Unknown to him, Chris Steadman and his henchman have been following him. Chris suspects Alex might be the disgraced son of the Ambrose family and is gathering information to potentially move against him.

As Chris contemplates his next move, he overhears the same beautiful woman from the plane mentioning Alex's name, adding another layer of mystery to the situation.
