Alex and Lindsey leave the Olympic stadium after the incident with the Ferrari. They spend time together in downtown D.C. and decide to have dinner at a restaurant called The Playful Hare. There, they notice a mysterious woman dressed all in white.

As they eat, other diners are asked to leave the restaurant. When the staff tries to forcibly remove the woman in white, Alex intervenes to protect her. He and Lindsey offer to take her to another restaurant.

However, the manager prevents them from leaving. As the situation escalates, a group of aggressive young men enter the restaurant, blocking their exit.

Meanwhile, David Drake visits Jason in the hospital and learns about Alex and Kelly Phillips. He decides to abandon his plan to win over Lindsey through Jason and instead opts for a more sinister approach, planning to have her kidnapped and drugged.

The episode ends with tension building in the restaurant as Alex, Lindsey, and the woman in white face a potentially dangerous confrontation with the manager and the newly arrived group of men.
