Here's a summary of the episode:

Alex agrees to go with a woman offering cheap accommodation, but it turns out to be a brothel. When he tries to leave, the pimp and madam threaten him. Nelly, the pancake seller Alex met earlier, intervenes and saves him by threatening to report them to the police.

Nelly offers Alex a job helping her sell food from her truck. They work together for 10 days, with Alex also searching for Debbie. One day, while Alex is away, a rude couple buys pancakes from Nelly without paying. As revenge, she puts shoe polish in their pancakes. 

When the couple returns angry, they try to force Nelly to eat the shoe polish. As they're attacking her, Nelly manages to kick the woman. The episode ends with the man going to help the fallen woman.
