Alex arrives at the Moon Palace with Debbie, who is still unconscious, to find it under siege by three united gangs led by Blaze Quinn. The gangs are threatening to take over the palace and harm the women there. Phoebe, temporarily in charge, is unsure how to respond to protect her sisters.

As the situation escalates, Callisto and Luna arrive, followed by Celeste, Selene, and Alex with Debbie. Alex claims to be a disciple of Georgina (keeping her death a secret) and confronts Blaze, offering him a chance to leave. Blaze refuses and a fight breaks out.

The Moon maidens struggle against Blaze and his men, with Callisto getting injured. Alex hands Debbie to Callisto and joins the fight, displaying impressive fighting skills reminiscent of Georgina's. He quickly takes down several gang members, shocking Blaze and the other gang leaders.

Realizing Alex's strength, Blaze calls on the other leaders to join forces against him. The situation remains tense as Alex faces off against the united gang leaders to defend the Moon Palace and its inhabitants.
