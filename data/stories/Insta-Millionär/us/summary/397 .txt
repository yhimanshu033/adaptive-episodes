Alex Ambrose, now working undercover as a teacher at Oak Ridge High School, addresses the school assembly regarding a recent incident involving Felicity Wright. To everyone's surprise, Felicity agrees to publicly apologize for her actions, shocking both students and teachers alike. This unexpected turn of events boosts Alex's reputation among the staff and students, while frustrating his rivals, Billy and Leonard.

However, the peace is short-lived as Lance, a troublemaker from Class 6, severely beats a substitute English teacher. When confronted by Alex, Lance denies any wrongdoing and asserts his dominance over Class 6, challenging Alex's authority. This incident demonstrates that despite Alex's initial success with Felicity, he still faces significant challenges in maintaining control over the problematic class and navigating the complex dynamics within the school.
