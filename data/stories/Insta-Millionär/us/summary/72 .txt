Alex and Karen are at a Japanese restaurant when Karen secretly drugs Alex's drink. As Alex becomes disoriented, Karen attempts to seduce him, undressing and making physical advances. Just as Alex is about to lose control, Zara, James, and others burst into the room, interrupting the scene.

Shocked by the intrusion and remembering Debbie, Alex tries to push Karen away. Karen, frustrated by the interruption, kisses Alex and continues her advances. James, enraged, smashes his bouquet of roses and physically attacks Alex, slapping him.

Karen rejects James's marriage proposal and diamond ring, showing her preference for Alex. Confused and guilt-ridden, Alex pushes past everyone and leaves the restaurant, repeatedly apologizing. The incident leaves everyone shocked and creates a complex situation involving jealousy, betrayal, and misunderstandings.
