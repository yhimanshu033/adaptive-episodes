Alex and Saul arrive at Julian's house, where Julian and Chris are celebrating with some girls. Chris sees them arrive and quickly warns Julian to follow his lead. When Alex and Saul confront them, Chris unexpectedly shifts all blame onto Julian, pretending he knew nothing about the plan to harm Alex. Julian, realizing Chris's strategy, reluctantly accepts responsibility to maintain his relationship with Chris's influential family. Alex, now focusing his anger on Julian, believes he is the mastermind behind the attack.
