Alex examines Kendall and eases her symptoms, but doesn't fully cure her. He suppresses her martial arts skills as a condition for helping her. Kendall, grateful for Alex's help, asks to work at the clinic. After initially ignoring her request, Alex eventually agrees to let her stay and work at the clinic, offering room and board with minimal pay.

Alex and Sophie struggle to recruit new employees for the Woodside Clinic. Alex takes advantage of the quiet time to train Kendall. He then visits Sawyer at Parkview Hospital for an examination and is surprised to find that Sawyer has recovered much faster than expected. Sawyer expresses deep gratitude to Alex for curing him and giving him hope for a normal life again.

Sawyer asks Alex to take him to the hospital chapel to light a candle in gratitude. Throughout their interaction, Alex notices that Sawyer looks at him strangely and seems reluctant to let go of his hand, hinting at a possible deeper attachment or admiration from Sawyer towards Alex.
