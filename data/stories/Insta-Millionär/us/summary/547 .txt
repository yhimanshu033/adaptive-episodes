Alex and Rufus discuss the ongoing search for Debbie and the Murdoch family's denial of involvement. While driving, they encounter Melanie Murdoch, who falls onto their car windshield, severely injured with a poisoned arrow in her back. Despite her attempts to attack Alex, he subdues her and takes her to the Fairfax compound for treatment.

At the compound, Alex tends to Melanie's wounds and explains his reasons for saving her: to find Debbie, acknowledge her hesitation at the amusement park, and protect her from those who attacked her for failing her mission. Melanie is confused by Alex's actions but remains wary.

Meanwhile, Ezra Murdoch leads a team to a run-down farm on the outskirts of Baltimore. They discover Debbie bound and gagged in a shed. Debbie, believing she's still being held captive, offers ransom money. Ezra asks if she's Debbie and then leaves to make a phone call, stating that her fate is not up to him.
