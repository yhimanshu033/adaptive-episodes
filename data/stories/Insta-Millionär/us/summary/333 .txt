Alex and Jessop Clifton descend into a secret underground palace beneath the Clifton estate. Jessop reveals the family's vast hidden wealth and offers Alex any item he desires, but Alex politely declines. Jessop then shows Alex a mysterious white jade phoenix, explaining its connection to the Black Orchid cult and the Clifton family's complicated relationship with them. Their conversation is interrupted by an emergency alarm.

Meanwhile, at the banquet, a group of performers led by Noel Jacobs enters the hall. The performance takes a sinister turn when the lion dancers reveal themselves as aggressive intruders. They knock Noel unconscious and threaten the Clifton family and guests. The leader, speaking with a Canadian accent, declares that everyone will die. The scene ends with tension as the intruders face off against the Clifton family members and security guards.
