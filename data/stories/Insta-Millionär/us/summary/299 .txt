Alex encounters Melissa at the university, where she attempts to apologize and make amends by bringing him soup. Debbie arrives, and Melissa, surprised to see her, quickly adapts her strategy to apologize to Debbie as well. Initially, Debbie is reluctant to forgive Melissa for her past actions, including her involvement with Lou Rork. However, after Celeste pushes Melissa into a puddle, Debbie's kind nature takes over, and she decides to give Melissa another chance.

Alex is hesitant to forgive Melissa but agrees to do so at Debbie's request. Debbie takes Melissa to her dormitory to help her dry off and change clothes. They spend some time talking, and Debbie leaves feeling happy about reconciling with Melissa.

Once alone, Melissa reveals her true intentions. She is actually working with Chris on a revenge plan against Alex and Debbie. Her apology and attempts at reconciliation are part of this scheme. Melissa is frustrated about having to kneel in the rain but sees it as a necessary step in their plan for revenge.
