Alex confronts Dr. Adams, suspecting him of being a member of the Ghosts, a secret organization of spies and assassins. Debbie supports Alex's suspicions by pointing out that Dr. Adams, as a graduate of Fleming Medical School, should be aware of the Ghosts. When pressured, Dr. Adams attempts to leave but is forcibly stopped by Clark Fairfax, who demands to see if the doctor has the Ghost's signature cherry blossom tattoo.

Dr. Adams initially resists but then agrees to remove his shirt. However, he suddenly attacks Clark with a scalpel. Clark, a skilled martial artist, quickly overpowers Dr. Adams, revealing the cherry blossom tattoo on his chest. Clark orders his men to take Dr. Adams away for questioning.

Clark explains to Debbie that the Ghosts are a secret organization operating in multiple countries. They infiltrate various institutions and aim to seize power by placing their members in influential positions throughout society. Their ultimate goal is to shape the world according to their desires.

The episode ends with Dr. Adams being taken away, having failed to activate a hidden cyanide capsule in his tooth, which was knocked out during the confrontation.
