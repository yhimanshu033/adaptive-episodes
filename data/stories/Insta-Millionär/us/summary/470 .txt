Alex and his group discover a toxic trap in the grave, containing a toad and a wooden token with dark energy. Alex destroys the token, believing it to be the cause of Sarah's affliction. The group discusses the implications of this deliberate placement of evil objects.

Meanwhile, at Baltimore Central Hospital, Michael Murdoch is desperately seeking help for his poisoned sister, Louisa. Despite consulting top doctors worldwide, no cure has been found. The hospital director mentions that the Ghosts, a skilled group, have been asked to assist. Michael threatens the director, demanding results.

Louisa's condition is critical, with the poison affecting her internal organs. A rare folk medicine has been keeping her alive, but it's becoming less effective and has dangerous side effects. Louisa experiences intense pain and hallucinations, crying out for help. The situation highlights the urgency of finding a cure and the potential consequences for the hospital if they fail to save her.
