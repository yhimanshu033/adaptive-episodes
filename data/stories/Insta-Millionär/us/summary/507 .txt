Alex confronts Kenneth Petrie and his associates at the Baltimore Martial Arts Association headquarters. When Ava, Kenneth's assistant, insults Alex and demands he kneel, Alex quickly defeats her in a brief fight, demonstrating his superior skills. He then faces Kenneth, refusing to back down from his decision to fight Jacob, the leader of the Ghosts.

As tensions escalate, they are interrupted by a commotion outside. Jacob's son has attacked the holding area and freed the captured Ghosts, including Marie. A battle ensues between the Ghosts and the Baltimore Martial Arts Association members.

Just as it seems the Association is about to win, a black SUV arrives, and a tall older man steps out, hinting at a new development in the conflict. Kenneth blames Alex for provoking Jacob and potentially endangering everything they've built, while Alex remains steadfast in his decision to confront the Ghosts.
