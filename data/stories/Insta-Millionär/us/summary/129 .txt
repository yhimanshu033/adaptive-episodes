In this episode, the tense situation at the Yellow Garden restaurant reaches a climax. Slayer, conflicted between his loyalty to Henry and his friendship with Alex, initially hesitates to follow Henry's orders to kill Alex. Henry, enraged by Slayer's disobedience, physically abuses him and tries to force him to attack Alex.

Alex encourages Slayer to stand up for himself, reminding him that he's not Henry's property. Slayer struggles with his identity and the trauma of his past, recalling instances of ridicule and rejection due to his appearance.

As the tension builds, Ben attempts to intervene but is easily subdued by Slayer. The confrontation reaches its peak when Slayer, remembering his promise of friendship to Alex, suddenly turns on Henry. He stabs Henry in the stomach, shocking everyone present.

Henry, mortally wounded, collapses to the floor, unable to comprehend Slayer's betrayal. As he bleeds out, Henry attempts to ask Cory for help but falls silent before finishing his plea. The episode ends with Henry's apparent death, marking a dramatic shift in the power dynamics and Slayer's allegiance.
