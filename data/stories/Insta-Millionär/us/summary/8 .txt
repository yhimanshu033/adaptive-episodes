Alex, now able to use his family's influence, contacts Mark, his family's butler, to address the situation with Donald Brennan of the Trader Joe's Group. Mark informs Alex that the family has lifted all restrictions on him and agrees to have Ken, the manager of the East Coast Division, handle the matter.

Meanwhile, efforts to help Rose continue. William Chase, a union president, abruptly ends an important business negotiation worth $125 million when he learns that Ken Stokes is about to call him regarding the Trader Joe's Group and Sea Fox Cargo Company issue. This demonstrates the significant influence of Alex's family connections.

Alex reflects on his past relationship with Cathy while sitting by Ramsey Lake on campus. His thoughts are interrupted by a call from Zara Fitzgerald, the captain of the school's cheerleading squad, demanding he come to the west sports field immediately. Alex, who serves as a "service assistant" for the cheerleading team, complies and heads to the location as requested.
