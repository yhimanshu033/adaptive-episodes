Alex and Debbie, along with Celeste and the Moon Maidens, are traveling to the Moon Palace in the Appalachian Mountains to find a cure for Debbie's worsening condition. During the journey, Debbie's health deteriorates rapidly, causing Alex great distress.

Callisto attempts to contact the Moon Palace but discovers it has been attacked by three gangs: Blood Sacrifice, Ice Edge, and Falling Souls. The gangs are taking advantage of the absence of leadership at the palace to try and occupy it.

As they near their destination, Debbie's condition becomes critical, with her vomiting blood. Alex becomes increasingly desperate to save her life. He tries to reassure Debbie, who is aware she's dying, that the special orchids at the Moon Palace will cure her.

The situation is complicated by the need to deal with the gang attack on the Moon Palace while also trying to save Debbie's life. Alex is torn between his responsibilities as the new lord of the Moon Palace and his desperate desire to save Debbie.

The episode ends with Debbie losing consciousness and Alex attempting to transfer his internal power to her in a last-ditch effort to keep her alive until they reach the Moon Palace and its potentially life-saving orchids.
