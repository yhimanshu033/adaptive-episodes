Alex receives a distressing call from Don's phone, answered by Saul, a dangerous enforcer sent to punish Alex and Don. Saul has brutally attacked Don in his small apartment, where Don lives with his sister Daisy. Don, a security guard at Johns Hopkins University, struggles financially to support himself and Daisy's education.

Saul breaks into their apartment and severely beats Don, threatening Daisy as well. As Saul is about to deliver a potentially fatal blow, Alex calls Don's phone. Saul answers and demands that Alex come to Don's apartment within 30 minutes, threatening to kill Don if Alex doesn't comply or if he involves the police.

The situation is dire, with Don severely injured and Daisy in danger. Alex is now faced with a difficult decision, knowing that going to the apartment could be dangerous, but not going could result in Don's death.
