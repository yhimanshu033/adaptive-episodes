Alex returns to Oak Ridge High School and is greeted by the Central District Education Bureau's director, who pleads with him to resume his position as head teacher of Class 6. After initial reluctance, Alex agrees to become an honorary head teacher with flexible duties, influenced by Heidi's emotional appeal. The director immediately orders the dismissal of Billy Wilde and Leonard Winters.

Upon arriving at the school, Alex and Heidi discover chaos in the school yard. Students, led by Class 6, have started a large fire using school furniture and textbooks. The entire school is in uproar, with students from other classes joining in and adding more items to the fire. Teachers and the principal are unable to control the situation, and their pleas go unheeded. Billy Wilde, as the current head teacher of Class 6, proves ineffective in stopping the rebellion. The scene highlights the complete breakdown of order and the urgent need for Alex's return to restore control.
