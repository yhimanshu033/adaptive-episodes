Alex takes Debbie, who has been poisoned with "black poison water," to the hospital. He decides not to tell her the full truth about her condition to keep her spirits up. The Moon girls suspect Melissa of poisoning Debbie and want revenge, but Alex's priority is finding a cure. Celeste suggests taking Debbie to the Moon Palace, where a special thousand-year-old orchid might save her. Alex agrees, and they fly to the Moon Palace the next day.

Meanwhile, Chris has someone monitoring Alex and Debbie at the airport. The spy reports that Debbie looks terrible and Alex is distraught. Chris and Melissa discuss the situation in a hotel room. Melissa believes Alex will commit suicide if Debbie dies, but Chris is impatient with the plan's progress. Chris questions whether Debbie might be from the Clifton family, but Melissa dismisses this idea. Melissa tries to get Chris to stay with her and discuss their wedding plans, but he leaves, claiming he has work to do. Melissa is left feeling frustrated but tries to reassure herself about their relationship.
