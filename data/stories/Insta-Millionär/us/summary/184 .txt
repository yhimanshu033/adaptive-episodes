Alex and Darryl engage in a high-stakes game of darts, with Alex's and Phillipa's participation in the Olympic volunteer program on the line. Darryl, known for his dart skills, starts strong, scoring 120 points in his first round. Alex struggles initially, scoring only 41 points, leading to mockery from onlookers.

In the second round, Darryl extends his lead, leaving him with only 41 points to win. Surprisingly, Alex matches Darryl's 140-point score, bringing his total down to 120. Despite this improvement, most spectators believe it was just luck.

Darryl, confident of victory, offers Alex a chance to concede and kiss his feet privately, threatening public humiliation if Alex continues. Alex refuses, and Darryl returns to throw his final round. Unexpectedly, Darryl struggles, missing his intended targets. Unknown to others, Alex secretly throws small knives at the dartboard, causing it to shake slightly and affecting Darryl's aim. This results in Darryl overshooting his target, leaving him with one point still to score.

The crowd speculates whether Darryl intentionally missed to be kind or to further humiliate Alex. Darryl, covering his mistake, challenges Alex to score 140 points in his final round to win, setting up a tense climax to their contest.
