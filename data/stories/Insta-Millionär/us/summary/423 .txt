Alex demonstrates his extraordinary abilities by intercepting a mortar shell fired at the factory and safely placing it on the roof without detonation. He then rescues Heidi from inside the building, carrying her out on his back. The special forces team enters the factory to find all the kidnappers have been eliminated in just two minutes.

Alex's success leaves everyone, including Ash Thornton and Director Gavin Abbott, stunned. Celia and Ethan criticize Gavin for his poor decisions that led to agent casualties. Alex returns with Heidi, who appears unharmed and peacefully asleep.

Alex then confronts Gavin, slapping him multiple times and lifting him by the neck. Despite protests from Ash and attempts by Gavin's subordinates to intervene, Alex continues to physically punish Gavin for his earlier actions and decisions. Celia and Ethan prevent others from interfering, allowing Alex to express his anger towards Gavin. The episode ends with Alex knocking Gavin to the ground, ignoring Ash's demands to stop.
