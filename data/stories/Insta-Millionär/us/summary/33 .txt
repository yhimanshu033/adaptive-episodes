Alex and Debbie encounter Donna and her boyfriend Darren at a university event celebrating donations for a village. Donna and Darren mock Alex and Debbie for their perceived poverty, with Darren claiming Alex is the poorest student in their class. They accuse Alex of stealing money to buy Debbie's clothes and criticize Debbie for accepting them.

The event's host announces the donation results, revealing that someone contributed over $100,000. This causes excitement among the attendees. Donna boasts about Darren's $350 donation and ridicules Debbie for only donating $5. When questioned, Alex admits to donating but doesn't specify the amount to avoid complications.

Donna and Darren continue to mock Alex, assuming he donated very little or nothing at all. Alex retaliates by reminding Darren of a past incident in a livestream where he was outdone by another donor. This angers Darren, but he restrains himself from physical confrontation.

The host then invites donors who contributed over $100 to come on stage for a speech and photo. Darren feels superior, believing his stage appearance will prove he's better than Alex, unaware of Alex's significant donation.
