Alex arrives at the location where Debbie is being held captive. He confronts Ezra Murdoch and his men, engaging in a fierce battle. Despite being outnumbered, Alex defeats Ezra's subordinates using his sword and combat skills. Ezra threatens to kill Debbie, but Alex remains undeterred.

As the situation escalates, some of Ezra's men recover and aim crossbows at Alex. In the ensuing chaos, they accidentally set the building on fire. Alex uses the smoke as cover to neutralize the remaining threats.

Ezra attempts to kill Alex with a poisoned dart but misses. In a final act of desperation, Ezra throws a knife at Debbie. Alex reacts quickly, killing Ezra with his sword to protect Debbie.

Will and Rufus arrive with paramedics. Debbie faints and is taken to the hospital for examination. Alex stays behind to help clean up the scene, regretting that he had to kill Ezra before getting answers.

As Alex stands by the road, trying to calm himself, a red sports car approaches and slows down in front of him.
