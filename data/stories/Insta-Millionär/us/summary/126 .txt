Alex encounters the mysterious old woman from the previous incident, now transformed into a beautiful teenage girl, at a diner. She causes a disturbance by criticizing the food and injuring a man who tries to flirt with her. When she refuses to pay for her meal, Alex intervenes to prevent further violence and pays her bill.

The girl, who Alex refers to as "Ma'am," reveals that she regained her youth by drinking Granger's blood. She asks Alex to let her stay at his place and provide her with live chickens daily. Despite his reservations, Alex agrees out of fear for his safety.

The girl instructs Alex to call her "Grandma" and accompanies him to his villa. On the way, they stop to buy a live chicken as requested. After dropping her off, Alex receives a call from Carl Cooper, inviting him to meet Ben's visiting sister.

The episode highlights the supernatural elements surrounding the mysterious woman and Alex's growing entanglement in dangerous situations beyond his control.
