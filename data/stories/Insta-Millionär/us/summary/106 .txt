Alex attends a family gathering with Kelly's wealthy family, the Phillips. The family, especially Margaret and Brian, are hostile towards Alex due to his apparent poverty. When Alex mentions ordering a Ferrari worth over two million dollars, the family becomes even more skeptical and mocking.

Brian, an ex-military member, challenges Alex about the details of his Ferrari purchase, trying to expose him as a liar. Alex remains calm and provides vague answers, further angering the family. They accuse him of making up stories based on recent news about Ferrari's CEO delivering a car to a mysterious buyer in America.

As tensions escalate, Brian threatens to physically remove Alex from the premises. Kelly tries to defend Alex, but he calmly faces Brian's threats. Alex warns Brian not to touch him, displaying an unexpected composure that unnerves Brian.

Just as the situation reaches a breaking point, a sapphire blue Ferrari arrives at the gathering, shocking the family and potentially validating Alex's claims. The episode ends with Walter, a family member, recognizing the car as a Ferrari, leaving the outcome of the confrontation uncertain.
