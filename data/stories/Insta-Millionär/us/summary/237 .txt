The episode revolves around the Casady family's desperate attempt to find an organ donor for Rome, who is in critical condition after a car accident. They decide to use Steven, who has recently returned but is confined to a wheelchair, as an unwilling donor for a kidney and cornea.

Rebecca and some family members abduct Steven from his home and bring him to the hospital. At the hospital, the family tries to force Steven to be a donor without his consent, claiming he doesn't need his organs due to his disability.

The doctors and medical staff are uncomfortable with the situation, insisting on proper consent. The Casady family attempts to manipulate the situation, even forging Steven's consent on donation documents.

Despite the ethical concerns raised by some medical staff, the family's influence seems to override these objections. Steven is taken for a medical examination, which confirms he is a suitable donor.

The episode highlights the family's disregard for Steven's autonomy and well-being, as well as the ethical dilemmas faced by the medical staff in such a situation.
