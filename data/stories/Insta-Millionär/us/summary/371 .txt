In this episode, the conflict with the Baltimore Horse Gang escalates as Micky orders his men to attack Heidi. Heidi reveals her hidden sword and demonstrates impressive fighting skills, taking down several gangsters. As more thugs join the fight, Heidi begins to tire. The students and tourists at the riding yard, inspired by Heidi's bravery, join the fight against the gang.

The battle becomes chaotic, with students and tourists using any means necessary to fight the gangsters. Even the horses become agitated, adding to the confusion. Despite the gangsters' fighting experience, they are outnumbered due to injuries, and Micky orders a retreat.

Alex observes the fight, noting that even underdogs can defeat seemingly stronger opponents. Micky is left behind by his fleeing gang members, much to the amusement of the victorious crowd. The students and tourists celebrate their victory, feeling proud of standing up to the bullies.

Heidi is admired by everyone for her role in the fight. When a student tries to learn more about her, Heidi reluctantly reveals her name and her father's name, Dr. Benedict Reels, but refuses to share more information. Her odd behavior and reluctance to share personal details amuse the crowd.
