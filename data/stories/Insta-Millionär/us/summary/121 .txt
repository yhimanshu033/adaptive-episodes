Alex visits Ramsey Lake daily, reminiscing about Debbie, who is still missing. He watches Preston University students graduate without her. Vivian calls Alex for help with Monica, as she needs to discuss the bar sale. Alex takes Monica to an amusement park and then to Sunrise Special Education School, where she interacts comfortably with other children.

At the school, Monica reveals to Alex that men visit their home at night, abusing both her and Vivian. Alex is shocked and angry upon learning this. While playing with friends, Monica suddenly disappears. Security footage shows two men luring her away with candy and abducting her. Vivian is distraught, and Alex promises to find Monica while Vivian calls the police.
