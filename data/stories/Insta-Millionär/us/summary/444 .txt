In this episode, Alex confronts Theo, a skilled martial arts master protecting the Mitchell family. Their intense battle leads to Theo throwing a grenade, causing the Mitchell villa to collapse. Most of the DominiCorp executives are buried in the rubble, with Dominic trapped but alive.

Alex and Theo continue their fight outside. Theo mentions a powerful master he once fought in the frozen north, but Alex reveals he has already killed this person, identified as Tyson Slade. This news shocks Theo.

Meanwhile, Oscar Steadman returns home and discusses his improved martial arts skills with his son Edward. They talk about recent events, including Ash Thornton's defeat and the ongoing conflict between the Mitchell and Ambrose families.

Their conversation is interrupted by the explosion at the Mitchell house. Using binoculars, they witness the intense battle between Alex and Theo, which Oscar describes as a fight between masters. The episode ends with Oscar watching the fight in disbelief.
