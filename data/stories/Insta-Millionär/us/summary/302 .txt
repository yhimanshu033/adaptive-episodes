Alex visits the university campus and observes students engaging in various activities. While watching Debbie's fitness class, Melissa approaches him with mushroom soup. She secretly adds poison to one of the bowls, intending to kill Alex. However, Debbie falls during her class, interrupting Alex before he can eat the soup. 

As they attend to Debbie, the soup bowls get mixed up. Melissa, unsure which bowl contains the poison, offers one to Debbie. Despite Callisto's suspicions, Debbie drinks the soup. Alex had already finished his bowl. Melissa, panicked and uncertain about who consumed the poison, flees the campus.

She meets Chris at a hotel, admitting her uncertainty about whether Alex or Debbie ingested the poison. Chris is angry and impatient, only concerned with Alex's potential death. Melissa tries to justify the situation, suggesting that even if Debbie dies, it would devastate Alex to the point where he might not want to live.
