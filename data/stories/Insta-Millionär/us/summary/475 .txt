Alex Ambrose is called to help Joe Hall's family, who have been poisoned. After saving them with an antidote, he realizes it's a trap set by the Ghosts. Harvey Kingston, a Ghost member, attacks Alex. They engage in a fierce battle, with Kingston using poisoned blades. Kingston reveals that Rose Scott is being held hostage by Jonah Walton, another Ghost. Alex pretends not to care about Rose to protect her.

The fight escalates, and Kingston manages to cut Alex with a blade coated in a powerful hallucinogenic and neurotoxic poison. Alex begins to hallucinate, seeing multiple versions of Kingston. Unable to defend himself effectively, Alex is repeatedly slashed by Kingston. Kingston gloats about his deadly poisons and stabs Alex in the stomach, claiming that Alex will die within three days if he doesn't bleed out first. The situation looks dire for Alex as he struggles to stay conscious under the effects of the poison.
