Alex arrives at the Baltimore Martial Arts Association headquarters after learning that Kenneth Petrie, an arbitrator from the American Martial Arts Association, has taken control of the building and beaten up several members. Russell explains that Kenneth has a long-standing grudge against their organization and is angry that Alex was elected president instead of his son.

Upon entering the hall, Alex finds four of his endorsed members beaten and on the floor. Kenneth, an elderly man with white hair and a long beard, sits in an ornate chair surrounded by his disciples. He orders Alex to kneel with the others, but Alex refuses and questions what rules he has broken.

Kenneth and his associate Ava accuse Alex of being foolish for accepting Jacob's challenge, stating that if he loses, it will ruin the reputation of both the Baltimore and American Martial Arts Associations. They claim Alex's decision affects more than just himself and could make them a laughingstock in martial arts circles worldwide if he is killed by the legendary master Jacob.

The situation is tense, with Kenneth's disciples ready to use force if Alex doesn't comply with their demands. Alex remains defiant, questioning Kenneth's authority and reasons for punishing him.
