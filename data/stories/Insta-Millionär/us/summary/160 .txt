Alex travels to Washington D.C. to search for Debbie. He spends hours searching the city using public transportation. Alex intervenes and resolves the situation by paying the difference.

The food truck vendor, a girl with burn scars and a bad temper, refuses to serve Alex despite his help. Alex sits nearby to rest, observing her interactions with customers. When he tries to order again, the girl angrily refuses, misinterpreting his earlier use of "beautiful" as mockery.

Alex apologizes, explaining it's a habit from college to address all girls that way. The girl softens slightly but expresses her dislike for comments on appearance. Alex empathizes, noting his own ordinary appearance and past experiences of being ridiculed. The episode ends with Alex reflecting on his own struggles and appearance, creating a moment of understanding between him and the scarred food truck vendor.
