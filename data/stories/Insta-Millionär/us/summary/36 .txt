Alex and Debbie have a heartfelt conversation by Ramsey Lake about their relationship and the revelation of Alex's wealth. Debbie initially feels unworthy, but Alex reassures her of his feelings. They share a romantic moment and their first kiss.

Later, they discover an abandoned phone, which Debbie decides to keep temporarily in case the owner claims it.

At the university, an emergency meeting is called. It's revealed that Mr. Morgan, a teacher and financial administrator, has had $350,000 stolen from the school's internship fund. Alex suggests crowdfunding to help, but his idea is dismissed and ridiculed.

Debbie, feeling concerned for Mr. Morgan who has helped her in the past, decides to visit him in the hospital. Alex, unable to join her, plans to anonymously return the money by pretending to be the thief and transferring the funds to Mr. Morgan's account.

Meanwhile, Cathy approaches Mr. Berkley, claiming to know who stole the money, setting up a potential new conflict.
