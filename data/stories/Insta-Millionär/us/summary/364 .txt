Alex rushes to the hospital to see Debbie, who is critically ill due to Tyson's freezing power. Debbie is barely responsive and close to death. Alex is devastated and blames himself for her condition. He announces he will fight Tyson in three days and asks everyone to leave the room.

Once alone with Debbie, Alex uses a newly learned power to warm her body, temporarily relieving her symptoms. Debbie regains some strength and they share an emotional moment. However, Alex struggles as he takes on some of Tyson's freezing power himself in an attempt to heal Debbie.

The situation remains critical as Tyson's power continues to fight against Alex's healing efforts. Debbie becomes alarmed as she sees Alex turning pale and his lips turning blue, realizing he is now suffering the effects of Tyson's power. She tries unsuccessfully to make Alex let go and stop taking on the damage himself.
