Alex, now wealthy, decides to invest in two apartment buildings in New York City worth $125 million after a brief tour with Robert Miller. He makes the decision quickly, surprising Miller with his decisiveness. After dropping Miller off, Alex checks on the search for Debbie, which yields no new information, leaving him worried and depressed.

Alex ignores and eventually blocks calls from his ex-girlfriend Cathy. He briefly hallucinates seeing Debbie before falling asleep. Later, Joe calls and asks Alex to accompany Suzan and Rose to the night market for safety reasons. Despite feeling awkward about Rose's dislike for him, Alex agrees to go.

Suzan informs Rose about Alex joining them, which upsets Rose. She recalls Alex helping her with an incident at the Berkeley Hotel, which had led them to briefly suspect he might be wealthy. However, they had dismissed this idea after failing to find evidence of his connection to Sam Woodsworth. Rose remains unhappy about Alex accompanying them to the night market.
