Alex visits Parkview Hospital with Maryann to see Sawyer Landon, a former high-ranking member of the Rumble Club who is now paralyzed and in poor health. Sawyer has just survived an assassination attempt but appears to have given up on life. Dr. Isaiah Allbrook, a respected physician, attempts to treat Sawyer using a special acupuncture technique called the Harmony Technique, but it fails to show any improvement.

Alex suggests that Sawyer's paralysis is caused by his state of mind rather than physical issues. This idea is met with skepticism and ridicule from other doctors present, including Isaiah's daughter Sophie, who has a previous negative encounter with Alex. Despite the hostility, Dr. Isaiah seems intrigued by Alex's perspective.

Frustrated with Sophie's attitude, Alex proposes a wager: if he can make Sawyer stand up, Sophie will have to be his maid for three years and do whatever he says. This bold challenge sets the stage for a potential demonstration of Alex's unique abilities and understanding of Sawyer's condition.
