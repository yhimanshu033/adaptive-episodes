Alex Ambrose, the new owner of Lawrence Heights, offers Sue Bradley the position of property manager after finding her in distress outside the building. Despite her initial confusion and reluctance, Alex insists on helping her, even tending to her injured feet. He explains the job details, including the salary and potential for growth.

Sue is skeptical about her ability to handle the job, but Alex expresses confidence in her. He buys her a pair of pink slippers, which she initially refuses to wear but eventually accepts. Alex helps Sue back to her apartment, where they have a conversation about relationships.

Sue asks Alex if he has a girlfriend, to which he responds negatively. However, when she inquires if there's someone he likes, Alex confirms there is, describing a kind and beautiful girl from Preston University with whom he has had misunderstandings. He expresses regret for causing her pain and uncertainty about her current feelings towards him.

As the conversation becomes emotionally difficult for Alex, he leaves abruptly, citing a headache. Sue is left alone, reflecting on Alex's gentleness and holding the pink slippers he bought her. She feels a mix of tenderness towards Alex and pain upon realizing he has feelings for someone else.
