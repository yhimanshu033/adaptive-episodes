Alex meets with Lynne, who warns him about the difficult students in Class 6 but also supports him against Billy and Leonard. Billy and Leonard plot to sabotage Alex by sending information about him to the students, hoping they'll make his first lesson impossible.

Meanwhile, in Class 6, the students are gossiping about a legendary figure known as "Master Alex." Lance Cartwright, a popular student, tells exaggerated stories about Master Alex's incredible feats and powers, captivating his classmates' attention. The students idolize this mysterious hero, unaware that he might be their new teacher.

Eric receives WhatsApp messages from Billy containing information about their new teacher, Alex Ambrose. The students dismiss the possibility that this new teacher could be the legendary Master Alex they've been discussing. Lance suggests playing a "game" with the new teacher.

It's revealed that Billy has been sending information about new teachers to Eric, possibly to gain favor with the students for future connections. The students believe that if they can get rid of their teachers, Billy will help them graduate with top grades, setting them up for life.
