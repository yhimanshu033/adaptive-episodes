Alex continues his training in the secret cave at the Moon Palace, guided by an old woman. His skills improve rapidly, and he notices physical changes in his appearance, looking healthier and more vibrant. Rufus arrives at the palace with urgent news about Debbie's condition, and Alex quickly leaves with him.

Meanwhile, news of the impending martial arts conflict between Tyson Slade and Alex spreads across the United States. Other martial arts groups are eager to observe the fight from a distance. Tyson and the Blood Brothers have set up camp along the Hudson River, waiting for Alex to appear. Jack, a Blood Brothers member, defeats several challengers, enhancing the group's reputation.

As weeks pass without Alex showing up, his reputation begins to suffer. Other martial artists start to doubt his abilities and mock him for not responding to Tyson's challenge. Rumors circulate about Tyson's attack on Debbie and Alex's apparent inaction.

Back at the Clifton residence, Jessop continues his desperate attempts to save Debbie's life, consulting numerous medical experts. However, none can find a solution to her condition, and they advise him to prepare for the worst.
