Alex Ambrose, after defeating the Thornton family attackers, continues his walk while Celia and Ethan are left stunned. A cleanup crew arrives to erase evidence of the fight.

Meanwhile, at the Mitchell family villa, Dominic Mitchell and his associates discuss their plan to destroy the Ambrose family. They have paid Wesley $15 million to report the Ambroses' illegal activities to the government. The Mitchells have also gained support from other powerful families and the Black Hand organization to dismantle the Ambrose empire.

The next morning, the Ambrose family faces a severe crisis. Their accounts are blocked, and key members are arrested. The stock market plummets, affecting their listed companies. Investors and business partners abandon them.

Justin calls Alex to inform him that the family is ruined. Banks demand immediate loan repayments, and companies cancel contracts with the Ambroses. The once-powerful family now faces complete destruction, leaving Alex in a difficult position to save them.
