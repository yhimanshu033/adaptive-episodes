Alex and Philipa share a meal at the university canteen, with Alex feeling conflicted about his growing feelings for her. Over the next few days, Alex attends dance rehearsals for the university's New Year's party, hoping to interact with Leona, who resembles his missing girlfriend Debbie. However, Leona seems to avoid him, and Colin Patterson, a wealthy and popular student, keeps them apart.

During a rehearsal, Alex accidentally falls from the rostrum while watching Leona sing. Embarrassed, he tries to leave, but Leona approaches to help him with his injury. As she tends to his wound, Alex is reminded of Debbie's care for him in the past, causing him to cry and call Leona "Debbie." This confuses and upsets Leona.

Later, Colin invites the group to lunch at a Michelin three-star restaurant. When deciding whether to include Alex, Leona suggests leaving him behind, citing his presumed inability to afford the meal. This decision leaves Alex feeling hurt and excluded, while Leona regrets her words but cannot take them back.

Throughout the episode, Alex struggles with his feelings for both Philipa and Leona (whom he believes might be Debbie), while navigating the social dynamics and financial disparities among his peers at Richmond University.
