Alex continues to build a friendship with Slayer, whose real name is revealed to be Daniel Wiseman (Danny). Alex convinces Danny that he sees him as an equal and a friend, which deeply affects Danny, who has longed for friendship. Danny promises not to harm Alex or let him be bullied.

Alex, Vivian, and Monica go to the police station to report Monica's abduction. However, the suspects are unexpectedly released due to pressure from higher authorities. The police commissioner, Hargreaves, apologizes but says it's out of his hands. The suspects mention working for someone named Dale Granger and threaten Monica again. Alex confronts and punches the suspects before leaving with Vivian and Monica.

Later, at Vivian's home, Alex confronts her about the men who have been assaulting her. Vivian reveals that she was previously in a relationship with a leader of the Azure Dragon Society. When the society was destroyed by someone from a powerful family, her partner had to leave New York, leaving her without protection. Since then, various gangs have been harassing her and demanding protection fees.

Alex realizes that he and Ken Stokes were responsible for dismantling the Azure Dragon Society, inadvertently causing Vivian's current predicament. Vivian expresses anger towards the person who forced her partner away, unaware that it was Alex himself.
